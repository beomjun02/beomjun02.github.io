  • World-Language-Action Model for Unified World Modeling, Language Reasoning, and Action Synthesis
    arXiv · 2026-06-06 · world-action model · VLAA new class of embodied foundation model that jointly predicts textual subtasks, subgoal images, and robot actions from an autoregressive Transformer backbone.   • 🆕 MoDex: A Diffusion Policy for Sequential Multi-Object Dexterous Grasping
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-08 · dexterous manipulationA diffusion policy for sequentially grasping multiple objects with a single dexterous hand without releasing already-held items. Uses opposition-space conditioning to allocate finger subsets per grasp while reserving remaining DOFs for subsequent grasps. Trained via imitation + RL fine-tuning on Allegro Hand, achieving 6.67–17.78% real-world success rate improvement over baselines.
