Papers

  • EquiVLA: SO(2)-Equivariant Vision-Language-Action Models — First general framework for end-to-end SO(2)-equivariant VLA models, applicable to any architecture with a frozen VL backbone and a flow-matching DiT action head. Introduces EquiPerceptor (approximately equivariant visual representations from frozen ViTs) and EquiActor (exactly equivariant flow-matching DiT). Applied to GR00T N1.5 across LIBERO (92.6% vs 78.1% baseline), CALVIN ABCD→D (4.03 vs 3.45 avg sequence length), and five real Mobile ALOHA tasks (72% vs 54%). (arXiv:2606.19784
  • SWAP: Symmetric Equivariant World Model for Agile Robot Parkour — Embeds left-right symmetry directly into both world model and actor-critic networks for quadruped locomotion. In real-world deployment, the robot leaps across a 2.13m gap and climbs a 1.63m platform — breaking records for quadruped parkour. Demonstrates robust geometric generalization to unseen mirrored terrains and zero-shot transfer across diverse outdoor environments. (arXiv:2606.19928
  • PAINT: Training-Free Asynchronous Execution via Initial Noise Selection — Reframes asynchronous inference for flow-based robot policies as a noise selection problem rather than trajectory steering. Uses backward Euler inversion to find initial noise that produces coherent next action chunks without modifying the policy. Requires no retraining; improves execution consistency across 12 simulated benchmarks and 6 real-world tasks spanning single-arm, bimanual, and humanoid embodiments. (arXiv:2606.19774
  • RATs: Playful Agentic Robot Learning — An embodied coding agent uses self-directed play to build a persistent code skill library before downstream tasks arrive. During play, it proposes tasks, executes policies, diagnoses failures with step-level feedback, and distills successes into reusable skills. Frozen play-learned skills improve held-out tasks by 20.6pp over no-play baselines and transfer to other Code-as-Policy agents (+8.9pp in RoboSuite, +8.8pp real-world). (arXiv:2606.19419
  • Pose6DAug: 6D Object Swapping for VLA Data Augmentation — Turns a VLA policy's own successful episodes into targeted demonstrations for its failure modes on novel objects, with zero new data collection. Operates in 3D using explicit mesh driven by temporally coherent 6D pose trajectories, ensuring multi-view consistency across all cameras. Fine-tuning on augmented data improves VLA success rates by 16.5% on novel objects over SOTA baseline while preserving in-distribution performance. (arXiv:2606.20118
  • Frequency-Aware Flow Matching for Robotic Manipulation — Transforms discrete action sequences into frequency domain via DCT, performs flow matching over coefficients, and reconstructs continuous actions via cosine basis expansion. A Sobolev-type regularizer suppresses high-frequency errors. Applies to standalone flow policies and VLA models with no additional parameters. Consistent gains on LIBERO, LapGym, obstacle avoidance, and real Franka robot in success rates, smoothness, and robustness to mixed-frequency input. (arXiv:2606.20135
  • Temporal Self-Imitation Learning (TSIL) — Mines temporally efficient successful trajectories generated during RL training and converts them into reusable supervision for future policy improvement. Uses configuration-conditioned adaptive temporal targets from fast trajectories and efficiency-weighted self-imitation learning. Consistently improves learning efficiency, task-completion speed, and robustness across 15 long-horizon manipulation tasks. (arXiv:2606.19752
  • One Demo is Worth a Thousand Trajectories: Action-View Augmentation — Generates visually realistic fisheye image sequences and physically feasible action trajectories from real-world eye-in-hand demonstrations using a single portable parallel gripper with a fisheye camera. Introduces a novel Gaussian Splatting formulation adapted to wide-FoV fisheye cameras for 3D scene reconstruction and editing with unseen objects. Trajectory optimization generates smooth, collision-free paths for novel-view rendering. (arXiv:2606.19586
