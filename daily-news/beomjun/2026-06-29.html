  • 🆕 **ManipArena: Reasoning-Oriented Robot Manipulation Dataset

  • 🆕 **jaxipm: GPU-Batched Nonlinear Optimizer for Robotics

Papers

  • 🆕 **TaskNPoint: How to Teach Your Humanoid to Hit a Backhand in Minutes
    A coach-learner protocol where a human provides one demo per skill and marks the key interaction window; RL in simulation fills in the rest. A Unitree G1 humanoid learns forehands, backhands, soccer kicks, and pick-and-place from short video demos in under an hour on a single GPU, with zero-shot sim-to-real transfer.
  • 🆕 **OmniContact: Humanoid Loco-Manipulation via Contact Flow
    A hierarchical framework chaining humanoid loco-manipulation skills using contact flow — compact key-body trajectories plus binary contact signals — as the interface between a low-level tracking policy and a high-level planner. Achieves 98.7% success on box carrying and 76.5% on push-stack, outperforming baselines by 40–67%.
  • 🆕 **CoStream: Composing Simple Behaviors for Complex Manipulation
    Complex manipulation capabilities emerge from composing simple, independent behavior streams — semantic (foundation-model spatial constraints), predictive (keypoint-tracking video imagination), and reactive (tactile/force corrections) — multiplied into a single SE(3) command per step. Demonstrated on 8 real-world tasks including precision GPU-into-PCIe-slot assembly.
  • 🆕 **Fast LeWorldModel: Faster Latent Planning via Action-Prefix Prediction
    Replaces the expensive autoregressive rollout in JEPA-based world models with action-prefix prediction — encoding all candidate action prefixes in parallel and predicting resulting future latents in one shot. Improves success rate over LeWM while substantially cutting planning time and slowing latent error growth.
  • 🆕 **BRIDGE: Bridging Handheld and Teleoperated Data for Contact-Rich Manipulation
    Addresses the data dilemma: handheld systems (like UMI) scale but lack desired-action supervision during contact, while teleoperation is precise but costly. BRIDGE routes between specialist diffusion-policy heads based on robot state — handheld data handles free-space phases, targeted teleop demos handle contact — lifting success by up to 36.7%.
  • 🆕 **SSI-Policy: Structured Scene Interface for VLA Manipulation
    An RGB-only intermediate representation jointly encoding monocular depth, language-grounded object layouts, and 2D motion trajectories — robot-agnostic and trainable from action-free video. On LIBERO with only 10 demonstrations per task, improves over the strongest prior method by ~15% and rivals 50-demo methods using large-scale pretraining.
  • **Play2Perfect: What Matters in Dexterous Play Pretraining for Precise Assembly?
    Investigates play pretraining for multi-fingered dexterous robots on precise assembly — contact-rich, sparse-reward tasks that have remained out of reach. Shows that unstructured play exploration builds foundational manipulation skills that transfer to assembly tasks without specialized grippers, tool attachments, or environment fixtures.
  • **Tactile-WAM: Touch-Aware World Action Model with Tactile Asymmetric Attention
    Extends World Action Models with tactile sensing for contact-rich manipulation. Identifies and addresses "tactile pollution" — a failure mode where fusing raw touch signals degrades visual predictions. Uses asymmetric attention to integrate slip, jamming, and contact normals for insertion, assembly, and reorientation tasks.
  • 🆕 **Ace: A Table Tennis Robot That Beats Professional Players
    A custom 8-DOF robot designed from inverse-dynamics analysis of elite human players, with topology-optimized mechanics for minimal mass and peak racket velocity of 22 m/s. RL control policy trained in simulation with identified per-joint dynamics; the system defeated multiple professional table tennis players.
