  • 🆕 Q-Guided Flow: RL for diffusion/flow policies via identity Jacobian
    Levine group (UC Berkeley) · 2026-06-10 · robot learningShows that optimizing flow matching actors with an identity Jacobian "approximation" outperforms the true Jacobian and BPTT for RL. QGF is entirely a test-time method: BC-trained policy + TD-trained Q-function, with Q-function optimization at inference. Opens a practical path for RL-tuning of diffusion/flow-based robot policies.
  • 🆕 Dexterous Point Policy: Learning from Human Videos Without Robot Demos
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-11 · dexterous manipulation · point-based modelA framework using unified 3D keypoint representation to bridge human and robot embodiments for dexterous manipulation. At the wrist and fingertip level, human and robot behaviors closely align, enabling direct policy transfer from human video. Achieves 75% success on real-robot tasks versus 1% for a SOTA VLA baseline — no robot demonstrations needed.
  • 🆕 Efficient-WAM: A 1B-Parameter World-Action Model with 30x Speedup
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-11 · world-action modelReduces inference cost of world-action models by treating future video prediction as a compact guidance signal rather than optimizing for visual fidelity. Uses a compact video expert from WAN-2.2-5B, token-sparse latents, and asymmetric denoising. Achieves ~100ms per-chunk latency in real deployment — 30x faster than existing WAMs while maintaining competitive manipulation performance.
  • 🆕 YUBI: Finger-Aligned Gripper for Bimanual Dexterous Manipulation at Scale
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-11 · dexterous manipulationIntroduces a finger-driven gripper that maps human finger movements directly to gripper jaw motion, enabling ergonomic data collection at unprecedented scale: 8,434 hours across 1.20M episodes and 119 tasks.   • 🆕 What Matters in Orchestrating Robot Policies: A Systematic Study of Hi-VLA Agents

  • 🆕 Flow Control: Steering VLA Models with Simple Real-Time Inputs
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-11 · VLAIntroduces a method to steer VLA actions in real-time through generic inputs like a keyboard — no retraining needed. Enables significantly higher success rates and faster task completion; fine-tuning on steered trajectories further improves the autonomous policy.
  • 🆕 OMG: Omni-Modal Motion Generation for Generalist Humanoid Control
