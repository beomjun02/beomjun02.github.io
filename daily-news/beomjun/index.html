  • 🆕 PhysVLA: Towards Physically-Grounded VLA
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-16 · VLAA plug-and-play inference-time framework that wraps any frozen VLA with physics-aware corrections — a phase-aware FSM for task structure and a selective Euler-Lagrange gate for kinodynamic consistency.   • 🆕 ReactVLA: Fast and Lightweight Reactive VLA

  • 🆕 ContactWorld: Vision-Tactile World Models for Contact-Rich Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-16 · world modelA benchmark and systematic study of vision-tactile world models across 12 contact-rich manipulation tasks. Finds that spatially structured, temporally continuous representations work best: point-cloud observations improve planning success from ~21% to 32%, and adding tactile force-fields pushes it to 36%. Tactile sensing importance grows with planning horizon.
  • 🆕 Self-Improving VLA Policies via Selected Diffusion Noise

  • 🆕 Output-Level Regularization Eliminates the Seed Lottery in VLA Fine-Tuning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-16 · VLAIdentifies a hidden danger in single-GPU VLA fine-tuning: 1 in 13 random seeds silently degrades success by 29 pp due to output collapse. Traces the cause to the Jacobian null-space and shows that output-level regularizers (VICReg, Dropout, halved LR) eliminate every catastrophic seed, while weight-level methods (L2, EWC) do not.
