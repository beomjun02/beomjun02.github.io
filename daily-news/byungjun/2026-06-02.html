  • 🆕 Feat2Go: Visual Feature-Grounded Value Estimation for Embodied RL

  • 🆕 ELAN4D: Embodiment-Centric 4D Supervision for VLA Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-02 · VLAEnhances VLA robustness by injecting future robot keypoint track predictions as auxiliary 4D supervision during training. The plug-and-play decoder is discarded at inference, adding zero overhead while achieving substantial gains under camera, background, and layout distribution shifts on LIBERO, LIBERO-Plus, and RoboTwin2.0.
  • 🆕 HARP-VLA: Human-Robot Aligned Representation Learning for VLA Models

  • 🆕 DeMaVLA: A VLA Foundation Model for Generalizable Deformable Manipulation
