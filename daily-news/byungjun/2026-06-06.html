  • 🆕 Auditing Demonstration Curation Metrics: Action-Only Scorers Fail on Structural Defects
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06 · Robot Data annotationBuilds a controlled testbed to audit seven demo curation metrics along two axes: separation of defective from clean demos, and downstream policy success. Key finding: structural errors (wrong action at a key moment) are invisible to every action-only metric tested, and two metrics are inverted — scoring defective demos as higher quality. Only state-trajectory metrics detect structural errors, and even the best recovers just a third of the downstream gap.
