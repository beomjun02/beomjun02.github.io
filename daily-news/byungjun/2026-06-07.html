  • 🆕 ActiveMimic: Egocentric Video Pretraining with Active Perception
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-07 · VLAA pretraining framework that recovers synchronized camera and wrist trajectories from a single body-worn RGB camera to jointly learn active perception and manipulation from in-the-wild egocentric human video. Real-world experiments show it matches SOTA robot-data-pretrained models — confirming that active perception (viewpoint repositioning during manipulation) is the key missing signal in egocentric pretraining.
  • 🆕 VOLT: Vision and Language Trajectory Segmentation for Faster-than-Demonstration Policies
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-07 · SubtaskA vision-and-language trajectory segmentation method that identifies which demonstration segments can safely be sped up versus which require slower, precise motion (e.g., contact events). Selective downsampling of speed-safe segments yields faster robot execution while maintaining task success — with ablations showing segmentation quality is critical for the speed-accuracy tradeoff.
  • 🆕 DexFuture: Hierarchical Future-State Visuomotor Targeting for Bimanual Dexterous Tool Use
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-07 · Hierarchical VLAA hierarchical system coupling a high-level future-state visuomotor target predictor with a low-level target-conditioned per-link dexterous policy for bimanual tool use. Decouples coarse future reference generation from fine-grained action control, running at 60 Hz — approximately 250x faster than CEM-style planning — achieving 90% of privileged-oracle performance on OakInk2 tasks.
  • 🆕 Dream.exe: Can Video Generation Models Dream Executable Robot Manipulation?
    HF papers · 2026-06-06An evaluation framework that tests whether video generation models encode actionable physical knowledge by converting synthesized manipulation videos into robot trajectories and executing them in simulation. Benchmarks 8 models across 101 tasks — finding that visual quality is a poor predictor of executability, revealing a capability dimension missed by standard visual evaluations.
  • 🆕 LadderMan: Learning Humanoid Perceptive Ladder Climbing
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-07 · long-horizon planningA unified humanoid system for robust ladder climbing and on-ladder manipulation via a two-stage pipeline: hybrid motion tracking to learn multiple climbing experts from a single reference, distilled into a depth-based visuomotor policy. Achieves zero-shot sim-to-real transfer across diverse ladder geometries and supports on-ladder manipulation via teleoperation.
  • World-Language-Action Model for Unified World Modeling, Language Reasoning, and Action Synthesis
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06 · VLA · SubtaskA new class of embodied foundation model that jointly predicts textual subtasks, subgoal images, and robot actions from an autoregressive Transformer backbone. A dedicated World Expert supervises physical dynamics while meta-queries let the world prediction implicitly guide action generation — or be activated at test time for improved control. WLA-0 (2B active params) hits 92.94% on RoboTwin2.0 Clean and 56.5% on RMBench at 40ms inference on an RTX 5090, and can learn from cross-embodiment robot videos without action annotations.
  • AffordanceVLA: Action Generation through Affordance-Aware Understanding
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06 · VLAIntroduces structured affordance forecasting as an intermediate representation to bridge the VLM-to-action gap. Three progressive modules — object-centric grounding (Which2Act), 2D interaction localization (Where2Act), and 3D geometric reasoning (How2Act) — are integrated into a Mixture-of-Transformer architecture. Includes an automated data augmentation pipeline to overcome the scarcity of dense affordance labels in robotic datasets.
  • MPCoT: Reward-Guided Multi-Path Latent Reasoning for Test-Time Scalable VLA
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06 · VLAInitializes M latent hypotheses, refines them for K weight-tied steps, and softly aggregates before action decoding — all without generating a single reasoning token. A training-only path-preference objective aligns the latent path scorer with downstream execution quality. Preserves the standard 8-step action interface while improving long-horizon performance on LIBERO and CALVIN with configurable inference-time compute (K, M).
  • Flash-WAM: Modality-Aware Distillation for World Action Models
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-06 · VLACompresses world-action model inference from tens of denoising steps to a single step per modality via consistency distillation with modality-aware parametrization. On LingBot-VA, reduces per-chunk latency from 8.1s to 348ms (23× speedup) on an L40S, enabling real-time inference. Preserves 85.5% task success on RoboTwin 2.0 and 60% on a real Unitree G1 humanoid, while naive consistency distillation drops to 24%.
