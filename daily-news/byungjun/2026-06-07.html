  • Flash-WAM: Modality-Aware Distillation for World Action Models

  • FlowPRO: Reward-Free Reinforced Fine-Tuning of Flow-Matching VLAs via Proximalized Preference Optimization
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06 · VLAA reward-free offline reinforcement fine-tuning framework for flow-matching VLAs that uses preference optimization with a proximal regularizer to eliminate reward hacking.   • MPCoT: Reward-Guided Multi-Path Latent Reasoning for Test-Time Scalable Vision-Language-Action
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06 · VLAIntroduces multi-path latent reasoning for VLAs that initializes M hypotheses, refines them for K weight-tied steps, and softly aggregates before action decoding — all without generating reasoning tokens. Improves long-horizon performance on LIBERO and CALVIN with configurable inference-time compute scaling.
  • AffordanceVLA: A Vision-Language-Action Model Empowering Action Generation through Affordance-Aware Understanding
