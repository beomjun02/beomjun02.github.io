  • 🆕 World-Language-Action Model for Unified World Modeling, Language Reasoning, and Action Synthesis
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-08 · VLA · SubtaskA new class of embodied foundation model that jointly predicts textual subtasks, subgoal images, and robot actions from an autoregressive Transformer backbone.     arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-08 · VLAIntroduces structured affordance forecasting as an intermediate representation for VLA models through three components: Which2Act (object grounding via visual latent prediction), Where2Act (2D interaction localization via affordance maps), and How2Act (3D geometric reasoning).   • 🆕 FlowPRO: Reward-Free Reinforced Fine-Tuning of Flow-Matching VLAs via Proximalized Preference Optimization
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-08 · VLAA reward-free offline RL fine-tuning framework for flow-matching VLAs. The RPRO objective pairs contrastive optimization with a proximal regularizer to eliminate reward-hacking, while a teleoperated intervention-and-rollback procedure generates naturally paired positive/negative trajectories from a single operator action. Achieves highest success rate on four long-horizon bimanual tasks.
  • 🆕 MPCoT: Reward-Guided Multi-Path Latent Reasoning for Test-Time Scalable Vision-Language-Action
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-08 · VLAInitializes M reasoning hypotheses, refines them for K weight-tied steps, and softly aggregates before action decoding — all in latent space with zero reasoning tokens generated. A training-only path-preference objective uses expert-action consistency and world-model progress feedback. Preserves the standard 8-step action interface while improving long-horizon performance on LIBERO and CALVIN.
  • 🆕 PiL-World: A Chunk-Wise World Model for VLA Policy-in-the-Loop Evaluation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-08 · VLAA chunk-wise world model that enables closed-loop VLA evaluation without real robot execution by alternating between VLA inference and world-model prediction. Conditions generation on action-derived visual control and latent histories, and learns from both successful and failed trajectories. Reduces the gap between estimated and real-world VLA success rates from 63.2% to 12.0%.
  • 🆕 MotionDisco: Motion Discovery for Extreme Humanoid Loco-Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-08 · long-horizon planningDiscovers contact-rich, long-horizon humanoid loco-manipulation motions from scratch — no teleoperation or motion retargeting required.   • 🆕 DexFuture: Hierarchical Future-State Visuomotor Targeting for Bimanual Dexterous Tool Use
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-08 · Hierarchical VLAA hierarchical system pairing a high-level Future-State Visuomotor Target Predictor with a low-level Target-Conditioned Structured Dexterous Policy. The high-level transformer generates multi-step future targets from egocentric RGB and proprioceptive history, while the low-level per-link transformer tracks them.
