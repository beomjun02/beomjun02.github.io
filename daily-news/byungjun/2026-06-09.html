  • 🆕 Coarse-to-Control: Action-Token Planning for Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · VLA · long-horizon planningA plan-execute VLA that introduces planning natively in the action-token space. The policy first predicts coarse action tokens summarizing the intended trajectory, then generates executable actions conditioned on this plan. Because planning and execution share a unified discrete vocabulary, the plan stays directly actionable. Largest gains on long-horizon multi-stage tasks across LIBERO, SimplerEnv-WidowX, and real-world Franka manipulation.
  • 🆕 ActionMap: Robot Policy Learning via Voxel Action Heatmap
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · VLAA voxel heatmap action head that drops into existing VLAs in place of their native decoder. Each voxel directly stores the probability of the corresponding action, exploiting geometric proximity of neighboring actions during training. Surpasses two architecturally distinct backbones (+8.2% over OpenVLA-OFT on LIBERO four-suite average) and is markedly more data-efficient at low training data.
  • 🆕 Robots Need More than VLA and World Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · VLAPosition paper arguing the VLA-scaling framing is incomplete. The central bottleneck is not only policy learning but the absence of mechanisms that convert unstructured behavioral data into grounded robot supervision. Identifies four missing interfaces: data interfaces for autolabelling, embodiment interfaces for retargeting, world-model interfaces for 3D reasoning, and reward interfaces for inferring task progress from video and language.
  • 🆕 AdaWAM: Advancing World Action Models with Adaptive Multi-Modal Reasoning

  • 🆕 AEGIS: A Backup Reflex for Physical AI
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-09 · long-horizon planningA selective escalation method for long-horizon manipulation that probes a weak policy's frozen activations to detect high-risk steps while recovery is still possible. When flagged, control switches to a stronger policy only for the steps that need it. Recovers 10.1% of failing trajectories on LIBERO-Spatial versus 4.6% for budget-matched blind escalation, activating the stronger policy on only 38% of steps.
