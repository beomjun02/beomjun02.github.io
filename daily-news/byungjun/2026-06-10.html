  • 🆕 Q-VGM: Q-Guided Value-Gradient Matching for Flow-Matching VLA Policies
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-10 · VLAAn off-policy RL method that fine-tunes flow-matching VLA policies via a value-gradient field, avoiding both likelihood estimation and backpropagation through the denoising chain. Starting from a few-shot-SFT π0.5 VLA, Q-VGM raises average success from 75.0% to 92.5% on LIBERO and from 40.0% to 67.5% on real-robot tasks — outperforming all same-backbone baselines.
  • 🆕 MotionVLA: Injecting Geometric Motion into Vision-Language-Action Model
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-10 · VLAReplaces sparse frame-level history in VLAs with a motion-history interface that converts a short past-only video window into compact trajectory-field tokens. Rather than remembering individual past frames, MotionVLA encodes physically coherent motion evidence, yielding smoother long-horizon executions and improved success on simulation and real-robot tasks.
  • 🆕 VoLo: A Physical Orchestrator for Open-Vocabulary Long-Horizon Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-10 · long-horizon planningFrames long-horizon manipulation as "Physical Orchestration" — a VLM steers a VLA/WAM as an interruptible tool alongside vision models and action primitives in a closed agent loop. Introduces RoboVoLo, a high-fidelity benchmark with common sense, memory tracking, and world knowledge tasks. Substantially outperforms single VLA/VLM systems with real-robot validation.
  • 🆕 TBD-VLA: Temporal Block Diffusion Vision Language Action Model
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-10 · VLAA discrete token-based VLA that partitions action sequences into temporal blocks and runs masked discrete diffusion within each block while keeping autoregressive generation across blocks. Unifies temporal coherence with parallel decoding for faster inference, and enables asynchronous action chunk execution via temporal in-painting. Outperforms prior VLAs in simulation and real-world manipulation.
  • 🆕 What Makes Video World Model Latents Action-Relevant: Prediction over Reconstruction
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-10Systematic probe-based evaluation across encoder families finds that action-relevant structure in world model latents is driven by temporal video pretraining — not reconstruction fidelity. Models with strong pixel decoding can exhibit near-zero action recoverability, while video-pretrained self-supervised encoders achieve the best fidelity/action-prediction trade-off.
  • Coarse-to-Control: Action-Token Planning for Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · VLAA plan-execute VLA that introduces planning natively in the action-token space. The policy first predicts coarse action tokens summarizing the intended trajectory, then generates executable actions conditioned on this plan. Because planning and execution share a unified discrete vocabulary, the plan stays directly actionable. Largest gains on long-horizon multi-stage tasks across LIBERO, SimplerEnv-WidowX, and real-world Franka manipulation.
  • AdaWAM: Advancing World Action Models with Adaptive Multi-Modal Reasoning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · VLAA world action model with adaptive multimodal reasoning that triggers textual reasoning during task transitions for high-level planning and visual reasoning during fine-grained manipulation for precise control. A lightweight dynamic router autonomously selects the reasoning mode, improving inference efficiency while outperforming state-of-the-art embodied policies on both simulated and real-world tasks.
  • ActionMap: Robot Policy Learning via Voxel Action Heatmap
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · VLAA voxel heatmap action head that drops into existing VLAs in place of their native decoder. Each voxel directly stores the probability of the corresponding action, exploiting geometric proximity of neighboring actions during training. Surpasses two architecturally distinct backbones (+8.2% over OpenVLA-OFT on LIBERO four-suite average) and is markedly more data-efficient at low training data.
  • Robots Need More than VLA and World Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · VLAPosition paper arguing the VLA-scaling framing is incomplete. The central bottleneck is not only policy learning but the absence of mechanisms that convert unstructured behavioral data into grounded robot supervision. Identifies four missing interfaces: data interfaces for autolabelling, embodiment interfaces for retargeting, world-model interfaces for 3D reasoning, and reward interfaces for inferring task progress from video and language.
