Papers

  • 🆕 Dexterous Point Policy: Learning Point-based Dexterous Hand Policies from Human Demonstrations
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-11 · VLAA framework that learns dexterous manipulation policies directly from human videos without any robot demonstrations. Uses unified 3D keypoint representations (wrist + fingertips) to bridge human and robot embodiments, achieving 75.0% real-robot success across pick-and-place and tool-use tasks — vs 1.0% for a SOTA VLA baseline. Generalizes to unseen multi-object environments and novel object categories.
  • 🆕 What Matters in Orchestrating Robot Policies: A Systematic Study of Hierarchical VLA Agents
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-11 · Hierarchical VLAFirst unified study of Hierarchical VLA system design. Benchmarks core choices — planner model, controller model, switching mechanism, observation/memory representation — across short-horizon, long-horizon, and reasoning-intensive tasks under an options-style control framework. Distills practical design principles that yield a system substantially stronger than both flat VLA control and naive hierarchies, validated in simulation and on a real ALOHA robot.
  • 🆕 GHOST: Hierarchical Sub-Goal Policies for Generalizing Robot Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-11 · Hierarchical VLAFactorizes control into a high-level policy predicting 3D end-effector sub-goals and a low-level goal-conditioned controller. A spatial interface projects goals as end-effector heatmaps in the image plane. Enables cross-embodiment transfer: train the high-level on human video for specifying skill composition, low-level purely on robot data. Consistently outperforms flat Diffusion Policy across manipulation tasks.
  • 🆕 SARM2: Multi-Task Stage Aware Reward Modeling for Self Improving Robotic Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-11 · SubtaskA multi-task stage-aware reward model combining an action-primitive-based stage estimator with a multi-gate MoE value head for dense per-step rewards across manipulation tasks. Paired with SPIRAL, an on-policy reward-guided framework, it improves VLA task success from ~50% to near-perfect on benchmark tasks (Folding Shorts 58%→100%, Cleaning Whiteboard 50%→90%), reducing value-estimation MSE by 80%.
  • 🆕 HiMem-WAM: Hierarchical Memory-Gated World Action Models for Robotic Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-11 · long-horizon planningAddresses the memory bottleneck in World Action Models for long-horizon manipulation. Introduces hierarchical latent actions (low-level motion + high-level skill latents) with a boundary-aware memory gate that writes compact task states at skill transitions. Enables causal inference without test-time video generation. Validated on LIBERO, LIBERO-PLUS, RMBench, and real-world tasks.
  • 🆕 Efficient-WAM: A 1B-Parameter World-Action Model with Low-Cost Future Imagination
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-11Reduces WAM inference cost via a compact video expert (distilled from WAN-2.2-5B), token-sparse video latents, and asymmetric video-action denoising. Treats future video prediction as a compact guidance signal rather than optimizing for visual fidelity. At 1B parameters, achieves ~100ms per-chunk latency during deployment — a 30x speedup over existing WAMs — while maintaining competitive manipulation performance.
  • 🆕 Flow Control: Steering Vision-Language-Action Models with Simple Real-Time Inputs
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-11 · VLAA training-free method that lets users steer frozen VLA policies in real time via simple inputs like a keyboard. User inputs are transformed into high-quality action samples from the VLA's learned expert distribution. Robust to suboptimal inputs, significantly boosts success rates and task completion speed, and fine-tuning on flow-control trajectories further improves the autonomous policy.
  • 🆕 YUBI: Yielding Universal Bidigital Interface for Bimanual Dexterous Manipulation at Scale
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-11 · Robot Data annoatationA finger-aligned gripper that maps human finger movements directly to jaw motion, enabling intuitive data collection for dexterous manipulation. Paired with VR-based 6-DoF tracking, it produces a UMI-based dataset of 8,434 hours across 1.20M episodes and 119 tasks. A single policy trained on YUBI data transfers across UR, Franka, and ELEY robots by simply mounting the gripper.
  • 🆕 UniDexTok: A Unified Dexterous Hand Tokenizer from Real Data
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-11Maps human and diverse robot hand states into a shared 22-DoF semantic interface, then learns embodiment-conditioned discrete tokens from standardized joint states — no retargeting or simulation needed. Reduces joint-angle and joint-position errors by ~99% over the UniHM baseline, achieving sub-millimeter reconstruction accuracy. Cross-embodiment data improves target-embodiment reconstruction, with strong zero-shot transfer to new dexterous hands.
