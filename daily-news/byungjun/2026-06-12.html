  • 🆕 Embodied-R1.5: Evolving Physical Intelligence via Embodied Foundation Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-12 · VLAA unified 8B-parameter Embodied Foundation Model integrating cognition, planning, correction, and pointing within a single architecture. With 15B+ tokens of training data and a multi-task balanced RL recipe, it achieves SOTA on 16 of 24 embodied VLM benchmarks — surpassing Gemini-Robotics-ER-1.5 and GPT-5.4. When fine-tuned into a VLA, it outperforms pi-0.5 across 4 manipulation benchmark suites. Fully open-sourced.
  • 🆕 DAM-VLA: Decoupled Asynchronous Multimodal Vision Language Action model
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-12 · VLAChallenges the synchronous-clock assumption inherited from VL pretraining by letting each modality (vision, language, proprioception) update at its own sensor rate via per-modality latent buffers. On seven contact-rich real-world manipulation tasks, DAM-VLA more than doubles the best synchronous baseline's success rate (95.2% vs 40.95%) while sustaining 100 Hz control.
  • 🆕 APT: Action Expert Pretraining Improves Instruction Generalization of Vision-Language-Action Policies
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-12 · VLAAddresses the poor out-of-distribution language generalization of continuous-action VLAs by factorizing the policy into a language-agnostic vision-action prior (pretrained separately) and a language-conditioned likelihood injected via gated fusion. Applies to both pi-style and GR00T-style architectures with consistent gains on unseen instructions and compositional tasks.
  • 🆕 CHORUS: Decentralized Multi-Embodiment Collaboration with One VLA Policy
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-12 · VLAAdapts a single VLA backbone to control diverse, decentralized multi-robot teams — each robot runs an independent copy conditioned only on its own observations and a robot-identifying prompt. In real-world experiments including mobile tape measurement and library book handovers, CHORUS achieves a 64pp improvement over decentralized from-scratch models and outperforms centralized baselines.
  • 🆕 InDex: Bridging the Morphology Gap — Adapting VLA Models to Dexterous Manipulation via Intent-Conditioned Fine-Tuning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-12 · VLARepurposes the pre-trained 1-DoF parallel grasp output as a continuous virtual grasp intent proxy to bridge VLA priors to high-DoF dexterous hands. A two-stage decoupled architecture — parameter-efficient arm/intent alignment followed by an intent-conditioned diffusion head for multi-fingered joints — substantially outperforms monolithic baselines on multi-stage contact-rich dexterous tasks.
  • 🆕 DEHP: Dynamic Execution Horizon Prediction for Chunk-based Robot Policies
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-12 · VLA · long-horizon planningTrains a lightweight RL branch to dynamically predict how many steps of an action chunk to execute before replanning — shorter horizons during fine-grained stages, longer during free-space motion — while keeping the pretrained chunk policy completely frozen. Compatible with black-box VLAs and diffusion policies; large success-rate gains on high-precision and long-horizon manipulation tasks.
  • What Matters in Orchestrating Robot Policies: A Systematic Study of Hierarchical VLA Agents
    arXiv · 2026-06-11 · Hierarchical VLAFirst unified study of Hierarchical VLA system design. Benchmarks core choices — planner model, controller model, switching mechanism, observation/memory representation — across short-horizon, long-horizon, and reasoning-intensive tasks under an options-style control framework. Distills practical design principles that yield a system substantially stronger than both flat VLA control and naive hierarchies, validated in simulation and on a real ALOHA robot.
  • Dexterous Point Policy: Learning Point-based Dexterous Hand Policies from Human Demonstrations
    arXiv · 2026-06-11A framework that learns dexterous manipulation policies directly from human videos without any robot demonstrations. Uses unified 3D keypoint representations (wrist + fingertips) to bridge human and robot embodiments, achieving 75.0% real-robot success across pick-and-place and tool-use tasks — vs 1.0% for a SOTA VLA baseline. Generalizes to unseen multi-object environments and novel object categories.
