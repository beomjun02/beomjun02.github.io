  • 🆕 SERF: Spatiotemporal Environment and Robot Feature Map for Long-Horizon Mobile Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13 · VLA · long-horizon planningConditions a VLA policy on a spatiotemporal feature map that represents both the environment and the articulated robot body as neural points in a shared latent space, updated online from egocentric observations and proprioception. SERF VLA outperforms image-only baselines on BEHAVIOR-1K, reaches subgoals faster via more direct trajectories, and recovers from object-drop failures.
  • 🆕 μVLA: On Recurrent Memory for Partially Observable Manipulation in VLA Models
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-13 · VLAA controlled isolation study showing that minimal in-backbone recurrence — a small set of learnable memory tokens carried across timesteps via self-attention — lifts a strong pretrained VLA from 0.42 to 0.84 success on partially observable manipulation tasks (MIKASA-Robo), with no regression on fully observable LIBERO (96.2%). No auxiliary losses or architectural changes beyond the memory tokens.
  • 🆕 See Selectively, Act Adaptively: Dual-Level Structural Decomposition for Bimanual Robot Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13 · VLAA bimanual VLA framework that decomposes visual processing and action generation into two complementary structures: a view-selective visual router that dynamically weights wrist-view contributions, and an interaction-aware action MoE that separates coordinated from arm-wise pathways. Improves average success over a monolithic VLA baseline by 27.7% in simulation (RoboTwin 2.0) and 43.3% in real-world evaluation.
  • 🆕 EA-WM: Event-Aware World Models with Task-Specification Grounding for Long-Horizon Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13 · long-horizon planningAugments frozen visual-feature world models with task-specification-grounded event prediction and verification. Rolls out candidate futures in pretrained feature space, decodes them into structured event states, and scores via task-progress, semantic-consistency, and physical-feasibility terms. Shown effective across navigation, deformable-object, and language-described manipulation studies.
  • 🆕 EWAM: An Enhanced World Action Model for Closed-Loop Online Adaptation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13A closed-loop online adaptation architecture built on a fully frozen Cosmos3 backbone, requiring zero extra task-specific demonstrations. Four lightweight inserted layers — neural experience memory, anomaly detection, policy routing, and action correction — provide inference-time co-reasoning. All evaluation is zero-shot; gains stem entirely from the inserted inference-time modules.
  • 🆕 FTP-1: A Generalist Foundation Tactile Policy Across Tactile Sensors for Contact-Rich Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13The first generalist foundation tactile policy, pretrained on ~3,000 hours of tactile data from 26 sources across 21 sensor types including image-, array-, and state-based signals. Heterogeneous encoders project inputs into unified morphology-aware latent tokens. Improves success on seen sensors by +17.2% and, notably, transfers to two unseen tactile-sensor setups with +31% gain.
  • 🆕 EgoEngine: From Egocentric Human Videos to High-Fidelity Dexterous Robot Demonstrations
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13 · Robot Data annoatationA scalable framework for converting egocentric human manipulation videos into robot-executable data by producing both a high-fidelity robot observation video (replacing human with robot while preserving scene context) and task-aligned, feasible robot action trajectories. Demonstrates the first zero-shot visuomotor dexterous policy learning from egocentric human videos without any real-robot demos.
  • 🆕 Learning to Assist: Collaborative VLAs for Implicit Human-Robot Collaboration
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13 · VLAShows that VLA models can support implicit human-robot collaboration and identifies a failure mode where demonstration action leakage in action-chunking policies causes premature assistive behavior. Proposes an inference-time steering method that mitigates erroneous assistance. A 16-participant user study on long-horizon collaborative assembly confirms faster collaboration and fewer failures.
  • Embodied-R1.5: Evolving Physical Intelligence via Embodied Foundation Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-12 · VLAA unified 8B-parameter Embodied Foundation Model integrating cognition, planning, correction, and pointing in one architecture. Achieves SOTA on 16 of 24 embodied VLM benchmarks — surpassing Gemini-Robotics-ER-1.5 and GPT-5.4 — and when fine-tuned into a VLA outperforms π0.5 across four manipulation suites. Weights, datasets, training code, and EmbodiedEvalKit open-sourced.
  • What Matters in Orchestrating Robot Policies: A Systematic Study of Hierarchical VLA Agents
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-11 · Hierarchical VLAA systematic study unifying Hi-VLA agent designs under an options-style control framework, benchmarking how planner, controller, switching, and memory design choices shape performance across short-horizon, long-horizon, and reasoning-intensive tasks.
