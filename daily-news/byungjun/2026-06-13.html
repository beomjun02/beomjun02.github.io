  • 🆕 WEAVER: An Effective World Model for Robotic Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13 · VLAMulti-view world model trained with flow-matching loss that jointly predicts future latents and rewards. Demonstrates state-of-the-art results: ρ=0.870 correlation with real-world success for policy evaluation, 38% real-world success improvement on top of the π₀.₅ foundation model, and 14% gain via test-time planning with 5–10× speedup over prior world models.
  • 🆕 Improving Robotic Generalist Policies via Flow Reversal Steering
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13 · VLAProposes Flow Reversal Steering (FRS) for flow-matching generalist policies: takes suboptimal actions, finds their latent noises by reversing the flow, and maps them to nearby good action modes. Enables zero-shot VLM-guided control and RL bootstrapping, showing up to 95% absolute success rate boosts with under a minute of distillation training.
  • 🆕 μVLA: On Recurrent Memory for Partially Observable Manipulation in VLA Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13 · VLAControlled study isolating recurrence in pretrained VLA backbones via learnable memory tokens carried across timesteps. On MIKASA-Robo, μVLA improves average success from 0.42 to 0.84 on training tasks and from 0.07 to 0.23 on held-out tasks, while maintaining 96.2% on LIBERO under full observability — calibrating when minimal recurrence suffices versus when richer memory structures are needed.
  • 🆕 SERF: Spatiotemporal Environment and Robot Feature Map for Long-Horizon Mobile Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13 · VLA · long-horizon planningConditions a VLA policy on a spatiotemporal feature map that represents both environment and articulated robot body as neural points in a shared latent space, updated online from egocentric observations and proprioception. On BEHAVIOR-1K long-horizon household tasks, the SERF VLA outperforms image-only baselines, reaches subgoals faster, and recovers from object-drop failures.
  • 🆕 Learning to Assist: Collaborative VLAs for Implicit Human-Robot Collaboration
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13 · VLA · long-horizon planningIdentifies a failure mode of action-chunking VLA policies in collaborative settings: demonstration action leakage causes premature assistive behavior (e.g., handing over a tool before the person is ready). Proposes an inference-time steering method that mitigates this while preserving policy performance, validated in a 16-participant user study on a long-horizon collaborative assembly task.
  • 🆕 See Selectively, Act Adaptively: Dual-Level Structural Decomposition for Bimanual Robot Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13 · VLABimanual VLA framework with a View-Selective Visual Router that dynamically adjusts wrist-view contributions and an Interaction-Aware Action MoE that decomposes action generation into coordinated and arm-wise pathways. Improves overall success rate by 27.7% in simulation (RoboTwin 2.0) and 43.3% in real-world evaluation over monolithic baselines.
  • 🆕 SPARC: Reliable Spatial Annotations from Robot Demonstrations at Scale
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13 · Robot Data annotationRisk-aware framework that automatically labels robot demonstrations with structured spatial annotations (bounding boxes, object trajectories, manipulation phases) and assigns reliability scores. On 1.7k human-annotated demonstrations, SPARC outperforms detection-only baselines in localization accuracy while retaining 3× more samples at high-precision operating points. Policies trained on SPARC annotations outperform baselines in cluttered, ambiguous real-world scenes.
  • 🆕 EgoEngine: From Egocentric Human Videos to High-Fidelity Dexterous Robot Demonstrations
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13 · Robot Data annotationScalable framework for converting egocentric human manipulation videos into robot data by producing high-fidelity robot observation videos (replacing human hands with robot while preserving scene context) and task-aligned executable action trajectories. Demonstrates the first zero-shot visuomotor dexterous policy learning from egocentric human videos without any real-robot demonstrations.
  • 🆕 EA-WM: Event-Aware World Models with Task-Specification Grounding for Long-Horizon Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13 · long-horizon planningAugments frozen visual-feature world models with task-grounded event prediction and verification. Rolls out candidate futures in pretrained feature space, decodes them into structured event states, and scores using task-progress, semantic-consistency, physical-feasibility, and uncertainty terms. Improves interpretability and task alignment across navigation, deformable-object, and language-described manipulation studies.
  • 🆕 Real-Time Execution with Autoregressive Policies
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13 · VLAShows that autoregressive VLA policies can achieve real-time execution via adjusted tokenization horizons and constrained decoding with strict latency bounds.   • 🆕 FTP-1: A Generalist Foundation Tactile Policy Across Tactile Sensors for Contact-Rich Manipulation
