  • FTP-1: A Generalist Foundation Tactile Policy Across Tactile Sensors for Contact-Rich Manipulation

  • 🆕 SPARC: Reliable Spatial Annotations from Robot Demonstrations at Scale

  • See Selectively, Act Adaptively: Dual-Level Structural Decomposition for Bimanual Robot Manipulation
    arXiv · 2026-06-14 · VLAA bimanual VLA framework with dual-level decomposition: a View-Selective Visual Router dynamically adjusts wrist-view contributions per task stage, and an Interaction-Aware Action MoE decomposes action generation into coordinated vs. arm-wise pathways. Improves average success rate by 27.7% in simulation and 43.3% in real-world over monolithic baselines.
  • Embodied-R1.5: Evolving Physical Intelligence via Embodied Foundation Models
    arXiv · 2026-06-12 · VLAA unified 8B-parameter Embodied Foundation Model integrating cognition, planning, correction, and grounding. Achieves SOTA on 16/24 embodied VLM benchmarks (surpassing Gemini-Robotics-ER-1.5 and GPT-5.4), and when fine-tuned as a VLA outperforms π0.5 across four manipulation suites. Weights, datasets, and code open-sourced.
  • DAM-VLA: Decoupled Asynchronous Multimodal VLA
    arXiv · 2026-06-12 · VLABreaks the synchronous clock assumption of standard VLAs by letting each modality (vision, language, proprioception) update at its own sensor rate via per-modality latent buffers. Doubles average success rate over the strongest synchronous baseline (95.2% vs 40.9%) across seven contact-rich real-world tasks at 100 Hz control.
