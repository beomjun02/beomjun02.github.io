  • 🆕 Sparse2Act: Learning Action-Aligned Sparse 3D Representations for Cross-Domain Robot Manipulation
    arXiv · 2026-06-15An observation-action alignment framework for pretraining sparse point-cloud encoders. Uses task-space end-effector actions as geometric supervision so masked sparse 3D tokens organize scene representations around action-relevant structure, decoupling the 3D encoder from specific policies and enabling cross-domain manipulation transfer.
  • 🆕 EquiDexFlow: Contact-Grounded SE(3)-Equivariant Dexterous Grasp Generative Flows
    arXiv · 2026-06-15An SE(3)-equivariant flow-matching model that jointly predicts wrist pose, joint angles, fingertip contacts, surface normals, and contact forces from an object point cloud. Projects contacts onto the object surface and forces into the Coulomb friction cone by construction, guaranteeing placement and friction compliance without loss penalties.
  • 🆕 EmbodiSteer: Steering Embodiment-Agnostic Visuomotor Policies with Joint-Space Guidance for Zero-Shot Cross-Embodiment Deployment
    arXiv · 2026-06-15 · VLAA training-free framework that steers embodiment-agnostic Cartesian VLA policies toward zero-shot, embodiment-aware deployment. Injects joint-space guidance from a robot-specific constraint model to handle whole-body collision avoidance without retraining, enabling a single Cartesian VLA to deploy safely across different robot embodiments.
  • 🆕 Action-Effect Memory Pretraining for Robot Manipulation
    arXiv · 2026-06-15Learns compact temporal representations from vision-action history for robot manipulation by interleaving visual and action features and applying masked modeling to recover missing content from incomplete histories, capturing causal action-effect relationships that single-frame visual encoders miss.
  • Embodied-R1.5: Evolving Physical Intelligence via Embodied Foundation Models
    arXiv · 2026-06-12 · VLAA unified 8B-parameter Embodied Foundation Model integrating cognition, planning, correction, and pointing within a single architecture. With 15B+ tokens of training data and a multi-task balanced RL recipe, it achieves SOTA on 16 of 24 embodied VLM benchmarks — surpassing Gemini-Robotics-ER-1.5 and GPT-5.4.   • DAM-VLA: Decoupled Asynchronous Multimodal Vision Language Action model
    arXiv · 2026-06-12 · VLAChallenges the synchronous-clock assumption inherited from VL pretraining by letting each modality (vision, language, proprioception) update at its own sensor rate via per-modality latent buffers. On seven contact-rich real-world manipulation tasks, DAM-VLA more than doubles the best synchronous baseline's success rate (95.2% vs 40.95%) while sustaining 100 Hz control.
  • WEAVER: An Effective World Model for Robotic Manipulation
    arXiv · 2026-06-13Multi-view world model trained with flow-matching loss that jointly predicts future latents and rewards. Demonstrates state-of-the-art results: ρ=0.870 correlation with real-world success for policy evaluation, 38% real-world success improvement on top of the π₀.₅ foundation model, and 14% gain via test-time planning with 5–10× speedup over prior world models.
  • Improving Robotic Generalist Policies via Flow Reversal Steering
    arXiv · 2026-06-13Proposes Flow Reversal Steering (FRS) for flow-matching generalist policies: takes suboptimal actions, finds their latent noises by reversing the flow, and maps them to nearby good action modes. Enables zero-shot VLM-guided control and RL bootstrapping, showing up to 95% absolute success rate boosts with under a minute of distillation training.
  • InDex: Bridging the Morphology Gap — Adapting VLA Models to Dexterous Manipulation via Intent-Conditioned Fine-Tuning
    arXiv · 2026-06-12 · VLARepurposes the pre-trained 1-DoF parallel grasp output as a continuous virtual grasp intent proxy to bridge VLA priors to high-DoF dexterous hands. A two-stage decoupled architecture — parameter-efficient arm/intent alignment followed by an intent-conditioned diffusion head for multi-fingered joints — substantially outperforms monolithic baselines on multi-stage contact-rich dexterous tasks.
  • DEHP: Dynamic Execution Horizon Prediction for Chunk-based Robot Policies
    arXiv · 2026-06-12Trains a lightweight RL branch to dynamically predict how many steps of an action chunk to execute before replanning — shorter horizons during fine-grained stages, longer during free-space motion — while keeping the pretrained chunk policy completely frozen. Compatible with black-box VLAs and diffusion policies; large success-rate gains on high-precision and long-horizon manipulation tasks.
