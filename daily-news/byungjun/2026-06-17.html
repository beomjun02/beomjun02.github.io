  • 🆕 Retrieve, Don't Retrain: Extending VLAs to New Tasks at Test Time
    HF papers · 2026-06-17 · VLAReplaces per-task fine-tuning with retrieval: a retrieval-augmented VLA policy is trained once on paired demonstrations from the target embodiment and a cheaper source (e.g., human-hand video), then frozen. New tasks are added by dropping demonstrations into the retrieval pool — no additional training or teleoperation needed.
  • 🆕 Token Steering: Steering Autoregressive VLA Policies via Action Token Intervention
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-17 · VLAInjects low-dimensional user inputs into the action-token representation of autoregressive VLAs at inference time, requiring no retraining. Improves success rates from 10% to 72.5% on drawer closing and 16.7% to 93.8% on object swapping, enabling lightweight human steering over frozen robot foundation models.
  • 🆕 Acting While Understanding: Asynchronous Semantic-Action Decoupling for Real-Time VLAs

  • 🆕 QPILOTS: Efficient Test-Time Q-Steering for Flow Policies
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-17 · VLASteers frozen flow/diffusion policies at test time using a learned Q-function, projecting noisy intermediates to clean actions for reliable critic gradients. Reaches 90% average success across 50 offline-to-online RL tasks and outperforms prior inference-time approaches when applied to a large frozen VLA foundation model across six manipulation tasks.
  • 🆕 SimWeaver: Zero-Shot RGB Sim-to-Real for Deformable Manipulation
