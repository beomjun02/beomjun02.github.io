  • 🆕 WOLF-VLA: Whole-Body Humanoid Optimal Locomotion Framework for VLA Learning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-26 · VLAA unified framework integrating whole-body optimal-control motion synthesis with large-scale multi-modal datasets to train VLAs for humanoid locomotion from language instructions. Builds a dataset of dynamically feasible trajectories across six locomotion task families with environmental variations and visual distractors, yielding a policy with strong reasoning and robustness to initial-condition variability.
  • 🆕 SVP-IL: Spatial Visual Prompts for Language-Conditioned Imitation Learning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-26 · VLADecouples semantic reasoning from spatial control in VLAs by extracting zero-shot geometric masks from language instructions and injecting them as spatial visual prompts into a continuous action generator. Trained on as few as 50–100 demonstrations, achieves 67.8% on standard benchmarks and improves average success on highly ambiguous tasks from 24.0% to 39.5%.
  • 🆕 HALO: Memory Retrieval in Visuomotor Policies for Long-Horizon Robot Control
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-26 · long-horizon planningAn attention-based memory retrieval mechanism for visuomotor policies that distills VLM priors via memory-dependent QA pairs and uses sparse attention to reduce error accumulation. Enables reliable retrieval of task-relevant information from up to eight minutes of past experience for long-horizon control without hand-designed retrieval rules.
  • 🆕 EBench: Elemental Diagnosis of Generalist Mobile Manipulation Policies
    HF papers · 2026-06-25 · VLAA simulation benchmark profiling generalist manipulation policies across 5 capability and 4 generalization dimensions over 26 tasks. Evaluates π₀, π₀.₅, XVLA, and InternVLA-A1, revealing that models with near-identical success rates have strikingly different capability profiles — exposing blind spots invisible to scalar metrics.
  • InSight: Self-Guided Skill Acquisition via Steerable VLAs
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-25 · VLA · SubtaskDecomposes demonstrations into labeled primitives via VLM plan decomposition and end-effector segmentation, then uses a VLM-guided data flywheel to identify missing primitives for novel tasks and autonomously attempt them — turning VLAs into self-improving learners that grow beyond their training data.
  • EventVLA: Event-Driven Visual Evidence Memory for Long-Horizon Vision-Language-Action Policies
    HF papers · 2026-06-25 · VLA · long-horizon planningAddresses the memory bottleneck in long-horizon manipulation by introducing selective, event-driven visual evidence buffering. Avoids the latency of decoupled dual systems and the redundancy of unselective history buffers — keeps only task-relevant visual cues across extended manipulation sequences.
  • G³VLA: Geometric inductive bias for Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-25 · VLAInjects calibrated camera geometry into VLA visual tokens via ray embeddings, projective positional encoding, and cross-view fusion — without altering the action space or training objective. Especially useful for multi-camera setups where views are coupled by known extrinsics but processed as independent images.
  • FOCA: Future-Oriented Conditioning for Data-Efficient VLA Adaptation
    arXiv · 2026-06-24 · VLAIntroduces future-oriented conditioning for few-shot VLA adaptation by predicting task-grounded future interaction embeddings in latent space. Achieves 95.7% success with just 20 demonstrations on LIBERO, improves 7–12% on RoboCasa, and delivers up to 26% absolute gains on real robots — a new state of the art in few-shot VLA adaptation.
