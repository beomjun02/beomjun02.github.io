  • 🆕 ForesightSafety-VLA: A Diagnostic Safety Benchmark for Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-28 · VLADefines a 13-category safety taxonomy for VLA systems covering physical interaction, instruction-side, and perception-side safety. Even the strongest policy incurs non-trivial safety cost, and visual/structural variation degrades safety far more than language variation.
  • 🆕 CoStream: Composing Simple Behaviors for Generalizable Complex Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-28Orchestrates foundation models and sensing modalities into three composable core behaviors — semantic (spatial constraints via foundation models), predictive (trajectory forecasting via keypoint tracking in imagined videos), and reactive (high-frequency tactile/force corrections). On a shared SE(3) interface these compose into a single pose command at each control step, demonstrated on 8 real-world tasks spanning everyday manipulation and precision assembly.
  • 🆕 BRIDGE: Bridging Handheld and Teleoperated Supervision for Contact-Rich Manipulation

  • 🆕 SSI-Policy: Learning Structured Scene Interfaces for Vision-Language Robotic Manipulation

  • 🆕 LiMoDE: Lifelong Mixture of Dynamic Experts for Robot Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-28Two-stage scheme for lifelong robot manipulation: dynamic MoE pre-training where heterogeneous experts are activated based on motion information for different short-term manipulations, followed by lifelong adaptation that learns new experts and dynamically combines them with frozen ones.
