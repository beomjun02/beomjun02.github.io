  • 🆕 SCORE: Support-Constrained Off-Domain Reinforcement for Real-World Policy Improvement
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-30A real-to-sim-to-real framework that constrains simulation RL to the support of a generative policy pretrained on real data via flow steering. Across eight real-world dexterous multi-fingered manipulation tasks, improves average success from 37.8% to 89.9% (best baseline: 59.5%) and reaches success in 36.8% fewer steps.
  • 🆕 Direct Action-Head Injection of A Grounded 3D Point Unlocks Spatial and Task Generalization
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-30 · VLAProposes a lightweight, model-agnostic module that lifts grounding into 3D and injects it directly into the VLA action head via adaptive layer normalization. On LIBERO-PRO, improves GR00T-N1.6 from 31.2 to 77.5 under task perturbation and from 28.1 to 60.2 under position perturbation; similar gains on π₀.5.
  • 🆕 DIM-WAM: World-Action Modeling with Diverse Historical Event Memory
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-30 · long-horizon planningA memory-augmented world-action model integrating multi-scale historical context, local future dynamics, and global task progress via independent similarity-based memory banks. Raises average success from 28.4% to 69.8% on RMBench and from 52.5% to 80.0% on four real-world Franka long-horizon tasks.
  • 🆕 Drop-Then-Recovery: How Redundant Are Vision-Language-Action Models?
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-30 · VLAIntroduces a Drop-Then-Recovery analysis protocol revealing strong asymmetry in VLA model redundancy: language backbones are highly redundant for standard manipulation (removing half of LLM blocks even improves OpenVLA-OFT from 95.0% to 98.3%), while vision and action pathways are far less tolerant to removal.
  • 🆕 PhysisForcing: Physics Reinforced World Simulator for Robotic Manipulation
    HF papers · 2026-06-30Addresses physical implausibility in video-generation-based world simulators for manipulation. Identifies deformation of moving objects and inconsistent robot-object interactions as root causes, and proposes physics-based reinforcement to produce physically consistent manipulation simulations.
  • 🆕 Object-Centric Residual RL for Zero-Shot Sim-to-Real VLA Enhancement
    HF papers · 2026-06-30 · VLAExplores whether a residual RL policy trained purely in simulation can improve real-world VLA robustness zero-shot.   • 🆕 Translation as a Bridging Action: Transferring Manipulation Skills from Humans to Robots
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-30 · VLAArgues that learning rotation-inclusive 6DoF actions from human data is sub-optimal due to noisy hand-pose estimates and fundamental contact differences. Proposes relative wrist translation as a bridging action space shared by humans and robots, built into a pi0-like VLA model with interleaved action tokens.
  • 🆕 WARP-RM: Warp-Augmented Relative Progress Reward Model for Data Curation

  • 🆕 SimFoundry: Modular and Automated Scene Generation for Policy Learning and Evaluation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-30A system for zero-shot real-to-sim scene construction from video, generating affordance-preserving digital cousins for training and evaluation.
