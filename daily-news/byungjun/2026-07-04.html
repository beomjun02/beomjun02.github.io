  • 🆕 Does VLA Even Know the Basics? Measuring Commonsense and World Knowledge Retention in Vision-Language-Action Models

  • 🆕 VLA-Corrector: Lightweight Detect-and-Correct Inference for Adaptive Action Horizon
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-03 · VLAAddresses the fragility of fixed-horizon action chunking in VLAs by adding a lightweight detect-and-correct module at inference time. The corrector monitors execution and adaptively shortens the action horizon when perturbations arise, recovering closed-loop reactivity without retraining the base VLA.
  • 🆕 Learning to Move Before Learning to Do: Task-Agnostic Pretraining for VLAs
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-03 · VLAArgues that VLA data scarcity stems from conflating physical competence (how to move) with semantic alignment (what to do). Proposes task-agnostic pretraining that learns motor primitives from unlabeled robot data, then fine-tunes semantic grounding with far fewer expert demonstrations.
  • 🆕 PhysisForcing: Physics Reinforced World Simulator for Robotic Manipulation
    HF papers · 2026-06-28A training framework that strengthens physical consistency in video world models for robotic manipulation through trajectory alignment and relational alignment losses. Improves Wan2.2-I2V and Cosmos3-Nano baselines by 22.3% and 9.2% on R-Bench, and raises closed-loop success rate from 16.0% to 24.0% under the WorldArena protocol.
  • 🆕 VT-WAM: Visual-Tactile World Action Model for Contact-Rich Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-03Introduces a Visual-Tactile World Action Model that jointly learns future visual prediction, tactile deformation prediction, and action generation. Unlike prior visual-tactile policies that feed tactile signals directly into action heads, VT-WAM explicitly models tactile dynamics during action generation for contact-rich tasks.
  • 🆕 Translation as a Bridging Action: Transferring Manipulation Skills from Humans to Robots
    HF papers · 2026-06-28 · VLAProposes using relative wrist translation as a shared action space for transferring manipulation skills from human demonstrations to bi-manual robots with parallel grippers. Builds a π₀-like VLA model with interleaved action tokens, showing that this bridging action transfers human knowledge far more effectively than noisy 6DoF hand-pose estimates.
