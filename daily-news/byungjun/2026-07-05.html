  • VLA-Corrector: Lightweight Detect-and-Correct Inference for Adaptive Action Horizon
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-04 · VLA · long-horizon planningProposes a plug-in corrective framework for action-chunked VLA policies that detects when predicted visual dynamics diverge from reality and triggers replanning. A latent-space vision monitor continuously compares predicted vs. actual feature evolution; on detection of persistent drift, stale actions are discarded and an online gradient guidance module generates corrective actions. Naturally induces an event-triggered adaptive action horizon without retraining the VLA backbone.
