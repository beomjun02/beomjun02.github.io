  • 🆕 VLA-Corrector: Lightweight Detect-and-Correct Inference for Adaptive Action Horizon
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-07 · VLA · long-horizon planningA lightweight corrective inference framework for action-chunked VLA policies. A latent vision monitor detects when predicted and actual visual dynamics diverge, triggering corrective replanning — inducing an adaptive action horizon that preserves long-horizon execution when reliable and invokes short-horizon corrections when the policy drifts. Works on top of existing VLA backbones without retraining.
  • 🆕 VLAFlow: A Unified Training Framework for Vision-Language-Action Models via Co-training and Future Latent Alignment
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-07 · VLAFirst controlled comparison of four VLA pre-training paradigms — action-only, language co-training, future latent alignment, and their combination — under the same pi0-style architecture using ~5,000 hours of heterogeneous robot data. Finds that combining language supervision with future latent alignment yields the most stable cross-benchmark transfer.
  • 🆕 Learning to Move Before Learning to Do: Task-Agnostic Pretraining for VLAs
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-07 · VLATask-agnostic pretraining for VLA models from unlabeled robot data. Learns general motor capabilities — reaching, moving, exploring — before task-specific fine-tuning, improving downstream manipulation performance and reducing the amount of task-labeled data required.
  • 🆕 PhysMani: Physics-principled 3D World Model for Dynamic Object Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-07Couples a physics-principled 3D Gaussian world model (learning divergence-free velocity fields) with a future-aware action policy for manipulating fast, dynamically moving targets. Introduces PhysMani-Bench with 16 dynamic manipulation tasks and demonstrates superior success rates in both simulation and real-robot experiments.
  • 🆕 BIFROST: Bridging Invariant Feature Representation for Observation-space Sim2Real Transfer
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-07Achieves zero-shot sim-to-real transfer by learning domain-invariant representations via cross-domain bisimulation metrics. Instead of randomizing the simulator or tuning reward, it bridges the visual and dynamics gap in observation space — policies trained entirely in simulation transfer directly to real robots.
  • 🆕 One Demonstration Is Enough for Real-World Robotic Reinforcement Learning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-07Real-world robotic RL from a single human demonstration.   • Guided Action Flow: Q-Guided Inference for Flow-Matching Vision-Language-Action Policies
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-04 · VLAKeeps a pretrained SmolVLA policy frozen and uses a learned action-chunk critic to guide its reverse-time flow sampler at inference. The critic is trained from real success/failure rollouts and can condition on task features, enabling test-time steering of VLA behavior without any retraining.
