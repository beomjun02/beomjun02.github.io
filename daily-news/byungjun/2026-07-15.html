  • 🆕 On the Efficiency of LoRA Fine-Tuning for Vision-Language-Action Models in Industrial Robotic Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-15 · VLASystematic study of LoRA adaptation for π₀ (flow-matching VLA) on four precision assembly tasks with a UR5e. Performance saturates at rank 32 with no significant advantage of full fine-tuning. Reduces static peak VRAM from 36.2 to 10.8 GiB. Key finding: freezing the VLM or restricting the vision encoder to LoRA significantly degrades performance—both semantic and visual plasticity are needed.
