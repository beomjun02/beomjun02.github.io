  • 🆕 VistaVLA: Geometry- and Semantic-Aware 3D Gaussian-Grounded VLA for Robotic Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-16 · VLAConstructs a 3D cognitive representation from Gaussian primitives and grounds it as compact context tokens for VLA policy learning. A Merge-then-Query mechanism compresses dense 3D Gaussians into a highly compact token set (99% reduction) while preserving action-relevant spatial layouts. Improves real-world success rates by 22.8% across seven tasks and 30.0% on OOD tasks over VLA-Adapter baselines.
  • 🆕 FlowWAM: Optical Flow as a Unified Action Representation for World Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-16 · VLAAdopts optical flow as a video-native action representation within a dual-stream diffusion WAM. Flow videos share RGB format and encode rich per-pixel displacement, enabling joint modeling in a shared pretrained video generator. Achieves 92.94% success on RoboTwin Clean and best EWMScore (63.71) on WorldArena with 18.4% relative trajectory accuracy improvement over VLA and WAM baselines.
  • 🆕 ExToken: Structured Exploration for Efficient Vision-Language-Action Reinforcement Fine-tuning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-16 · VLAConditions VLA policies on discrete behavioral priors derived from offline demonstrations for structured RL exploration. Different tokens during rollout encourage diverse behavioral modes, substantially improving state-action coverage. A state-conditioned token selector adaptively predicts effective modes at deployment. Consistently accelerates convergence across simulated and real manipulation tasks under constrained interaction budgets.
  • 🆕 ChunkFlow: Towards Continuity-Consistent Chunked Policy Learning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-16 · VLAAddresses the boundary jitter problem in chunked VLA action heads, where overlapping regions between consecutive chunks yield inconsistent predictions degrading temporal coherence. Partitions each chunk into frozen, editable, and future zones with deterministic overlap blending, seam continuity losses, and history corruption for robustness. Improves success-stability trade-off on CALVIN, LIBERO, and real robots.
  • 🆕 TrustVLA: Mechanism-Guided Inference-Time Defense Against Vision-Language-Action Backdoors
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-16 · VLAIdentifies a recurring internal mechanism in poisoned VLAs: a compact causal footprint that is attention-seeded, spatially compact, and causal — masking it returns evidence-evolution to the normal operating region. Adapts the Dirichlet evidence framework for per-token epistemic uncertainty monitoring in VLA policies. Detects, localizes, and recovers from visual-triggered VLA backdoors across OpenVLA/LIBERO and π₀.₅ without retraining.
  • 🆕 Reducing Temporal Redundancy for Efficient Vision-Language-Action Inference
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-16 · VLAIdentifies two sources of temporal redundancy in VLA pipelines: repeated visual encoding of similar consecutive frames and multi-step diffusion sampling. Incrementally updates only tokens corresponding to dynamic scene regions and compresses diffusion sampling to a 2-step schedule. Achieves over 2x speedup while maintaining up to 98% success rate on general manipulation benchmarks.
  • 🆕 Jetson-PI: Towards Onboard Real-Time Robot Control via Foresight-Aligned Asynchronous Inference
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-16 · VLATackles VLA deployment on low-power Jetson Orin hardware. A lightweight future correction module predicts future environment representation conditioned on committed actions, enabling the action expert to predict from the future time step. Confidence-based scheduling and system-level CUDA optimizations achieve 8.66x improvement in control frequency over naive PyTorch while outperforming VLASH by 14.8% on LIBERO.
  • 🆕 Mind the Gap: Promises and Pitfalls of Hierarchical Planning in LeWorldModel
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-16 · long-horizon planningInvestigates whether temporal hierarchy improves LeWorldModel on long-horizon goal-conditioned control. Adds high-level planning over latent subgoals on a frozen low-level LeWM. Key finding: subgoal generation is the main bottleneck — unconstrained search selects latent macro-actions that appear favorable but produce poor control targets. Constraining search around training-encoded macro-actions recovers useful hierarchical regimes, improving +14.7 pp at the longest PushT horizon.
  • 🆕 Mixture of Frames Policy: Multi-Frame Action Denoising for Bimanual Mobile Manipulation
    HF papers · 2026-07-16Argues that committing to a single predefined action frame forces one denoiser to model unnecessarily complex distributions.
