  • 🆕 Anchor-Align: Generalizable VLA Finetuning via Representation Anchoring and Language-Action Alignment
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-17 · VLAAugments behavior cloning with two objectives to prevent pretrained representations from being overwritten during VLA finetuning: vision-language anchoring via layer-wise distillation from a frozen VLM, plus language-action alignment that jointly trains language and action prediction on the same observation. Improves real-robot xArm7 success from 28→54% and 37→60% across two VLA architectures.
  • 🆕 Semantic Anchoring for Robotic Action Representations
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-17 · VLAInspired by mirror neuron theory, examines whether VLA action representations preserve the semantic structure of pretrained encoders. Confirms structure erodes during finetuning and correlates with task success. Introduces a plug-and-play method that anchors action representations to a semantic manifold with a shared/private channel decomposition — discarded at inference, leaving the deployed model unchanged. Up to +18.7% on real-world tasks and +21.5% OOD.
  • 🆕 An Empirical Study on Stage-Information Interfaces for VLA Fine-Tuning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-17 · VLA · SubtaskInvestigates how to inject subtask stage information into VLAs for long-horizon manipulation. Compares current-stage text vs. ordinal stage index as input to GR00T N1.6 on LIBERO-10. Under continuation fine-tuning, the ordinal stage-state interface consistently outperforms both full-task instruction and current-stage text, suggesting compact progress signals may beat verbose stage descriptions.
  • 🆕 GigaWorld-Policy-0.5: A Faster and Stronger WAM Empowered by AutoResearch
    HF papers · 2026-07-17Addresses the inference overhead of World Action Models that explicitly generate future videos at runtime. GigaWorld-Policy uses an action-centered formulation where future visual dynamics serve as training supervision only, eliminating video generation at inference. Enables real-time closed-loop deployment while retaining the benefits of world-model grounding.
  • 🆕 WANDA: Worlds in One Demo — A Synthetic Data Engine for Open-World Mobile Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-17 · long-horizon planningMaximizes the value of a single real demonstration by reconstructing Gaussian splats and interaction trajectories, rearranging them into diverse spatial configurations via whole-body motion planning, and synthesizing photo-realistic observations on generated 3D worlds from everyday photos. Achieves long-horizon robustness and cross-environment generalization from one demo, including zero-shot cross-embodiment transfer.
  • 🆕 HRIBench: Benchmarking Interaction-Centric Human-Robot Collaboration
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-17 · VLADiagnostic benchmark for intent-aware human-robot collaboration defining three interaction roles (Instructor, Collaborator, Intruder) with 13 tasks and 650+ episodes. Evaluates adapted GR00T, pi0.5, and ACT policies with interaction-centric metrics beyond binary success. Current VLA foundation policies struggle substantially in collaborative settings despite strong manipulation ability.

  • NVIDIA RoboTTT: 8000-Timestep Context for Robot Policies
    NVIDIA · 2026-07-16Scales a robot model natively to 8,000 timesteps of context — 5 minutes of muscle memory — with constant inference cost. Previous robot policies operated on just a few frames (<0.1 sec), instantly forgetting what happened.   • FlowWAM: Optical Flow as a Unified Action Representation for World Action Models
    arXiv · 2026-07-16Proposes optical flow as a universal action representation for World Action Models, bridging pretrained video generators and robot control. Numerical actions fail to align with video priors while carrying insufficient motion cues; optical flow naturally captures both pixel-level motion and physical intent, enabling tighter integration of world modeling with action prediction.
