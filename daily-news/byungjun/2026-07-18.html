  • 🆕 LIFT: Never Too Late for Force — Accelerating VLA Post-Training with Reactive Force Injection

  • 🆕 Reflex: Real-Time VLA Control through Streaming Inference
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-18 · VLAAchieves 50Hz stable streaming for flow-matching VLA policies by exploiting timestep-invariance to enable O(1) incremental KV-cache updates. AdaRMSNorm prevents BFloat16 numerical collapse during continuous high-frequency inference. 2.58x speedup with 54% lower reaction latency on LIBERO and Kinetix benchmarks.
  • 🆕 DiMaS: Distribution Matching for Steering Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-18 · VLAProposes distribution-matching steering for flow-matching VLAs, transporting between representation distributions rather than shifting along fixed directions. Shows behavioral features in VLAs are linearly decodable but not linearly steerable, motivating a non-linear steering approach. Validated across two state-of-the-art VLA architectures.
  • 🆕 LifelongVLA: Lifelong Vision-Language-Action Learning for Robotic Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-18 · VLAAddresses plasticity-stability trade-off in VLAs with dual-timescale LoRA gating: a short-term adapter for plasticity and a long-term adapter for stable consolidation, integrated via a task-aware gate. Cache-efficient stochastic replay preserves retention signals without full-trajectory storage. Outperforms lifelong learning baselines on real xArm robot.
  • 🆕 MEMORA: Embodied Action Memory from Egocentric Videos for Reasoning and Planning
