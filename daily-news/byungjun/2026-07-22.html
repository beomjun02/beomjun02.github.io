  • 🆕 Foresight Residual RL for Long-Horizon Robot Manipulation with Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-22 · VLA · SubtaskShows that standard residual RL over a frozen VLA improves each subtask in isolation but fails when skills are chained, because terminal state quality is uncontrolled. Proposes foresight value — the probability of future subtask success conditioned on the current subtask’s terminal state — as a reward multiplier. Achieves 85.6% full-task success on a three-phase nut-tightening assembly vs. 54.5% for standard subtask residual RL.
  • 🆕 Test-Time Scaling for World Action Models via Zero-Shot Geometric Evaluation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-22Training-free selective test-time scaling framework for WAMs. Ranks sampled rollouts by cross-view depth reprojection consistency of predicted futures using a frozen geometry foundation model. A gating mechanism invokes extra sampling only when the initial rollout appears internally inconsistent, recovering 74.8% of the always-on gain while triggering on only 26.2% of decision points.
