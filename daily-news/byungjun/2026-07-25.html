  • 🆕 Emergent Compositional Skills in Mixture-of-Experts VLAs
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-25 · VLA · SubtaskShows that a VLA with a simplified MoE action head can emergently decompose tasks into reusable, interpretable behavioral primitives without any pre-specified task hierarchy.   • Xiaomi-Robotics-1: Scaling Vision-Language-Action Models with over 100K Hours of Real-World Trajectories

  • AC-VLA: Robust Out-of-Distribution Action Execution via Compositional Learning
    arXiv · 2026-07-21 · VLA · SubtaskAddresses VLA failure on recombined sub-tasks via two architecture-agnostic components: an LLM-driven instruction decomposer generating dense sub-task supervision, and a state-conditioned masking strategy forcing global spatial grounding over wrist-view shortcuts. Achieves ~28% absolute improvement on compositional OOD tasks when applied to π0.5 on LIBERO.
  • Foresight Residual RL for Long-Horizon Robot Manipulation with Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv · 2026-07-22 · VLA · long-horizon planningShows that standard residual RL over a frozen VLA improves each subtask in isolation but fails when skills are chained, because terminal state quality is uncontrolled. Proposes foresight value — the probability of future subtask success conditioned on the current subtask’s terminal state — as a reward multiplier. Achieves 85.6% full-task success on a three-phase nut-tightening assembly vs. 54.5% for standard subtask residual RL.
  • Closing the Lab-to-Store Gap: A Data-Efficient Post-Training and Experience-Driven Learning VLA Framework for Retail Humanoids
    arXiv · 2026-07-24 · VLAPresents DEED, a systems-level approach for deploying VLA humanoid robots in real retail settings. Evaluated on a supermarket chip-restocking task with a Unitree G1-Edu humanoid and the GR00T N1.6 model, it combines control-frequency aligned post-training, data-efficient fine-tuning, and experience-driven self-improvement to bridge the gap between lab benchmarks and reliable store operation.
  • RouterVLA: Budgeted Commissioning and Expert Onboarding for Growing VLA Pools
    arXiv · 2026-07-24 · VLATackles the practical problem of managing multiple VLA policies: which expert to deploy for a new condition and which candidate to add to the pool. Uses split-clean priors and outcome-disjoint probes to reach 60.5% held-out success under a fixed probe budget, a +1.64pp gain over semantic shortlisting.
  • HOST: Robots Acquire Manipulation Skills in Seconds from a Single Human Video
