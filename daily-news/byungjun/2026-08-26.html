Papers

  • 🆕 Mamba-based SmolVLA: Selective State Space Modeling for VLA Action Experts
    Replaces causal self-attention with Mamba's selective state-space modeling inside SmolVLA's action expert. When executing 50 actions before replanning, the Mamba expert outperforms the Transformer baseline by 7.8% on LIBERO while cutting parameter count by 24%.
  • 🆕 CounterAlign: Counterfactual Supervision for VLA Models
    Generates dense corrective supervision for VLA training by pairing expert actions with mismatched instructions. Combined with adversarial discriminator training, the method improves VLA robustness to object position and task perturbations on LIBERO-PRO and real-robot experiments.
  • 🆕 DELE-w0.5: Inferring Action from Future Latent State for Robotic Manipulation
    Argues that video generation is unnecessary for world-action modeling and instead infers robot actions from predicted compact future latent states. Across 480 real-robot trials on four long-horizon tasks, DELE-w0.5 achieves 62.5% full-task success — outperforming the strongest baseline by 47.5 points.
  • 🆕 Selective Cross-View Consistency for World Action Models
    Shows that naively applying cross-view consistency loss to WAM video-prediction coordinates provably shrinks legitimate view-specific content. Constraining only the action/proprioception block (SCVC) improves held-out viewpoint success by 12–15 points without requiring camera labels at training or test time.
  • 🆕 DreamMimic: Visuomotor Whole-Body Loco-Manipulation via World Model
    Distills privileged teacher policies into vision-based humanoid controllers through world-model-assisted distillation. Performance-Conditioned Guidance dynamically balances teacher guidance and student exploration, improving tracking-based loco-manipulation over vision-based baselines.
  • 🆕 WorldToken: Time-First Sequence Modeling for Robotic Imitation Learning
    Fuses multiview images, proprioception, and task conditioning within each timestep into a single "world token," then models the sequence with a causal temporal Transformer and diffusion action head. An 85.3M-parameter policy trained on 23 RoboCasa tasks outperforms alternatives that interleave modalities across the sequence.
  • 🆕 TONAV: Task-Oriented Navigation and Action-Velocity Chunk Learning for Quadruped Mobile Manipulation
    Vision-language reasoning decomposes instructions into subgoals, while action-velocity chunk learning jointly models positions and temporal transitions under velocity supervision for smooth sustained-contact manipulation on quadrupeds.
  • 🆕 GOLEM: Modular Humanoid Autonomy Towards EV Battery Disassembly
    Open-source Docker-based ROS 2 system for the Unitree H1-2 humanoid with independent modules for walking, manipulation, navigation, and spatial memory. Evaluated end-to-end on real Hyundai Ioniq 5 battery disassembly, with a capability ladder from tethered (97%) to free-standing (87%) to navigation-induced pose variance (37%).
  • 🆕 CIDER: Continual Interactive Distillation for Embodied Reinforcement Learning
    Freezes the accumulated policy as a teacher before each new task, interleaving task learning with distillation-based retention and gradient routing. On a six-task real-robot sequence, each new task is acquired in 10–20 minutes while maintaining high success on all previously learned tasks.
