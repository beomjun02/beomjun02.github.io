  • 🆕 VLAct: Representation-Centric Pre-training for Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-31 · VLAVLAct trains a VLA-oriented VLM backbone on broad, multi-embodiment robot data before task-specific fine-tuning, prioritizing representation quality over data scaling. With fully open-source data and 16 GPUs, it achieves 82.6% on LIBERO-Plus and 92.5% on RoboTwin 2.0 — surpassing ABot-M0 and LingBot-VLA — and outperforms GR00T-N1.6 on an unseen humanoid embodiment using only 20% of downstream trajectories.
  • 🆕 PHR-VLA: Planning Horizon Reasoning for Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-31 · VLA · long-horizon planningPHR-VLA equips VLAs with privileged latent representations of future dynamics via a lightweight auxiliary future head aligned with latent states from upcoming observations during training. Contact-centric patch-level supervision from a wrist camera improves LIBERO success from 84.1% to 88.4% and real-world disassembly from 63.3% to 82.5%, showing that anticipatory reasoning significantly benefits contact-rich manipulation.
  • 🆕 DeicticVLA: Unifying Instruction Modes in a Single VLA
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-31 · VLADeicticVLA canonicalizes language, vision-language, and visual instructions into text prompts and deictic masks, enabling one pretrained VLA to handle all three modes without separate model variants. In real-world tasks with unseen categories, vision-language and visual instructions achieve 100% success versus 16.7% for language-only, demonstrating the value of deictic gestures for disambiguation.
  • 🆕 Act with Intent: Distilling Behavior Intent for VLA Models

This intent signal provides a more structured VLA learning target than future frames or latent trajectories, grounding action prediction in purpose rather than particular realizations.
  • TacForcing: Streaming Action Generation with Execution-Time Tactile Feedback
    HF Papers · 2026-08-31 · VLATacForcing injects execution-time tactile feedback directly into a chunk-based VLA's action generation stream, adapting to contact states that evolve within an action horizon without requiring a separate high-frequency controller.   • PredVLA: A Sub-Million-Parameter Predictive-Coding Policy for Robot Manipulation
