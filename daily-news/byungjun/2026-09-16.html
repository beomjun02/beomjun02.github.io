  • 🆕 GeomVLA: Unifying Scene, Motion, and Action in 3D
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-16 · VLAA VLA model that lifts pretrained VLM features into spatially grounded 3D scene tokens using depth and camera calibration, then introduces a 3D Scene Trajectory Denoiser for latent motion prediction. Rather than executing predicted trajectories open-loop, GeomVLA conditions a 3D flow-based action denoiser via geometry-aware attention. Achieves SOTA on CALVIN, competitive on LIBERO and RoboTwin2.0, without robot-action pretraining.
  • 🆕 How to Better Train VLAs: Lessons From the REAL-I Challenge at ICRA 2026
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-16 · VLAReports from the first Real-world Embodied AI Learning Challenge at ICRA 2026, where teams combined pretrained VLA models with task-specific imitation policies on a shared dual-arm humanoid platform. Key findings: adapting to the deployment environment while retaining prior capabilities matters more than scale, demonstration quality at the right temporal granularity is critical, and offline action-prediction metrics poorly predict closed-loop success.
  • 🆕 What Makes an Efficient VLA? Navigating Action-Head Design, Scaling, and Latency
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-16 · VLAFixes backbone families (SigLIP2, Qwen2.5) and sweeps action-head design under controlled latency-paired conditions. The single largest lever is initializing the action head by copying the language backbone's last transformer layers — a free lunch at no latency cost that helps at every scale. Flow matching and heavier decoders only help while the head is misaligned; once aligned, they reverse. EffVLA matches strongest open-source VLAs on LIBERO at lower latency.
