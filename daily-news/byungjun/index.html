  • 🆕 FOCA: Future-Oriented Conditioning for Data-Efficient VLA Adaptation
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-24 · VLAIntroduces future-oriented conditioning for few-shot VLA adaptation by predicting task-grounded future interaction embeddings in latent space. Achieves 95.7% success with just 20 demonstrations on LIBERO, improves 7–12% on RoboCasa, and delivers up to 26% absolute gains on real robots — a new state of the art in few-shot VLA adaptation.
  • 🆕 SafeDojo: Safe RL for VLA via Interactive World Model
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-24 · VLAThe first model-based safe reinforcement learning framework for VLA policies. Performs online RL inside an interactive video world model, using a decoupled task-reward and safety-cost signal balanced through Lagrangian-constrained GRPO. Achieves the best safe-success rate on SafeLIBERO and validates on real-world Franka deployment across five tasks.
  • 🆕 NAC: Neural Action Codec for Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-24 · VLARepurposes neural audio codec architecture (multi-scale RVQGAN) to tokenize robot action trajectories for VLAs. Replaces mel-spectrogram losses with time-domain and spectral reconstruction, achieving lower reconstruction error and higher task success than binning, FAST, and prior VQ tokenizers across LIBERO-10, RoboMimic, and real-world tasks.
  • 🆕 World Action Models: A Survey
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-24 · VLAComprehensive survey clarifying the boundaries among world models, video generation models, VLA policies, and World Action Models. Organizes the field through two views — what each method generates and how it couples prediction to action — revealing a design pattern where WAMs generate less of the future while preserving what control requires.
  • 🆕 Vesta: A Generalist Embodied Reasoning Model
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-24 · long-horizon planningUnifies localization, spatial reasoning, navigation, and long-horizon planning into a single foundation model with a multimodal memory harness. Beats individual SOTA baselines by >20% on average and an ensemble of per-category-best baselines by >10%. Improves real-world robotic task success by >35% on tasks requiring memory and reasoning.
  • 🆕 MemoryVAM: Integrating Memory into Video Action Model for Robot Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-24 · long-horizon planningAdds episodic memory to video-world-model policies via a Perceiver-based Recap Compressor and Cue Gate. On LIBERO-Mem, improves success from 5% to 42.5%; on real robots achieves 78.3% on counting tasks, 80% on spatial recall, and 75% on sequential tracking. Works with both UNet and DiT backbones.
  • 🆕 CAMP: Compressed Action Memory for Partially Observable Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-24 · long-horizon planningIntroduces a compressed representation of the robot's own action history as a self-supervised memory signal for long-horizon, contact-rich manipulation. First policy to demonstrate substantial success on contact-rich partially observable tasks purely through learned memory, with gains across four real-robot setups and two novel simulation benchmarks.
  • 🆕 OpenHLM: An Empirical Recipe for Whole-Body Humanoid Loco-Manipulation
    HF papers · 2026-06-24 · VLASystematic study asking what it takes to build a whole-body native VLA model that maps language and pixels directly to all of a humanoid's degrees of freedom. Finds that decoupling upper and lower body yields behaviors similar to a wheeled dual-arm platform, and that whole-body native training is necessary for true humanoid coordination.
