Papers

  • 🆕 ACE-Ego-0: Unifying Egocentric Human and Robotic Data for VLA Pretraining
    A unified VLA pretraining framework that jointly leverages egocentric human video and robot demonstration data. Builds a scalable pipeline converting human videos into robot-format pseudo-action trajectories with reliability-aware training, achieving state-of-the-art on RoboCasa GR1 TableTop and RoboTwin 2.0 benchmarks.
  • 🆕 ThinkingVLA: Interleaved Vision and Language Reasoning for Robotic Manipulation
    Proposes a VLA that decomposes manipulation planning into forward prediction (anticipating next visual state) and inverse dynamics (inferring actions to reach it), bridging both via interleaved textual and visual reasoning within a unified Mixture-of-Transformers architecture. Shows particularly large gains on long-horizon manipulation tasks.
  • 🆕 PearlVLA: Progressive Embodied Action-Plan Refinement in Latent Space
    Moves VLA deliberation into the latent space of a vision-language model, separating visual grounding from iterative plan refinement. A lightweight frozen latent world model guides progressive refinement of coarse semantic drafts into fine-grained action plans, achieving state-of-the-art on LIBERO with low inference latency.
  • 🆕 MuseVLA: Adaptive Multimodal Sensing VLA for Robotic Manipulation
    Extends VLA models beyond RGB by integrating novel sensors (temperature, sound, radar) as on-demand tools. Generates a sensor token selecting which modality to invoke, then converts readings into a unified grounded sensor image for fusion and action generation. Achieves 80.6% success on real-world dexterous tasks requiring multimodal sensing.
  • 🆕 WAM-RL: World-Action Model Reinforcement Learning
    First framework to introduce reinforcement learning into the World-Action model paradigm for joint optimization of world model and action model through online interaction. Key finding: jointly optimizing both components is critical for long-horizon tasks, where actor-only optimization fails to provide significant gains.
  • 🆕 EgoInfinity: A Web-Scale 4D Hand-Object Interaction Data Engine
    A universal data engine that converts arbitrary internet human manipulation videos into robot training data at web scale. Lifts in-the-wild videos into agent-agnostic 4D hand-object representations and compiles them into executable joint trajectories for diverse robot morphologies, enabling video-to-action retargeting from arbitrary viewpoints.
  • 🆕 CAIP: Contrastive Action-Image Pre-training for Visuomotor Control
    A vision encoder that uses 3D hand keypoints from 32K hours of egocentric human video as a proxy for end-effector actions, learning a unified action-image representation through contrastive pre-training. Outperforms DINOv2, SigLIP, MVP, and R3M on real-world dexterous manipulation with 30%+ gains on tasks like folding and pouring.
