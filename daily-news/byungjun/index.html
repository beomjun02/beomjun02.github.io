Papers

  • NVIDIA RoboTTT: 8000-Timestep Context for Robot Policies
    HF papers · 2026-07-18 · long-horizon planningScales a robot model natively to 8,000 timesteps of context — 5 minutes of muscle memory — with constant inference cost. Previous robot policies operated on just a few frames (<0.1 sec), instantly forgetting what happened. This pushes context by 3 orders of magnitude, enabling much longer-horizon manipulation without growing compute.
  • Steering Robustness into World Action Models via Mechanistic Interpretability and Optimal Control
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-20 · VLAUses mechanistic interpretability to study how distribution-shift perturbations are represented in WAM activations. Finds that Cosmos-Policy and DiT4DiT exhibit linearly separable robustness features, enabling training-free steering via contrastive activation directions and a reduced-order LQR controller (WA-LQR) that generalizes across tasks and perturbation types.
  • DiMaS: Distribution Matching for Steering Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-20 · VLAProposes distribution-matching steering for flow-matching VLAs, transporting between representation distributions rather than shifting along a fixed direction. Shows that behavioral features in VLAs are linearly decodable but not linearly steerable — motivating a fundamentally different approach to fine-grained behavioral control at inference time.
  • 🆕 Action QFormer: Structured Representation Shaping under Action Supervision in VLAs
    HF papers · 2026-07-20 · VLAStudies action supervision as a force that shapes inherited multimodal representations in VLAs. Finds that applying action supervision too directly to the multimodal pathway destabilizes language-side representations. Introduces Action QFormer as a structured bottleneck that decouples action-compatible representation learning from the inherited pathway.
  • LifelongVLA: Lifelong Vision-Language-Action Learning for Robotic Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-20 · VLAAddresses the plasticity-stability tradeoff in VLA models via a dual-timescale LoRA gating module — a short-term adapter for new tasks and a long-term adapter for consolidation, integrated through a task-aware gate. Includes a cache-efficient stochastic replay strategy that reduces memory requirements for lifelong deployment on a real xArm robot.
  • Representation-Aligned Tactile Grounding for Contact-Rich Robotic Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-20 · VLALinear probe analysis reveals that future tactile states are most predictable from intermediate action-expert features in VLAs, not from vision-language features or final action outputs. Introduces a Latent Tactile Predictor that grounds intermediate representations to future contact consequences without predicting noisy raw signals.
  • AeroAct: Action-Centered World-Action Models for Language-Conditioned Quadrotor Flight
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-20 · VLAThe first WAM instantiated for real-world aerial flight. Adapts a pretrained video diffusion Transformer for trajectory-action chunk prediction from egocentric visual history, proprioception, and language. Future first-person frames serve as dense supervision during training; only actions are decoded at deployment. Includes a low-cost handheld data collection device.
  • Reflex: Real-Time VLA Control through Streaming Inference
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-18 · VLASolves a fundamental real-time bottleneck in flow-matching VLA models: global timestep injection invalidates KV-caching, forcing expensive O(N²) re-computation. Reflex reformulates inference as a streaming pipeline, enabling real-time closed-loop VLA control without sacrificing precision.
