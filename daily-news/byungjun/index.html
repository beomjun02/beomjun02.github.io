  • 🆕 RhinoVLA Technical Report
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · VLAA deployment-oriented VLA co-designed with the Huixi R1 edge SoC. Uses a token-efficient Qwen3-VL backbone and a continuous Action Expert, plus a unified cross-robot interface with View Registry and 72D physical state-action slots. Achieves performance comparable to pi0.5 at a similar parameter scale while hitting 11.69 Hz end-to-end inference -- meeting the 10 Hz real-time closed-loop control target.
  • 🆕 ActionMap: Robot Policy Learning via Voxel Action Heatmap
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · VLAReplaces single-point action decoders in VLAs with a voxel heatmap head that directly predicts per-voxel action probabilities, exploiting geometric proximity of neighboring actions. Surpasses OpenVLA-OFT L1 regression head by +8.2% on LIBERO across two architecturally distinct backbones, and is markedly more data-efficient at low training data.
  • 🆕 Coarse-to-Control: Action-Token Planning for VLA Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · VLA · long-horizon planningIntroduces planning natively in the VLA action-token space: the policy first predicts a compact sequence of coarse action tokens summarizing the intended trajectory, then generates executable tokens conditioned on this plan. Because both stages share a unified discrete action vocabulary, the plan stays on the control manifold. Largest gains on long-horizon multi-stage tasks.
  • 🆕 WIZARD: Robotic Policy Adaptation via Weight-Space Meta-Learning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · VLAA weight-space meta-learning framework that generates task-specific LoRA parameters for a frozen VLA in a single forward pass, given only a language instruction and a short demo video -- no target-task action labels or test-time optimization needed. Improves performance by up to ~14x on unseen tasks on LIBERO and transfers to real Franka.
  • 🆕 AEGIS: A Backup Reflex for Physical AI
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · VLAA selective escalation method using a lightweight probe on a weak VLA policy frozen activations to detect high-risk steps before failure cascades. When the probe flags a step, control switches to a stronger policy -- but only for the flagged steps. Recovers 10.1% of trajectories the weak policy loses on LIBERO-Spatial, at only 38% strong-policy utilization.
  • 🆕 AdaWAM: Advancing World Action Models with Adaptive Multi-Modal Reasoning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · long-horizon planningObserves that WAMs need different reasoning modes at different execution stages: textual reasoning at task transitions for high-level guidance, visual reasoning during fine-grained manipulation for precise control. A lightweight dynamic router autonomously triggers the right mode during execution, improving inference efficiency while outperforming state-of-the-art embodied policies.
  • 🆕 Neuro-Symbolic Learning for Long-Horizon Task Planning Under Complex Logical Constraints
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · long-horizon planningFormulates object-importance learning as bilevel optimization: a neural scorer prunes the search space at the upper level while symbolic planning runs in the pruned space at the lower level. A parallel Repair-Restart-Rollback strategy stabilizes learning. Achieves 80% failure-rate reduction and 57% planning-time reduction, validated on a real quadruped mobile manipulator.
  • 🆕 Task-Edit: Task Editing for Generalizable 3D Visuomotor Policy Learning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · long-horizon planningDecomposes tasks into scene, skill, and object components that can be flexibly recombined for scalable demonstration generation. Enables 3D visuomotor policies to generalize across different scenario setups, handle disturbance resistance and obstacle avoidance, and work in unseen cluttered scenes -- all from task-centric editing of a small set of real demonstrations.
  • 🆕 Spline Policy: A Structured Representation for Robot Policies
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · VLAReplaces fixed-resolution action chunks with spline parameters as the policy output, enabling compact continuous trajectories, temporal resampling, and a local corrective vector field from an analytical distance-field construction. Compatible with diffusion, flow-matching, transformer, and VLA backbones; supports uncertainty propagation and null-space collision avoidance without retraining.
  • 🆕 Robots Need More than VLA and World Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · VLAA position paper arguing the VLA-scaling framing is incomplete: the central bottleneck is not policy learning but the absence of mechanisms converting the world abundant unstructured behavioral data into grounded robot supervision.
