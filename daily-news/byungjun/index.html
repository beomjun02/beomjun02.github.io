  • 🆕 World-Language-Action Model for Unified World Modeling, Language Reasoning, and Action Synthesis
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06 · VLAProposes WLA models that jointly predict textual subtasks, subgoal images, and robot actions from an autoregressive Transformer backbone, conjoining the world modeling interface of WAMs with the language reasoning of VLAs.   • 🆕 Flash-WAM: Modality-Aware Distillation for World Action Models
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-06 · VLACompresses world-action model inference from tens of denoising steps to a single step per modality via consistency distillation adapted to the different noise regimes of video and action streams. Reduces per-chunk latency from 8.1s to 348ms (23x speedup) on NVIDIA L40S, preserving 85.5% success on RoboTwin 2.0 and substantially recovering real-world performance on a Unitree G1 humanoid.
  • 🆕 FlowPRO: Reward-Free Reinforced Fine-Tuning of Flow-Matching VLAs via Proximalized Preference Optimization
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06 · VLA · long-horizon planningA reward-free offline reinforcement fine-tuning framework for flow-matching VLAs that uses preference optimization with a proximal regularizer to eliminate reward hacking. A teleoperated intervention-and-rollback paradigm produces naturally paired positive/negative trajectories on a real robot. Achieves the highest success rate on four long-horizon bimanual tasks versus four representative baselines.
  • 🆕 MPCoT: Reward-Guided Multi-Path Latent Reasoning for Test-Time Scalable Vision-Language-Action
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06 · VLA · long-horizon planningIntroduces multi-path latent reasoning for VLAs that initializes M hypotheses, refines them for K weight-tied steps, and softly aggregates before action decoding — all without generating reasoning tokens. A training-only path-preference objective with expert-action consistency and world-model-based progress feedback aligns the path scorer. Improves long-horizon performance on LIBERO and CALVIN with configurable inference-time compute scaling.
  • 🆕 AffordanceVLA: A Vision-Language-Action Model Empowering Action Generation through Affordance-Aware Understanding
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06 · VLAIntroduces structured affordance forecasting as an intermediate representation for VLAs: Which2Act for object-centric grounding, Where2Act for 2D interaction localization via affordance maps, and How2Act for 3D geometric reasoning. Integrated into a Mixture-of-Transformer architecture with a three-stage training strategy, achieving strong performance across diverse simulation and real-world manipulation scenarios.
  • 🆕 PiL-World: A Chunk-Wise World Model for VLA Policy-in-the-Loop Evaluation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06 · VLAA chunk-wise world model that enables closed-loop VLA evaluation without real robot execution by alternating between VLA inference and world-model prediction. Learns from both successful and failed trajectories to better match real policy execution distributions. Reduces the error between real and estimated VLA success rates from 63.2% to 12.0% on three real dual-arm manipulation tasks.
  • 🆕 MotionDisco: Motion Discovery for Extreme Humanoid Loco-Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06 · long-horizon planningDiscovers contact-rich, long-horizon humanoid loco-manipulation motions from scratch — no teleoperation or motion retargeting needed. Couples LLM-guided evolutionary search over interaction sequences with a sequential kinodynamic trajectory optimizer. First work to discover and deploy long-horizon humanoid loco-manipulation skills entirely through automated evolutionary search, transferring to a real humanoid via RL tracking policies.
  • 🆕 RealDexUMI: A Wearable Universal Manipulation Interface for Dexterous Robot Learning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06A wearable dexterous data collection interface built around a shared end-effector module with a lightweight dexterous hand, in-hand vision, and fingertip tactile sensing. A palm-side isomorphic teleoperation glove enables retargeting-free control. Policies trained on RealDexUMI data achieve 88.75% average success across eight contact-rich tasks and transfer across three embodiments.
  • 🆕 LadderMan: Learning Humanoid Perceptive Ladder Climbing
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06A unified system for humanoid robots to climb diverse ladders and perform on-ladder manipulation. Uses a two-stage pipeline with hybrid motion tracking to learn climbing experts from a single reference motion, then distills into a depth-based visuomotor policy. Successfully transfers to real hardware zero-shot across a wide range of ladder geometries, with a dual-agent formulation enabling stable on-ladder manipulation via teleoperation.
  • 🆕 ActiveMimic: Egocentric Video Pretraining with Active Perception
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06Identifies active perception (continuous viewpoint repositioning during manipulation) as the key missing signal in egocentric human video pretraining for robot policies.
