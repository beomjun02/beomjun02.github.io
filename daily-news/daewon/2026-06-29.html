  • InfoKV: Information-Aware KV Cache Compression for Long Reasoning
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-27 · kv cache compressionIntroduces entropy-aware KV cache compression that combines token-level predictive uncertainty with layer-wise representation evolution. Outperforms existing attention-based methods on long-context reasoning benchmarks with Llama-3.1, Llama-3.2, and DeepSeek-R1.
