  • 🆕 PyroDash: Cost-Efficient Token-Level Small-Large Language Model Collaborative Inference
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-24 · sLM · routingPyroDash routes inference token-by-token between a cheap SLM and an expensive LLM using a cost-aware framework. During generation, each token is first attempted by the SLM; a lightweight confidence monitor decides whether to escalate to the LLM. This achieves near-LLM quality at substantially reduced cost by only invoking the large model when the small one is uncertain.
