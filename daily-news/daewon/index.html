  • 🆕 Quantization Inflates Reasoning: Token Inflation as a Hidden Cost

  • 🆕 Internal Data Repetition Destroys Language Models
    arXiv · 2026-06-26 · efficient trainingRevisits data repetition under Chinchilla-era scaling laws. Key finding: the most damaging repeat count follows a power law in model size — moderate-sized subsets repeated moderately hurt more than large subsets repeated few times or small subsets repeated many times. At 10% FLOP budget for repeated data, the worst case matches a no-repetition run at 67% of the FLOPs.
