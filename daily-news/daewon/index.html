  • 🆕 Affix Cache for Diffusion Large Language Models
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-08-29 · kv cache compressionDiffusion LLMs can't naively reuse KV cache because bidirectional attention couples shared-context tokens with evolving generated tokens. ACache identifies ~20% of critical "anchor tokens" to selectively recompute while reusing the rest, cutting recompute latency by 55.7% and improving end-to-end throughput by 1.68x on a Nano-vLLM prototype.
  • 🆕 The Reasoning Tax: Token Economics of LLM Reasoning Across Task Types and Deployment Contexts
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-08-29 · token costIntroduces Token Economy Score (TES), measuring accuracy gain per reasoning-token multiplier across 151 evaluation runs. Sequential inference-chain tasks (AIME 2025, LiveCodeBench) yield high TES; knowledge-recall tasks show low TES despite high difficulty. Systematic diminishing returns at higher reasoning effort, including cases where more thinking hurts accuracy.

  • 🆕 Activation Outliers Matter: Robust Recovery for Quantized Multimodal LLMs
