  • 🆕 Latent Communication Between Language Model Agents: Channels, Alignment, and the Limits of Text
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-18 · Multi Agent · AlignmentInvestigates whether LLM agents communicating via text lose information versus latent-space channels.
