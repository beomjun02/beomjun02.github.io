  • 🆕 Reliability Scales Inversely: Bigger Models Compound Mistakes Faster via a Hidden Auto-Regressive Risk Regime

  • 🆕 Operational Hallucination and Safety Drift in AI Agents
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-07-23 · Safety · AgentEmpirically characterizes two failure modes across multiple frontier LLMs in multi-turn execution: Safety Drift (gradual erosion of declared safety intent leading to constraint-violating actions despite textual refusal) and Operational Hallucination (persistent repetitive tool calls from flawed state perception).
