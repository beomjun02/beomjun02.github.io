  • 🆕 Confidently Deceptive: How Confidence Amplifies the Risk of LLM Deception
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-25 · Confidence · SafetyLLMs deliver deceptive responses with substantial verbalized confidence, and humans prefer the higher-confidence deceptive answer 78% of the time.   • 🆕 PhantomFill: When the Form Demands an Answer, Language Models Invent One
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-25 · TrustworthyShows that structured output formats cause hallucination: given unanswerable inputs, GPT-5.5 answers honestly 98% of the time in free text but fabricates 100% of the time when a JSON field is required. Required fields drive fabrication to 100% in 10 of 13 models — an explicit "insufficient evidence" option rescues only frontier models. The fabrication hides where hedging is impossible.
  • 🆕 Robust Critics: Defending LLMs Against Multi-Turn Attacks
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-25 · SafetyModels adversarial dialogue as an MDP and learns value/regret-based critics at token and utterance levels. Proves that inference-time reweighting approximates exponential tilting of the base policy with guaranteed improvement — a property group-relative objectives lack. Outperforms strong baselines and frontier models on adversarial dialogue benchmarks.

  • 🆕 StabilityBench: Benchmarking Instability in LLMs

  • 🆕 From Agent Failures to Text Policies: What Works and What Breaks
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-25 · Agent · Agent LearningStudies TextGrad applied to agents and finds a clear gap between following a useful policy and learning that policy from experience.   • 🆕 The Dark Room in the Reward Channel: Dense Prediction Rewards Collapse GRPO-Trained LLM Agents
