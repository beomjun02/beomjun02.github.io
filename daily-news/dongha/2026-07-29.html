  • 🆕 Not All LLM Reasoning is Visible in the Chain-of-Thought
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-29 · SafetyDemonstrates that frontier models perform consequential computation with no interpretable trace: semantically irrelevant filler tokens improve accuracy by up to 13 pp across 13 models.   • 🆕 Two Regimes of Chain-of-Thought Unfaithfulness
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-29 · TrustworthyReveals that 69% of annotated unfaithful CoT occurs on incorrect answers, splitting detection into two regimes: moderate behavioral separation on correct answers (AUROC 0.63–0.67) but no tested signal above chance on incorrect ones. The standard step-removal metric anti-correlates with human labels, and linear probes find no shared direction across regimes.
  • 🆕 Same Question, Different Answers: Evaluating LLM Reliability Beyond Accuracy
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-07-29 · ConfidenceStudies model reliability under meaning-preserving paraphrases across 4 benchmarks and 13 models. While overall accuracy shifts modestly, instance-level mismatch rates exceed 23%, and answer flip rates on originally-correct questions are even higher. A simple self-paraphrasing strategy at inference time partially recovers the latent knowledge that single-prompt evaluation misses.
