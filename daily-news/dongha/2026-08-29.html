  • 🆕 PILOT in the Loop: Live Self-Improvement for Long-Horizon Agents

  • 🆕 NeuronFuzz: Safety Neuron Guided Fuzzing for LLM Safety Evaluation
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-08-28 · Safety · LLMUses internal safety neurons as continuous execution feedback for LLM safety evaluation, eliminating the need for response generation during the fuzzing loop. A SafetyOracle converts safety-neuron activations into a differentiable alarm score that guides mutation.   • 🆕 Can a Model Catch Its Own Hallucinations for Free?
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-08-28 · Confidence · LLMFine-tunes models to abstain when their own frozen confidence is low, using no correctness labels at all. Across six open-weight models (1B–8B) on factual QA, this label-free recipe shows no statistically detectable difference from label-supervised abstention tuning at matched coverage. The signal's one blind spot is confidently wrong facts, which it cannot flag.
  • 🆕 How Unlikely Is "Unlikely"? Assessing Verbal Probability Perception Across Large Language Models
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-08-28 · Confidence · CalibrationSystematically evaluates 11 uncertainty expressions across 19 models, finding that LLMs track human verbal probability benchmarks with surprising fidelity — word ordering is preserved and three anchor points are recovered. However, models show a systematic upward bias for negative expressions like "unlikely." Frontier models maintain more coherent bidirectional representations in a novel roundtrip consistency test.
  • 🆕 Don't Overthink, Don't Underthink: Toward Adaptive Reasoning in Agentic AI
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-08-28 · Agent · LLMCharacterizes over-reasoning and under-reasoning as recurring failure modes where agentic LLMs misallocate inference-time reasoning. Evaluates on MATH-500 and GAIA, showing that over-reasoning cases cost more compute without proportional accuracy gains, while under-reasoning consistently produces incorrect or incomplete solutions. Motivates work on adaptive reasoning allocation for agentic settings.

  • 🆕 SKILL.state: Scalable Long-Horizon Agent Skills

  • 🆕 Video-FLAIR: Not Whether to Reason, But How
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-08-28 · VLMLearns to select the appropriate reasoning mode (perceptual, compositional, or deliberative) for each video query using reinforcement learning.   • 🆕 Echo-GRPO: Idiolectal Paraphrasing Off-Policy Traces for Reasoning Distillation in VideoLLMs
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-08-28 · VLMAddresses the distribution mismatch between teacher and student policies in GRPO-based reasoning distillation: off-policy teacher traces cause gradient clipping on critical reasoning tokens, rewarding correct answers while leaving the justifying reasoning unlearned. Echo-GRPO rewrites teacher traces into the student's own idiolect via Dual-Reference Decoding, consistently improving across three VLM backbones and five benchmarks.
