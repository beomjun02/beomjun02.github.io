  • 🆕 Reference-Grafting Matches Fine-Tuning at Eliciting Sandbagged Capabilities
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-09-01 · SafetyRecovers +94–101% of the honest-sandbagging gap in password-locked models (1.5–32B) without weight updates or training labels, matching fine-tuning elicitation.   • 🆕 Recognition-Refusal Misalignment in LLMs: Why Models Answer Structurally Unanswerable Questions
