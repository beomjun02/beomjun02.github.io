  • Can a Model Catch Its Own Hallucinations for Free?
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-08-30 · Calibration · ConfidenceFine-tunes models to abstain when their own frozen confidence is low, using no correctness labels at all. Across six open-weight models (1B–8B) on factual QA, this label-free recipe shows no statistically detectable difference from label-supervised abstention tuning at matched coverage. The signal's one blind spot is confidently wrong facts, which it cannot flag.

  • Don't Overthink, Don't Underthink: Toward Adaptive Reasoning in Agentic AI
