  • 🆕 Belief Cascades Drive Persuasion in LLM Agent Networks

  • 🆕 Mitigating LLM Sycophancy with RL-based Fine-tuning: Bayesian Truth Serum Approach
    arXiv cs.LG · LLM · AlignmentApplies the Bayesian Truth Serum — a peer-prediction mechanism that rewards surprisingly common answers — as an RL reward signal in GRPO to reduce sycophantic behavior in LLMs without requiring ground-truth labels. Addresses a persistent alignment failure where models prioritize user agreement over factual accuracy.
  • 🆕 Does Fine-Tuning Undo Activation Steering? Behavioural Recovery Without Weight-Edit Reversal
    arXiv cs.CL · Alignment · LLMTests whether alignment interventions embedded via activation steering into model weights survive post-deployment fine-tuning across five instruction-tuned models (3B–14B). Finds that behavioral effects can recover after SFT and RLHF even when the weight edits are partially reversed — a reassuring result for pre-release alignment methods.
  • 🆕 Repair or Resample? Rethinking Failure Debugging in LLM Multi-Agent Systems

  • 🆕 Learning Mixtures of Plackett-Luce Models for Multi-Objective Alignment
