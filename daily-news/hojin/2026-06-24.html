  • 🆕 World Action Models: A Survey
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-24 · WAMComprehensive survey organizing the rapidly growing WAM landscape through two complementary views: what methods generate (rendered/latent futures vs video-free action reasoning) and how they decompose by backbone, action coupling, and deployment regime. Identifies a consistent design pattern — WAMs are moving toward generating less of the future while preserving what control requires.
  • 🆕 Vesta: A Generalist Embodied Reasoning Model
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-24 · robot foundation modelA unified foundation model consolidating localization, spatial reasoning, navigation, and long-horizon planning into a single model, eliminating the multi-model stack. On average beats individual SOTA baselines by >20% and an ensemble of per-category-best baselines by >10%, with >35% real-robot task success improvement on memory-and-reasoning tasks.
  • 🆕 MV-WAM: Manifold-Aware World Action Model with Value Augmentation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-24 · WAMAddresses the visual-action manifold mismatch that limits WAM generalization under distribution shift via cross-modality causal masking and manifold-aware optimization. Introduces a progress-value regulation mechanism for autonomous deviation detection and value-guided rollback. Achieves 55.7% on RoboTwin random scenarios without randomized action supervision and 77.5% on real dual-arm tasks.
  • 🆕 FOCA: Future-Oriented Conditioning for Data-Efficient VLA Adaptation
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-24 · VLATackles the sharp performance degradation of VLA models under few-shot imitation by combining explicit future interaction prediction with implicit future goal alignment in latent space. Achieves 95.7% success with just 20 demonstrations on LIBERO, 7-12% improvement on RoboCasa, and up to 26% absolute gains on real robots — new state of the art in few-shot VLA adaptation.
  • 🆕 SafeDojo: Safe RL for VLA via Interactive World Model
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-24 · VLA · sim-to real RLFirst model-based safe RL framework for VLA policies, learning safe actions through world-model-based imagination. Decouples task-reward and safety-cost signals via Lagrangian-constrained GRPO, with a ResNet classifier for task progress and a lightweight safety head for per-step cost. Best safe-success rate on SafeLIBERO with 8.25pp improvement over strongest baseline, plus real Franka deployment.
  • 🆕 NAC: Neural Action Codec for VLA Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-24 · VLARepurposes neural audio codec architectures (multi-scale RVQGAN with Vocos-style ISTFT decoder) as action tokenizers for VLA models. Replaces audio-specific mel-spectrogram objectives with time-domain kinematic losses. Achieves lower reconstruction error and higher success rates than binning, FAST, and prior VQ-based tokenizers across LIBERO-10, RoboMimic, and real-world tasks.
  • 🆕 MemoryVAM: Integrating Memory into Video Action Model for Robot Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-24 · WAMAdds episodic memory to video-world-model policies via a Recap-Cue module that maps per-frame CLIP embeddings into compact memory tokens, injected into both video backbone and action decoder. Fixes the non-Markovian failure mode of short-window conditioning — improves average success from 5% to 42.5% on LIBERO-Mem and 78.3% on real-robot counting tasks.
  • 🆕 CAMP: Compressed Action Memory for Partially Observable Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-24 · robot learningIntroduces a compressed action-history representation as a self-supervised memory signal for contact-rich manipulation under partial observability. Enables implicit task progress tracking and learning from failed attempts without additional supervision. First policy to demonstrate substantial success on contact-rich POMDP manipulation tasks through learned memory alone.
  • 🆕 Inductive Generalization for Robotic Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-24 · VLAProposes a formal evaluation protocol for measuring inductive (OOD) generalization in manipulation policies on progressively harder task variants. Finds that SOTA VLA models that appear to generalize to standard domain shifts (distractors, lighting) fail inductive generalization tests — exposing a class of challenges orthogonal to data and model scaling.
  • 🆕 BayesFP: Posterior Estimation for Flow-Based Policies via Feynman-Kac Sampling
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-24 · robot learningExtends the Feynman-Kac corrector framework from diffusion models to deterministic flow-matching policies, enabling inference-time constrained trajectory generation without retraining. Validated on pretrained Diffusion Policy, GR00T-N1.6, and pi_0.5 checkpoints for sim and real manipulation tasks, including planning around non-convex obstacles introduced at inference time.
  • 🆕 AnyMug: Pose-Agnostic Robotic Functional Grasping via Canonicalization
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-24 · dexterous manipulation · sim-to real RLA canonicalized visuomotor RL framework for functional grasping that transforms observations and actions into a shared object-centric frame. A single closed-loop policy trained entirely in simulation with handle-aware reward and pose curriculum achieves 93%+ sim success on unseen upright and inverted mugs and transfers zero-shot to a real Franka Panda at 80%.
  • 🆕 MotionPyramid: Hierarchical Motion Representation and Residual Interfaces
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-24 · robot learningLearns a recursive multi-level motion hierarchy from a motion tracking teacher, where lower latent levels decode immediate motor commands and higher levels unfold into temporally extended motion programs. Introduces Residual Interfaces letting downstream RL policies maintain coarse segment-level and fine frame-level residual commands — analogous to skip connections in deep networks — through a frozen hierarchy.
