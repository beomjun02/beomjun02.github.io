  • 🆕 CoStream: Composing Simple Behaviors for Generalizable Complex Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-27 · robot learningShows that complex manipulation capabilities emerge naturally from composing independent behaviors — semantic (spatial constraints via foundation models), predictive (trajectory forecasting via keypoint tracking in imagined videos), and reactive (tactile and force corrections).
