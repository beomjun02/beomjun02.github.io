  • Tactile-WAM: Touch-Aware World Action Model with Tactile Asymmetric Attention
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-27 · WAMIntroduces tactile sensing into world action models, addressing "tactile pollution" — a failure mode where unconstrained tactile-token injection degrades video and action prediction. A Tactile Asymmetric Attention Mechanism blocks video-query access to tactile tokens while preserving action-query access via a touch-aware bias. Improves mean success rate by 38.9% overall and 86% on contact-rich tasks on ManiFeel.
  • PAMAE: Phase-Aware-MoE Action Experts Towards Reliable Flow-Matching VLA Policies

Improves task success by up to 9.2% over strong VLA baselines on multi-stage manipulation.
