  • 🆕 UniTacVLA: Unified Tactile Understanding and Prediction in Vision Language Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-02 · VLA · dexterous manipulationIntegrates tactile sensing into VLA models not as passive input but as an active prediction target — the model learns to understand current tactile signals and predict future contact outcomes.   • 🆕 Position: Vision-Language-Action Models Cannot Be Verified to Perform Physical Reasoning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-02 · VLAArgues that current VLA benchmarks cannot distinguish whether models are performing genuine physical reasoning or merely exploiting semantic shortcuts from VLM pretraining. The position is that semantic generalization alone is insufficient for physical action decisions, and existing evaluation protocols fail to isolate the physical reasoning component that would make VLAs truly reliable.
  • 🆕 Revisiting Parameter Redundancy in Vision-Language-Action Models: Insights from VLM-to-VLA Adaptation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-02 · VLAChallenges the assumption that VLA pruning sensitivity is inevitable by analyzing how redundancy patterns change during VLM-to-VLA adaptation. Finds that certain parameter subsets become disproportionately important during fine-tuning while others remain dispensable, suggesting that targeted pruning strategies informed by the adaptation process can substantially reduce VLA compute without the usual performance degradation.
  • 🆕 Does VLA Even Know the Basics? Measuring Commonsense and World Knowledge Retention in Vision-Language-Action Models
    HF papers · 2026-07-01 · VLAIntroduces Act2Answer, a protocol where VLAs answer knowledge questions through object-placement actions rather than text. Evaluating 7 VLAs against 9 VLM baselines reveals that robotics fine-tuning degrades semantic knowledge — simple concepts survive but richer categories show larger gaps relative to source VLMs. VQA co-training helps, and answer-relevant signals peak in middle layers but attenuate in upper ones.
  • 🆕 Delta-JEPA: Learning Action-Sensitive World Models via Latent Difference Decoding
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-07-02 · WAMAddresses the action-insensitivity collapse problem in reconstruction-free world models (JEPA-style) by introducing a Latent Difference Action Decoder that reconstructs executed actions from latent state transitions rather than concatenated endpoints. This ensures the learned dynamics remain sensitive to control inputs — a critical requirement for planning — without falling back to pixel reconstruction.
  • 🆕 Warp RL: Reshaping Base Policy Distributions for Dynamics Adaptation
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-02 · sim-to real RLShows that residual RL's additive corrections are fundamentally limited under dynamics shift — they can shift the base policy's distribution but cannot change its shape, scale, or state-dependent geometry. Formalizes three failure modes (wrong variance, miscalibrated confidence, non-uniform correction) and proposes distribution warping to handle the geometric mismatch that arises in sim-to-real transfer.
  • 🆕 Reasoning-aware Speculative Decoding for Efficient Vision-Language-Action Models in Autonomous Driving
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-07-02 · VLATargets the inference bottleneck in chain-of-causation VLA planners where autoregressive reasoning dominates latency while the trajectory head is parallel and cheap. Proposes reasoning-aware speculative decoding that distinguishes routine setup tokens (draftable) from critical causal reasoning tokens, accelerating VLA inference for real-time driving without sacrificing planning quality.
  • 🆕 Agentic RAG-VLM: Affordance-Aware Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Self-Reflective Planning for Robotic Grasping
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-07-02 · VLMMoves beyond visual-similarity matching for robotic grasping in clutter by grounding VLM reasoning in physical affordances — handle graspability, material fragility, spatial accessibility.
