  • 🆕 VT-WAM: Visual-Tactile World Action Model for Contact-Rich Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-03 · WAMIntroduces a unified flow-matching framework that jointly predicts future visual frames, tactile deformation, and robot actions. An asymmetric Mixture-of-Transformers bridges a visual anchor with temporal tactile dynamics, and a contact-gated attention mechanism forces action queries to attend to tactile evidence during contact phases. Achieves 71.67% average success on six real-world contact-rich tasks, outperforming Fast-WAM by 26.67%.
  • 🆕 Bridge-WA: Predicting Where and How the World Changes for Robotic Action
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-03 · WAMA lightweight world-action framework that distills a frozen future-change teacher into three compact priors — future tokens, change maps, and motion-flow maps — and feeds them into an action transformer via multi-source attention. The teacher is discarded at inference, eliminating dense future-image generation at deploy time. Shows particularly clear gains under out-of-distribution visual shifts on VLABench, RoboTwin2.0, LIBERO-Plus, and real-robot evaluations.
  • 🆕 VLA-Corrector: Lightweight Detect-and-Correct Inference for Adaptive Action Horizon
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-03 · VLAProposes a plug-in corrective framework for action-chunked VLA policies that detects when predicted visual dynamics diverge from reality and triggers replanning. A latent-space vision monitor continuously compares predicted vs. actual feature evolution; on detection of persistent drift, stale actions are discarded and an online gradient guidance module generates corrective actions. Naturally induces an event-triggered adaptive action horizon without retraining the VLA backbone.
  • 🆕 VLAFlow: A Unified Training Framework for Vision-Language-Action Models via Co-training and Future Latent Alignment
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-03 · VLA · robot foundation modelProvides the first controlled comparison of VLA pre-training paradigms — action-only, language co-training, future-latent alignment, and their combination — under the same pi0-style architecture, shared VLM backbone, and 14-D action space over ~5,000 hours of heterogeneous robot data. Finds that combining language supervision with future-latent alignment yields the most stable cross-benchmark transfer, suggesting language and latent representations serve as complementary intermediate constraints.
  • 🆕 PhysMani: Physics-principled 3D World Model for Dynamic Object Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-03 · robot learningCouples a physics-principled 3D Gaussian world model with a future-aware action policy for manipulating fast-moving targets. The world model learns a divergence-free Gaussian velocity field via online optimization for physically grounded dynamics prediction. Introduces PhysMani-Bench, a 16-task dynamic manipulation benchmark, and demonstrates strong results over baselines in both simulation and real-robot experiments.
  • 🆕 AutoSERL: One Demonstration Is Enough for Real-World Robotic Reinforcement Learning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-03 · robot learningAutomates the intervention process in real-world robot RL using just a single demonstration. A sliding-window intervention mechanism guides exploration, a safety recovery module corrects failure states via trajectory recovery points, and an automatic termination criterion disables guidance once the policy is self-sufficient. Evaluated on six contact-intensive manipulation tasks across two platforms, matching HIL-SERL's performance with 20 demos while needing only one.
  • 🆕 TAP: Task-Agnostic Pretraining for VLAs — Learning to Move Before Learning to Do
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-03 · VLA · robot foundation modelArgues that VLA data scarcity stems from conflating physical competence (how to move) with semantic alignment (what to do), and that only the latter requires language labels. Proposes a two-stage framework: self-supervised inverse-dynamics pretraining on cheap unlabeled play data, then lightweight language grounding with minimal expert demos. Matches models trained on 1M+ expert trajectories while using orders of magnitude less labeled data, and retains 25% success under camera perturbations where internet-scale baselines collapse to 0%.
  • 🆕 Actuator Reality Shaping for Zero-Shot Sim-to-Real Robot Learning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-03 · sim-to real RLIntroduces an alternative to sim-to-real domain adaptation: instead of modifying the simulator to match reality, shape physical actuator behavior to match the idealized dynamics used in training. A two-degree-of-freedom feedforward-feedback controller provides a standardized actuator interface. Validated on a 7-DOF arm reaching task (zero-shot), a wheeled-legged robot on slopes, and a humanoid walking — all without task-level fine-tuning.
