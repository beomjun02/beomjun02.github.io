  • 🆕 EgoWAM: World Action Models Beyond Pixels with In-the-Wild Egocentric Human Data

Introduces Temporal Ratio (TR), an attention-based measure predicting generalization capacity, and proposes inference-time adaptive guidance that mitigates the OOD-ID gap on LIBERO and real-world tasks.
  • 🆕 TFP: Temporally Conditioned Memory-Fusion Policies for Visuomotor Learning
