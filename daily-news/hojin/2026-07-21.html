  • 🆕 AC-VLA: Robust Out-of-Distribution Action Execution via Compositional Learning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-21 · VLAIdentifies two failure modes behind VLA OOD collapse: trajectory overfitting to holistic patterns and perceptual shortcuts via wrist-view textures.
