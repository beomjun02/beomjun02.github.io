  • 🆕 Scaling Cross-Embodiment World Models for Dexterous Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-23 · dexterous manipulation · robot foundation modelRepresents human and robot hands as 3D particle sets with actions as end-effector displacement fields, training a graph-based world model on random interaction data from diverse simulated and real hands.   • 🆕 ImplicitRDP: An End-to-End Visual-Force Diffusion Policy with Structural Slow-Fast Learning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-23 · robot learningProposes a unified end-to-end policy integrating visual planning and reactive force control within a single diffusion network. Structural Slow-Fast Learning uses causal attention to process asynchronous visual and force tokens at different rates, while Virtual-target-based Representation Regularization prevents modality collapse. Significantly outperforms vision-only and hierarchical baselines on contact-rich tasks.
  • 🆕 STeP: Signal Temporal Logic for Precise Specifications for Action Generation with Vision Language Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-23 · VLAA hierarchical framework using Signal Temporal Logic as a shared representation connecting VLMs with low-level robot execution. A VLM decomposes language instructions into subtasks with STL specifications, selecting between STL-guided MPC and learned policies. Evaluated on real-world tabletop tasks, demonstrating improved precision, reliability, and interpretability of language-conditioned planning.
  • 🆕 TacRefineNet: Goal-Conditioned Tactile Grasp Refinement for Edge-Prominent Objects
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-23 · dexterous manipulationA tactile-only, goal-conditioned framework for local grasp refinement along tactilely observable pose dimensions. A Siamese policy predicts corrective wrist increments from current and target multi-finger tactile images. Trained entirely in MuJoCo simulation and deployed zero-shot to a real 11-DoF five-fingered hand, achieving 80.7% success at 10°/10mm and mean errors of ~5.2mm and ~3.5°.
  • 🆕 Agentic Real2Sim: Physics-based World Modeling with Vision-Language Agents

  • 🆕 FARO: Feasibility-Aware Robot Motion Optimization
