  • 🆕 Robots Acquire Manipulation Skills in Seconds from a Single Human Video
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-24 · robot learningHOST enables a robot to acquire novel manipulation skills in an average of 29 seconds from a single human video, achieving 62% success rate. A cascade of self-grounded prediction estimates task progress, translates upcoming progression into the robot’s own future observations, and derives actions — exceeding a baseline fine-tuned on 50 robot demos per task while acquiring each skill 507x faster.
  • 🆕 Closing the Lab-to-Store Gap: A Data-Efficient Post-Training and Experience-Driven Learning VLA Framework for Retail Humanoids
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-24 · VLA · robot foundation modelDEED demonstrates that bridging VLA lab-to-real gaps is primarily a systems integration challenge. Using a Unitree G1 with the GR00T N1.6 foundation model on a supermarket chip-restocking task, careful data curation, control-frequency alignment, and experience-driven refinement transform a failing naive fine-tuned policy into a competent real-world system — all on a single GPU.
  • 🆕 Leveraging Offline Supervision for Efficient and Generalizable RL in Large-Scale Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-24 · VLA · sim-to-real RLShows that incorporating offline supervision (via offline data or reference policy) into online RL for VLAs preserves strong out-of-distribution capability while cutting training budget roughly in half. The hybrid approach retains near-standard-RL OOD performance without the typical speed-vs-generalization trade-off, suggesting a practical recipe for efficient VLA post-training.
  • 🆕 LENS: LLM-guided Environment Simplification for Planning and Control in Clutter

  • 🆕 Diffusion ReRoll: Revisable Denoising for Robotic Sequential Prediction
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-24 · robot learningIntroduces structured re-noising into diffusion-based robotic prediction: selectively re-noises locally stable regions while denoising continues elsewhere, enabling iterative cross-horizon revision.   • 🆕 KineBench: Benchmarking Embodied World Models via IDM-Free Kinematic Grounding

  • 🆕 ReferTrack: Referring Then Tracking for Embodied Visual Tracking
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-24 · VLAA referring-then-tracking paradigm for embodied visual tracking using a single forward camera: the model selects a target from indexed bounding boxes, then decodes tracking waypoints conditioned on the image-grounded decision. Achieves 89.4% / 73.3% / 74.1% success on single-target, distracted, and ambiguity tracking splits — matching multi-camera baselines. Deployed zero-shot on legged and humanoid robots.

  • 🆕 EgoRecovery: Acquiring Failure Recovery Ability Through Human Recovery Demonstration
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-24 · robot learningShows that egocentric human recovery demonstrations generate 10x more valid recovery data per hour than robot teleoperation. A learned recovery gate activates correction only in failure states — improving real-world recovery success over robot-only and direct co-training baselines.
