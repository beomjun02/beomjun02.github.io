  • 🆕 Addressing the Orchestration Gap in Generalist Robots via Physical Agency
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-28 · VLA · robot foundation modelIntroduces Pigey, a closed-loop physical agent orchestrator that decomposes goals, commands VLA policies or parametric skills, verifies outcomes from observations, and recovers from failures — all without additional data collection or post-training.   • 🆕 Ordered Action Tokens for Visuomotor Policy Learning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-28 · VLAIdentifies three desiderata for action tokenization — high compression, total decodability, and an ordered token space — and introduces OAT, a learned tokenizer satisfying all three. Each token prefix decodes into a valid action chunk, yielding an anytime inference-cost/fidelity tradeoff. Validated across three policy backbones and 60+ tasks spanning five simulation benchmarks and real-world settings.
  • 🆕 Robot-Factored World Models via Robot Rendering
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-28 · WAMProposes factoring robot-specific geometry, kinematics, and appearance out of world models by rendering the nominal robot trajectory through its URDF and conditioning video prediction on the rendered geometry. The shared visual interface stays consistent across viewpoints and embodiments; the model generalizes to unseen robots at inference and generates manipulation videos from human demonstrations via retargeted geometry.

  • 🆕 GRACE: Gradient-Free Robot Action Generation via Combined Diffusion-MPPI Posterior Mean Estimation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-28 · robot learningGuides a pretrained diffusion policy with MPPI using only forward cost evaluations, enabling nondifferentiable deployment-time constraints (collision checks, joint limits, black-box rollouts).
