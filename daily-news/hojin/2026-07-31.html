  • 🆕 RL²-VLA: Adaptive RL Latent Compositional Steering with Test-Time Scaling for Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-31 · VLATrains a lightweight offline RL policy on VLA latents and composes its flow velocity with the frozen VLA at inference time. Real-world experiments confirm the gains transfer beyond simulation.
  • 🆕 Enfold: Folding World-Generator Computation into Predictive Representations for Efficient Embodied Control
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-31 · WAMTransfers the internal computation of a world-action model generator into a representation predicted from the current visual context alone, so the generator need not run at every step. Reduces action latency by 3.7× relative to Fast-WAM on LIBERO, RoboTwin2.0, and real-robot tasks, with an Enfold-Flash variant reaching 10.1× speedup.
  • 🆕 KinRT: Kinematics-Supervised Expert Routing in MoE-Augmented VLA

Outperforms both dense and MoE-featured VLAs by 23.26% on RoboTwin and 20.27% on a custom DIY robot platform built for under $2,000.
  • 🆕 CheckVLA: Execution-Time Verification with Action-Conditioned World Model for Long-Horizon Mobile Manipulation

  • 🆕 DLAM: Distributional Latent Actions with Temporal Constraints

  • 🆕 HumanCLAW: Can Vision-Language Models Act Through a Body?
