  • 🆕 TacWAM: Anchor-Guided World Action Model with Mechanics-Aware Tactile Prediction
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-01 · WAMFirst WAM to incorporate tactile futures alongside visual prediction. A Spatially Aligned Fusion encoder maps force fields and deformation flow into a shared prediction space, while Anchor-Guided Tri-Modal Attention prevents future tactile states from becoming privileged cues for the action branch. Achieves 75.0% average success on four real-world contact-rich tasks, exceeding the strongest baseline by 37.5 percentage points.
  • 🆕 RedFlow: Redirect Failure into Action-Level Corrections for Flow-matching VLA Policy
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-01 · VLAFine-grained offline RL framework for flow-matching VLAs that converts failure rollouts into action-level corrective supervision rather than discarding them. Context-Aware Corrective Matching retrieves successful alternatives from similar states, and an Adaptive Redirection Objective jointly reinforces, suppresses, and redirects. Improves real-world success from 56.7% to 74.7%, matching on-policy methods (PPO, GRPO) with roughly 10x fewer samples.
  • 🆕 UniCross: Unified Cross-Skill Dexterous Manipulation Synthesis
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-01 · dexterous manipulationUnifies grasping, relocation, in-hand rotation, and in-hand translation under a single formulation sharing state/action spaces and objective structure. A single distilled cross-skill policy generalizes to unseen objects, stays robust to disturbances, and chains skills seamlessly into long-horizon manipulation — also transferring across different hand morphologies.
  • 🆕 World Action Planner: Generalizable Decision-Making with Action-Conditioned World Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-01 · WAMRobot planning system combining VLM reasoning with a multi-task pose-image conditioned world model. The agent proposes action plans and iteratively refines them via optimization and search over imagined rollouts, significantly outperforming end-to-end VLAs and WAMs on compositional tasks, new layouts, and zero-shot generalization.
  • 🆕 PhiZero: A World Model Built Around Physical Language
    HF papers · 2026-08-01 · WAMIntroduces "physical language" — a compact discrete representation of world-state transitions learned from in-the-wild interactions. Rather than predicting future videos in pixel space, PhiZero abstracts predictive structure into language-like tokens for explicit physical reasoning, offering a new paradigm for world modeling that separates dynamics understanding from visual reconstruction.
  • 🆕 ACE-Data-0: Human-Centric Ambient Capture as Embodied Data Engine
    HF papers · 2026-08-01 · dexterous manipulationAddresses the fundamental data bottleneck in embodied intelligence by introducing the Ambient Capture Engine (ACE), which transforms real home environments into instrumented capture studios. Captures first-person perception, whole-body motion, dexterous manipulation, object state, sound, and touch evolving together — providing the full perception-action loop that fragmented existing datasets miss.
  • 🆕 X-NavDP: Generalizing Navigation Diffusion Policy to Novel Behavior and Embodiments
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-01 · robot learningPost-trains a pretrained navigation diffusion policy with RL via Group Q-score Reweighted Matching (GQRM). Self-bootstrapped exploration preserves the pretrained prior while a per-trajectory normalization mechanism stabilizes score matching. Distributed RL across heterogeneous embodiments improves success from 61.2% to 84.3% in simulation and 10% to 65% in real-world hard cases.
  • 🆕 The Geometric Nature and a Free Proxy for Flow-Matching Uncertainty
    HF papers · 2026-08-01 · robot learningReveals that flow matching action heads used in modern embodied models do not expose their inherent uncertainty, producing faulty action chunks on OOD inputs. Proposes a geometry-based free proxy for FM uncertainty that requires no additional sampling or ensemble, enabling safe deployment decisions for FM-based robot policies without the overhead of existing uncertainty estimation methods.
  • TurboVLA: Real-Time Vision-Language-Action Model at 32 Hz on an RTX 4090 with <1 GB VRAM
    HF papers · 2026-07-31 · VLAReplaces the standard V→L→A pipeline with a direct V+L→A mapping via lightweight bidirectional vision-language interaction and a compact decoder. With only 0.2B parameters, achieves 97.7% average success on LIBERO at 31.2 ms inference latency and 0.9 GB VRAM on an RTX 4090 — matching or exceeding substantially larger VLA policies.
  • πR²: Reactive Real-time Flow Policies
    HF papers · 2026-07-30 · robot learningMakes action-chunking flow policies reactive and real-time by splitting conditioning into a fast proprioception channel (every tick) and an asynchronous slow vision-language channel. Applied to GR00T-N1.7 on a real xArm6+XHand platform, replans at ~25Hz on an A5000 GPU — roughly 4x faster than the base policy — with up to 30% real-world success rate improvement.
  • INTACT: Isomorphic Intent-to-Action Learning for Search-Free World Models
    HF papers · 2026-07-30 · WAMTurns a JEPA world model into a deployable intent-to-action interface without test-time search. An isomorphic architecture between local and goal motion-intent uses shared parameters and action-law semantics. On LeWM tasks, one-epoch zero-search models reach 85.8–100% success; optional local CEM with 384 (vs. 9,000) candidate sequences reaches 96.9% macro success.
  • HumanCLAW: Can Vision-Language Models Act Through a Body?
    HF papers · 2026-07-31 · embodied MLLMAn evaluation framework that decouples VLM action decisions from motor execution by harnessing full-body humanoid motion with factored-out balance and motor errors. Across 1,218 long-horizon find-navigate-interact episodes and nine state-of-the-art VLMs, the best model reaches only 16.8% success — revealing that current VLMs lack embodied self-awareness.
