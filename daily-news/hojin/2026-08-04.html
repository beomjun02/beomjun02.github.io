  • 🆕 CLIFT: Turning Gemini Robotics On-Device into Humanoid Specialists via Non-Invasive Closed-Loop Iterative Fine-Tuning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-04 · robot foundation modelOne of the first empirical studies of managed-API adaptation on a real humanoid, instantiated on Gemini Robotics On-Device (GROD). Direct SFT through the API substantially outperforms a leading open-weight VLA, yet still falls short of deployment-level mastery on agile, contact-rich tasks. CLIFT introduces closed-loop iterative fine-tuning that turns deployment-time reward feedback into API-compatible supervised data, pushing GROD to near-perfect success after two flywheel cycles — all without accessing model weights.
  • 🆕 ST-WAM: Semantic-Temporal World Action Model for Robust Manipulation under Visual Distribution Shifts
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-04 · WAMIdentifies Training-Distribution Hallucination in WAMs — futures conditioned on visually shifted observations hallucinate training-domain content. Uses DINOv3 as a shared semantic representation for future prediction and history retrieval while retaining fine-grained VAE dynamics. Achieves 98.7% on LIBERO, 92.8% on RoboTwin 2.0, and more than doubles real-world success under visual shifts from 25.8% to 61.5% compared to Fast-WAM.

    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-04 · VLAProposes a lightweight LeJEPA-based critic that jointly predicts future latent states and estimates values, addressing the state approximation problem in VLA RL post-training.   • 🆕 Mirror Learning
