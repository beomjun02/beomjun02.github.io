Papers

  • 🆕 WAM-Diff2: Hierarchical AR-to-Diffusion Distillation for Highly Efficient Autonomous Driving VLA
    Introduces a distillation framework that transfers holistic visual-linguistic reasoning from autoregressive VLA models into a parallel diffusion policy, cutting inference latency while preserving multi-task understanding. Directly tackles the deployment bottleneck of VLA models by enabling low-latency parallel execution without retraining a narrow single-task architecture from scratch.
  • 🆕 The Embodiment Gap in Robot Foundation Models
    A survey examining what can and cannot be reused across robot embodiments when deploying robot foundation models including VLA policies. Maps existing methods along two axes — the type of shared structure and the adaptation stage — and proposes a reporting framework for the hidden work required to move from a general-purpose model to execution on a specific robot body.
  • 🆕 Role-Conditioned Sub-Token Routing for Efficient Vision-Language-Action Policies
    Proposes RoleSub, a learned compression method for VLA token representations that reduces KV-cache to 9–11% of the original while maintaining strong control performance. A lightweight router conditions compression on token role (vision, language, action) and language context, offering a complementary path to aggressive token pruning for efficient VLA deployment.
  • 🆕 ADEPT: Accelerating Dexterity via Pre-Training and Post-Training using Reinforcement Learning
    A large-scale RL framework that pretrains a generic dexterous reposing policy and post-trains it for downstream tasks with a stable recipe (BC distillation, critic warm-up, conservative updates). Demonstrates zero-shot sim-to-real transfer on 23-DoF and 29-DoF multi-fingered robots with RGB + tactile sensing, solving long-horizon tasks at human-level speed.
  • 🆕 GigaBrain-WBC-0.5: A Behavior World Model for Robust Whole-Body Control
    Presents the first Behavior World Model for humanoid whole-body control — a causal Transformer that jointly predicts next action, next state, and the distribution over feasible behaviors conditioned on terrain contact geometry. Achieves 81.3% success on terrain interaction (4.3x the strongest baseline) and 99.3% fall recovery, with hardware transfer from Unitree G1 to Maker L01.
  • 🆕 LabDex: A Hierarchical Benchmark for Dexterous Manipulation in Laboratories
    A large-scale real-world dataset and benchmark for dexterous manipulation in chemistry labs, organized into atomic skills, compositional tasks, and long-horizon experimental procedures. First benchmark to unify real-world and simulation platforms under a common framework for laboratory dexterous manipulation, enabling cross-level evaluation of robot learning methods.
  • 🆕 Dream2Reward: Transition-Alignment Reward Models from Positive Demonstrations for Robotic Manipulation
    Learns a language-conditioned successful transition field from positive demonstrations and scores observed displacements through directional and magnitude agreement, penalizing wrong-direction, overshooting, and stagnant motion. Produces dense causal rewards without failure annotations or synthetic negatives, reducing reward hacking in both online and offline robot policy learning including real-robot manipulation.
  • 🆕 RoboEdit: Turning Human Manipulation Videos into Scalable Robot Experience
    A human-to-robot video editing suite that transforms human manipulation videos into action-consistent robot videos with aligned 3D hand states across seven robot embodiments. The automatic pipeline reconstructs and retargets 3D interactions from RGB alone, generating RoboEdit-14M (174K aligned video pairs, 14M frames) and supporting downstream real-world manipulation policies.
  • 🆕 Reinforced Planning with Latent World Models
    Introduces RP1, the first method to fully learn how to improve multi-step plans by training both a critic and an optimizer offline from imagined world-model rollouts. Achieves near-perfect success across visual navigation, arm reaching, and robotic manipulation using 1,000x fewer rollouts and up to 67x faster than the strongest search-based alternative.
  • 🆕 Zetta: An Efficient Closed-Loop Embodied Harness for Self-Evolving Physical Intelligence
    Proposes a closed-loop embodied agent harness that governs physical execution as it unfolds rather than reflecting only after episode completion. Addresses the gap between open-loop agentic approaches and the real-time decision requirements of robot-environment interaction, enabling self-evolving physical intelligence during rollout.
  • 🆕 Decision-Metric Alignment in Latent World Models
    Shows that strong decoding of task variables in JEPA-style world models does not guarantee that the latent cost correctly ranks action sequences for MPC planning. Introduces Plan-Real Spearman and CEM-stage Spearman diagnostics plus action-conditioned training objectives that improve decision-metric alignment for model-predictive control.
  • 🆕 MANIGUARD: Specification-Grounded Safety Evaluation for Robotic Manipulation
    A framework for evaluating and improving safety of foundation-model manipulation policies, with 200 base tasks across six contact-rich household task families and a safety-annotated trajectory-generation pipeline. Addresses the gap between advancing task success rates and rigorous evaluation of whether those successes happen safely.
  • 🆕 Vision-Language Models for Egocentric Video: From Hand-Object Interaction to Embodied AI
    A comprehensive survey of VLMs for egocentric video understanding, covering hand-object interaction, temporal reasoning, multimodal representation learning, and how first-person perception supports robot skill learning, human-to-robot transfer, and embodied decision making. Identifies temporally grounded reasoning and interaction-aware supervision as key priorities for deployable embodied intelligence.
