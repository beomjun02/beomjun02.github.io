Papers

  • 🆕 GigaBrain-0.7: Scaling Embodied Foundation Models to Emergent Capabilities with a Three-System Architecture
    A large-scale embodied foundation model with a three-system architecture that scales VLA models to substantially larger and more heterogeneous data regimes, targeting broader generalization across tasks and embodiments.
  • 🆕 GaussianWAM: Distilling Geometry and Semantics from 3D Gaussian Fields into World-Action Models
    Distills geometric depth and dense semantic features from 3D Gaussian fields into WAM video latents during training, with all teacher models and Gaussian components removed after training. Improves FastWAM from 52% to 71% on LIBERO-Plus.
  • 🆕 GlanceWAM: Sparse Test-Time Imagination for World-Action Models
    Decouples imagination from control within a single video DiT — an asynchronous proposer imagines a single lookahead frame in the background while an action head decodes action chunks at 48ms, achieving 72.2% on the 24-task RoboCasa benchmark.
  • 🆕 PonderPounce: A Pretrained MLLM as an Episode Context Engine for Robot Control
    Reuses a pretrained MLLM's native causal context as robot episodic memory — a System-2 'Ponder' module accumulates observations and reasoning, asynchronously feeding cognition tokens to a System-1 VLA 'Pounce' running at 20Hz. Reaches 60.8% on RoboMME with 9B.
  • 🆕 Learning to Act While Waiting: RL Finetuning of Generalist Robot Policies Under Inference Latency
    A latency-aware RL framework (ARLI) that enables effective finetuning of large VLA policies under real-world inference delays where standard RL fails entirely, using state augmentations that restore near-Markovian structure with committed actions and mid-inference observations.
  • 🆕 Latent Action as Intention Enables Efficient Future Imagination for World Action Models
    A WAM architecture using compact latent actions as future-intention representations, enabling efficient test-time imagination without generating future video frames — achieves 65.6% on RoboCasa few-shot and 80.8% full-data, with 42.9% lower latency than matched baselines.
  • 🆕 ConsensusTAS: Self-Supervised Temporal Action Segmentation for Long-Horizon Construction Videos
    Label-free temporal action segmentation that exploits internal consensus of candidate segmentations for human-robot collaboration in construction — runs on CPU, outperforming methods requiring large VLMs.
  • 🆕 MoTE: Mixture of Task Experts for Multi-Task Video Understanding
    A sparse MoE decoder for procedural video-language models that routes feed-forward computation by task identity rather than per-token learned routing, supporting action recognition, forecasting, and procedure prediction from shared visual evidence.
