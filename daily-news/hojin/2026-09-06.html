    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-05 · VLA · WAMPost-trains VLA policies by scheduling world-model imagination only at interaction-relevant states, performing bounded multi-view rollouts, and using progress/completion signals to refine actions.   • 🆕 MINERVA: How Small Can a Manipulation Policy Be and Still Solve LIBERO?
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-05 · VLAA deliberately compact 0.54M-parameter visuomotor policy achieves 95.1% average success on LIBERO's four standard suites — only 2.4 points below π0.5 with 7,700x fewer parameters. Performance saturates near 1M parameters and collapses below 0.25M, establishing LIBERO's first empirical capacity floor.
  • 🆕 FWBC-VLA: Force-Aware Whole-Body Compensation for Contact-Rich Loco-Manipulation

Validated on real-world whiteboard wiping and door opening with a door closer.

  • 🆕 Toward Physically Grounded JEPA World Models for Goal-Conditioned Robotic Planning
