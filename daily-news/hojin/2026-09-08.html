  • 🆕 VLA-Precision: Asymmetric Co-Bootstrapping for Efficient Real-World Online RL of Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-08 · VLA · sim-to-real RLEfficient real-world online RL framework for VLA post-training. Asymmetric Co-Bootstrapping (ACoB) uses early intervention-guided behavioral learning for rapid improvement, then progressively calibrates value estimates via global return propagation and local preference ranking.   • 🆕 TacPAC: Tactile Prediction and Real-Time Action Correction in World-Action Models for Contact-Rich Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-08 · WAM · dexterous manipulationTurns tactile prediction into real-time action correction for WAMs. Once the base model plans an action chunk, TacPAC caches the predicted contact and plan representation; a tactile expert reads each new tactile image against that cache to correct unexecuted actions at 20.7x less cost than regenerating the chunk. On five real-robot contact-rich tasks spanning precision insertion, fragile-object handling, and reorientation, raises average success from 22% (vision-only) to 64%.
  • 🆕 Reasoning Without Inference Cost: Latent Semantic Scaffolding for Robot VLA Policies
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-08 · VLA · robot learningAuxiliary training loss that aligns VLA action-token representations to text embeddings of physical-reasoning rationales through a small projection head, dropped at inference for zero added cost. Dense per-phase alignment (Dense LSS) — matching each action token to the rationale of its own manipulation phase — outperforms episode-level pooled alignment, yielding roughly 2x per-phase separability in the backbone and the best held-out task transfer among all variants.
