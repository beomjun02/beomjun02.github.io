  • Can VLA Models Learn from Real-World Data Continually without Forgetting?
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-05-28 · VLABuilds the first real-world continual-learning benchmark for VLA policies — four sequential manipulation tasks spanning rigid-object pick-and-place, contact-rich pressing, and deformable folding. Finds significant catastrophic forgetting on heterogeneous real-world demonstrations and dissects which experience-replay implementation factors actually recover performance, with practical guidance for deploying long-lived robot policies.
  • FineVLA: Fine-Grained Instruction Alignment for Steerable Vision-Language-Action Policies
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-05-28 · VLAArgues current VLA datasets only pair trajectories with coarse goal-level language, leaving execution details (active arm, approach direction, contact region) unspecified. Releases FineVLA-Data — 47,159 human-verified fine-grained trajectories unified across 10 open robot datasets — plus a 500-video held-out benchmark, and shows steerability gains without sacrificing goal-level success.
  • RoboMME: Benchmarking and Understanding Memory for Robotic Generalist Policies
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-05-28 · VLA16-task standardized benchmark for memory in long-horizon, history-dependent manipulation (counting, temporary occlusion). The team builds 14 memory-augmented variants on the π0.5 backbone and finds no single memory representation dominates — effective design is highly task-dependent across temporal, spatial, object, and procedural categories.
  • WBench: Multi-turn Benchmark for Interactive Video World Model Evaluation
    HF papers · 2026-05-25289 test cases / 1,058 interaction turns evaluating interactive world models on five axes: video quality, setting adherence, interaction adherence, consistency, and physics compliance. Covers navigation (text + 6-DoF), subject action, event editing, and perspective switching — first unified standard for the interactive video world-model space.
  • Scaling World-Model RL Through Diffusion Policy Optimization
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-05-28Identifies a structural misalignment between search and value learning as the underexplored scaling bottleneck for model-based RL. MBDPO unifies the two via a diffusion process over searched trajectories in latent world models, extracting an implicit energy function from collected data instead of constructing an explicit planner over the learned model.
  • SpatialBench: Is Your Spatial Foundation Model an All-Round Player?
    HF papers · 2026-05-26Cross-paradigm benchmark that stress-tests spatial foundation models across arbitrary viewpoints, shifting scene domains, varying input densities, and hardware constraints — the dimensions that matter for embodied / robotic deployment but get glossed over by domain-specific evals.
  • LocateAnything: Fast and High-Quality Vision-Language Grounding with Parallel Box Decoding
    HF papers · 2026-05-26Replaces serial coordinate-token decoding in VLMs with Parallel Box Decoding (PBD), treating bounding boxes and points as atomic units decoded in a single step. Preserves intra-box geometric coherence and unlocks parallelism, improving both decoding throughput and localization accuracy.
  • Quantized Keys Steal Attention: Bias Correction for KV-Cache Compression in Video Diffusion
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-05-28 · video diffusion modelPinpoints "Jensen bias" — softmax convexity makes quantization noise inflate cached-key attention weights — as the failure mode in low-bit KV-cache video diffusion. Derives an on-the-fly per-score correction recovering near-BF16 quality at INT2 on MAGI-1, SkyReels-V2, and HY-WorldPlay with negligible overhead.
