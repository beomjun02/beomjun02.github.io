  • 🆕 Continuous Reasoning for Vision-Language-Action

  • 🆕 PACE: Phase-Aware Chunk Execution for Robot Policies with Action Chunking
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-03 · VLAA training-free test-time method that adaptively selects execution horizons for VLA action chunks by identifying low-speed transition points in the predicted motion profile. Plug-and-play with no retraining or access to policy internals needed. Raises average success from 57.8% to 64.2% on 50 RoboTwin2.0 tasks and from 50.7% to 70.4% on real-robot Franka/ALOHA platforms.

  • 🆕 From Noise to Control: Parameterized Diffusion Policies

Shows significant improvements on multimodal benchmarks in both simulated and real-robot experiments.

  • 🆕 When Softmax Fails at the Top: Extreme Value Corrections for InfoNCE
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-03 · visual representation learningUses extreme value theory to show that InfoNCE's softmax assumption is misaligned with normalized embeddings in contrastive learning.
