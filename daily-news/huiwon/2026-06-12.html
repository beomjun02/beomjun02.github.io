  • 🆕 DAM-VLA: Decoupled Asynchronous Multimodal Vision Language Action model
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-12 · VLA architectureChallenges the shared synchronous clock VLAs inherit from VLM pretraining by maintaining per-modality latent buffers refreshed at sensor rates and read continuously by the action head via gated cross-attention. On seven contact-rich real-world tasks, more than doubles the strongest synchronous baseline's success rate (95.2% vs. 40.95%) while sustaining smooth 100 Hz control.
  • 🆕 APT: Action Expert Pretraining Improves Instruction Generalization of Vision-Language-Action Policies
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-12 · VLA generalizationIdentifies a structural imbalance in VLA data where language diversity is far lower than visual/action diversity, causing noisy gradients that corrupt the VLM backbone. APT pretrains the action expert as a vision-action prior on a frozen VLM, then injects language through gated fusion in a second stage. Achieves consistent gains on unseen instructions and compositional tasks across both π- and GR00T-style architectures.
