  • 🆕 DAM-VLA: Decoupled Asynchronous Multimodal Vision Language Action model
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-12 · VLA · VLA architectureArgues synchronous VLAs are misaligned with physical interaction where modalities update at vastly different rates.   • 🆕 APT: Action Expert Pretraining Improves Instruction Generalization of Vision-Language-Action Policies
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-12 · VLA · VLA generalizationIdentifies that continuous action experts in VLAs start from random init and learn from language-imbalanced data, producing noisy gradients that corrupt the VLM. Two-stage fix: pretrain the action expert as a language-agnostic vision-action prior on a frozen VLM, then inject language via gated fusion. Applies to mainstream VLA architectures including π-style and GR00T-style, with consistent gains on unseen instructions and compositional tasks.
