  • 🆕 Improving Robotic Generalist Policies via Flow Reversal Steering
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13 · VLA generalizationTakes suboptimal but reasonable actions, finds their latent noises by passing them through a flow-matching generalist in reverse, and maps them to nearby good action modes. Enables zero-shot control from coarse human/VLM guidance and up to 95% absolute success rate gains when distilled via behavioral cloning in under a minute. Also bootstraps RL on tasks standard RL alone fails.

  • 🆕 μVLA: On Recurrent Memory for Partially Observable Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13 · VLA · VLA architectureControlled isolation study of minimal recurrence in a pretrained VLA backbone (OpenVLA-OFT). Learnable memory tokens carried across timesteps improve success from 0.42 to 0.84 on partially observable tasks, while maintaining 96.2% on LIBERO under full observability. Identifies the regime boundary where minimal recurrence suffices vs. where additional memory structure is needed.
