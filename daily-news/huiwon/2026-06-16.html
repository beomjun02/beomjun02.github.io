  • 🆕 Diffusion Policy Optimization without Drifting Apart
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-16Identifies the "double-drift" phenomenon in RL post-training of diffusion policies: optimizing a variational surrogate lets the ELBO separate from true log-likelihood, making proxy policy gradients misalign with the true policy gradient.
