  • 🆕 Geometric Action Model for Robot Policy Learning
    HF papers · 2026-06-17 · robot foundation modelRepurposes a pretrained geometric foundation model (GFM) as a unified backbone for perception, temporal prediction, and action decoding. The GFM is split at an intermediate layer: shallow layers encode observations while a causal future predictor inserted at the split forecasts future latent tokens conditioned on language, proprioception, and action history. Across simulation and real-robot benchmarks, GAM is reported to be more accurate, robust, faster, and lighter than current foundation-model-scale baselines. From KAIST AI and ETH Zurich.
  • 🆕 LaWAM: Latent World Action Models for Efficient Dynamics-Aware Robot Policies
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-17 · world-action modelExposes predictive dynamics to robot policies through compact latent visual subgoals instead of reconstructed future video. Trains a latent-action-conditioned world model in the latent space of a pretrained vision foundation model, then conditions action generation on predicted latent subgoals. Achieves SOTA on LIBERO (98.6% SR), RoboTwin (91.22%), and real-world tasks at 187ms per action-chunk — up to 24× lower latency than pixel-space WAMs.
  • 🆕 QPILOTS: Efficient Test-Time Q-Steering for Flow Policies
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-17 · VLASteers frozen pretrained VLA and flow-matching policies at inference time without modifying the original model. At each denoising step, projects the noisy intermediate state to a clean action estimate and computes critic gradients there. Reaches 90% average success rate across 50 offline-to-online RL tasks, and outperforms or matches prior inference-time approaches when applied to a large frozen VLA foundation model across six sim manipulation tasks.
  • 🆕 SimWeaver: Zero-Shot RGB Sim-to-Real for Deformable Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-17 · VLA generalizationTrains zero-shot RGB VLA policies on just 200 simulated demonstrations per task, reaching 91% average real-world success across five diverse deformable tasks — including plastic bag manipulation — without teleoperation or per-task calibration. Combines a measurement-backed simulator, single-image asset generation, deterministic trajectory synthesis, and ISP-aware photometric augmentation for robust sim-to-real transfer at two orders of magnitude lower cost than real-data collection.
  • 🆕 Steering Autoregressive Vision-Language-Action Policies via Action Token Intervention
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-17 · VLA architecturePresents Token Steering (TS), an inference-time method for dynamically steering VLA-generated trajectories by injecting low-dimensional user inputs into the model's native action-token representation — no retraining or architecture changes required. User inputs guide rather than override the pretrained policy, preserving learned dexterity and task priors. Improves success from 10.0% to 72.5% on drawer closing and from 16.7% to 93.8% on state-aware object swapping.
  • 🆕 How Should World Models Be Evaluated? A Decision-Making-Centric Position
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-17 · world-action modelPosition paper arguing that world model evaluation suffers from recurring claim/evidence mismatch: papers often make stronger claims about utility than their evaluation supports. Proposes an L0–L7 evaluation ladder ranging from visual plausibility to policy optimization utility, and a decision-making-centric benchmark protocol foregrounding counterfactual action fidelity, closed-loop rollout validity, reward prediction, and model exploitability.
  • 🆕 DreamX-World 1.0: A General-Purpose Interactive World Model
    HF papers · 2026-06-17 · video diffusion modelA general-purpose interactive world model supporting camera navigation, scene revisits, and promptable events across photorealistic, game, and stylized domains. Introduces E-PRoPE for camera-aware attention, converts a bidirectional video generator into a few-step autoregressive world model via causal forcing and DMD-style distillation, and adds memory-conditioned scene persistence for long-horizon consistency. Reaches 16 FPS on eight RTX 5090 GPUs and outperforms HY-WorldPlay 1.5 and LingBot-World.
  • 🆕 Trust-Region Diffusion Policies for Massively Parallel On-Policy RL
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-17Introduces TruDi, enabling diffusion policies for on-policy RL with massively parallel simulations — a setting where rapid data distribution shifts make stable training with complex policies particularly challenging. Integrates a trust-region optimization rule enforcing KL-divergence constraints over the entire diffusion trajectory. Evaluated across 73 tasks on four benchmarks, TruDi consistently outperforms or matches strong baselines with clear gains on challenging humanoid control tasks.
