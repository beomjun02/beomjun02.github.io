  • EquiVLA: A General Framework for Rotationally Equivariant Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv · 2026-06-20 · VLAFirst general framework for end-to-end SO(2)-equivariant VLAs, applicable to any architecture pairing a frozen VLM backbone with a flow-matching DiT action head. Introduces EquiPerceptor for approximately equivariant visual representations and EquiActor for exactly equivariant action generation. Instantiated on GR00T N1.5 and tested on LIBERO, CALVIN, and real-robot Mobile ALOHA — achieves 92.6% on LIBERO (vs. 78.1% baseline) and improves real-robot success from 54% to 72%.
  • LaWAM: Latent World Action Models for Efficient Dynamics-Aware Robot Policies
    arXiv · 2026-06-17 · world-action modelExposes predictive dynamics to robot policies through compact latent visual subgoals instead of reconstructed future video. Trains a latent-action-conditioned world model in the latent space of a pretrained vision foundation model, then conditions action generation on predicted latent subgoals. Achieves SOTA on LIBERO (98.6% SR), RoboTwin (91.22%), and real-world tasks at 187ms per action-chunk — up to 24× lower latency than pixel-space WAMs.

  • Geometric Action Model for Robot Policy Learning
    HF weekly · 2026-06-17 · robot foundation modelRepurposes a pretrained geometric foundation model (GFM) as a unified backbone for perception, temporal prediction, and action decoding.
