  • 🆕 World Action Models: A Survey
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-24 · world-action modelComprehensive survey organizing the rapidly expanding World Action Models landscape through two complementary lenses: what each method generates (rendered futures, latent futures, video-free action reasoning) and how it couples prediction to action (backbone, action coupling, deployment regime). Clarifies boundaries between broad world models, video generators, VLAs, and WAMs, then maps design tradeoffs — representational richness vs. compute, memory, latency, and action-label cost — identifying a convergent pattern: WAMs are moving toward generating less of the future while preserving what control requires.
  • 🆕 MV-WAM: Manifold-Aware World Action Model with Value Augmentation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-24 · world-action modelEnd-to-end framework jointly modeling visual prediction, action generation, and value estimation to leverage video priors for action generalization. A cross-modality causal mask hierarchically grounds actions in predicted frames, while a manifold-aware optimization scheme explicitly accounts for structural heterogeneity across visual and action modalities. A progress-value regulation mechanism detects execution deviations and recovers via value-guided rollback. Achieves 55.7% mean success on RoboTwin random scenarios (+29.3% over strongest baseline) and 77.5% across four real-world dual-arm tasks.
  • 🆕 SafeDojo: Safe Reinforcement Learning for VLA via Interactive World Model
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-24 · VLA · World models for roboticsFirst model-based safe RL framework for VLA policies, performing online RL atop an interactive video world model. A ResNet success classifier estimates per-step task progress from imagined frames while a lightweight safety head predicts per-step costs from latent context, balanced through Lagrangian-constrained GRPO. Achieves best aggregate safe-success rate on SafeLIBERO with an 8.25pp improvement over the strongest baseline on Level I, and demonstrates real-world safe deployment on a Franka arm across five tasks.
