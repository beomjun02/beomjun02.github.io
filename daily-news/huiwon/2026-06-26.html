  • 🆕 Decoupling Semantics and Geometric Grounding: Spatial Visual Prompts for Language-Conditioned Imitation Learning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-26 · VLADecouples semantic reasoning from spatial control in VLA models by extracting zero-shot geometric masks from vision-language foundation models as Spatial Visual Prompts (SVP). Trained on as few as 50–100 demos, SVP-IL improves average success on ambiguous language-conditioned tasks from 24.0% to 39.5% and achieves 67.8% on standard benchmarks, validated on real robot.
  • 🆕 HALO: Memory Retrieval in Visuomotor Policies for Long-Horizon Robot Control
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-26 · long-term planningAttention-based memory retrieval mechanism for visuomotor policies enabling long-horizon control. Distills VLM priors via question-answering to guide retrieval toward task-relevant history, and uses sparse attention to mitigate accumulated memory errors — retrieving from up to 8 minutes of past experience across both simulated and real-world settings.

  • Qwen-AgentWorld: Language World Models for General Agents

  • G³VLA: Geometric inductive bias for Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv · 2026-06-25 · VLA architectureProposes a camera-aware geometric module that injects calibrated 3D structure (ray embeddings, projective positional encoding, cross-view fusion) into a pretrained VLA's visual-token stream without altering its action head. Instantiated on π₀, G³VLA yields consistent gains across LIBERO, RoboCasa24, RoboTwin2.0, and real-robot settings, with results also validated on π₀.₅ and GR00T 1.5.
  • InSight: Self-Guided Skill Acquisition via Steerable VLAs

  • EquiVLA: A General Framework for Rotationally Equivariant Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv · 2026-06-20 · VLA generalizationFirst general framework for end-to-end SO(2)-equivariant VLAs, applicable to any architecture pairing a frozen VLM backbone with a flow-matching DiT action head. Introduces EquiPerceptor for approximately equivariant visual representations and EquiActor for exactly equivariant action generation. Instantiated on GR00T N1.5 and tested on LIBERO, CALVIN, and real-robot Mobile ALOHA — achieves 92.6% on LIBERO (vs. 78.1% baseline) and improves real-robot success from 54% to 72%.
  • SWAP: Symmetric Equivariant World-Model for Agile Robot Parkour
