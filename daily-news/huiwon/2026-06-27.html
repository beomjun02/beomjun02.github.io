    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-27 · VLA · VLA architectureReplaces the single shared action expert in flow-matching VLA policies with a sparse Mixture-of-Experts action module.   • 🆕 Tactile-WAM: Touch-Aware World Action Model with Tactile Asymmetric Attention
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-27 · world-action modelIdentifies "tactile pollution" — a failure mode where naively injecting tactile tokens into a World Action Model degrades both video and action prediction. Proposes a Tactile Asymmetric Attention Mechanism that lets tactile signals inform action generation without corrupting the visual dynamics pathway. Enables WAMs to handle contact-rich tasks (insertion, assembly, search) where vision alone is insufficient.

  • 🆕 Fast LeWorldModel
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-27 · world modelAccelerates visual planning in JEPA-based world models by replacing autoregressive one-step latent rollouts with parallel action-prefix prediction. Given a current latent and candidate action sequence, Fast-LeWM predicts future latents for all prefixes simultaneously, eliminating accumulated latent errors from sequential rollout and substantially reducing planning compute.
  • Qwen-AgentWorld: Language World Models for General Agents

  • World Action Models: A Survey — Dream Less, Act More
    HF papers · 2026-06-24 · world-action model · world modelComprehensive survey clarifying the boundaries among world models, video generation models, VLA policies, and World Action Models (WAMs). Organizes existing work by what they generate (rendered futures, latent futures, video-free action reasoning) and by design anatomy (backbone, action coupling, deployment regime). Key takeaway: WAMs are moving toward "generating less of the future while preserving what control requires."
