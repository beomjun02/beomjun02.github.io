  • 🆕 Drop-Then-Recovery: How Redundant Are Vision-Language-Action Models?
    arXiv · 2026-06-30 · VLA architectureSystematic study of VLA architectural redundancy via transformer block removal. Key finding: language backbones are highly redundant — removing half of LLM blocks improves OpenVLA-OFT from 95.0% to 98.3% on LIBERO, and retaining only two language blocks still recovers baseline. Vision and action pathways are substantially less tolerant to removal.

  • PAMAE: Phase-Aware-MoE Action Experts Towards Reliable Flow-Matching Vision-Language-Action Policies

  • Tactile-WAM: Touch-Aware World Action Model with Tactile Asymmetric Attention
    arXiv · 2026-06-27 · world-action modelIdentifies "tactile pollution" — a failure mode where naively injecting tactile tokens into a World Action Model degrades both video and action prediction. Enables WAMs to handle contact-rich tasks (insertion, assembly, search) where vision alone is insufficient.
  • Improving Vision-Language-Action Model Fine-Tuning with Structured Stage and Keyframe Supervision
    arXiv · 2026-06-29 · VLAProposes StaKe, a plug-in auxiliary supervision framework that adds stage classification and keyframe prediction heads to VLA fine-tuning. Both signals are derived automatically from demonstration gripper states without manual annotation, enriching learned representations while leaving the base VLA unchanged at inference. Achieves 14% and 56% relative success-rate gains on bimanual sim and single-arm Franka real-robot tasks, with larger improvements on longer-horizon manipulation.
  • ForesightSafety-VLA: A Unified Diagnostic Safety Benchmark for Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv · 2026-06-29 · VLAFirst diagnostic benchmark making safety the primary evaluation target for VLA systems. Defines a 13-category safety taxonomy across physical interaction, instruction-side, and perception-side risks, with process-level metrics (cumulative cost, risk exposure time) beyond binary success. Across 66 safety-augmented scenarios in RoboTwin on 5 embodiments, even the strongest VLA incurs non-trivial safety cost — and structural/visual variation causes far worse safety degradation than language variation.
  • Inference-Time Robot Behavior Steering through Physically-Aware Reconfiguration of Task-Structure
    arXiv · 2026-06-29 · VLAIntroduces ReStruct, which decomposes a visuomotor policy into a high-level state-machine skeleton capturing task structure and a low-level continuous controller as a residual policy. At test time, user preferences are encoded as automaton constraints and composed with the skeleton via synchronous product — reconfiguring the task structure without retraining. Exceeds VLA baselines in both task success and preference-following by up to 25% across simulation and real-world experiments.
