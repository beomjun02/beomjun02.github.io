  • 🆕 TAP-VLA: Tactile Annotation Prompting for Vision Language Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-01 · VLAIntegrates tactile feedback into VLAs without architectural changes by overlaying shear-field vectors from visuo-tactile sensors onto existing RGB images — keeping cues in the VLA's native observation space with no tactile pre-training. Achieves 78% success across four contact-rich tasks vs. under 50% for vision-only fine-tuning and alternative tactile-fusion baselines.
  • 🆕 RoboGaze: Evaluating Robot World Models via Structured Vision-Language Analysis
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-01 · world modelTraining-free multi-agent VLM framework for structured evaluation of robot world model videos. Uses task-scene grounding, dimension-specific specialist routing, and critic verification under a 6-dimension, 30-type robotics taxonomy. Closes ~85% of the gap to human evaluation on 382 manipulation clips, lifting clean-clip accuracy from under 25% to over 80%.
  • 🆕 SurgVLA-Bench: Towards Evaluating Vision-Language-Action Models for Laparoscopic Surgical Robotics
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-07-01 · VLAFirst comprehensive VLA benchmark for surgical robotics, built on SurRoL simulation. Systematically evaluates autoregressive (OpenVLA) vs. flow-matching (π₀, π₀.₅, SmolVLA) paradigms across a hierarchical task taxonomy.   • 🆕 CORE: Common Outcome Regularities from Action-Free Visual Demonstrations for Robot Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-01Extracts stable terminal-state regularities from action-free visual demonstrations (e.g., human videos) as visual goal prototypes for robot policy conditioning. Bypasses the need for robot-specific demonstrations by leveraging shared endpoint configurations across successful trajectories. Boosts success by up to +17pp on real-world manipulation over text-conditioned variants.
  • 🆕 ReGuide: From Test-Time Guidance to Self-Improving Diffusion Policies
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-01Self-improving framework for behavior-cloned diffusion policies that generates phase-conditioned corrective rollouts in the drifted-but-recoverable regime, then incorporates successful recoveries back into training data.   • DIM-WAM: World-Action Modeling with Diverse Historical Event Memory
