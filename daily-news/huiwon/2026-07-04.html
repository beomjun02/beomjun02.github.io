  • Trimming the Long-Tail of Visual World Modeling Evaluation
    HF papers · 2026-07-02 · world modelIntroduces Tailor-Bench with three progressive scenario types — regular (common tools), unconventional (attribute-compatible substitutes), and impossible (attribute-violating tools) — to test whether visual world models truly internalize physics.
