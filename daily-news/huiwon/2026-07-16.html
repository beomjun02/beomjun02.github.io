  • 🆕 ChunkFlow: Towards Continuity-Consistent Chunked Policy Learning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-16 · VLA architectureAddresses boundary jitter in VLA chunked action heads with seam-aware training that partitions each chunk into frozen, editable, and future zones with overlap blending and first/second-order continuity losses.   • 🆕 Reducing Temporal Redundancy for Efficient Vision-Language-Action Inference
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-16 · VLAIdentifies two sources of VLA inference latency: repeated visual encoding of similar frames and multi-step diffusion sampling. Incrementally updates only dynamic-region tokens and compresses sampling to a 2-step schedule. Achieves 2x+ speedup at up to 98% success rate on manipulation benchmarks.
