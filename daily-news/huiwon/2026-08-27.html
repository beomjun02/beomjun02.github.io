  • 🆕 GaussianWAM: Distilling Geometry and Semantics from 3D Gaussian Fields into World-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-27 · world-action modelEnhances WAM representations at training time by organizing geometric and semantic supervision through a 3D Gaussian field. Frozen geometry and vision foundation models provide depth, camera poses, and dense semantic features, which are distilled into current-observation representations. All Gaussian components and auxiliary heads are removed after training, leaving the original WAM inference path unchanged. Improves FastWAM from 52% to 71% on LIBERO-Plus.
  • 🆕 LAWA: Latent Action as Intention Enables Efficient Future Imagination for World Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-27 · world-action modelIntroduces compact latent actions as an operational representation of future intentions in WAMs, enabling test-time future imagination without generating future video frames. A discrete tokenizer enhanced by action-free pre-training produces manipulation-centric codebook targets. Achieves 65.6% and 80.8% success on RoboCasa few-shot and full-data settings, improving over Fast-WAM by 9.6 and 4.5 pp while cutting inference latency by 43%.
  • 🆕 GlanceWAM: Sparse Test-Time Imagination for World-Action Models
    HF papers — weekly · 2026-08-27 · world-action modelShows that visual imagination achieves both real-time inference and superior success rates when generated asynchronously off the critical path and consumed directly in latent space. Decouples imagination from the control loop, generating sparse future latents that the policy consumes without waiting for full video rollouts. Addresses the fundamental WAM trade-off between synchronous generation latency and task performance.
  • 🆕 WorldSync: Do Robotic World Models Really Follow Actions?
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-27 · world-action modelIntroduces WorldEcho, a diagnostic that probes action following over a broad action distribution using visual integrity and SE(3) trajectory alignment, revealing that current world models struggle with diverse off-expert trajectories. WorldSync then strengthens action following along three axes: distributional coverage, representational grounding via an Action-Forcing Expert, and intervention-effect alignment. Enables more reliable iterative policy improvement in simulation.
  • 🆕 TrAct: Bridging Robot Control and Visual Prediction with Visual Tracks
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-27 · World models for roboticsProposes visual tracks as an embodiment-agnostic intermediate interface between control and prediction. A VLAT model jointly predicts candidate actions and visual tracks, a track-conditioned world model rolls out their visual consequences, and a VL reward model selects the best track. Improves success rates from 27% to 55% in simulation and from 49% to 76% on real-world Franka tasks over the pi_0.5 baseline.
  • 🆕 Hierarchical Skill Retrieval for Data-Efficient Adaptation of Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-27 · VLA generalizationAddresses VLA adaptation with limited demonstrations by decomposing target tasks into candidate skill sequences and evaluating plans based on both semantic plausibility and skill reliability from prior data. Combines subtask-level language retrieval with behavior-feature reranking, followed by a two-stage pretraining-then-finetuning pipeline. Improves average success rate by 10.3% on LIBERO and 21.3% on real-world tasks over the strongest baseline.
  • 🆕 ARLI: Learning to Act While Waiting — RL Finetuning of Generalist Robot Policies Under Inference Latency
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-27 · VLAIntroduces a latency-aware framework for RL-based improvement of VLA policies under inference delays. Builds on asynchronous inference that interleaves action generation with execution, and restores near-Markovian structure through state augmentations incorporating committed actions and mid-inference observations. Enables effective finetuning where standard RL fails entirely under latency, matching or exceeding no-latency RL performance.
  • 🆕 PonderPounce: A Pretrained MLLM as an Episode Context Engine for Robot Control
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-27 · VLA architectureReuses an MLLM native causal context as robot episode memory instead of purpose-built history mechanisms. Ponder (System 2) accumulates episode observations and demonstrations; Pounce (System 1 VLA) receives a continuous cognition token asynchronously. Jointly trained end-to-end at 20 Hz, achieves 60.8% on RoboMME (9B), up from 44.5% for prior modular approaches and 17.9% for observation-only pi_0.5.
  • 🆕 GVLA: Gripper-aware Vision Language Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-27 · VLA generalizationIntroduces MiGA, a 103K-demonstration multi-gripper dataset spanning five gripper types, and GVLA, which combines a multi-gripper tokenizer with adapter-based policy routing. Gripper encoding balances parameter sharing and strategy differentiation, enabling a single model to handle parallel-jaw, suction, and other gripper types. Improves zero-shot generalization and few-shot adaptation to unseen tasks across simulation and real robots.
  • 🆕 XP-JEPA: Cross-Predictive Physics Grounding for Forecastable Latent Dynamics
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-08-27 · World models for roboticsGrounds visual latent dynamics in privileged physical trajectories by separately encoding visual observations and physical states, advancing both through a shared action-conditioned predictor, and matching predictions cross-modally. The physical branch is discarded at deployment. Reduces rollout drift from 0.361 to 0.104 and increases mean control success from 53.6% to 78.2% on a multi-task evaluation suite.
