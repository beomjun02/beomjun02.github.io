  • 🆕 GaussianDream++: Efficient 3D Gaussian World Modeling for Robotic Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-28 · VLA · world-action modelInserts World State Tokens and World Prediction Tokens directly into the VLA backbone, with a training-only World Representation Head that decodes them into current + future 3D Gaussian fields with static-dynamic factorization.
