  • 🆕 DriftingVLA: Native One-Step Vision-Language-Action Generation via Per-Dimension Temporal Drifting
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-01 · VLA · VLA architectureA VLA that generates complete action chunks in a single forward pass instead of multi-step flow refinement.   • 🆕 SMILE: Smooth Motion for Improved Long-Horizon VLA Execution
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-01 · VLA · VLA architectureAn architecture-preserving interface that replaces raw action chunks with B-spline coefficients, decoding them into smooth trajectories. Applied to SmolVLA, Evo1, VPP, and DAWN, it improves accuracy and inference efficiency — SMILE-Evo1 hits 98.0% on LIBERO with 1.1x speedup, and real-world xArm tests show fewer drops and contacts.
  • 🆕 Hydra: A Navigation World Action Model with Discrete Latent Planning and Continuous Flow-Matching Execution
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-01 · world-action modelUnifies visual states, poses, and actions on a shared manifold compressed through vector-quantized bottlenecks. Discrete Latent Planning ranks candidates natively in the discrete space without decoding to pixels, paired with flow matching for smooth continuous execution. Outperforms state-of-the-art world models in goal-directed planning on two physical robot platforms.
  • 🆕 Does Latent Planning Survive Point Clouds? Action-Conditioned JEPA World Models for Geometric Observations
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-09-01 · World models for roboticsLifts three JEPA world model designs (frozen-encoder, distribution-prior, action-sensitive) from images to point clouds and shows latent planning works without collapse — the distribution-prior model matches its image counterpart. Object positions are almost perfectly decodable, and attention falls on the few moving points. Introduces a 3D goal interface at no cost in success rate.
  • 🆕 Flow-JEPA: Flow Matching for Robust Latent Dynamics in JEPA World Models
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-09-01 · World models for roboticsReplaces the deterministic autoregressive predictor in JEPA world models with conditional flow matching that jointly generates future latent state sequences. Raises mean success from 86% to 92% under clean observations and from 67% to 86% under noisy conditions, suggesting flow matching is a strong alternative for world model dynamics.
