  • 🆕 Spatially Aware World Action Model via Geometric Latent Diffusion
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-04 · world-action modelExtends world-action models with joint RGB + depth prediction via geometric latent diffusion. Nonlinear depth encoding maps unbounded depth signals into the frozen VAE’s bounded domain, preserving pretrained video-model knowledge while incorporating spatial geometry. Achieves top performance on RoboCasa and LIBERO-Plus, with strong real-world UR5 results in randomized settings.
  • 🆕 ZETA: A Controlled Study of Zero-Shot Cross-Embodiment VLA Transfer for Tabletop Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-04 · VLA · VLA generalizationBenchmarks strict zero-shot VLA transfer across 14 unseen embodiments in sim and real. Local end-effector representations, source embodiment diversity, and auxiliary co-training improve performance by ~15, 18, and 7 pp respectively; adding just 5% target data during pretraining yields 13.4 pp more, showing that strict and pretrain-exposed transfer merit separate evaluation.
  • 🆕 Latent Cluster Analysis for Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-04 · VLA · VLA architectureIntroduces LAVLA, a framework analyzing internal representations of GR00T N1.5’s action decoder. A cross-attention weighting method enhances relevant features in latent space, revealing that latent clusters progressively disentangle spatiotemporal and kinematic properties across layers, with representations stabilizing toward the output.
  • 🆕 SolarWM: Open Data and Scalable Training for Long-Horizon Video World Models
    HF papers · 2026-09-04 · World models for roboticsFully open framework for interactive video world models: a unified data pipeline standardizing 1.43M clips from 10 sources with metric camera geometry and quality metadata. Supports Wan2.2, LTX-2.5, and MiniMax-H3 backbones. Three-stage training enables real-time interaction across minutes to hours despite training only on 5-second clips. Data, code, and weights released.
  • H3-World: Turning Language Understanding into World Control
    HF papers · 2026-09-03 · World models for roboticsTurns MiniMax’s 33B H3 video generator into an interactive world model. Key finding: as large video generators scale, language emerges as a natural interface for zero-shot control of character behavior and camera motion. H3-World converts this coarse language interface into precise, temporally grounded world control.
