  • 🆕 VLA-Precision: Asymmetric Co-Bootstrapping for Efficient Real-World Online RL of Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-08 · VLAIntroduces asymmetric co-bootstrapping for real-world online RL of pretrained VLAs. A compact value network provides reliable reward signals while a lightweight proxy policy improves sample throughput, solving both the value-drift and large-model-overhead bottlenecks of applying RL to VLA post-training. Enables autonomous trial-and-error improvement on precision tasks beyond what demonstrations alone can achieve.
  • 🆕 TacPAC: Tactile Prediction and Real-Time Action Correction in World-Action Models for Contact-Rich Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-08 · world-action modelExtends world-action models with tactile prediction for contact-rich tasks. Naively adding tactile as another predicted view recovers only a third of the achievable gain due to a timing mismatch: predictions come before execution while tactile feedback arrives during it. TacPAC resolves this by turning tactile prediction into a real-time action correction signal, substantially improving contact-rich manipulation.
  • 🆕 Towards Neuro-Symbolic Procedural Reasoning for Long-Horizon Vision-Language-Action Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-08 · long-term planningCombines VLA control with explicit task graphs and multimodal procedural memory for long-horizon manipulation. Task graphs encode action dependencies, valid transitions, and branch conditions, while procedural memory maintains active steps and completed actions. Addresses persistent VLA brittleness in tasks requiring state tracking, dependency-aware reasoning, and conditional decisions across many steps.
  • 🆕 Spectral-Target Physical Latent Structuring for JEPA-Style World Models
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-09-08 · World models for roboticsIdentifies "physical representation laziness" as a new failure mode in JEPA-style latent world models where latent states avoid collapse yet fail to encode key physical properties, causing downstream planning failures. Proposes a Fourier auxiliary head that enforces physically-informed latent structuring at training time with zero inference cost, substantially improving planning success rates in dynamic environments.
