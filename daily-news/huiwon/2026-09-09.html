    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-09 · VLAProposes fine-grained timestamp-level failure detection for VLAs during long-horizon execution, using action-derived weak supervision signals to capture abnormal patterns like inconsistent chunks, frozen actions, and aggressive motions.   • 🆕 Reasoning Without Inference Cost: Latent Semantic Scaffolding for Robot VLA Policies
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-09 · VLA · VLA architectureIntroduces an auxiliary training loss that aligns VLA action-token representations to text embeddings of physical-reasoning rationales through a projection head dropped at inference — zero added cost. Dense per-phase alignment outperforms pooled per-episode alignment, yielding both the best in-distribution success and best transfer to unseen tasks.
  • 🆕 Continual Field-Adaptive Models (CFAMs) for Post-Deployment Physical AI
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-09 · robot foundation modelIntroduces a complementary learning architecture with a frozen slow component and a fast-learning Capsule Field for post-deployment adaptation. Evaluated across five embodiments (manipulator, quadruped, humanoid, quadrotor, off-road vehicle) against pi0, CogACT, and SpatialVLA. Reaches the operating point of a full-data policy using 40% of the data, with 13.9 pp improvement from autonomous near-OOD capture.
  • VLA-Precision: Asymmetric Co-Bootstrapping for Efficient Real-World Online RL of Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-08 · VLAIntroduces asymmetric co-bootstrapping for real-world online RL of pretrained VLAs. A compact value network provides reliable reward signals while a lightweight proxy policy improves sample throughput, solving both the value-drift and large-model-overhead bottlenecks of applying RL to VLA post-training. Enables autonomous trial-and-error improvement on precision tasks beyond what demonstrations alone can achieve.

  • 🆕 CoFreeVLA: Collision-Free Dual-Arm Manipulation via VLA Model and Risk Estimation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-09 · VLAAugments end-to-end VLA policies with a lightweight self-collision risk estimator for dual-arm platforms.   • TacPAC: Tactile Prediction and Real-Time Action Correction in World-Action Models for Contact-Rich Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-08 · world-action modelExtends world-action models with tactile prediction for contact-rich tasks. Naively adding tactile as another predicted view recovers only a third of the achievable gain due to a timing mismatch: predictions come before execution while tactile feedback arrives during it. TacPAC resolves this by turning tactile prediction into a real-time action correction signal, substantially improving contact-rich manipulation.
  • 🆕 ROBORMBENCH: Paraphrase Fragility in Vision Language Reward Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-09Reveals that VLM-based reward models for robotic learning are fragile to instruction paraphrasing — the same trajectory can flip between failure and success under semantically equivalent goal descriptions. Benchmark with 2,390 real-robot trajectories and 21,673 verified paraphrases shows coverage drops from calibrated 90% to 74% under pressure.
