  • 🆕 GRA: Geometry-Guided Representation Alignment for VLA Adaptation from Synthetic Robot Videos
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-25 · VLAProposes an Asymmetric Preservation Principle: generated human-to-robot videos preserve geometry (the "where") but lose control signals (the "how"). Routes surviving geometry as 2D end-effector waypoints to the VLA vision backbone via an auxiliary head, while training the action head on real demonstrations only. Outperforms pseudo-action baselines under matched data budgets on real-robot tasks.
  • 🆕 SPACE: Enabling Learning from Cross-Robot Data Toward Generalist Policies

  • 🆕 NavWM: A Unified Navigation World Model for Foresight-Driven Planning
