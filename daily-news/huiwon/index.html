  • 🆕 G³VLA: Geometric inductive bias for Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-25 · VLA · VLA architectureProposes a camera-aware geometric module that injects calibrated 3D structure (ray embeddings, projective positional encoding, cross-view fusion) into a pretrained VLA's visual-token stream without altering its action head.   • 🆕 InSight: Self-Guided Skill Acquisition via Steerable VLAs
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-25 · VLAEnables autonomous skill acquisition by making VLAs steerable at the primitive-action level (e.g., "move gripper to the bowl"). A VLM-guided data flywheel identifies missing primitives, autonomously attempts demonstrations, and integrates successful ones back into VLA training — no human demos needed for new target skills.
  • 🆕 World Value Models for Robotic Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-25 · world modelMarries world models with value estimation to build a generalist robotic value model (WVM) that assesses data quality via accurate task-progression scoring. Achieves SOTA value-order correlation on standard benchmarks and a new Suboptimal-Value-Bench (800 suboptimal trajectories), and improves manipulation performance in both sim and real when used to guide policy learning from mixed-quality data.
  • 🆕 World Action Models: A Survey — Dream Less, Act More
    HF papers · 2026-06-20 · world-action modelComprehensive survey clarifying the boundaries among world models, video generation models, VLA policies, and World Action Models (WAMs). Organizes existing work by what they generate (rendered futures, latent futures, video-free action reasoning) and by design anatomy (backbone, action coupling, deployment regime). Key takeaway: WAMs are moving toward "generating less of the future while preserving what control requires."
  • 🆕 Qwen-AgentWorld: Language World Models for General Agents
    HF papers · 2026-06-24 · world modelIntroduces Qwen-AgentWorld-35B-A3B and 397B-A17B, language world models trained on 10M+ interaction trajectories across 7 domains to simulate agentic environments via long chain-of-thought reasoning. A three-stage CPT→SFT→RL pipeline sharpens simulation fidelity. Used as a decoupled environment simulator, the model enables scalable agentic RL that surpasses real-environment training alone.
  • 🆕 Neuro-Symbolic Drive: Rule-Grounded Faithful Reasoning for Driving VLAs
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-25 · VLASupervises a driving VLA (Qwen3.5-4B) with structured rule-grounded reasoning traces extracted from classical rule-based planners, ensuring the rationale is causally coupled to the planned trajectory by construction. On a simulator benchmark, this reduces ADE@3s from 0.47 to 0.26 and miss rate from 8.30% to 6.40% under three-camera perception.
