  • 🆕 Robots Need More than VLA and World Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · VLA · robot foundation modelPosition paper arguing that VLA scaling alone is incomplete — the central bottleneck is the absence of mechanisms to convert unstructured behavioral data (human motion, internet video, simulation) into grounded robot supervision.   • 🆕 RhinoVLA Technical Report
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · VLA architectureDeployment-oriented VLA co-designed with the Huixi R1 edge SoC, using a token-efficient Qwen3-VL backbone and a continuous Action Expert to cut VLM-side token burden. Introduces a unified cross-robot interface (View Registry, 72D state-action slot space, robot-instance LoRA) and achieves 11.69 Hz end-to-end inference on edge hardware, matching π₀.₅ performance at similar scale.
  • 🆕 ActionMap: Robot Policy Learning via Voxel Action Heatmap
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · VLADrop-in voxel heatmap action head for existing VLAs that replaces native decoders (autoregressive bins, L1 regression, flow matching) with a structured probability map over discretized action space. Improves +8.2% over OpenVLA-OFT's L1 head on LIBERO and shows cross-backbone consistency — evidence that action representation is a genuine lever for VLA performance beyond backbone scaling.
  • 🆕 Dreaming when Necessary: Advancing World Action Models with Adaptive Multi-Modal Reasoning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · world-action modelProposes AdaWAM, a world action model with a lightweight dynamic router that selectively triggers textual reasoning during task transitions and visual reasoning during fine-grained manipulation. Improves inference efficiency while outperforming state-of-the-art embodied policies on simulated and real-world tasks.
  • 🆕 Coarse-to-Control: Action-Token Planning for Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · long-term planningIntroduces native action-token-space planning for VLAs: the policy first predicts a compact sequence of coarse action tokens summarizing the intended trajectory, then generates executable actions conditioned on the plan. Largest gains appear on long-horizon multi-stage tasks in LIBERO, SimplerEnv, and real-world Franka manipulation.
  • 🆕 WIZARD: Robotic Policy Adaptation via Weight-Space Meta-Learning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · VLA generalizationWeight-space meta-learning framework that generates task-specific LoRA parameters for a frozen VLA in a single forward pass from only a language instruction and a short demo video — no target-task action labels or test-time optimization. Improves up to ~2x on unseen LIBERO collections and ~14x on unseen tasks; real-robot gains on Franka Panda.
  • 🆕 AEGIS: A Backup Reflex for Physical AI
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · VLASelective escalation method that trains a lightweight probe on a weak VLA policy's frozen activations to detect high-risk steps, then switches to a stronger policy only when needed. On LIBERO-Spatial, recovers 10.1% of failed trajectories while activating the stronger policy on only 38% of steps — the lever is timing, not compute.
  • 🆕 AxisGuide: Grounding Robot Action Coordinate System in RGB Observations
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · VLA generalizationLightweight guidance method that renders robot base-frame axes as cue channels in camera views, bridging the gap between VLA semantic understanding and action-coordinate interpretation. Yields substantial generalization gains on LIBERO and real-world tasks, addressing degradation when objects appear at unseen locations.
  • 🆕 What Matters When Cotraining Robot Manipulation Policies on Everyday Human Videos?
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-09 · robot foundation modelInvestigates factors enabling transfer from everyday internet video to robot manipulation, using 532 human videos with 28 hours of triangulated hand labels.
