    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-01 · VLAShows VLA models for autonomous driving are highly vulnerable to realistic input perturbations, achieving up to 89% attack success rate on reasoning and 72% on trajectory manipulation in closed-loop simulation. Demonstrates that the assumed tight coupling between reasoning and trajectory generation in current VLAs is fragile.
  • 🆕 LLM-Guided Future Hypotheses for Horizon-Aware Exploration in Multi-Step Robot Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-01 · video diffusion modelProposes Future-Experience Conditioning (FEC), using LLM-generated short-horizon future videos via video diffusion models as structured priors for closed-loop robot policy learning. An LLM task reasoner specifies subgoal targets, a video diffusion model renders plausible futures, and a policy is conditioned on latent future representations to guide exploration.
  • 🆕 Mechanistic origins of catastrophic forgetting: why RL preserves circuits better than SFT?
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-01Extends the RL vs SFT forgetting debate to the mechanistic level via differential circuit vulnerability analysis on Qwen2.5-3B. SFT adapts faster but causes substantially greater disruption of internal computational circuits; RL preserves more of the base model's circuit structure at the cost of slower task adaptation. Implications for VLA post-training strategy.
