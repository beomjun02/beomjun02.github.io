  • 🆕 LA4VLA: Learning to Act without Seeing via Language-Action Pretraining
    HF papers · 2026-06-27 · VLAVLA models pretrained on visual-action demonstrations can rely on visual shortcuts rather than learning how language conditions action execution. LA4VLA proposes language-action pretraining that removes visual input entirely, forcing the model to learn the language-action mapping directly. This makes downstream VLA policies more robust to visual variations at test time.
  • 🆕 SSI-Policy: Learning Structured Scene Interfaces for Vision-Language Robotic Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-27 · VLA generalizationModular framework built around a Structured Scene Interface — a unified, RGB-only intermediate representation encoding monocular depth, language-grounded object layouts, and instruction-conditioned 2D motion trajectories. Robot-agnostic and trainable from action-free video, so the downstream policy learns from few demonstrations. Improves over the strongest prior method by nearly 15% on LIBERO with only 10 demos per task.
  • 🆕 ReStruct: Inference-Time Robot Behavior Steering through Physically-Aware Reconfiguration of Task-Structure
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-27 · VLAEnables inference-time behavior steering without retraining by building on a neural automaton policy that decomposes a visuomotor policy into a high-level state-machine skeleton and a low-level continuous controller. Reconfigures task structure via synchronous product with user-specified preferences. After steering, surpasses VLA models in both task success and preference-following by up to 25%.
  • 🆕 ForesightSafety-VLA: A Unified Diagnostic Safety Benchmark for Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-27 · VLADiagnostic benchmark making safety the primary evaluation target for VLA systems. Defines a 13-category taxonomy covering physical interaction, instruction-side, and perception-side safety, measuring process-level risk through cumulative safety cost and risk exposure time. Even the strongest policy incurs non-trivial unsafe nominal success, suggesting safety is tightly coupled to perception and grounding rather than reducible to post-hoc filtering.

  • 🆕 LiMoDE: Rethinking Lifelong Robot Manipulation from a Mixture-of-Dynamic-Experts Perspective
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-27 · robot foundation modelTwo-stage learning scheme for lifelong robot manipulation: a dynamic MoE structure activates heterogeneous experts based on motion information during multi-task pre-training, then a lifelong adaptation mechanism combines new learned experts with frozen pre-trained ones for novel tasks. Enables knowledge transfer with moderate parameter overhead and demonstrates strong lifelong adaptation on both simulated and real-world tasks.
  • PAMAE: Phase-Aware-MoE Action Experts Towards Reliable Flow-Matching VLA Policies
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-27 · VLA architectureReplaces the single shared action expert in flow-matching VLAs with a sparse mixture-of-experts, where a phase-aware router allocates action generation based on execution-phase cues. A two-stage training scheme warms up under standard flow-matching loss then optimizes phase-consistent routing. Improves task success by up to 9.2% over strong VLA baselines on multi-stage manipulation.
  • Tactile-WAM: Touch-Aware World Action Model with Tactile Asymmetric Attention
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-27 · world-action modelIntroduces tactile sensing into world action models, addressing "tactile pollution" — a failure mode where unconstrained tactile-token injection degrades video and action prediction. A Tactile Asymmetric Attention Mechanism blocks video-query access to tactile tokens while preserving action-query access via a touch-aware bias. Improves mean success rate by 38.9% overall and 86% on contact-rich tasks on ManiFeel.
