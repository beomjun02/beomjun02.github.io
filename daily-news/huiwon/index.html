  • 🆕 PhysisForcing: Physics Reinforced World Simulator for Robotic Manipulation

  • 🆕 Drop-Then-Recovery: How Redundant Are Vision-Language-Action Models?
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-30 · VLA architectureAnalysis protocol removing transformer blocks from pretrained VLAs and measuring downstream recoverability via a one-shot virtual-gate sensitivity metric (GateProbe). Reveals a strong asymmetry: language backbones are highly redundant for standard manipulation — removing half the LLM blocks actually improves OpenVLA-OFT from 95.0% to 98.3% on LIBERO — while vision and action pathways are substantially less tolerant to removal.
  • 🆕 Translation as a Bridging Action: Transferring Manipulation Skills from Humans to Robots
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-30 · VLAProposes relative wrist translation within the initial head-camera frame as a bridging action representation shared by humans and bimanual robots, sidestepping the noisy rotation estimates of full 6DoF transfer. Builds a π₀-like VLA model with interleaved action tokens and attention masking; on novel bi-manual tasks, the bridging action transfers human manipulation knowledge far more effectively than noisy 6DoF actions and scales with human data.
  • 🆕 SimFoundry: Modular and Automated Scene Generation for Policy Learning and Evaluation

  • LA4VLA: Learning to Act without Seeing via Language-Action Pretraining
    HF papers · 2026-06-29 · VLAVLA models pretrained on visual-action demonstrations can rely on visual shortcuts rather than learning how language conditions action execution. LA4VLA proposes language-action pretraining that removes visual input entirely, forcing the model to learn the language-action mapping directly. This makes downstream VLA policies more robust to visual variations at test time.
  • Hallucination in World Models is Predictable and Preventable
    HF papers · 2026-06-27 · World models for roboticsFinds that world model hallucination concentrates in low-coverage regions of the state-action space, where lightweight data-centric signals can both detect and mitigate it. Introduces MMBench2, a 427-hour, 210-task dataset for visual world modeling with ground-truth actions and reward labels, providing the first large-scale evaluation grounding hallucination analysis in actual control performance.
