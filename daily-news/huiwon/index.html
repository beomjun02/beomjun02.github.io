  • 🆕 Tri-Info: Generalizable, Interpretable Failure Prediction for VLA Models via Information Theory
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-21 · VLAFormalizes VLA control as a closed-loop information pipeline and derives three information-theoretic signals — action diversity, temporal consistency, and state-action coupling — that distinguish successful from failed rollouts. Across six VLA models and three benchmark environments, Tri-Info matches the strongest baselines in-domain and transfers across architectures, environments, and the sim-to-real gap without retraining, reaching 83% accuracy on real-world tasks where prior detectors collapse to chance.
  • 🆕 Start Right, Arrive Right: Asynchronous Execution via Initial Noise Selection
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-21Reframes asynchronous inference for flow-based robot policies as a noise selection problem rather than trajectory steering. PAINT finds an appropriate initial noise via backward Euler inversion so the unmodified flow ODE produces coherent next chunks — no gradients, retraining, or policy modification needed. Improves execution consistency and task performance across 12 simulated benchmarks and 6 real-world manipulation tasks spanning single-arm, bimanual, and humanoid embodiments.
  • 🆕 Frequency-Aware Flow Matching for Continuous and Consistent Robotic Action Generation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-21Transforms discrete action sequences into the frequency domain via DCT, performs flow matching over coefficients, and reconstructs continuous actions via cosine basis expansion. A Sobolev-type constraint on first-order temporal derivatives promotes smooth actions. Introduces no additional parameters and applies to both standalone flow-matching policies and VLA models. Improves success rates, motion smoothness, and robustness across simulated and real-robot benchmarks.
  • 🆕 Can In-Context Learning Support Intrinsic Curiosity?
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-21 · world modelInvestigates whether in-context learning capabilities of sequence models can serve as update-free world models for curiosity-driven exploration, eliminating the expensive inner-loop gradient updates needed by classic learning-progress rewards. Proves impossibility in general MDPs but establishes a positive result for active learning and Bayesian experimental design settings, where ICL-derived rewards successfully bound and converge to true learning progress.
  • DreamX-World 1.0: A General-Purpose Interactive World Model
    HF papers · 2026-06-17 · world modelA general-purpose interactive world model supporting camera navigation, scene revisits, and promptable events across photorealistic, game, and stylized domains. Introduces E-PRoPE for camera-aware attention, converts a bidirectional video generator into a few-step autoregressive world model via causal forcing and DMD-style distillation, and adds memory-conditioned scene persistence for long-horizon consistency. Reaches 16 FPS on eight RTX 5090 GPUs and outperforms HY-WorldPlay 1.5 and LingBot-World.
  • Looped World Models
    HF papers · 2026-06-20 · world modelIntroduces LoopWM, the first looped architecture for world modeling that iteratively refines latent environment states through a parameter-shared transformer block. Achieves up to 100× parameter efficiency over conventional approaches with adaptive computation that automatically scales depth to match the complexity of each prediction step. Establishes iterative latent depth as a new scaling axis for world simulation.
  • Geometric Action Model for Robot Policy Learning
    HF papers · 2026-06-17Repurposes a pretrained geometric foundation model (GFM) as a unified backbone for perception, temporal prediction, and action decoding. The GFM is split at an intermediate layer: shallow layers encode observations while a causal future predictor inserted at the split forecasts future latent tokens conditioned on language, proprioception, and action history. Across simulation and real-robot benchmarks, GAM is reported to be more accurate, robust, faster, and lighter than current foundation-model-scale baselines. From KAIST AI and ETH Zurich.
