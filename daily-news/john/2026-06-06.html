    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06 · VLA · world modelsProposes WLA models as a new class of embodied foundation models that jointly predict textual subtasks, subgoal images, and robot actions from instructions and images.   • 🆕 Flash-WAM: Modality-Aware Distillation for World Action Models
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-06 · world action modelsAddresses the inference bottleneck of world-action models (WAMs) that jointly generate video and robot actions through iterative diffusion. Flash-WAM uses modality-aware consistency distillation that respects the different noise schedules of video vs. action streams, compressing inference to a single step per modality. On RoboTwin 2.0, reduces per-chunk latency from 8.1s to 348ms (23x speedup) while preserving 85.5% task success and recovering 60% real-world performance on a Unitree G1 humanoid.

    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06 · VLAPresents a reward-free offline reinforcement fine-tuning framework for flow-matching VLAs.   • 🆕 PiL-World: A Chunk-Wise World Model for VLA Policy-in-the-Loop Evaluation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06 · world models · VLAAddresses the gap between open-loop world models and closed-loop VLA evaluation.
