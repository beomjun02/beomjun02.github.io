  • 🆕 WEAVER: An Effective World Model for Robotic Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13 · world modelsMulti-view world model trained via flow-matching loss that simultaneously achieves fidelity, long-horizon consistency, and speed for robotic manipulation. Applied on real hardware: 0.870 correlation with real-world success for policy evaluation, 38% real-world success improvement on top of the π0.5 robot foundation model, and 14% improvement for test-time planning at 5–10x speedup over prior world models. Code and models available.

  • 🆕 μVLA: Recurrent Memory for Partially Observable Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-13 · VLAControlled isolation study showing that minimal in-backbone recurrence — a small set of learnable memory tokens carried across timesteps — doubles VLA success rate under partial observability (0.42 → 0.84) with no auxiliary losses or architectural changes. On LIBERO, the recurrent variant reaches 96.2% average success, confirming no regression under full observability.
