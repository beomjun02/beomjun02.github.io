  • 🆕 Neural Voxel Dynamics: Learning Implicit 3D Physics via Volumetric Feature Advection
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-27 · JEPA models · world modelsSelf-supervised framework that unprojects V-JEPA features into a voxelized 3D grid grounded by monocular depth, then learns action-conditioned state advection as implicit physics. Tracks material states in high-dimensional JEPA features rather than explicit simulation, enabling emergent heterogeneous phenomena (rigid bodies in fluid) from monocular video alone. Benchmarked on CLEVERER, PhysInOne, and PhysGaia.
  • 🆕 Tactile-WAM: Touch-Aware World Action Model with Tactile Asymmetric Attention
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-27 · world action modelsIdentifies "tactile pollution" — a failure mode where unconstrained tactile injection degrades both video and action prediction in WAMs. Proposes asymmetric attention that blocks video-query access to tactile tokens while preserving action-query access, plus a touch-aware bias derived from predicted contact changes. Improves mean success rate by 38.9% overall and 86% on contact-rich tasks on ManiFeel.

Up to 9.2% task success improvement over strong VLA baselines on multi-stage manipulation.
  • 🆕 Play2Perfect: What Matters in Dexterous Play Pretraining for Precise Assembly?
