  • Flash-WAM: Modality-Aware Distillation for World Action Models
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-06 · world action modelsAddresses the inference bottleneck of world-action models (WAMs) that jointly generate video and robot actions through iterative diffusion. Flash-WAM uses modality-aware consistency distillation that respects the different noise schedules of video vs. action streams, compressing inference to a single step per modality. On RoboTwin 2.0, reduces per-chunk latency from 8.1s to 348ms (23x speedup) while preserving 85.5% task success and recovering 60% real-world performance on a Unitree G1 humanoid.
  • FlowPRO: Reward-Free Reinforced Fine-Tuning of Flow-Matching VLAs
