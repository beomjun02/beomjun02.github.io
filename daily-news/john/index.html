  • World-Language-Action Model for Unified World Modeling, Language Reasoning, and Action Synthesis
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06 · world modelsProposes WLA models as a new class of embodied foundation models that jointly predict textual subtasks, subgoal images, and robot actions from instructions and images. An autoregressive Transformer backbone with dedicated World and Action Experts achieves 92.94% on RoboTwin2.0 Clean and 56.5% on RMBench. The 2B-active-parameter WLA-0 runs at 40ms per inference on an RTX 5090 and can learn from cross-embodiment robot videos without action annotations.
  • Flash-WAM: Modality-Aware Distillation for World Action Models
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-06 · world action modelsAddresses the inference bottleneck of world-action models (WAMs) that jointly generate video and robot actions through iterative diffusion. Flash-WAM uses modality-aware consistency distillation that respects the different noise schedules of video vs. action streams, compressing inference to a single step per modality. On RoboTwin 2.0, reduces per-chunk latency from 8.1s to 348ms (23x speedup) while preserving 85.5% task success and recovering 60% real-world performance on a Unitree G1 humanoid.
  • FlowPRO: Reward-Free Reinforced Fine-Tuning of Flow-Matching VLAs
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06 · VLAPresents a reward-free offline reinforcement fine-tuning framework for flow-matching VLAs. RPRO pairs a contrastive optimizer with a proximal regularizer that eliminates reward-hacking, while a teleoperated intervention-and-rollback paradigm produces naturally paired positive/negative trajectories from a single operator action. Achieves highest success rate on four long-horizon bimanual tasks, outperforming four baselines.
  • PiL-World: A Chunk-Wise World Model for VLA Policy-in-the-Loop Evaluation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-06 · VLAAddresses the gap between open-loop world models and closed-loop VLA evaluation. PiL-World generates multi-view future observations conditioned on VLA rollouts, enabling policy-in-the-loop evaluation without real robot execution at every step. On three real dual-arm manipulation tasks, reduces the error between real and estimated VLA success rates from 63.2% to 12.0%.
