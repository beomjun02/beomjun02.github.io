  • 🆕 Tactile-WAM: Touch-Aware World Action Model with Tactile Asymmetric Attention
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-28Introduces touch-aware World Action Models that predict future tactile states alongside video and actions. Proposes Tactile Asymmetric Attention to prevent "tactile pollution" where unconstrained tactile-token injection degrades video prediction, while keeping contact information available for action generation.   • 🆕 CoStream: Composing Simple Behaviors for Generalizable Complex Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-28A framework that composes foundation models and diverse sensing modalities (semantic, predictive, reactive) into composable core behaviors for manipulation. All outputs compose on a shared SE(3) interface into a single pose command at each control step. Demonstrates on 8 real-world tasks spanning everyday manipulation and precision assembly with robust perturbation recovery.
  • 🆕 OmniContact: Chaining Meta-Skills via Contact Flow for Generalizable Humanoid Loco-Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-28A hierarchical framework using "contact flow" — key body trajectories plus binary contact signals — as a compact interface between high-level planning and low-level control for humanoid manipulation. Achieves 98.7% success on Carry Box and 76.5% on Push-Stack Boxes, outperforming baselines by 40.9% in meta-skill and 66.5% in skill chaining.
  • 🆕 Play2Perfect: What Matters in Dexterous Play Pretraining for Precise Assembly?
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-28An RL framework for task-agnostic pretraining through play on diverse objects, then fine-tuning on precise assembly. The play prior is 33x more sample-efficient than training from scratch. Zero-shot sim-to-real transfer achieves 60% success on tight insertions with only 0.5mm clearance, and over 50% on long-horizon multi-part assembly.
