  • 🆕 SoftNav: Injecting 3D Scene Tokens into VLMs for Embodied Navigation

  • 🆕 G²SR: Geometric Methods for Fast and Memory-Efficient Gaussian-based Surface Reconstruction

  • 🆕 LIFT: Never Too Late for Force — Accelerating VLA Post-Training with Reactive Force Injection
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-19Grafts a reactive action expert with 6D end-effector force memory onto a frozen VLA backbone via zero-initialized cross attention, adding contact reactivity without losing general manipulation knowledge. Online DAgger loop with mixed offline/corrective data enables robust towel folding, book insertion, and ring placement.
  • 🆕 DiMaS: Distribution Matching for Steering Vision-Language-Action Models

Shows that VLA behavioral features are linearly decodable but not linearly steerable — motivating this approach for fine-grained behavioral control across two SOTA VLAs.
