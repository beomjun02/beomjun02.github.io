  • 🆕 The Curse of Precision: A Data Scaling Law for High-Precision Robotic Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-29 · Robot Data AugmentationDiscovers that demonstrations required grow super-exponentially as target precision approaches a limit: log(N) ∝ 1/(P−c). Crucially, the precision limit c is an emergent property of the full agent system — improving sensors or expert quality measurably lowers c, expanding achievable precision. Provides a practical methodology for debugging high-precision manipulation systems.
