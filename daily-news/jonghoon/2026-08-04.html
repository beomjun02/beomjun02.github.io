  • 🆕 WCM: A World Critic Model for Vision-Language-Action Reinforcement Learning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-04Introduces a LeJEPA-based world critic that jointly predicts future latent states and values for VLA RL post-training. Compatible with Pi0, Pi0.5, and OpenVLA-OFT backbones, WCM achieves SOTA across 149 tasks on four benchmarks and validates on seven real-robot tasks with stable off-policy RL deployment.
  • 🆕 ST-WAM: Semantic-Temporal World Action Model for Robust Manipulation under Visual Distribution Shifts
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-04Identifies Training-Distribution Hallucination in WAMs and addresses it with DINOv3 semantic features for both future prediction and history retrieval. Achieves 98.7% on LIBERO, 92.8% on RoboTwin 2.0, and more than doubles real-world success under visual shifts from 25.8% to 61.5% vs Fast-WAM.
  • 🆕 On the Efficacy of Self-Supervised Point Cloud Encoders for Efficient 3D Large Language Models
