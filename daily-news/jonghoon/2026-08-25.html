  • 🆕 Beyond Imitation: Self-Improving Robot Policies via Off-Policy Q-Planning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-25Pairs a large frozen BC policy with a small off-policy Q-function that absorbs both successes and failures, enabling value-guided action selection and online self-improvement without retraining the policy weights. On real bimanual tasks, the method improves stack-cups from 40% to 90% and insert-wallet from 25% to 80% in five iterations with no human intervention.
  • 🆕 RISE: Adaptive Imagination for World Action Models

Includes CounterDrive, a counterfactual dataset with diverse outcomes and risk levels for safety-critical world-modeling research; achieves best overall planning performance on NAVSIM and nuScenes.
  • 🆕 AffordAny: Open-World 3D Affordance Grounding from Monocular RGB Images

  • 🆕 Graph-Operator World Models for Morphology-Parameter Generalization in Continuous Control
