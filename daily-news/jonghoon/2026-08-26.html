  • 🆕 Inferring Action from Future Latent State for Robotic Manipulation (DELE-w0.5)
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-26 · 3D / 4D world modelsArgues that video generation is unnecessary for world-action models: instead of predicting dense visual trajectories, DELE-w0.5 infers actions from compact future latent states that capture action-relevant physical outcomes. Across 480 real-robot trials on four long-horizon tasks, achieves 62.5% full-task success — outperforming the strongest baseline by 47.5 percentage points.
  • 🆕 DreamMimic: Learning Visuomotor Whole-Body Loco-Manipulation via World Model
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-26 · 3D / 4D world modelsDistills privileged teacher policies into vision-based humanoid controllers using world-model-assisted distillation. Repurposes RSSM-style latent dynamics as both a representation space and multi-step supervision signal, with auxiliary heads for contact, object state, and reward. Introduces Performance-Conditioned Guidance to balance teacher guidance and exploration. Improves tracking-based loco-manipulation on OMOMO and BEHAVE benchmarks.
  • 🆕 CounterAlign: Counterfactual Supervision for Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-26 · point based policy VLAShows that offline RL for VLA models doesn't need curated non-expert data: expert demonstrations alone can generate dense corrective supervision through instruction relabeling. Pairs expert actions with mismatched instructions to synthesize counterfactual tuples, training an instruction-grounded reward model via adversarial discrimination. Improves robustness on LIBERO-PRO and outperforms baselines on real-robot TX-G2 experiments.
  • 🆕 Beyond Instance Slots: Semantically Rich World Models for Physical Interaction Planning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-26 · 3D / 4D world modelsIntroduces SR-WM, a task-conditioned world model structured around five functional roles (gripper, target, goal, relation, fixture) rather than generic object slots. A role binder maps visual entity hypotheses to task-specific roles, while action-conditioned dynamics predict role transitions with fine-grained semantics including grasp/contact and predicate establishment. Evaluated across all four LIBERO suites plus cross-suite transfer.
  • 🆕 WorldToken: Time-First Sequence Modeling for Robotic Imitation Learning
    HF Papers · 2026-08-26Fuses multiview images, proprioception, and task conditioning within each policy timestep into a single "world token." A causal temporal Transformer models the world-token sequence, and a diffusion action head generates action chunks. An 85.3M-parameter policy trained on 23 RoboCasa tasks demonstrates that time-first organization improves both sample efficiency and task success.
  • 🆕 RISE: Adaptive Imagination for World Action Models
    HF Papers · 2026-08-26 · 3D / 4D world modelsProposes a system-level adaptive imagination framework for world-action models that makes sequential Roll/Stop decisions based on expected planning benefit. A Latent Evaluator estimates risk from the current rollout prefix, allocating computation only to scenes that benefit from longer imagination horizons rather than using a fixed budget for every scene.
  • 🆕 Towards Alias-Free 4D Gaussian Representations with Motion-Aware Filtering
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-08-26 · 4D vision for roboticsAddresses aliasing artifacts in 4D Gaussian Splatting by proposing a motion-aware 3D smoothing filter that adapts filter strength based on local motion. Estimates the joint density of time and focal-to-depth ratio via non-parametric methods, sampling appropriate smoothing at inference. Outperforms prior 4D methods on standard dynamic scene benchmarks.
  • 🆕 FixAnything: 3D-Consistent Rendering Refinement via Video Generative Priors
