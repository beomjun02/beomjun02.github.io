  • GaussVLA: Geometry-Aware Spatial Reasoning for Vision-Language-Action Model

  • GlanceWAM: Sparse Test-Time Imagination for World-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-27Decouples imagination from control within a single video DiT: an asynchronous proposer imagines a lookahead frame on a slow clock while an action head decodes at control rate (48 ms) in latent space. Achieves 72.2% on the 24-task RoboCasa benchmark (vs. 67.1% synchronous Cosmos Policy) and 99.0% on LIBERO — 24x faster than synchronous baselines.
