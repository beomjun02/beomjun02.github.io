  • 🆕 Beyond Relative Geometry: Metric-Aware Geometry Perception for Robotics
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-01 · 3DAddresses the problem that existing 3D reconstruction methods produce relative geometry with arbitrary scales, making predicted dimensions inconsistent across scenes and misaligned with real-world robotic actions.   • 🆕 AcrossVAM1.0: Particle World Modeling for Text-Assisted Robot Video Prediction
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-01 · point-based world modelsFactorizes robot video prediction into object-centric particle motion and dense appearance via a frozen SAM3-DLP codec that decomposes context frames into semantic particles for robot, arm, and gripper. A lightweight text-assisted model reasons over particle trajectories separately from pixel appearance, avoiding the entanglement typical of monolithic pixel models.
  • 🆕 Beyond Data Scaling: Representation-Centric Continued Pre-training for Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-01 · point based policy vlaProposes VLAct, a VLA-oriented VLM backbone trained via continued pre-training on web-scale image-text data rather than scaling costly robot trajectories. Shows representation quality is the central bottleneck — under a fixed robot-data budget, better visual-action representations outperform simply collecting more demonstrations.
  • 🆕 RoboEdit: Turning Human Manipulation Videos into Scalable Robot Experience
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-01 · Robot Data AugmentationTransforms abundant human hand-object manipulation videos into action-consistent, physically plausible robot videos with aligned 3D hand states. RoboEdit-ADC automatically reconstructs and retargets 3D interactions from RGB across embodiments, enabling large-scale synthetic robot training data generation without physical collection.
  • 🆕 Act with Intent: Distilling Behavior Intent for Vision-Language-Action Models
    HF papers · 2026-09-01 · point based policy vlaGoes beyond behavior cloning by distilling the local objective — the intent — behind demonstrated actions into VLA training. Rather than supervising only which motor command was executed, it captures the underlying purpose that the behavior serves under the instruction, providing richer learning signal than trajectory-level future supervision.
  • 🆕 PHR-VLA: Planning Horizon Reasoning for Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-01 · point based policy vlaEnables VLAs to reason over future task dynamics by introducing explicit planning-horizon mechanisms, addressing the limitation that most VLAs condition only on current observations.   • 🆕 Manifold4D: Denoising on Point Cloud Rendered Manifolds for Video Re-shooting
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-09-01 · 4D vision for roboticsRe-renders monocular dynamic-scene videos along novel camera trajectories by formulating the problem as denoising on point-cloud-rendered manifolds.   • 🆕 Revisiting Local Context for Long-Horizon Streaming 3D Reconstruction
