  • 🆕 Robot-Factored World Models via Robot Rendering
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-28 · 3D / 4D world modelsProposes factoring robot-specific elements out of action-conditioned video world models: action commands are first rolled through a kinematic forward model, and the robot’s visual appearance is rendered separately. The world model then only needs to predict scene-level contact and object dynamics, decoupling robot embodiment from environment prediction and improving policy transfer.
  • 🆕 ViTacWorld: Scaling Visuo-Tactile World Models for Contact-Rich Robot Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-28 · 3D / 4D world modelsAction-conditioned world model that jointly predicts visual and tactile futures for contact-rich manipulation. Scales training by combining public real tactile datasets with a constructed simulation environment, exploiting the fact that tactile signals are directly grounded in physical contact and exhibit a smaller sim-to-real gap than vision alone.
  • 🆕 SM4RT: Learning Structured Motion Geometry for 4D Reconstruction
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-07-28 · 3D / 4D world modelsExtends geometry foundation models from 3D to 4D dynamic understanding by modeling motion as structured rigid-body SE(3) transformations rather than unstructured point-wise displacements. Key insight: real-world objects move collectively under rigid-body kinematics, so motion itself has geometric structure that can be learned.
  • 🆕 Ordered Action Tokens for Visuomotor Policy Learning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-28 · point based policy vlaIdentifies three desiderata for action tokenization — high compression, total decodability, and an ordered token space — and introduces OAT, a learned tokenizer using transformers with register tokens that satisfies all three. Ordered tokens enable compatibility with autoregressive policies while maintaining a structured, interpretable action space.
  • 🆕 Learning Adaptive Semantic Gaussian Allocation for 3D Occupancy
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-07-28 · 3DAddresses the allocation bottleneck in 3D Gaussian-based occupancy prediction: redundant Gaussians waste capacity in simple regions while complex regions are under-served. Proposes a semantic-aware reallocation strategy that adaptively distributes Gaussian primitives based on region difficulty, improving efficiency and accuracy.

  • 🆕 Closing the Loop: Training-Free Revisit Consistency for Autoregressive Generative Rendering
