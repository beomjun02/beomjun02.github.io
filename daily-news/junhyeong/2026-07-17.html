  • 🆕 Generalizable VLA Finetuning via Representation Anchoring and Language-Action Alignment
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-17 · VLABC finetuning progressively overwrites VLM representations, and co-training on web data doesn't fix the language-action misalignment it introduces. Anchor-Align distills layer-wise features from a frozen VLM copy while jointly training language and action prediction on discrete motion-direction labels from the same robot observation. Real-robot results on xArm7 across two VLA architectures show improved generalization.
  • 🆕 An Empirical Study on Stage-Information Interfaces for VLA Fine-Tuning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-17 · VLAInvestigates how to feed stage-level action annotations into VLA policies during long-horizon manipulation. Compares current-stage text vs. ordinal stage index on NVIDIA GR00T N1.6 with LIBERO-10. Under continuation fine-tuning, ordinal stage-state encoding outperforms both full-task instruction and current-stage text in all paired runs, suggesting structured progress signals help more than language-based stage cues.
