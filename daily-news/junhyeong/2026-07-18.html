  • 🆕 Reflex: Real-Time VLA Control through Streaming Inference
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-18 · VLASolves a fundamental incompatibility between flow-matching VLAs and real-time control: global timestep injection invalidates KV-caching, forcing a choice between slow O(N²) recomputation or mathematically incorrect cache reuse. Reflex exploits a Timestep-Invariance Property to enable real-time streaming inference for flow-matching policies.

  • 🆕 RxBrain: Embodied Cognition Foundation Model with Joint Language-Visual Reasoning and Imagination
    HF papers · 2026-07-18 · robot foundation modelAn embodied cognition foundation model that represents embodied plans in a single planning sequence interleaving language and visual imagination tokens.
