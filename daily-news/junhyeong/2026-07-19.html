  • 🆕 BadWAM: When World-Action Models Dream Right but Act Wrong
    HF papers · 2026-07-19 · VLA · generative modelsChallenges the assumption that coupling action generation with future world prediction makes WAMs inherently robust.   • 🆕 AeroAct: Action-Centered World-Action Models for Language-Conditioned Quadrotor Flight
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-19 · generative modelsAdapts World Action Models to quadrotor flight by grounding semantic language goals and anticipating visual consequences of ego-motion. Unlike prior aerial VLA methods that use discrete actions or high-level waypoints, AeroAct outputs smooth, dynamically executable control references by modeling how flight actions change future observations under rapidly changing first-person views.
