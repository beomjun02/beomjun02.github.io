  • 🆕 AC-VLA: Robust Out-of-Distribution Action Execution via Compositional Learning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-21 · VLAIdentifies two failure modes in VLA OOD generalization — trajectory overfitting and perceptual shortcut — and proposes a plug-and-play compositional learning framework.   • 🆕 See like a Robot: Robot-Centric Pointmaps for Vision-Language-Action Models

  • RoboTTT: Context Scaling for Robot Policies

  • 🆕 IMBench: A Benchmark for Intuitive Robotic Manipulation

Strong OOD performance where end-to-end approaches break down.
