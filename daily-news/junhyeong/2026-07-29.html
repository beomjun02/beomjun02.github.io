  • 🆕 N₀-VTLA: Scaling Vision-Tactile-Language-Action Model with Latent Tactile Tokens
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-29 · VLAThe first VTLA model pretrained on tactile data at scale.   • 🆕 N₀-TWAM: Scaling Tactile-Native World-Action Model for Contact-Rich Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-29 · VLACompanion to N₀-VTLA — the first tactile world-action model trained at large scale. Jointly predicts future vision and future contact via NeoForce, a unified force-based tactile representation. Demonstrates benefits of data scaling for precise tactile and action prediction.

  • 🆕 FeelWorld: Visuo-Tactile World Model for Hierarchical Contact Prediction and Planning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-29A hierarchical visuo-tactile world model predicting future visual latents and three tactile states (contact, 3D tactile latent, slip). Achieves 81.7% zero-shot planning success on chip grasping, fruit grasping, and USB insertion, with 61% lower LPIPS than visual-only baselines after 80-step rollout.
  • 🆕 PRISM: Polynomial Representations for Interaction-Structured Motor Control
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-29 · humanoid robot learningMakes polynomial interactions among physical variables explicit and learnable in robot policies. In RL, applies a gradually activated element-wise polynomial function after the MLP backbone; in imitation learning, replaces linear proprioceptive conditioning in Diffusion Policy with a polynomial layer. Improves performance across humanoid locomotion and contact-rich manipulation, yielding sensorless compliant behavior without force/tactile input.
  • 🆕 Embodied GPT-5.1: Evidence of a World Model?
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-29 · robot foundation modelAn exploratory study testing whether GPT-5.1 can serve as a physical mobile robot's high-level controller with zero embodiment-specific training. Using only low-resolution first-person images and discrete actions, the model demonstrates emergent spatial reasoning — maintaining short-term memory of occluded objects, inferring physical consequences of its actions, and executing coherent collision-and-verify sequences. Challenges the view that embodied experience is necessary for world-model-like behavior.
  • 🆕 WCM: World-Cognition Model for Generalizable Human-Robot Interaction
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-29 · VLAA human-centered embodied agent built on the SLAK architecture (Sensing, Logic, Action, Knowledge) with asynchronous runtime enabling concurrent reasoning, dialogue, and execution. Introduces a human-in-the-loop teaching mode where users can interactively teach difficult tasks, with teaching episodes refined into chain-of-thought supervision. Achieves 73.8% average success across nine real-world HRI tasks including held-out tasks and taught long-horizon behaviors.
