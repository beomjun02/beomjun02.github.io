    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-23 · VLAParameter-efficient framework for continual skill learning in pretrained VLA models without demonstration replay.   • 🆕 DECOWAM: Decoupled Whole-Body World-Action Model for Legged Mobile Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-23 · robot foundation modelWhole-body world-action model that explicitly separates camera ego-motion from base and arm actions through dedicated conditional interfaces. Freezes a FastWAM backbone and trains residual adapters with adversarially separated base/arm latents and base-velocity conditioning. Introduces ARMDOG, a real-robot dataset with synchronized video, state, action, and language. Reduces action MSE by 21.7%.
  • 🆕 AdaPT: Adaptive Motion Planning and Tracking for Professional Tennis Styles on Humanoid Robots
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-23 · humanoid robot learningLearns professional tennis serving and rally styles directly from broadcast videos via a hierarchical planner-tracker design. An adaptation mechanism bridges the sim-to-real gap by learning to track randomized execution speeds and conditioning the planner on a learned motion-speed adapter. Deployed on the Unitree G1 and full-size Dobot Atom (1.7m) humanoid, demonstrating in-the-wild serving without motion capture.

  • EXIMO: VLM Guided Exploration of VLA Policies
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-08-22 · VLAThree-stage VLA finetuning pipeline: a VLM planner decomposes long-horizon tasks into shorter subproblems for the VLA (explore), the VLA is finetuned on the orchestrated data (imitate), then residual off-policy RL refines the policy (optimize).   • HiTac-WAM: A Hierarchical Tactile World Action Model for Contact-Rich Robot Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-08-22Forecasts a hierarchy of future tactile states — contact state, 3D deformation field, slip risk — for each candidate action chunk before execution. Ranks candidates by tactile forecast quality and triggers corrective replanning when observed touch diverges from prediction. Raises average real-robot success from 31.1% to 72.2% on chip grasping, blackboard erasing, and USB insertion.
