  • 🆕 ZimaBlue: Evolving Generalizable World Action Models through Scalable Video Pre-training
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-09-03 · VLA · robot foundation modelIntroduces a three-stage curriculum for learning World Action Models from large-scale egocentric video: causal video pre-training, video-action mid-training with a unified action representation, and target-robot specialization.     arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-03 · robot foundation modelUnifies multimodal representation learning and RL post-training around joint action-wrench prediction: a causal wrench history is aligned with vision-language semantics, and flow matching generates action chunks together with expected future wrench profiles.   • 🆕 Knowing When to Stop: Adaptive Action Chunking via Internal Cross-Attention Dynamics in VLAs
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-03 · VLAProposes a training-free truncation mechanism for VLA action chunking based on cross-attention entropy plateaus. As the prediction horizon extends, action-to-observation attention disperses, signaling limited grounding for further open-loop execution. Evaluations on pi0.5 and X-VLA across RoboTwin 2.0, LIBERO, and three real-world tasks show improved success over fixed-horizon baselines.
  • 🆕 ADAPT: Agile Diffusion Action Priors for Robust and Steerable Online Text-Driven Humanoid Control
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-03 · humanoid robot learningLearns a diffusion-based action prior from text-labeled humanoid state-action trajectories for end-to-end closed-loop language-grounded whole-body control. A lightweight residual RL policy on top of the frozen diffusion controller improves long-horizon robustness and smooth prompt switching. The same policy can serve as a steerable text-conditioned motion prior for downstream task adaptation.
  • 🆕 IMPACT: Attention Is the Interaction Map for Scalable Interaction-Aware World Model Training
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-03 · generative modelsIdentifies a supervision-allocation mismatch in world model training where static content dominates the MSE denoising objective, leaving dynamic interactions under-supervised. Consistently outperforms MSE baselines on robot-arm and human-hand manipulation across DiT backbones.

  • 🆕 Does Imitation Learning Preserve Temporal Robustness in Dexterous Manipulation?
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-03Compares expert and ACT-based learner performance across task execution speeds in a contact-rich parcel-stowing task. Both achieve 100% at nominal speed, but diverge under speedup: expert drops to 84% while ACT drops to 53%, with 35 of 47 failures being insertion misalignments. Equal nominal success does not imply preserved temporal robustness.
