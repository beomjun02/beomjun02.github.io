  • 🆕 WISE: World-model-guided Imagination Scheduling for Efficient Post-training of Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-04 · VLAWISE selectively schedules world-model imagination at interaction-relevant states during VLA post-training, performing bounded multi-view rollouts to generate contrastive supervision for policy refinement.   • 🆕 Sensing Which Modality Matters: Evidence-Gated Regularization for Robust VLA Policies
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-04 · VLAProposes Evidence-Gated Regularization (EGR), a zero-overhead training objective that derives per-frame, per-sensor task-relevance signals to gate consistency objectives in VLA policies. Real-robot experiments show +183% success rate under physical distractors on a bimanual setup and +27% on a tactile setup.
  • 🆕 FWBC-VLA: Force-Aware Whole-Body Compensation for Contact-Rich Loco-Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-09-04 · VLAIntroduces a sensorless residual-torque estimator (HSR-Force) that injects contact-state tokens into VLA action decoding, enabling a wheeled-legged robot to perceive contact onset, sustained loading, and release during loco-manipulation. Fine-tuned on a 5,000-episode dataset, demonstrated on real-world whiteboard wiping and door opening.
  • 🆕 RoboTok: An Internet-Scale Data Engine for Human Demonstration Retrieval and Dexterous Manipulation Learning
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-09-04Learns a latent motion space from 3D hand trajectories in actor-centered reference frames to retrieve manipulation-relevant human demonstrations from web video at internet scale. Retrieval is viewpoint- and appearance-invariant, and downstream dexterous robot policy training shows improved task success over existing data retrieval approaches.
  • 🆕 LEAP: Latent Energy Action Planning with World Models
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-09-04Optimizes action sequences through a frozen LeWorldModel by coupling terminal latent goal matching with terminal-window state energy via a quasi-Newton solver. Raises mean success from 77.5% (LeWM+CEM) to 94.8% across four control domains, a +17.3pp improvement while reusing the frozen representation.
  • 🆕 Building Pretraining Data for World Models: An Unreal Engine-Based Pipeline for Action-Conditioned Video Generation
