    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-05-28 · MoEReplaces the single entangled latent in prognostic VLMs with biologically supervised experts that explicitly separate hepatic-function vs. tumor-related factors in HCC.   • ARBITER: Reasoning Trajectory Basins and Majority Vote Failures in Test-Time Sampling
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-05-28 · test-timeShows that test-time-sampled reasoning chains aren't independent — they cluster into a few "reasoning basins," and plain majority vote picks the most stable basin rather than the correct one. ARBITER reweights basins using hidden-state evidence to recover the right-but-outvoted answers without retraining.
  • Self-Verified Distillation: Your Language Model Is Secretly Its Own Synthetic Data Pipeline

A clean ablation of how far self-improvement can go without external feedback.
