  • 🆕 MechELK: A Mechanistic Interpretability Framework for Eliciting Latent Knowledge in LLMs

  • 🆕 Mechanistic origins of catastrophic forgetting: why RL preserves circuits better than SFT
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-05-31 · fundamentalExtends the behavioral observation that RL retains prior capabilities better than SFT to the mechanistic level. Introduces differential circuit vulnerability: SFT adapts faster but causes substantially greater circuit disruption, while RL preserves a larger fraction of the base model's computational circuits.
  • Parallax: Parameterized Local Linear Attention for Language Modeling

  • The Chain Holds, the Answer Folds: Trace-Answer Dissociation in Reasoning Models Under Adversarial Pressure
