  • 🆕 dMoE: dLLMs with Learnable Block Experts
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-02 · MoE · architectureA block-level MoE framework for diffusion language models that aggregates token-level expert distributions into unified block-level routing. Reduces uniquely activated experts from 69.5 to 14.6 while retaining 99.1% of original performance, cutting memory by ~77–80% and achieving 1.1–1.7x latency speedup.
  • 🆕 VLM3: Vision Language Models Are Native 3D Learners
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-02 · VLMShows that standard VLMs can master diverse 3D tasks — depth estimation, pixel correspondence, camera pose, object-level 3D — with just focal length unification, text-based pixel reference, and data scaling. No architecture changes needed. Advances VLM depth estimation accuracy from 0.84 to 0.90, matching expert vision models.
  • 🆕 What Makes LVLMs Hallucinate Less? Architectural Factors Behind Hallucination Robustness
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-02 · VLM · architectureFirst systematic study linking architecture design to hallucination robustness across three dimensions: linguistic foundation, visual representation, and semantic alignment. Finds that scaling parameters alone has limited impact; stronger visual encoders reduce similarity errors, and effective alignment strategies alleviate uncertainty hallucinations.
  • 🆕 UniScale: Adaptive Unified Inference Scaling
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-02 · test-timeUnifies model routing and test-time scaling into a single optimization space. Models adaptive inference as a contextual multi-armed bandit (LinUCB), jointly deciding which model to call and how much test-time compute to allocate. Delivers a finer-grained quality-cost tradeoff than treating the two independently.
  • 🆕 DecomposeR: Planner-Centric RL for Deep Research with Structure-Aware Reward
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-02 · agentRepresents research plans as typed directed acyclic graphs and trains a planner with RL to learn graph structure and query decomposition, followed by branch-level answerer RL. DecomposeR-8B improves over strong open baselines by 5.1–8.0 points on long-form benchmarks.
