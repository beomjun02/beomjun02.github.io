  • 🆕 AsyMoE: Hyperbolic and Evidence-Prioritized Experts for Large Vision-Language Models
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-03 · MoE · VLMA novel MoE architecture for VLMs that addresses the inherent asymmetry between visual and textual modalities. Hyperbolic inter-modality experts capture hierarchical cross-modal relationships, while evidence-priority language experts suppress parametric memory to maintain contextual grounding. Achieves +1.5% over MoE baselines and up to 3.8% on hallucination-sensitive tasks while activating 25.45% fewer parameters.
  • 🆕 BitsMoE: Spectral Energy-Guided Bit Allocation for MoE LLM Quantization
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-03 · MoEA spectral-energy-guided framework for ultra-low-bit MoE compression that decomposes each layer via SVD into shared basis and expert-specific factors, then solves an integer linear program for mixed-precision allocation. Under 2-bit quantization on Qwen3-30B-A3B, accelerates quantization by 12.3x and improves accuracy by 27.83pp over GPTQ.
  • 🆕 The Deterministic Horizon: When Extended Reasoning Fails and Tool Delegation Becomes Necessary
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-03 · fundamental · architecture · agentProves an Attention Bottleneck Theorem establishing that decoder-only attention has a fundamental capacity ceiling for state-tracking tasks. Identifies a **Deterministic Horizon d* ∈ [19, 31] beyond which tool delegation outperforms neural chain-of-thought. Tool-integrated reasoning reaches 86–94% accuracy vs. 24–42% for CoT** across 12 models and 8 task domains.
  • 🆕 Agentic Transformers Provably Learn to Search via Reinforcement Learning
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-03 · agent · architecture · fundamentalFirst theoretical proof that transformer-based policies can acquire depth-first search capabilities from sparse RL feedback alone. A two-head attention mechanism — one for action tracking, one for failure-triggered backtracking — emerges through policy gradient training under a depth-wise curriculum. Exhibits depth generalization: training on depth-1 and depth-2 trees transfers to deeper structures.
  • 🆕 TrustLDM: Benchmarking Trustworthiness in Language Diffusion Models
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-03 · trustworthy · architectureFirst comprehensive trustworthiness benchmark for language diffusion models, evaluating safety, privacy, and fairness. Finds that while LDMs are generally trustworthy with user prompts alone, alignment degrades noticeably with malicious post-contexts. Proposes TrustLDM-Auto to systematically identify vulnerable configurations across all evaluated models.
  • 🆕 Demo2Reward: Test-Time Prompt Optimization for VLM Reward Models
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-03 · VLM · test-timeA test-time adaptation technique that optimizes VLM reward model prompts using just 3–10 expert demonstrations to reduce false positives in robotic policy learning. Requires no additional training — works entirely at inference time. Consistently outperforms existing zero- and few-shot VLM reward models and transfers to real-world robotic learning without manual reward engineering.
  • 🆕 CTRL-STEER: Closed-Loop Neural Activation Control in Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-03 · agentReplaces static steering coefficients in VLA models with adaptive, time-varying control signals via a closed-loop framework. Decouples representation from regulation by steering along motion-aligned residual directions while a PID or RL controller adjusts intervention magnitude online. Achieves better steering-task success trade-offs on LIBERO without modifying the base model.
  • 🆕 Capability Self-Assessment: Teaching LLMs to Know Their Limits
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-03 · trustworthy · agentShows that modern LLMs systematically overestimate their competence, then formulates self-assessment as a policy-learning problem. Learned self-assessment generalizes out of distribution and improves local-cloud inference routing.
