  • 🆕 Regret Pre-training: Bridging Prior and Posterior Views for Enhanced Knowledge Grounding
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-04 · MoE · architectureIntroduces a self-supervised framework that augments standard language modeling with a future-conditioned teacher distribution and a regret loss. On the OLMoE-1B-7B MoE architecture, GlobalRegret achieves 33.9% average accuracy (vs. 30.2% baseline) across nine downstream tasks, with a +18.1pp boost on BoolQ — all with zero additional parameters.
  • 🆕 Do Value Vectors in Deep Layers Need Context from the Residual Stream?
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-04 · architecture · fundamentalFinds that deep transformer layers perform better when they learn a context-free value vector (a per-token lookup table) instead of computing values from the residual stream. Bank of Values (BoV) replaces the last third of layers' value computation with a sparse lookup, improving validation loss and matching the best prior method at 780M scale with less compute and memory.
  • 🆕 Representational Capacity: Geometric Limits on Feature Representation in Transformer Language Models
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-04 · architecture · fundamentalDevelops a framework for estimating how many near-orthogonal feature directions a model's latent space can support. An adjusted capacity formula — depending on the ratio k/d rather than raw count — cuts prediction error by two orders of magnitude with no free parameters. Larger models favor tighter orthogonality constraints over maximizing raw capacity.
  • 🆕 TGV-KV: Text-Grounded KV Eviction for Vision-Language Models
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-04 · VLM · architectureProposes text-grounded KV cache eviction for VLMs: assigns per-layer budgets via text-vision mutual information, ranks visual token importance using weighted text-image attention, and prioritizes text KV retention. Preserves 99.2% full-KV accuracy on VizWiz-VQA with LLaVA-NeXT while boosting throughput by 52.6% at an extreme 5% retention budget.
  • 🆕 BYORn: Bootstrap Your Own Responses to Defend Large Vision-Language Models Against Backdoor Attacks
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-04 · VLM · trustworthyA backdoor-robust fine-tuning framework for VLMs that identifies semantically implausible poisoned target responses and dynamically replaces them with model-generated alternatives. Consistently improves robustness to backdoor attacks while preserving clean-task performance, establishing a new safety–accuracy Pareto frontier — and remains effective against adaptive attacks.
  • 🆕 Thinking Past the Answer: Evaluating Harmful Overthinking in Large Reasoning Models
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-04 · test-time · trustworthyIntroduces a prefix-level trajectory evaluation showing that many "reasoning-intensive" instances require surprisingly little reasoning. Stopping at the first correct prefix improves accuracy over standard reasoning by up to 21%, revealing that models are limited not just by reasoning ability but by their inability to stop at the right time. Common efficiency strategies reduce verbose but not harmful overthinking.
  • 🆕 Adaptive Latent Agentic Reasoning
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-04 · agentProposes ALAR, a dual-mode framework that uses compact latent reasoning for routine agent turns and escalates to explicit chain-of-thought only when deeper deliberation is needed. Maintains comparable or better task accuracy while reducing generated tokens by up to 43.6% in search and 84.6% in tool use — learning to reserve explicit CoT for harder decisions.
  • 🆕 Economy of Minds: Emerging Multi-Agent Intelligence with Economic Interactions
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-04 · agent · fundamentalInspired by Hayek's decentralized coordination theory, agents compete via auctions for the right to act, exchange payments, and accumulate wealth from environmental rewards — driving planning without global orchestration. Initialized with weak agents, the economy produces emergent multi-step reasoning strategies that outperform stronger monolithic baselines across five agentic tasks including math reasoning and scientific research.
  • 🆕 Inducing Reasoning Primitives from Agent Traces
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-04 · agentMines successful ReAct traces, clusters recurrent reasoning moves, and converts frequent moves into a typed pseudo-tool library with natural-language docstrings. The induced libraries outperform the very agent that generated their traces: +44pp on RuleArena NBA, +30pp on MuSR team allocation, and +22pp on NatPlan — at lower average inference cost than expert-authored decompositions.
