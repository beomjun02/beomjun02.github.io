  • 🆕 Adaptive Latent Agentic Reasoning
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-04 · agent · test-timeDual-mode framework that uses compact latent reasoning for routine agent turns and escalates to explicit chain-of-thought only when deeper deliberation is needed. Reduces generated tokens by up to 43.6% in search and 84.6% in tool use while maintaining or improving task accuracy.
  • 🆕 Representational Capacity: Geometric Limits on Feature Representation in Transformer Language Models
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-04 · architecture · fundamentalDevelops a framework estimating how many near-orthogonal feature directions a transformer can support. Derives an adjusted capacity formula where packing efficiency depends on the ratio of vectors to dimensions, cutting prediction error by two orders of magnitude over the Johnson-Lindenstrauss bound. Larger models favor tighter orthogonality over maximizing raw capacity.
  • 🆕 Do Value Vectors in Deep Layers Need Context from the Residual Stream?
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-04 · architecture · fundamentalFinds that deeper attention layers improve when learning a context-free value vector that preserves original token information without drawing on the residual stream. Proposes Bank of Values (BoV), a lookup table of token-specific value vectors for the last third of layers, which improves validation loss over standard attention at both 135M and 780M scale.
  • 🆕 Hallucination Is Linearly Decodable from Mid-Layer Hidden States in Quantized LLMs
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-04 · trustworthyA linear probe on a single mid-network layer achieves 0.904–1.000 AUROC for hallucination detection across Llama-3.1-8B, Mistral-7B, and Qwen2.5-7B in 4-bit quantization. The truthfulness signal is approximately linear — MLP probes rarely exceed linear probes by more than 0.01 AUROC. Peak layers fall in a consistent band across model families.
  • 🆕 Consistent Yet Wrong: Evidence Insensitivity in Spatial Vision-Language Models
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-04 · VLM · trustworthyIntroduces ViewDiag, a multi-view evaluation showing that leading VLMs produce view-invariant answers even when those answers are incorrect on metric distance queries. High prediction stability paired with substantial error indicates predictions reflect prior-driven collapse rather than evidence-sensitive geometric reasoning.
  • 🆕 MetaWorld: Scaling Multi-Agent Video World Models from Single-view Video Data
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-04 · videoScales multi-agent video world models to open-domain environments directly from single-view videos. Decomposes monocular footage into ego-motion and subject trajectory, introduces per-frame cross-attention for world-state alignment across egocentric views, and achieves superior cross-view consistency and identity fidelity without multi-camera setups.
  • 🆕 Regret Pre-training: Bridging Prior and Posterior Views for Enhanced Knowledge Grounding
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-04 · MoE · architectureSelf-supervised framework where a single model generates both a causal student and a future-conditioned teacher distribution, minimizing KL divergence to transfer future-aware signals. On the OLMoE-1B-7B architecture, GlobalRegret improves average accuracy from 30.2% to 33.9% across nine tasks, with BoolQ jumping +18.1pp. No additional parameters required.
  • 🆕 Thinking Past the Answer: Evaluating Harmful Overthinking in Large Reasoning Models
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-04 · test-timeIntroduces a prefix-level trajectory evaluation showing that stopping at the first correct prefix improves accuracy up to 21% over standard reasoning. Many instances considered reasoning-intensive require surprisingly little reasoning budget. Harmful overthinking — where continued reasoning destabilizes a correct trajectory — is driven by logical drift and visual reinterpretation and is not mitigated by common early-stopping strategies.
