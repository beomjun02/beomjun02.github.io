  • 🆕 Spectral Scaling Laws of Muon
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-05 · architecture · fundamentalFirst systematic study of momentum singular-value spectra during Muon optimizer training, from 77M to 2.8B parameters. Discovers remarkably clean power-law scaling of singular-value quantiles by model size, with layer-dependent exponents — late layers scale aggressively (up to M^−0.96), warning that the standard 5-step Newton-Schulz configuration will fail at frontier scale without layer-aware adjustments.
  • 🆕 Do Transformers Need Three Projections? Systematic Study of QKV Variants
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-05 · architectureSystematically evaluates projection sharing constraints in attention (shared QK, shared KV, single projection) across vision and language tasks up to 1.2B parameters. Q-K=V sharing achieves 50% KV cache reduction with only 3.1% perplexity degradation; combining with GQA yields 87.5% cache reduction, and with MQA achieves 96.9% — enabling practical on-device inference.
  • 🆕 3DThinkVLA: Endowing VLA Models with Latent 3D Priors via 3D-Thinking-Guided Co-training
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-05 · VLMA co-training framework that enables VLA models to perform implicit 3D spatial reasoning during action prediction, without 3D sensors at deployment. Disentangles geometry perception and spatial reasoning into separate feature hierarchies with an online 3D reasoning distillation module. Achieves SOTA on LIBERO, LIBERO-PLUS, SimplerEnv, and real-world manipulation tasks using only 2D images.
  • 🆕 Stateful Visual Encoders for Vision-Language Models
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-05 · VLMIntroduces visual encoders that condition each image&#x27;s representation on prior visual features instead of encoding independently. Consistent improvements on cross-image spatial aggregation, visual differencing, and trajectory behavior cloning across resolutions and model sizes. Validated on longitudinal radiology, fine-grained comparison, and remote sensing.
  • 🆕 Expert-Aware Refusal Steering
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-05 · MoE · trustworthyExtends refusal steering attacks to MoE LLMs and finds that steering performance is uninhibited by complex routing patterns. Proposes two expert-aware methods leveraging refusal-specific routing and expert-specific steering directions. Reveals that refusal signals differ from expert routing behavior, suggesting attention plays a substantial role in MoE refusal.
  • 🆕 Sparse Mixture-of-Experts Reward Models Learn Interpretable and Specialized Experts
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-05 · MoEProposes a sparse MoE reward model that learns interpretable routing patterns and specialized experts from binary preference data without additional annotation costs. Sparse routing and expert diversity during training capture coherent, disentangled preference components, improving test-time personalization and enabling qualitative analysis of expert weight shifts during adaptation.
  • 🆕 Not All Errors Are Equal: Consequence-Aware Reasoning Compute Allocation
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-05 · test-timeProposes consequence-aware test-time compute allocation — routing higher-consequence tasks to larger compute tiers based on predicted error severity rather than just difficulty. Under matched budgets, reduces cost-weighted loss by 22–33% vs. difficulty-aware routing on SWE-bench. The issue-only predictor never misclassifies high-consequence tasks as low across 300 tasks.
  • 🆕 Video2LoRA: Parametric Video Internalization for Vision-Language Models
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-05 · videoA hypernetwork reads VLM layer representations during video encoding and generates LoRA adapters in a single forward pass, enabling query-time inference with zero visual tokens in context. Non-inferior to direct video-in-context across 5 captioning benchmarks. Reduces visual-token load by up to 1,500x and query TTFT by 6–80x, stable up to 1,024 frames.
  • Regret Pre-training: Bridging Prior and Posterior Views for Enhanced Knowledge Grounding
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-04 · MoEIntroduces a self-supervised framework that augments standard language modeling with a future-conditioned teacher distribution and a regret loss. On the OLMoE-1B-7B MoE architecture, GlobalRegret achieves 33.9% average accuracy (vs. 30.2% baseline) across nine downstream tasks, with a +18.1pp boost on BoolQ — all with zero additional parameters.
