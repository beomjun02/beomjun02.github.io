  • 🆕 VideoKR: Towards Knowledge- and Reasoning-Intensive Video Understanding

  • 🆕 Pattern Selectivity is Not Task-Causal Structure: A Cross-Architecture Mechanistic Study of Composed-Task Circuits in 1B-Class Language Models
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-06 · MoE · architectureTests whether attention-head circuits generalize across architectures by studying Pythia 1B, OLMo 1B, and OLMoE 1B-7B (MoE) on four composed tasks. Finds no two models share the same primary causal screen for the same task. Proposes that the MoE model builds composed-task circuits atop a previous-token positional substrate.
  • 🆕 Trust, but Don't Verify: Epistemic Blind Spots in LLM Source Evaluation
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-06 · trustworthyReveals that LLMs can detect fabricated statistics in isolation (0.76-1.00 accuracy) but fail to recruit this capability during multi-source synthesis. A methodology-register gate responds to analytical text style but not numeric validity — even statistically impossible confidence intervals receive equal weight. Tested across five models from three families (Claude, Qwen, OLMo).
  • 🆕 CVT-RL: Policy-Conditioned Counterfactual Credit for Verifiable Reinforcement Learning of Long-Horizon Language Agents
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-06 · agent · trustworthyA constrained policy-gradient algorithm with dense verifiable rewards and a counterfactual contribution estimator for long-horizon agents. Deletion, substitution, and perturbation interventions create controlled advantages. Improves task success from 71.8% to 78.9% while reducing measured reward hacking from 7.2% to 3.9%.
  • 🆕 State Commitment Learning: Training Language Models to Distinguish Computation from Memory
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-06 · fundamental · architectureAddresses a fundamental limitation of reasoning LMs: they cannot distinguish persistent state from temporary scratch work. Proposes Counterfactual Erasure RL (CERL), rewarding only when answers remain correct after hidden thoughts are erased. Substantially reduces answer dependence on hidden computation across math, logic, QA, and tool-use without sacrificing accuracy.
