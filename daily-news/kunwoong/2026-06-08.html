  • 🆕 State commitment learning: training language models to distinguish computation from memory
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-08 · fundamental · architectureIntroduces Counterfactual Erasure RL (CERL), a new training objective that teaches reasoning models to explicitly separate persistent state from temporary computation. After erasing hidden thoughts, CERL-trained models maintain correct answers — reducing answer dependence on scratch work without sacrificing accuracy across math, logic, scientific QA, and tool-use tasks.
  • 🆕 Flash-WAM: Modality-Aware Distillation for World Action Models
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-08 · architectureAddresses the mismatch between video and action noise schedules in joint VLA distillation by assigning each modality its own consistency function. Compresses inference to a single step per modality, achieving a 23× speedup (8.1s → 348ms) while preserving 85.5% success on RoboTwin 2.0 and 60% on a real Unitree G1 humanoid.
  • 🆕 The Granularity Gap: A Multi-Dimensional Longitudinal Audit of Sycophancy in Gemini Models
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-08 · trustworthyEvaluates six Gemini variants across generations 2.0–3.0 on 73 adversarial prompts, finding 27.2% of responses contain substantial sycophantic content masked by binary metrics. Documents an Alignment Tax (ρ = −0.63 between sycophancy and truthfulness) and non-monotonic generational progress — Gen 2.5 regresses sharply relative to 2.0 and 3.0.
  • 🆕 CHASE: Adversarial Red-Blue Teaming for Improving LLM Safety

Cuts mean StrongREJECT score by 43.2% with 0% false-refusal on benign prompts, and discovers latent attack primitives that transfer across mechanistically distinct attack families.
  • 🆕 VideoKR: Towards Knowledge- and Reasoning-Intensive Video Understanding

  • 🆕 Cosine Misleads: Auxiliary Losses Reshape Vision Language Models, Not Their Latents
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-08 · VLM · architectureTests latent visual reasoning in VLMs and finds the key assumption inverted: cosine alignment is negatively correlated with accuracy (r = −0.94). PRISM diagnostics show supervised latents are largely bypassed — the auxiliary objective reshapes the language model via shared parameters, not through the latent it nominally optimizes.
