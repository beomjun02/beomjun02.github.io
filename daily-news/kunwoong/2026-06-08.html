  • 🆕 Flash-WAM: Modality-Aware Distillation for World Action Models
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-08 · architectureA modality-aware step distillation framework that compresses world-action models to single-step inference per modality. Reduces per-chunk latency from 8.1s to 348ms (23x speedup) on NVIDIA L40S, enabling real-time VLA control. Preserves 85.5% task success on RoboTwin 2.0 and 60% on a real Unitree G1 humanoid.
  • Let It Be Simple: One-Step Action Generation for Vision-Language-Action Models
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-07 · architectureChallenges the assumption that VLA models need iterative denoising for action generation. Since policies are conditioned on rich observations but predict only compact, low-dimensional action chunks, simple one-step generation suffices — no need for the advanced one-step methods developed for image synthesis.
  • Less is MoE: Trimming Experts in Domain-Specialist Language Models
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-07 · MoETraces the failure of prior MoE compression to the granularity of pruning: important capabilities are distributed across experts but concentrated in FFN sparse intermediate dimensions. Uses Fisher information to identify these dimensions, enabling compression that preserves performance on general-purpose benchmarks — not just commonsense reasoning.
  • Value-and-Structure Alignment for Routing-Consistent Quantization of MoE Models
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-07 · MoEAddresses the routing instability problem in MoE quantization: small perturbations change top-k expert selection, breaking the computation path. Proposes value-and-structure routing alignment that preserves routing consistency during quantization — a problem unique to MoE architectures that dense-model quantization methods miss.
  • 🆕 Cosine Misleads: Auxiliary Losses Reshape Vision Language Models, Not Their Latents
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-08 · VLMTests the assumption behind latent visual reasoning that better alignment between latent tokens and visual targets yields better answers. Finds the assumption inverted: cosine alignment is negatively correlated with answer quality across five LVR variants. Suggests auxiliary losses reshape the VLM&#x27;s computation rather than its latent representations.
  • 🆕 What Should Agents Say? Action-state Communication for Efficient Multi-Agent Systems
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-08 · agentProposes PACT (Protocolized Action-state Communication) that compresses free-form inter-agent messages into compact state-update records. Across different multi-agent topologies, achieves comparable or better task performance with substantially fewer tokens — lifts OpenHands resolve rate at 10% fewer tokens and halves SWE-agent input tokens.
  • 🆕 Do More Agents Help? Controlled and Protocol-Aligned Evaluation of LLM Agent Workflows
