  • 🆕 Skip a Layer or Loop It? Learning Program-of-Layers in LLMs

  • 🆕 The Piggyback Hypothesis of Generalization: Explaining and Mitigating Emergent Misalignment

Token-Regularized Finetuning (TReFT) reduces emergent misalignment by 33.5% more than data interleaving on Llama-3.1-8B, extending to tool use and refusal settings with 54.3% average reduction in off-topic generalization.
