  • 🆕 SHAPE: Coalition-Aware Expert Pruning for Sparse Mixture-of-Experts LLMs
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-11 · MoEProposes Shapley-value-based expert importance scoring that models intra-layer expert cooperation, rather than scoring experts independently. Retains the minimal expert subset covering an α fraction of non-negative Shapley mass per layer. Tested on Qwen3-30B-A3B, GPT-OSS-20B, and DeepSeek-V2-Lite — maintains competitive accuracy under 20–40% expert pruning with no additional training and clear peak GPU memory savings.
  • 🆕 Alignment Collapse Under KV Cache Quantization: Diagnosis and Mitigation
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-11 · trustworthyShows that low-bit KV cache quantization can silently destroy safety alignment — Mistral-7B loses 15.2% of refusals at only 1.03× perplexity. Identifies the root cause: safety features occupy a subspace 10²–10³× more vulnerable to quantization noise than the full representation space. Proposes Per-Channel Reduction (PCR), a training-free protocol (~35 GPU-min) that recovers up to 97% of lost alignment. Confirmed in production vLLM serving with FP8 KV cache on NVIDIA GPUs.

  • 🆕 Rotate2Think: Geometric Priming via Orthogonal Rotation to Improve LM Reasoning
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-11 · test-timeDiscovers that thinking embeddings occupy a geometrically distinct region from input embeddings. Estimates the rotation via orthogonal Procrustes from a small set of solved examples and injects a synthetic thinking vector at inference. Training-free, improves accuracy in 30 of 32 model-benchmark configs across math, science, and code — and generalizes zero-shot to multimodal reasoning on MATH-Vision.
  • 🆕 Blurry Window Attention
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-11 · architectureA novel bounded-memory attention mechanism inspired by SSMs that stores a frequency window and reconstructs a blurry KV history via Dirichlet kernel interpolation. Generalizes Sliding Window Attention (resolution-dependent) and is a special case of Gated Slot Attention (Dirichlet-kernel decay). On MQAR, achieves 8× better state efficiency than SWA while remaining competitive with popular linear attention models.
  • 🆕 SD-GRPO: Verifiable Segment Decomposition for Long-Form Vision-Language Generation

  • 🆕 TD-Grokking: Learning from Zero-Reward Problems by Training-Time Decomposition
