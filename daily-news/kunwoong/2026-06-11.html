  • 🆕 SHAPE: Coalition-Aware Expert Pruning for Sparse MoE LLMs
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-11 · MoEFormulates MoE routing traces as a cooperative game and assigns Shapley-style expert importance over observed top-k coalitions.   • 🆕 TRACE: Routing-Aware Expert Calibration for Machine Unlearning in MoE LLMs
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-11 · MoEIdentifies that forget-critical experts receive disproportionate activation while retain data under-activates them, leaving those experts under-regularized during unlearning. Reweights token-level retain losses to match forget-side activation frequencies, yielding 9% relative utility improvement over the strongest baseline on WMDP and MUSE-BOOKS.
  • 🆕 Alignment Collapse Under KV Cache Quantization: Diagnosis and Mitigation
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-11 · trustworthyLow-bit KV cache quantization can silently destroy safety alignment — Mistral-7B loses 15.2% of refusals at only 1.03× perplexity. Safety features occupy a subspace 10²–10³× more vulnerable to quantization noise. Training-free PCR protocol recovers up to 97% of lost alignment in ~35 GPU-minutes, confirmed in production vLLM serving with FP8.

  • 🆕 SD-GRPO: Verifiable Segment Decomposition for Long-Form Vision-Language Generation
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-11 · VLMDecomposes long-form VL outputs into verifiable segments and computes per-segment advantages instead of a single scalar.
