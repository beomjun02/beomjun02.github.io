  • 🆕 Latent Spatial Memory for Video World Models
    Microsoft Research · 2026-06-12 · videoIntroduces a persistent 3D cache operating in diffusion latent space instead of pixel space for video world models (Mirage).   • SHAPE: Coalition-Aware Expert Pruning for Sparse Mixture-of-Experts LLMs
    arXiv · 2026-06-11 · MoEProposes Shapley-value-based expert importance scoring that models intra-layer expert cooperation, rather than scoring experts independently. Retains the minimal expert subset covering an α fraction of non-negative Shapley mass per layer. Tested on Qwen3-30B-A3B, GPT-OSS-20B, and DeepSeek-V2-Lite — maintains competitive accuracy under 20–40% expert pruning with no additional training and clear peak GPU memory savings.
