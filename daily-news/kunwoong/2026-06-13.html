  • 🆕 Bag of Dims: Training-Free Mechanistic Interpretability via Dimension-Level Sign Patterns
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-13 · architectureShows that the standard basis of transformer hidden states already provides a training-free, architecture-general feature basis. Validated across Qwen 3.5-4B, Gemma 3-4B, and Mistral 7B.
