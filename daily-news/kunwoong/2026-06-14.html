  • 🆕 The Hidden Power of Scaling Factor in LoRA Optimization
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-14 · architecture · fundamentalReveals that LoRA's scaling factor α, not the learning rate, is the dominant driver of effective optimization. The optimal α follows a square-root law with rank — existing rank-tied heuristics severely underscale it. LoRA-α, a minimalist fix that restores α to its principled regime, consistently improves performance across diverse tasks while simplifying hyperparameter search.

  • 🆕 Normative Robustness as a Frontier for Non-Verifiable Reasoning in LLMs
