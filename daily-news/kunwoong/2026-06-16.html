Papers

  • 🆕 Zeta: Dual Whitening for Matrix Optimization via Coordinate-Adaptive Preconditioning
    Proposes dual whitening — coordinate whitening followed by spectral whitening — to fix scale heterogeneity that degrades Newton-Schulz iteration in matrix-aware optimizers like Muon. Proves this ordering is a mathematical dependency, not a tunable choice. Matches or surpasses baselines on 0.6B to 8B LMs, MoE architectures, and vision tasks.
  • 🆕 What Drives Test-Time Adaptation for CLIP? A Controlled Empirical Study
    Systematic controlled study of TTA methods for CLIP across 20+ methods and three unified paradigms. Reveals that adaptation gains are primarily driven by test-time evidence and reliable proxies rather than heavy optimization. Demonstrates that no single adaptation paradigm is universally optimal — the preferred approach depends on the nature of the distribution shift.
  • 🆕 Mirage Probes: How Vision Models Fake Visual Understanding
    Introduces a contrastive probing framework exposing two distinct failure modes in VLMs: textual biases (answering from language priors without visual engagement) and spurious images (constructing false visual content in latent space). Text-distribution cleaning addresses the first mode but cannot reach the second — faithful visual grounding requires representational-level interventions.
  • 🆕 WebDecept: Benchmarking Web Agent Safety under Deceptive Interfaces
    Benchmarks web agent safety under realistic deceptive interfaces in e-commerce. Injects 7 deceptive patterns including targeted ads, domain redirection, and shopping manipulation. Current web agents are highly susceptible to multiple deceptive interface classes, and prompt-based safety constraints are often insufficient to mitigate failures.
  • 🆕 Orchestra-o1: Omnimodal Agent Orchestration
    Unified omnimodal agent orchestration framework handling text, image, audio, and video via modality-aware task decomposition and parallel sub-task execution. Surpasses the next-best approach by 10.3% accuracy on OmniGAIA. Introduces DA-GRPO for training an open-source 8B variant achieving SOTA among open-source omnimodal agents.
  • 🆕 When the Tool Decides: LLM Agents Defer Blindly to GNN Tools
    Finds that LLM agents defer blindly to GNN tools — predictions agree with raw GNN output 97.6–99.2% of the time, collapsing into a "GNN parrot." Crucially, deference increases with model capability (0.60→0.98 from 1.5B to 7B), and the opportunity cost grows as alternatives improve. A cautionary result: evaluations of agent+tool systems cannot assume the agent adds judgment.
  • 🆕 Risk-Aware Causal Gating for Least-Privilege LLM Agents
    Introduces Risk-Aware Causal Gating (RACG) — a framework that gates each decision according to estimated counterfactual risk rather than raw predictive confidence. Derives distribution-free bounds on acting under high-risk conditions and proposes an adaptive gating policy for distribution shift. Reduces high-cost errors substantially while preserving most utility.
  • 🆕 Trust but Verify: Mitigating Medical Hallucinations via Multi-Agent Auditing
    Five-agent system that audits LLMs for recommending banned or withdrawn pharmaceuticals. Tests on 103 adversarial clinical MCQs where historically correct answers now reference banned substances. Reduces Hallucination Error Rate by ~53% across model families. Enforces regulatory compliance even when parametric knowledge favors the banned substance.
  • 🆕 NULLs: Natively Unlearnable Large Language Models
    New model class that makes source-level unlearning a native capability rather than an afterthought. Trains shared backbone neurons alongside sparsely activated sinks — removing a source at deployment requires no gradient updates and no retained data access. Scales to Wikipedia's ~6M articles and resists adversarial extraction and relearning.
  • 🆕 Temporal Backtracking Search for Test-Time Generative Video Reasoning
    Unlocks test-time scaling for video generation via temporal generate-verify-restart loops. Shows that searching over denoising steps cannot rescue flawed rollouts because spatial trajectories commit early. In strict OOD settings where one-shot generation collapses to 0.7%, TBS achieves 22.7% — every solved episode stems from a restarted branch.
