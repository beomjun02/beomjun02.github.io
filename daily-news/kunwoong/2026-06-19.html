Papers

  • 🆕 GraphPO: Graph-based Policy Optimization for Reasoning Models
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-19 · agentRepresents RL rollouts as a directed acyclic graph where semantically equivalent reasoning paths merge into equivalence classes, sharing suffixes and reallocating budget from redundant to diverse exploration. Assigns efficiency advantages to incoming edges and correctness advantages to outgoing edges. Theory shows reduced advantage-estimation variance; consistently outperforms chain- and tree-based baselines under the same token/response budgets.
  • 🆕 Sparsity Curse: Understanding RLVR Model Parameter Space from Model Merging
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-19 · MoEUncovers a sparsity curse in RLVR-trained models: the sparse parameter updates that prevent catastrophic forgetting also spread far apart in weight space, forming near-orthogonal shortcuts that make merging inherently fragile. Proposes SAR-Merging with Fisher Information-based sensitivity arbitration to resolve conflicts in overlapping update regions, enabling both single-task enhancement and multi-capability fusion.
  • 🆕 SAE Interventions are Unreliable: Post-Intervention Recovery of Suppressed Behavior
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-19 · trustworthyShows that clamping a "harmful" SAE feature may block one visible route to a behavior without eliminating the behavior itself. A constrained residual-space optimization achieves 95.8% recovery rate in refusal-steering while keeping defended-feature drift low. Recovery localizes to the SAE reconstruction residual — the component the SAE leaves unexplained — exposing a gap between feature-level control and behavioral completeness.
  • 🆕 STARE: Surprisal-Guided Token-Level Advantage Reweighting for Policy Entropy Stability
    HF papers · 2026-06-19 · fundamentalIdentifies a token-level credit assignment mismatch in GRPO: per-token entropy variation decomposes into the product of trajectory-level advantage and an entropy sensitivity function. Proposes surprisal-guided reweighting that stabilizes policy entropy during RLVR training, addressing the well-known entropy collapse failure mode.
  • 🆕 REVES: Revision and Verification-Augmented Training for Test-Time Scaling
    HF papers · 2026-06-19 · test-timeAddresses the misalignment between single-shot post-training objectives and multi-step test-time inference. Exploits high-quality mistakes in intermediate revision steps rather than optimizing over full multi-step trajectories directly, providing a more targeted training signal for sequential test-time revision.
  • 🆕 Dual Dimensionality for Local and Global Attention
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-19 · architectureChallenges the assumption that key/value dimensionality should be uniform across token positions. Local tokens get full-dimensional representations; distant tokens get 1/4 dimensionality. Across 70M–1B parameter scales, this Distance-Adaptive Representation closely matches full-dimensional baselines while enabling further KV cache reduction during inference.
  • 🆕 VISUALSKILL: Multimodal Skills for Computer-Use Agents
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-19 · agentProposes hierarchical multimodal skills (text + figures) for computer-use agents, delivered via an MCP tool. A Claude Code CLI agent backed by Claude Opus 4.6 reaches 0.456 average score — a +15.3 point lift over the no-skill baseline. Retaining visual figures in skills (vs. text-only) provides a further +8.3 point gain, showing that visual modality matters for GUI action selection.
  • 🆕 Skill-Guided Continuation Distillation for GUI Agents
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-19 · agentAddresses the off-trajectory supervision gap in GUI agent training: when the policy deviates from expert trajectories, no expert demonstration exists for recovery. SGCD runs the plain policy to reach realistic off-trajectory states, then a skill-guided policy completes the task. Improves three base models from the low-30% to over 50% success rate on OSWorld-Verified.
  • 🆕 CF-GRPO: Consensus-Frame Alignment for Visual Focus in Video-MLLMs
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-19 · VLMIntroduces a temporal-annotation-free process-level reward for video reasoning. Constructs a consensus frame prior from intrinsic video cues and optimizes agreement between model frame-use and this prior. Achieves competitive performance on complex video reasoning benchmarks with an interpretable view of which evidence frames the model emphasizes.
