  • 🆕 HydraHead: From Head-Level Functional Heterogeneity to Specialized Attention Hybridization
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-20 · architectureProposes head-axis hybridization of full and linear attention, driven by an interpretability analysis showing individual heads within the same layer display distinct functional specialization. At a 7:1 linear-to-full ratio, matches the long-context performance of 3:1 layer-wise hybrids. Trained on only 15B tokens, achieves 69% improvement over baseline at 512K context length.
  • 🆕 GRACE: Granularity-Regulated Adaptive Computational Efficiency for Optimal Verification in Test-Time Scaling
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-20 · test-timeEstablishes a unified framework characterizing the optimal verification granularity as a function of problem difficulty, verifier accuracy, and compute budget. Proves a phase transition: fine-grained verification dominates when compute is large or the problem is hard; coarse-grained wins in low-budget easy regimes. Adaptive strategy outperforms fixed-granularity baselines by up to 3.1% accuracy at matched compute on MATH-500, GSM8K, AIME.
  • 🆕 ImageWAM: Do World Action Models Really Need Video Generation, or Just Image Editing?
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-20 · architectureArgues that image editing provides a better prior than video generation for World Action Models. Repurposes pretrained image editing models for robot action prediction, conditioning a flow-matching action expert on KV caches from editing denoising. Outperforms standard VLA baselines with 1/6 FLOPs and 1/4 latency of video-based WAMs across simulator and real-world experiments.
  • 🆕 Mix-QVLA: Task-Evidence-Aware Mixed-Precision Quantization of VLA Models
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-20 · VLMMixed-precision post-training quantization framework for VLA models that evaluates whether quantization preserves task-relevant evidence across functional boundaries. Reduces OpenVLA-OFT memory from 15.4 GB to 4.1 GB while retaining 96.3 average success (vs 97.1 BF16) and achieving 1.52x inference speedup on LIBERO.
  • 🆕 LIVE: Language-Instructed Vision Embeddings for Controllable Perception
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-20 · VLMInstead of feeding visual features into language models, uses language itself to dynamically guide the vision encoder at inference time. Produces task-centric embeddings without task-specific retraining. Reduces visual hallucinations by +34 points on MMVP and surpasses VLMs with orders of magnitude more parameters on VQA.
  • 🆕 A Systematic Evaluation of Black-Box Uncertainty Estimation Methods for LLMs
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-20 · trustworthyLargest systematic evaluation of black-box uncertainty estimation to date: 24 methods across 5 categories, benchmarked on 4 models and 4 dataset settings. Finds that no single method consistently dominates, but methods reasoning over candidates in the answer space are generally effective, and hybrid methods combining multiple signals perform best.
