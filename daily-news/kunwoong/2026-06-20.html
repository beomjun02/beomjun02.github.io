Papers

  • 🆕 ITNet: A Learnable Integral Transform That Subsumes Convolution, Attention, and Recurrence
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-20 · architectureShows that convolution, self-attention, and autoregressive recurrence (LSTM, GRU, S4, Mamba) are all special cases of a single learnable integral transform. A shared ITNet operator with lightweight modality-specific encoders matches or exceeds specialized baselines on ImageNet-1K, GLUE, ModelNet40, and VQA v2 — demonstrating one interaction mechanism can recover all three architectural families from data.
  • 🆕 GRACE: Granularity-Regulated Adaptive Computational Efficiency for Optimal Verification in Test-Time Scaling
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-20 · test-timeEstablishes a unified framework characterizing optimal verification granularity as a function of problem difficulty, verifier accuracy, and compute budget. Proves a phase transition: fine-grained verification (PRMs) dominates when budget is large or problems are hard, while coarse-grained (ORMs) wins in low-budget easy regimes. Adaptive strategy outperforms fixed-granularity baselines by up to 3.1% accuracy at matched compute on MATH-500 and AIME.
  • 🆕 Trustworthy Multi-Agent Systems: Mitigating Semantic Drift with the Argent Signaling Protocol
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-20 · trustworthy · agentIntroduces ASP, a compact machine-readable header accompanying every AI response with structured quality signals (certainty, grounding, stochasticity, assumption index). Enables distinguishing repairable from containment failures in multi-agent LLM systems. In multi-agent mode, blocks 100% of ungrounded upstream outputs from reaching downstream agents.
  • 🆕 LIVE: Language-Instructed Vision Embeddings for Controllable and Generalizable Perception
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-20 · VLMUses language as high-level guidance to dynamically steer a vision encoder at inference time, producing task-centric embeddings without task-specific retraining. Reduces visual hallucinations by +34 points on MMVP, surpasses VLMs with orders of magnitude more parameters on VQA, and generalizes to unseen instructions — a direct path toward instruction-driven visual intelligence.
  • 🆕 CARE: Competence-Aware Reward Shaping for Adaptive Reasoning Length in Video-MLLMs
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-20 · videoMaintains a smoothed competence estimate via EMA of pass rates and uses it to route training from exploration-oriented long reasoning to efficiency-oriented concise reasoning. Exhibits an inverted-U trajectory of reasoning length during training, yielding shorter yet more informative traces at convergence. Consistently improves reasoning accuracy and token efficiency across multiple video benchmarks.
  • 🆕 Current World Models Lack a Persistent State Core
    HF papers · 2026-06-20 · fundamentalArgues that modeling the physical world requires an internal state that keeps evolving decoupled from observation — objects must endure and events must run to completion whether or not a camera is watching. Identifies this as a blind spot of existing benchmarks, which focus on rendering convincing frames rather than testing persistent state maintenance.
  • GraphPO: Graph-based Policy Optimization for Reasoning Models
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-19 · architectureRepresents RL rollouts as a directed acyclic graph where semantically equivalent reasoning paths merge into equivalence classes, sharing suffixes and reallocating budget from redundant to diverse exploration. Assigns efficiency advantages to incoming edges and correctness advantages to outgoing edges. Theory shows reduced advantage-estimation variance; consistently outperforms chain- and tree-based baselines under the same token/response budgets.
  • STARE: Surprisal-Guided Token-Level Advantage Reweighting for Policy Entropy Stability
    HF papers · 2026-06-19 · fundamentalIdentifies a token-level credit assignment mismatch in GRPO: per-token entropy variation decomposes into the product of trajectory-level advantage and an entropy sensitivity function. Proposes surprisal-guided reweighting that stabilizes policy entropy during RLVR training, addressing the well-known entropy collapse failure mode.
