  • 🆕 ITNet: A Learnable Integral Transform That Subsumes Convolution, Attention, and Recurrence
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-22 · architecture · fundamentalShows that convolution, self-attention (including multi-head), and autoregressive recurrence (LSTM, GRU, S4, Mamba) are special cases of a single learnable integral transform. A shared-operator ITNet with lightweight modality-specific encoders matches or exceeds specialized baselines on ImageNet-1K, GLUE, ModelNet40, VQA v2, and NLVR2 — a strong step toward architecture unification.
  • 🆕 GRACE: Granularity-Regulated Adaptive Computational Efficiency for Optimal Verification in Test-Time Scaling
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-22 · test-timeProves a phase transition in optimal verification granularity for test-time scaling: fine-grained verification dominates when compute budget is large or the problem is hard, while coarse-grained is preferred in low-budget easy regimes. Unifies Best-of-N, beam search, and step-level MCTS within a single Pareto framework. Adaptive strategy gains +3.1% accuracy on MATH-500 at matched compute.
  • 🆕 LIVE: Language-Instructed Vision Embeddings for Controllable and Generalizable Perception
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-22 · VLMProposes using language itself to dynamically guide the vision encoder at inference time, producing task-centric embeddings without retraining. Reduces visual hallucinations by +34 points on MMVP, surpasses VLMs with orders of magnitude more parameters on VQA, and generalizes to unseen instructions and tasks.
  • 🆕 How Linear Is a Transformer Feed-Forward Block? Per-Block Linear Recoverability Is Learned, Not Architectural
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-22 · architectureMeasures each FFN block's linearity via an optimiser-free metric (R²_lin). Finds highly heterogeneous profiles across blocks — near-linear (>0.99) to strongly nonlinear (<0.3) in adjacent blocks — and shows this is a learned property, not set by the activation function. Same-width GELU models GPT-2 and Pythia-160m have sharply different profiles. Enables targeted 8× compression of recoverable blocks.
  • 🆕 ViCoStream: Streaming VideoLLMs Can Run Beyond 100 FPS with Stage-Wise Coordinated Inference
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-22 · VLM · architectureFormulates streaming VideoLLM inference as a coordinated pipeline and introduces chunk-wise execution, visual token control, bounded attention, and query-side retrieval. Achieves 134 FPS video throughput and &lt;50 ms TTFT on a single A100 GPU with Qwen2.5-VL while maintaining accuracy close to full-history baselines.
  • 🆕 CARE: Competence-Aware Reward Shaping for Adaptive Reasoning Length in Video-MLLMs
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-22 · VLMProposes a competence-aware reward shaping framework that dynamically shifts reward preference from exploration-oriented long reasoning to efficiency-oriented concise reasoning as the model improves.   • 🆕 Think Again or Think Longer? Selective Verification for Budget-Aware Reasoning
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-22 · test-timeStudies when extra reasoning is worth the compute — it can repair failures, waste tokens on correct answers, or introduce harmful flips. A serving-layer controller (SEVRA) decides whether to preserve or verify each answer. Reduces harmful answer flips from 2.2% to 1.0% while cutting post-generation tokens by 26.8%. Key deployment rule: tune the initial budget first, then use selective recovery.
  • 🆕 Can In-Context Learning Support Intrinsic Curiosity?
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-22 · agent · fundamentalInvestigates whether ICL can replace expensive gradient-based inner loops for curiosity-driven exploration. Proves that in general MDPs, ICL-derived rewards suffer from irreducible bias — but for non-temporal settings (active learning, Bayesian experimental design), they successfully converge to true learning progress. A fundamental result connecting ICL mechanics with exploration theory.
