  • 🆕 SARA: Unlocking Multilingual Knowledge in Mixture-of-Experts via Semantically Anchored Routing Alignment

Aligns routing distributions using symmetric JS divergence with high-resource semantic anchors, yielding +0.8% on Qwen3-30B-A3B and +1.2% on Phi-3.5-MoE on Global-MMLU.

  • 🆕 Efficient and Trainable Language Model Test-Time Scaling via Local Branch Routing

Defines a tractable tree-trajectory likelihood enabling end-to-end RL with verifiable rewards. Improves both Pass@1 and Pass@32 over chain-of-thought and vanilla RLVR baselines.

  • 🆕 Evidence for feature-specific error correction in LLMs
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-26 · fundamentalTests whether LLMs perform computation in superposition by perturbing residual-stream activations. Replicated across Gemma-2-9B, Qwen3-1.7B, Llama-3.1-8B, Mistral-7B, and others.
  • 🆕 Internal Data Repetition Destroys Language Models
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-26 · fundamentalRevisits repetition damage under Chinchilla-era scaling laws. Shows damage peaks at an intermediate repeat count (not the extremes), the most damaging count follows a power law in model size, and 10% FLOPs on exact duplicates can cost the equivalent of 33% total compute wasted on FineWeb-Edu-Dedup.
  • 🆕 Reflective VLA: In-Context Action Consequences Make VLAs Generalize

  • 🆕 TRUSTMEM: Learning Trustworthy Memory Consolidation for LLM Agents
