  • A-Evolve-Training: Autonomous Post-Training of a 30B Model

  • Grouped Query Experts: Mixture-of-Experts on GQA Self-Attention
    arXiv · 2026-06-24 · MoE · architectureProposes GQE, a mixture-of-experts layer on top of grouped-query attention where a router selects k query-head experts per token while all KV heads remain dense. At 250M scale on 30B tokens, GQE matches the all-active GQA baseline while activating half the query heads, combining MoE efficiency with GQA's KV cache benefits.
