  • 🆕 Agentic Abstention: Do Agents Know When to Stop Instead of Act?
    UW / AI2 · 2026-07-01 · agentDefines the sequential decision problem of when agents should stop acting under uncertainty — distinct from single-turn LLM abstention. Evaluates 13 LLM-as-agent systems on 28K+ tasks; finds that larger models sometimes perform worse at timely abstention.   • 🆕 SeKV: Resolution-Adaptive KV Cache with Hierarchical Semantic Memory
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-01 · kv cacheAddresses the linear memory growth of KV caches in long-context LLM inference. Unlike token eviction (irreversible) or fixed-time semantic grouping, SeKV introduces a hierarchical semantic memory that adaptively adjusts compression resolution during decoding, preserving faithful context at lower memory cost.
  • 🆕 CHERRY: Compressed Hierarchical Experts with Recurrent Representational Yield
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-01 · MoE · architectureCombines three techniques for compute-efficient LMs: (1) selective supervision concentrating on ~15% of semantic-payload tokens (achieving 4.5× per-supervised-token efficiency via positive gradient coupling), (2) hierarchical MoE structure, and (3) recurrent representational yield. A unified approach to MoE efficiency.
  • 🆕 Learning to Select, Not Relearn: Hard-Routed Mixtures of Reasoning LoRAs
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-07-01 · MoEProposes hard routing over frozen reasoning LoRA experts instead of soft MoE-style weighted combinations. Soft mixtures change the unit-scale additive updates each LoRA was trained under; hard routing preserves each adapter's original calibration. Enables multi-domain composition without retraining or sharing original data.
  • 🆕 ReGRPO: Reflection-Augmented Policy Optimization for Tool-Using Agents
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-07-01 · VLM · agentTackles the fragility of tool-augmented VLMs on multi-step tasks. SFT lacks failure-recovery signal; trajectory-level RL rewards are too sparse. ReGRPO adds step-level reflection that identifies which step failed and how to repair it, combining per-step feedback with group relative policy optimization.
  • 🆕 Seeing Is Not Sharing: VLMs Overestimate Common Ground in Asymmetric Dialogue
