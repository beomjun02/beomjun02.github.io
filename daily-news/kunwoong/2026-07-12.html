  • 🆕 Towards Efficient Large Language Model Serving: A Survey on System-Aware KV Cache Optimization
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-11 · kv cacheComprehensive survey organizing KV cache infrastructure for LLM serving into three dimensions: temporal (execution/scheduling), spatial (placement/migration), and structural (representation/retention).   • 🆕 It Takes a MAESTRO To Prune Bad Experts
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-11 · MoEIntroduces MAESTRO, a structured pruning framework for Mixture-of-Experts models that models expert activation trajectories as Ergodic Markov chains whose stationary distributions encode cross-layer dependencies. Under 50% compression, outperforms baselines by up to 10.61% in average performance retention across five domains including safety and ethics, with lower cross-task variance.
  • 🆕 Linear Attention Architectures: Mechanisms, Trade-offs, and Cross-Layer Routing
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-11 · architectureComparative study of softmax attention vs. four recurrent linear-attention architectures — DeltaNet, Gated DeltaNet, Kimi Delta Attention, and Gated DeltaNet-2 — expressed in a common recurrent-memory notation. At 350M params / 15B tokens, Kimi Delta Attention + Muon achieves lowest validation loss; introduces Cross-Layer Value Routing (CLVR) for DeltaNet-style memories.
  • 🆕 Hidden Decoding at Scale: Latent Computation Scaling for Large Language Models
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-11 · architectureProposes sequence-length scaling as an alternative to widening Transformer layers: each token is expanded into n streams with independent embedding tables, using Stream-Factorized Attention to keep cost roughly linear. Trained WeLM-HD4-80B and WeLM-HD4-617B (Tencent) at n=4 — the first demonstrated sequence-length scaling at 100B+ MoE scale, improving matched non-HD baselines.
  • 🆕 What to Keep, What to Forget: A Rate–Distortion View of Memory Compaction in LLMs and Agents
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-11 · kv cache · agentArgues that KV cache eviction, prompt pruning, architectural state bounding, and agent memory consolidation are instances of one problem — a rate–distortion decision about what to retain under a resource budget. Proposes a unified compaction objective with a layer-agnostic lower bound and a seven-axis taxonomy classifying methods uniformly across the stack.
  • 🆕 Rethinking Small VLM Quantization: From Component-Wise Analysis to Hardware-Aware Edge Deployment
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-07-11 · VLM · MoESystematic evaluation of quantization for sub-3B VLMs on Jetson Orin NX/AGX, separating vision encoder, projector, and LLM backbone. Key finding: quantization sensitivity is governed by structural paradigm (MoE vs. dense) rather than scale alone — MoE backbones mitigate INT4 noise where dense ones degrade. Also finds INT4 can paradoxically slow token generation due to dequantization overhead.
  • 🆕 Hardware-aware dynamic speculative decoding
    Cohere · 2026-07-11 · architectureTechnical deep-dive from Cohere Foundations (Ekagra Ranjan) on adapting speculative decoding to hardware characteristics at runtime.   • 🆕 DeepSearch-World: Self-Distillation for Deep Search Agents in a Verifiable Environment
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-07-11 · agentPresents DeepSearch-Evolve, a self-distillation framework for web agents using a deterministic, verifiable environment with 420K multi-hop QA tasks. Without distillation from stronger models, DeepSearch-World-9B achieves 31.2% on BrowseComp, 61.5% on GAIA, 93.4% on HotpotQA — showing verifiable environments enable scalable self-evolution for long-horizon agents.
