  • Hidden Decoding at Scale: Latent Computation Scaling for Large Language Models
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-12 · architecture · MoEProposes sequence-length scaling as an alternative to widening Transformer layers: each token is expanded into n streams with independent embedding tables, using Stream-Factorized Attention to keep cost roughly linear.   • 🆕 Uncertainty-gated selection for block-sparse attention
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-12 · kv cacheProposes a value-of-information router for block-sparse attention that measures how decisively the top-k cut was made and doubles the kept set for ambiguous queries. On LongBench-v2 at 32K, paired recall jumps from 0.47 to 0.75 (+28 pp over SSA baseline) while running at 0.62–0.80x dense wall time across Qwen2.5, Mistral-Nemo, and Qwen3.6.
  • 🆕 Jet-Long: Efficient Long-Context Extension with Dynamic Bifocal RoPE
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-12 · architectureTuning-free zero-shot long-context extension pairing a local RoPE-faithful window with a long-range window whose rescaling factor adapts dynamically to sequence length. At 128K on Qwen3, preserves 0.81–0.89 of dense accuracy while SSA-style top-k drops to 0.09. Fused CuTe kernel reaches up to 1.39x FA2 throughput on H100.
  • 🆕 Selective Left-Shift: Turning Test-Time Compute and Difficulty-based Curation into Training Data for Low-Resource Code Generation

On Qwen3-8B for Julia, improves pass@1 by +14.2 points on LiveCodeBench using 1/3 data and 1/6 cost of prior SOTA. Generalizes to Ballerina (49.7% pass@1) — a language with near-zero pretraining representation.
  • 🆕 Overthinking: Amplifying Reasoning Weights to Extract Learned Secrets

  • 🆕 Dive Into the Implicit Biases of Low-rank Vision-language Alignment
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-07-12 · VLMFinds that low-rank adaptation during VL alignment outperforms full-parameter alignment on most benchmarks. Key insight: full-parameter alignment disrupts per-token linear separability of visual features (termed ‘LS-curse’), while low-rank alignment preserves modality-specific structure. Over 100 alignment configurations ablated across three low-rank operator families.
  • It Takes a MAESTRO To Prune Bad Experts

  • DeepSearch-World: Self-Distillation for Deep Search Agents in a Verifiable Environment
