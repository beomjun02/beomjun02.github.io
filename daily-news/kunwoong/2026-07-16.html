  • 🆕 Less Experts, Faster Decoding: Cost-Aware Speculative Decoding for MoE
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-16 · MoEIntroduces EcoSpec, addressing expert scattering where confidence-driven draft tokens route to disjoint experts, increasing memory traffic.   • 🆕 How Query Visibility Changes KV-Cache Compression Rankings
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-16 · kv cacheAudits six KV-cache compression methods under query-aware vs. query-agnostic protocols across three 7–9B models (144K+ paired evaluations on RULER-8192). Under the agnostic protocol (compress once, answer many), only KeyDiff consistently beats trivial baselines, and SnapKV loses to "keep start + recent window." Per-method degradation correlates with how visible the query is to each method&#x27;s scoring signal.
  • 🆕 Visual Access Boundaries in Vision-Language Model Reasoning
    HF papers · 2026-07-16 · VLMIntroduces Visual Access Sweep, a causal intervention masking attention from generated tokens to image tokens along layer depth and time. Across Qwen2.5-VL and InternVL3 (14B–38B), CoT does not extend direct image-token access — gains come from language-side computation over image-derived hidden states. The bottleneck is at perceptual readout, not reasoning depth.
