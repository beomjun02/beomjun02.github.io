  • 🆕 Adaptive Filtering of the KV Cache: Diagnosing and Correcting Structural-Role Bias in LLM Inference
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-17 · kv cacheReveals that attention-based KV cache eviction (H2O family) acts as a non-stationary filter that over-retains structural tokens (delimiters, whitespace) at 1.8× the rate of answer-carrying tokens, collapsing exact-match accuracy from 88% to 0% at 5% budget. A retraining-free, role-conditional allocation atop SnapKV closes 63–98% of the gap at sub-20% budgets using a lightweight 15 MB linear role probe.
  • 🆕 Consensus as Privileged Context for Label-Free Self-Distillation (CANON)
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-17 · test-timeTurns majority-vote consensus into dense, token-level supervision: samples multiple solutions per unlabeled prompt, extracts the majority answer, and uses a consensus-anchored frozen teacher to supervise every token. Improves pass@1 by up to 12 points, outperforming label-free RL by 6 points at 1/7th compute, and enables the model to solve problems it never solved in 32 attempts before training.
  • 🆕 DeepLoop: Depth Scaling for Looped Transformers
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-17 · architectureFormalizes the tied-depth effect in looped transformers where one shared parameter set aggregates gradients from repeated visits. Derives that the scaling exponent must increase from 1/4 to 1/2 as loop count grows, yielding the DeepLoop residual scaling rule. On GPT-2 scale, improves validation loss and downstream accuracy once recurrent depth is activated — neutral at zero loops, strictly beneficial with reuse.
  • 🆕 TrustVLA: Mechanism-Guided Inference-Time Defense Against Vision-Language-Action Backdoors

  • 🆕 Memory as a Controlled Process (MemCon): Learned Adaptive Memory Management for LLM Agents
