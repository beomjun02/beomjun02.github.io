  • 🆕 MixCompress: Mixture of Experts for Variable Rate Learned Image Compression
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-07-18 · MoEProposes using MoE to resolve the rate-distortion conflict in learned image compression: low-rate smoothing gradients conflict with high-frequency texture preservation in shared backbones. MixCompress assigns specialized experts per rate point, avoiding feature entanglement while sharing most parameters. Achieves competitive BD-rate savings across multiple operating points with a single model instead of rate-specific checkpoints. (No major lab affiliation listed)
  • 🆕 D-cut: Adaptive Verification Depth Pruning for Batched Speculative Decoding
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-18 · architectureAddresses speculative decoding’s degradation under high request concurrency: long drafts waste computation on rejected tokens. D-cut adaptively prunes verification depth per batch based on a draft-quality signal, cutting verification cost while preserving output quality. Under batched serving, D-cut recovers speedups that fixed-length speculative decoding loses at high concurrency. (No major lab affiliation listed)
  • 🆕 Multi-Head Latent Control: A Unified Interface for LLM Agent Decision Making
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-18 · agent · architectureInstead of routing agent decisions (proceed, defer, abstain, tool-call) through prompts or external orchestration, this paper trains dedicated latent control heads alongside the standard LM head. Each head handles a distinct decision type — a unified architectural interface that replaces prompt-level routing with learned representations. Eliminates the need for task-specific fine-tuning of the routing logic itself. (No major lab affiliation listed)
  • 🆕 CIPHER: A Decoupled Exploration-Selection Framework for Test-Time Scaling of Data Science Agents
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-07-18 · test-timeData science agents are vulnerable to cascading errors from suboptimal initial states. CIPHER decouples the problem into an exploration phase (generating diverse candidate initial states) and a selection phase (choosing the best via a learned evaluator), enabling test-time compute scaling — more exploration budget leads to monotonically better outcomes. Evaluated on data science benchmarks with significant gains over single-trajectory agents. (No major lab affiliation listed)
  • 🆕 AI Agents Do Not Fail Alone: The Context Fails First
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-07-18 · trustworthyValidates that context engineering quality is a leading indicator of agent reliability. When instructions, tools, memory, guardrails, or retrieved knowledge in the context are weak, agents drift, hallucinate, misuse tools, and become vulnerable to injection. The paper proposes measurable context-quality dimensions and shows they predict downstream agent failures independently of the underlying LLM’s capability. (No major lab affiliation listed)
  • 🆕 VideoSEMA: a scalable and efficient Mamba-like attention for video understanding
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-07-18 · architecturePresents a split space-time attention model for video classification: spatial frames use SEMA (local window attention + global averaging in a Mamba macro-architecture), temporal dimension uses standard softmax attention. Under certain rank conditions, proves that this computationally cheaper split attention is equivalent to full space-time attention. Achieves strong video classification results with significantly fewer FLOPs. (No major lab affiliation listed)
