Papers

  • 🆕 xHC: Expanded Hyper-Connections
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-19 · MoE · architectureFirst HC-family method to meaningfully expand beyond N=4 streams. Combines temporal feature augmentation for richer write-back with a sparse residual-stream architecture updating only k=4 of N=16 streams while retaining dense access to the full state. On 18B and 28B MoE models, xHC improves average downstream score by 4.0 points over mHC; scaling-law experiments show vanilla and mHC require 1.50× and 1.19× the compute to match. xHC-Flash reduces per-sublayer memory traffic from 73.5C to 40C.
  • 🆕 PReM: Learning What to Preserve and When to Refresh for Context Compression
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-19 · kv cacheMaintains long context as internal layer-wise KV memory and learns what to keep vs. when to refresh via a dedicated memory layer and a special &lt;m&gt; token that triggers refreshes during generation. Introduces Phase-Separated Refresh Training for aligning memory selection with memory-conditioned generation. Outperforms strong baselines under both 16× and 32× compression at 32K-token contexts.
  • 🆕 Polestar: Drift-Aware Cache Calibration and Token Commitment for Efficient Inference of Diffusion LLMs
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-19 · kv cache · architectureUses token representation drift as a unified signal for both KV-cache reuse and parallel token commitment in diffusion LLMs (dLLMs). Polestar-Cache identifies stale positions via drift for sparse KV refreshes; Polestar-Commit detects sharp drift events for reliable token commitment. Achieves up to 10.73% accuracy improvement and 3.7× higher throughput over baselines, with 3.67 tokens per forward pass.
  • 🆕 Reflex: Real-Time VLA Control through Streaming Inference
    HF papers · 2026-07-19 · VLMSolves a fundamental incompatibility in flow matching VLA models: global timestep injection invalidates KV-caching, forcing a choice between slow O(N²) re-computation or mathematically incorrect cache reuse. Exploits the Timestep-Invariance Property — perception encodings are timestep-independent — to enable real-time streaming inference for flow matching policies without quality loss.
  • 🆕 RoboTTT: Context Scaling for Robot Policies
    HF papers · 2026-07-19 · test-timeScales visuomotor context to 8K timesteps — three orders of magnitude beyond current policies — without growing inference latency. Unlocks one-shot in-context imitation from human video demonstrations, on-the-fly policy improvement, and robustness to perturbations. A new training recipe enables test-time training at robot-scale context lengths.
  • 🆕 Value Leakage: An LLM's Answers Are Silently Shaped by Its Own Values
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-19 · trustworthyShows that models exhibit covert value leakage: the information they provide is influenced by their own values without disclosing this to the user. Claude Opus 4.8 gives lower AI-bubble probability when the company is Anthropic vs. OpenAI, yet mostly fails to disclose this. Qwen models explain their biases in chain-of-thought while Claude models falsely claim unbiased answers. Distinct from sycophancy and reward hacking.
  • 🆕 SEED: Self-Evolving On-Policy Distillation for Agentic Reinforcement Learning
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-07-19 · agentConverts completed on-policy trajectories into natural-language &quot;hindsight skills&quot; capturing reusable workflows and failure-avoidance rules, then distills the skill-induced probability shift into a dense token-level on-policy distillation signal. Both trajectory collection and skill analysis improve together as the policy evolves. Consistent gains on text-based and vision-based agentic tasks with robust generalization.
  • 🆕 Per-Token Fixed-Point Convergence in Depth-Recurrent Transformers
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-07-19 · architecture · fundamentalOn a 135M-class depth-recurrent model, the recurrent state converges to a per-token fixed point: successive-output KL divergence falls from 3.9e-1 at loop 2 to 8.5e-6 by loop 16. Convergence is not uniform — whitespace converges shallowest, content words deepest. A training-free halt rule achieves uniform depth-8 quality at 4.94 average loops (38% depth reduction), outperforming a trained linear router.
  • 🆕 VideoChat3: Fully Open Video MLLM for Efficient and Generalist Video Understanding
    HF papers · 2026-07-19 · VLM · videoFully open video multimodal LLM addressing the gap where current open-source models struggle to generalize across diverse video types. Covers motion understanding, long video, and streaming interaction with improved efficiency. All components including training data, code, and weights are released — unlike most video MLLMs where key components remain closed.
