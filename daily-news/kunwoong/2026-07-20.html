  • 🆕 Smarter and Cheaper at Once: Byte-Exact KV-Cache Grafting Turns a Frozen Small Model into a Verified-Knowledge Flywheel
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-20 · kv cacheDeposits verified solutions as byte-exact KV state artifacts that are later restored bit-identically (SHA-256 verified, zero KL divergence) into fresh inference contexts. On AIME 2025, a frozen Gemma-4-12B improves from 80.0% to 93.3% via grafted solution library; for recurring problems, answers come in 61 decode tokens vs. 401K, a ~6,574x token reduction. Also extends usable context from 32K to 2.85M tokens at zero extra accelerator memory.
  • 🆕 LongStraw: Long-Context RL Beyond 2M Tokens under a Fixed GPU Budget

Bridges the 256K training ceiling to 2M+ tokens under a fixed GPU budget — particularly relevant for AI agents whose observations, tool outputs, and prior decisions accumulate over long trajectories.
  • 🆕 Branching Policy Optimization: Sandbox-Native Language Agent Reinforcement Learning
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-20 · agentExploits a defining property of agent sandboxes (deterministic, snapshottable, resumable) to design a new rollout topology: instead of N independent trajectories, BPO forks at high-entropy decision points and computes advantages from sibling returns sharing common prefixes. Achieves +3.6–6.1 points over GRPO and RLOO on WebShop, ALFWorld, and SWE-bench Verified with Qwen2.5-7B and Llama-3.1-8B, using 38% fewer policy updates.
  • 🆕 AdaTurn: Budget-Aware Test-Time Scaling for Active Visual Perception Agents
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-07-20 · test-timeAddresses catastrophic truncation — when an agent's preferred rollout exceeds the available turn budget and no valid answer is produced. Introduces Forced-Answer DAPO (FA-DAPO), which trains the boundary behavior explicitly so the model synthesizes partial evidence when further tool use is cut off. Raises VisualProbe-Medium accuracy from 36.7% to 47.6% at four turns while preserving scaling at larger budgets.
  • 🆕 Sharp Stability Threshold and Certification for Designing Stable Residual Architectures
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-20 · architectureEstablishes the sublinear-growth principle as the sharp stability threshold for deep residual architectures: the velocity field exponent q ≤ 1 is both necessary and sufficient for stable training and inference. An arithmetic of input-magnitude exponents certifies stability at the primitive level. A parameter-free fix reduces the supercritical Mamba block from q = 5 to q = 1 without layer normalization — validated on Mamba and PatchTST.
  • 🆕 SD-MAR: Multi-image Analytical Reasoning via Synthetic Data and Reinforcement Learning
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-07-20 · VLMTrains VLMs on multi-image analytical reasoning using controlled perturbation pairs and GRPO-lite with Backward Discounted Allocation (credit to later reasoning steps). On Qwen2.5-VL-7B, in-domain accuracy improves by up to 36.95%, outperforming GPT-4.1 on the benchmark, while out-of-domain performance on MME, MMMU-Pro, and MathVista is preserved or improved.
  • xHC: Expanded Hyper-Connections
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-19 · MoE · architectureFirst HC-family method to meaningfully expand beyond N=4 streams. Combines temporal feature augmentation for richer write-back with a sparse residual-stream architecture updating only k=4 of N=16 streams while retaining dense access to the full state. On 18B and 28B MoE models, xHC improves average downstream score by 4.0 points over mHC; scaling-law experiments show vanilla and mHC require 1.50× and 1.19× the compute to match. xHC-Flash reduces per-sublayer memory traffic from 73.5C to 40C.
  • PReM: Learning What to Preserve and When to Refresh for Context Compression
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-19 · kv cacheMaintains long context as internal layer-wise KV memory and learns what to keep vs. when to refresh via a dedicated memory layer and a special \&lt;m\&gt; token that triggers refreshes during generation. Introduces Phase-Separated Refresh Training for aligning memory selection with memory-conditioned generation. Outperforms strong baselines under both 16× and 32× compression at 32K-token contexts.
