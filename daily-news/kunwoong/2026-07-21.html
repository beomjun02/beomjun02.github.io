  • 🆕 Loop the Loopies!
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-21 · MoE · architectureThe most powerful looped Transformer to date: two MoE models (20B/2B-active and 6B/0.6B-active) that substantially outperform vanilla Transformer baselines trained with the same compute budget. Extensive ablations against a vanilla 30B-A3B model confirm the advantage. Novel post-training pipeline yields gold-medal performance on IMO and IPhO without tools — the first looped architecture to beat the parameter-scaling baseline head-to-head.
  • 🆕 Looped Latent Attention: Cross-Loop KV Compression for Looped Transformers
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-21 · kv cache · architectureShows that in looped Transformers, KV vectors trace short low-rank trajectories across loops while head/layer axes stay flat. LLA stores compact KV latents and reconstructs loop-specific vectors on demand — outperforming head-axis MLA, cross-layer sharing, and KV quantization at matched budget. Achieves 21.3x compression on Ouro-1.4B (batch capacity 32→768 on one H200); SVD codec near-lossless to 32x on Huginn-3.5B.
  • 🆕 VarRate: Training-Free Variable-Rate KV Cache Compression for Long-Context LLMs
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-21 · kv cacheAssigns each token a variable low-rank budget by query salience instead of evicting — no token is dropped, so it degrades by only 3.5–5.5 points where query-aware selection collapses (11–15 points). At matched 20% budget on LongBench, stays within 0.8 points of uncompressed on both Llama-3.1-8B and Qwen2.5-7B. Accuracy-equivalent to KVzip at ~1/8th the prefill overhead.
  • 🆕 QUADS: Stabilizing NVFP4 Reinforcement Learning for MoE
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-21 · MoEFinds that directly applying NVFP4 to MoE RL rollout collapses after ~150 steps — activation error, not weight error, is the dominant source. Proposes asymmetric QAT (fake-quantize weights, keep activations unquantized) plus residual activation compensation for high-error channels. Achieves BF16-level accuracy in MoE RL with ~16% higher rollout throughput than FP8 and +21.49 pass@1 over naive NVFP4.
  • 🆕 Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-21 · fundamentalUses the Jacobian lens to identify representations a model is poised to verbalize — the "J-space." These exhibit global workspace properties: reportable, deliberately summoned, used for silent reasoning. In alignment audits, J-space reveals strategic deliberation and misaligned dispositions that never appear in outputs. Post-training installs the Assistant's viewpoint in this workspace; counterfactual reflection training improves behavior by training only what a model would say if interrupted.
  • xHC: Expanded Hyper-Connections
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-19 · architectureFirst HC-family method to meaningfully expand beyond N=4 streams. Combines temporal feature augmentation for richer write-back with a sparse residual-stream architecture updating only k=4 of N=16 streams while retaining dense access to the full state. On 18B and 28B MoE models, xHC improves average downstream score by 4.0 points over mHC; scaling-law experiments show vanilla and mHC require 1.50× and 1.19× the compute to match. xHC-Flash reduces per-sublayer memory traffic from 73.5C to 40C.
  • Smarter and Cheaper at Once: Byte-Exact KV-Cache Grafting Turns a Frozen Small Model into a Verified-Knowledge Flywheel
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-20 · kv cacheDeposits verified solutions as byte-exact KV state artifacts that are later restored bit-identically (SHA-256 verified, zero KL divergence) into fresh inference contexts. On AIME 2025, a frozen Gemma-4-12B improves from 80.0% to 93.3% via grafted solution library; for recurring problems, answers come in 61 decode tokens vs. 401K, a ~6,574x token reduction. Also extends usable context from 32K to 2.85M tokens at zero extra accelerator memory.
