  • 🆕 AgentKVShift: Efficient KV Cache Reuse for Agentic Memory Systems
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-07-28 · kv cache · agentTraining-free, probe-guided KV residual correction for agentic memory: the key insight is that per-memory KV reuse residual decomposes into a shared memory-level offset plus small token-wise fluctuations. Corrects all reused tokens (not just refreshed ones), achieving near full-recompute quality at 10–30% refresh — prior methods need 45–55%. Delivers 2–3.5x prefill speedup on A100, composes with KV cache quantization down to 2-bit.
  • 🆕 J-CoT: Chain-of-Thought in J-Space
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-28 · architectureIntroduces J-space, a vocabulary-indexed coordinate system within hidden representations, as an intermediate reasoning interface. At each reasoning cycle boundary, the model expresses intermediate state as vocabulary-indexed coefficients (a "J-thought"), carries them forward, and maps back — requiring neither decoded text nor full hidden-state recurrence. J-CoT-Train achieves the highest score across math, science, coding, and path-reasoning among evaluated latent-reasoning methods.
  • 🆕 Parameter-free Adaptive Sparse Attention via Compression-Based Content Selection
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-28 · architectureUses gzip compression ratios as a masking signal for sparse attention — blocks that gzip cannot compress contain information not predictable from local repetition, making them natural long-range targets. No additional parameters, no custom kernels. On PG-19 at 92M params with 8K context, achieves 1.71 BPB vs. dense attention's 2.89 and BigBird's 2.34, with a gap that widens with sequence length.
  • 🆕 Learning What Matters: Supervising Sparse Attention Routing with Causal Evidence Sets
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-28 · architectureShows that attention and causal dependence often disagree — distilled sparse-attention selectors inherit this mismatch. In a two-step reference task, a selector trained on attention achieves 41% accuracy while the same selector trained on causal evidence reaches 99%. Validated on pretrained Qwen2.5-3B and Gemma-2-9B: attention gives more weight to outdated facts than current ones on 58% of conflicting examples. Directly challenges attention-score-based KV-cache eviction heuristics.
  • 🆕 Scaling Native Multimodal Pre-Training From Scratch

  • 🆕 Procedural Knowledge Is Not Low-Rank: Why LoRA Fails to Internalize Multi-Step Procedures
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-07-28 · fundamental · agentShows that LoRA uniformly fails on procedural knowledge (multi-step tasks with conditional branching) at all efficient ranks. SVD analysis reveals full fine-tuning update effective rank of 761–1,026, with rank 128 capturing only 43–51% of squared Frobenius norm. Replicated across travel booking, Zoom support, and insurance claims at 8B. A fundamental limitation for agentic applications relying on parameter-efficient tuning.
