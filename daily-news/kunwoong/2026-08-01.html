  • 🆕 Multi-Head Attention Residuals

  • 🆕 Beyond KV Reconstruction: Functional Reconstruction for MLA Draft Models in Speculative Decoding

  • 🆕 Prox: Training-Free FFN Activation Sparsity via Approximate Intermediate-Channel Salience
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-08-01 · architectureKey insight: sparse FFN execution needs only the channel mask from the SwiGLU intermediate state, constructable from magnitude ranking rather than exact values. Achieves up to 1.99x end-to-end decoding speedup at 70% FFN sparsity across ten LLMs from six families, compatible with quantization and sparse attention.
