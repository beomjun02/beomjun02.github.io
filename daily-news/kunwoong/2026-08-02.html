  • 🆕 SVR: Self-Verifying Refinement via Joint Verdict-Confidence Reinforcement Learning for Adaptive Test-Time Compute

  • Chimera: Designing and Chinchilla-Scaling Hybrid Visual Diffusion Transformers
    HF papers · 2026-08-01 · architectureMoonshot AI (Kimi team) introduce Chimera, a hybrid visual diffusion backbone combining Kimi Delta Attention (KDA) for O(N) long-context state tracking with interleaved Multi-head Latent Attention (MLA) — the same components powering Kimi K3. Processes text, image, and video tokens in one raster-ordered stream without positional embeddings, with a Chinchilla-style scaling recipe for compute-optimal training across resolutions.
  • Multi-Head Attention Residuals
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-08-01 · architectureRelaxes the single-query depth-routing bottleneck of attention residuals by reshaping the routing query into H per-subspace heads, each with its own softmax over layer history. At 1B params, MHAR achieves -0.140 validation loss vs. standard Transformer; an identity-preserving delta conversion enables 8B mid-training with +3.2 GSM8K and +3.1 GPQA gains. Fused Triton kernels recover 0.55–0.88x training throughput.
  • Beyond Geometric Complementarity: Coherent Overlap in Sparse MoE Routing
    HF papers · 2026-08-01 · MoEChallenges the assumption that co-selected MoE experts should contribute distinct representation directions. Introduces an Expert Subspace Separation Index (ESSI) and uses frozen-route interventions + prefix-controlled 2x2 factorial to disentangle route coherence from candidate quality. Finds that route coherence effects are smaller than previously assumed — candidate-by-context interaction drives most of the benefit.
  • Beyond KV Reconstruction: Functional Reconstruction for MLA Draft Models in Speculative Decoding
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-08-01 · kv cacheShows that direct MHA/GQA-to-MLA conversion can sharply reduce speculative decoding agreement due to attention-function errors. Proposes E2E functional reconstruction that optimizes each converted MLA module to reproduce the post-output-projection response of the original attention. Evaluated across 192 configurations spanning Llama/Qwen pairs, TransMLA, MHA2MLA, and vLLM — materially improves acceptance in 37/64 cells.
  • Back from the Future: Key-Value Cache Management by Counter-Causal Surprise
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-08-01 · kv cacheProposes scoring KV entries for eviction by running a counter-causal attention mask — past tokens well-predicted from their future context are redundant. Reuses existing cache representations with no additional training. A fast single-layer approximation restricts the counter-causal pass to the last transformer layer for practical speedup. Competitive or improved performance across open-source LLMs.
  • A Sparse Glimpse of the Whole: Train-Free Self-Speculative Decoding
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-08-01 · kv cacheGenerates lightweight drafts from the target model itself using a dynamically sparsified and recallable KV cache.   • Prox: Training-Free FFN Activation Sparsity via Approximate Intermediate-Channel Salience

Achieves up to 1.99x end-to-end decoding speedup at 70% FFN sparsity across ten LLMs from six families, compatible with quantization and sparse attention.
  • CaRe: Calibrate Before Reason — Robust Visual Token Reduction against Semantic Drift in VLMs
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-08-01 · VLMIntroduces the principle of &quot;Calibrate Before Reason&quot; for visual token reduction in VLMs. Perturbation-Robust Calibration Anchoring identifies stable anchors under multi-directional perturbations, then Confidence-Gated Token Calibration injects reliable signals from unselected tokens. While pruning 94.4% of visual tokens, retains 96.4% of full-token performance with up to 2.30x faster inference.
