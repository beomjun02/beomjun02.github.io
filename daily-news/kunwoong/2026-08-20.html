  • 🆕 Cacheable by Design? Training MoE Routers for Locality Against the Edge Memory-Bandwidth Wall

  • 🆕 Allocating Recurrent Compute in Looped Language Models

MixerLoop reduces recurrent-backbone FLOPs by 45.9% while retaining 41.5% of full-loop gains at 110M scale — a principled approach to selective recurrence.
  • 🆕 From Inference to Adaptation: A Unified Optimal Transport View of Vision Language Model

  • 🆕 SparsePR: Training-Free Sparse Attention for Video Generation and World Models
