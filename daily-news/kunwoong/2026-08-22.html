  • 🆕 ReCache: Efficient KV Cache Reuse and Compression for Tool-Augmented LLM Agents
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-08-22 · kv cache · agentIntroduces composition-invariant KV blocks for agentic LLMs that repeatedly encode recurring tool/skill schemas. Resource-wise attention removes cross-resource interactions and assigns local positions, enabling reuse regardless of tool ordering. On H20 GPUs, delivers up to 47x speedup over FlashAttention-2 at 128K context in FP8 and 30x over an FA3/4-aligned dense baseline.
  • 🆕 Learning how to Forget: Fine-tuning for Long-Context Sparse Attention
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-08-22 · kv cacheProposes a fine-tuning method for any KV cache eviction policy on moderate hardware (single A100 40GB), letting the model co-adapt with the policy.   • 🆕 Let's Scale Step by Step: Compute-Efficient Hyperparameter Transfer for Large-Scale MoE

  • 🆕 ClustRS: Clustering and Token Denoising for Faster and More Robust VLMs

  • WhiteMatter: All-to-All Cross-Layer Connections via KV Mixing
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-08-20 · architecture · kv cacheProposes a novel architecture where every attention layer can access representations from all layers of each past token via a lightweight router that mixes L layer states into k cached KV channels. This cross-layer connectivity outperforms a vanilla Transformer with 50% more layers and achieves 50% KV-cache compression with minimal quality loss — a strong result for both architecture design and cache efficiency.
  • MoE-ViE: Mixture of Experts Vision Encoder for Efficient Image and Video Understanding

  • Cacheable by Design? Training MoE Routers for Locality Against the Edge Memory-Bandwidth Wall
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-08-20 · MoEA pre-registered study measuring MoE expert caching behavior on Qwen3-235B (134GB, Q4_K_M) at the edge. Finds that 95% of routing traffic uses only 52.5% of experts and an LRU cache of 13.4% serves 66% of requests. Attempts to train routers for better locality reduce cache misses by up to 60% but fail the <=1% perplexity gate — training-free cache-aware rerouting achieves ~80% miss reduction at <=3.4% perplexity cost instead.
