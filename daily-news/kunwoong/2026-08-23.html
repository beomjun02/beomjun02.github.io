  • 🆕 Asymmetric Attention Heads: Structured Head-Wise Context Allocation for Transformer Attention

Several AAH variants outperform pure full attention in validation loss at 4096-token scale, with ablations showing that stable local allocation and head-window structure matter more than adaptive hierarchy.
  • 🆕 Daedalus-150M: A Convolution-Attention Hybrid Designed for CPU Inference
    HF papers · 2026-08-23 · architectureAn architecture designed target-first for CPU inference (single user, one token at a time, 4-bit weights). Only 6 of 18 blocks use full attention; the other 12 use short convolutions with 2-timestep memory, so two-thirds of the network never re-reads a growing KV cache. A practical approach to on-device deployment where the architecture is shaped by the hardware constraint.

  • 🆕 Does Marginal Coverage Guarantee Class-Conditional Safety for Zero-Shot VLMs Under Shift?
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-08-23 · VLM · trustworthyAudits conformal prediction as an abstention layer for zero-shot VLMs (CLIP, OpenCLIP, SigLIP) under distribution shift.
