Papers

  • 🆕 Affix Cache for Diffusion Large Language Models
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-08-29 · kv cache · architectureIntroduces ACache, a cache reuse mechanism for diffusion LLMs that identifies a small set of "Anchor Tokens" and selectively recomputes only their KV states while reusing the rest. Built on Fast-dLLM and Nano-vLLM, it reduces recompute latency by up to 55.7% and improves end-to-end throughput by 1.68×, recovering accuracy lost by naive affix-cache reuse when recomputing ~20% of tokens.
  • 🆕 Don't Overthink, Don't Underthink: Toward Adaptive Reasoning in Agentic AI
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-08-29 · agent · test-timeCharacterizes over-reasoning and under-reasoning as distinct failure modes in agentic systems, where fixed reasoning budgets lead to either wasted computation or incomplete solutions. Evaluated on MATH-500 and GAIA, the study finds over-reasoning incurs higher cost without accuracy gains, while under-reasoning consistently produces incorrect results — motivating future work on dynamic reasoning allocation for tool-using agents.
  • 🆕 Multi-Image Visual Token Pruning in Large Visual Language Models
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-08-29 · VLMProposes AVTP, a training-free adaptive visual token pruning framework that determines pruning layers from attention distribution analysis and assigns higher token budgets to more important images. On multi-image benchmarks, Qwen3VL-8B achieves 2× inference speedup while retaining 96.1% accuracy; InternVL3.5-8B retains 94.1%, and LLaVA-OV-7B even exceeds its original baseline (Tsinghua, Alibaba).
  • 🆕 Activation Outliers Matter: Robust Recovery for Quantized Multimodal LLMs
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-08-29 · VLM · architectureSystematic study showing that activation quantization, not weight quantization, is the primary bottleneck in ultra-low-bit MLLMs. Proposes Residual Fallback Quantization (RFQ), a lightweight framework that adds an auxiliary quantized residual pathway to recover activation fidelity. Tested on Wan2.2 and Qwen3-VL, RFQ substantially narrows the gap to BF16 baselines at MXFP4/HiF4 precision with negligible overhead.
  • 🆕 Syntax vs. Semantics: How Transformers Learn Deep Dependencies
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-08-29 · fundamental · architectureIdentifies a &quot;Gradient Starvation&quot; phenomenon where error signals for sparse semantic dependencies are actively suppressed during early training, causing deep reasoning to emerge as a sudden phase transition. The framework also explains why Chain-of-Thought works — it bypasses the suppression regime. A proposed topology-aligned contrastive objective achieves 2× improvement over standard fine-tuning on variable binding tasks, validated on Llama-3.1-8B and Qwen2.5-Coder-7B.
  • 🆕 TreeGraft: Adaptive Multi-Drafter Grafting for Tree-Based Speculative Decoding
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-08-29 · architectureMulti-drafter speculative decoding framework where drafters of different sizes jointly construct a shared tree — the stronger drafter rescores, reselects grafting positions, and recovers unexplored paths, while a lightweight scheduler (distilled from an offline value system) decides when to invoke the costlier drafter. Across 10 model pairs and 6 benchmarks, TreeGraft improves over the best single-drafter strategy by 15.1% on average (max 26.6%).
  • 🆕 Visual Information-Guided Parallel Decoding for Diffusion MLLMs
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-08-29 · VLMProposes VIG-Sampler for diffusion multimodal LLMs, which prioritizes token unmasking based on attention to image tokens and penalizes candidates with redundant image-attention distributions. This increases information gain per decoding step and outperforms Info-Gain Sampler by 19.3 CIDEr points on captioning benchmarks, while matching performance on VQA with fewer steps.
  • 🆕 Privacy Without Regret: Differentially Private Inference-Time Alignment
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-08-29 · trustworthy · test-timeShows that adding calibrated Gumbel noise to reward scores in Best-of-N sampling simultaneously provides ε-differential privacy and KL-regularized alignment. Above a critical privacy threshold ε*, the privacy-mandated noise is the regret-optimal regularization — privacy at zero additional alignment cost. The companion method PrivITP achieves (ε,δ)-DP with cost independent of candidate count n, and both methods are scaling-monotonic unlike vanilla BoN.
  • 🆕 J-Zero: Unified Challenger–Solver–Judge Co-Evolution from Zero Data
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-08-29 · agentSelf-evolving framework where a Challenger generates harder tasks, a Solver improves responses, and a Judge co-adapts using preference pairs whose ordering is known in advance (solver's answer over challenger's). J-Zero outperforms baselines by 4.2 pts on verifiable and 8.0 pts on unverifiable domains, and continues improving through at least 10 iterations while baselines degrade after two.
  • 🆕 TTPO: Test-Time Policy Optimization
    HF papers · 2026-08-29 · test-timeAddresses test-time training without ground-truth labels by using majority-vote pseudo-labels for on-policy reinforcement learning. Tackles the fragility of pseudo-label corruption through an asymmetric failure mode analysis, enabling policy optimization at inference time for mathematical reasoning tasks without requiring gold annotations.
  • 🆕 LLaVAFlow: Preserving Latent Alignment Flow for Parameter-Efficient Multimodal Fine-Tuning
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-08-29 · VLMInformation-theoretic distillation framework that preserves cross-modal alignment flow during MLLM fine-tuning. Compresses mutual information between extracted relations and embeddings into a refined alignment flow, then maximizes mutual information between pretrained and fine-tuned model flows. A plug-and-play module that prevents catastrophic forgetting while enhancing both downstream task performance and generalization.
