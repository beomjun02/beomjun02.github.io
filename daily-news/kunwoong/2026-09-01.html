  • 🆕 A Probabilistic Interpretation of KV Cache Eviction
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-08-31 · kv cacheFormalizes KV cache eviction through probabilistic reasoning, moving beyond creative heuristics. The framework reveals that eviction is equivalent to approximate marginalization over dropped tokens, providing theoretical guarantees for when eviction is safe and explaining why popular heuristics work. Offers a principled lens for designing future compression strategies.
  • 🆕 CoRe-MoE: Compact Reusable MoE for Continual Multimodal Instruction Tuning
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-08-31 · MoEObserves that LoRA expert subspaces overlap substantially across tasks and exploits this by decomposing experts into shared basis components and compact task-specific residuals. Reduces parameter overhead of continual MoE expansion while preserving previously learned knowledge — directly addressing the cost of repeatedly adding full LoRA experts for each new task.
  • 🆕 DensityKV: Density-Guided KV Cache Compression for Long Video Generation
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-08-31 · kv cacheProposes a training-free KV cache compression method for autoregressive video diffusion models. Uses kernel density estimation to identify and merge redundant historical states while preserving distinctive subject/scene information. Targets the growing memory cost of sliding-window attention in long-horizon video generation.
  • 🆕 Sliding-window beats linear attention
    HF Papers · 2026-08-31 · architectureDirectly compares sliding-window attention against linear attention retrofits for LLMs and finds that a well-tuned sliding window consistently matches or outperforms linear alternatives in quality while being simpler to implement. Challenges the assumption that linear attention is the default fix for quadratic scaling — a relevant data point for the architecture choice landscape.
  • 🆕 DAMP: Decay-Aware Mixed-Precision Recurrent-State Quantization
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-08-31 · kv cacheFirst study of post-training quantization for recurrent states in Gated DeltaNet and Kimi Delta Attention models — the fixed-size states that replace KV caches in most layers. These states are typically stored in FP32 and dominate inference memory; DAMP introduces decay-aware mixed precision to compress them without retraining, reducing both memory and latency.
  • 🆕 Spectral Query-Key Product Weight Steering for Training-Free VLM Hallucination Mitigation
    HF Papers · 2026-08-31 · VLMProposes QK Product Steering: a data-free, training-free weight edit that suppresses dominant singular modes in the query-key product of selected middle layers to reduce object hallucination in VLMs. Achieves zero inference-cost mitigation — the edit is applied once to the weights, with no runtime overhead.
  • 🆕 Trajectory-Level Speculative Decoding for Diffusion Language Models
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-08-31 · architectureExtends speculative decoding to diffusion-based language models (dLLMs), which generate tokens via iterative denoising rather than left-to-right autoregression. Constructs draft denoising trajectories via confidence-stratified tree search, enabling parallel verification of multi-token updates — addressing the throughput bottleneck where existing dLLM strategies collapse to single-token generation.
  • 🆕 HyQuant: Hybrid-Precision Quantization for LLM Attention
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-08-31 · architectureProposes a hybrid quantization framework for attention that retains a small fraction of states in higher precision while quantizing the rest to very low bit-widths. Unlike smoothing-based approaches for handling outliers, HyQuant's design better balances accuracy and efficiency at sub-4-bit attention quantization where existing methods degrade.
