  • 🆕 SemKV: Semantic Mixed-Precision KV Cache Quantization Guided by the Quality Cliff
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-09-02 · kv cacheIdentifies a quality cliff in KV cache quantization: Llama-3.1-8B is statistically indistinguishable from FP16 down to 2.322 bits/value but collapses at 2.0 bits. SemKV ranks tokens by a model-internal importance score and assigns two adjacent above-cliff precisions, achieving 6.0x storage reduction with no detectable quality loss (n=900, 3 seeds). Swapping to TurboQuant-MSE pushes the operating point to 7.9x.
  • 🆕 Locked at the Entrance, Open Inside: Where RLVR Narrows the Solution Space
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-09-02 · test-timeShows that RLVR improves pass@1 but contracts the solution space by up to 67%, and this contraction is concentrated at the entrance — per-token likelihood shifts are 11-16x larger before the first operation than during downstream reasoning. Supplying an unselected entrance prefix restores completion rates by >10x (0.018 to 0.212). Late-layer parameter interpolation with early checkpoints increases solution coverage by 37% at no pass@1 cost. Tested on Qwen2.5-3B/3B-Instruct.
  • 🆕 Test-Time Scaling for Scientific Equation Discovery
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-09-02 · test-timeStudies test-time scaling for open-ended equation discovery (not closed-ended math/code). Formulates LLM-driven symbolic regression as an iterative search unifying Best-of-N, refinement, tree search, and evolution methods. Key finding: search width is the dominant allocation parameter — increasing width with budget improves both quality and wall-clock efficiency via parallelism. Evaluated on LLM-SRBench.
  • 🆕 The Hallucination Signal Is a Mean Shift: Why Simple Probes Suffice
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-09-02 · fundamentalAcross three 7B models, finds that the hallucination detection signal in hidden states is dominated by a single mean-shift component — removing this direction collapses detection to chance. A simple L2-regularized logistic regression (0.952 AUROC) matches or beats 12 more complex architectures. LayerMix aggregation across a contiguous layer band matches oracle-layer performance without oracle access.
