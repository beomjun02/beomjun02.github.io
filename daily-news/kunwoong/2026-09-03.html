    HF papers · 2026-09-03 · MoEStudies looping on MoE Transformers while closely matching per-token FLOPs, total parameters, and KV cache — avoiding the confound of extra compute that plagues most looped-architecture comparisons.   • 🆕 HBQ: Hierarchical Scaling Block Quantization with Hardware-Efficiency-Aware Design
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-09-03 · kv cacheProposes hierarchical block quantization covering weights, activations, and KV cache on a unified datapath.   • 🆕 Faster Than Flash: Exploiting Attention Sparsity for Efficient Long-Context Decoding
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-09-03A hardware-algorithm co-design that fuses block selection and attention computation into a single kernel, replacing external metadata with content-aware scanning via low-bit quantization. The top-delta strategy dynamically filters blocks for distribution-adaptive sparsity. Training-free and plug-and-play: up to 11.6× kernel-level speedup, scales to 256K context with 2.37× end-to-end throughput. Accuracy preserved on RULER and LongBench.
  • SemKV: Semantic Mixed-Precision KV Cache Quantization Guided by the Quality Cliff
    arXiv · 2026-09-02 · kv cacheIdentifies a quality cliff in KV cache quantization: Llama-3.1-8B is statistically indistinguishable from FP16 down to 2.322 bits/value but collapses at 2.0 bits. SemKV ranks tokens by a model-internal importance score and assigns two adjacent above-cliff precisions, achieving 6.0x storage reduction with no detectable quality loss (n=900, 3 seeds). Swapping to TurboQuant-MSE pushes the operating point to 7.9x.
  • 🆕 Vision Is Not Overhead: One-Pass Block Drafting for Lossless Speculative Decoding in VLMs
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-09-03 · VLMPresents GLANCE, the first one-pass block drafter that is lossless on an unmodified VLM target. Breaks the self-defeating cycle of VLM speculative decoding where small drafters must compress vision: a block-diffusion head reads the target's already-fused vision-language state, making vision cost nothing, and fills a whole block in one forward pass. A wide candidate tree is verified in one target pass, reproducing greedy decoding exactly.

  • 🆕 Good Memory Has ECC: Evaluating the Memory of Vision-Language Models Beyond Accuracy
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-09-03 · VLMIntroduces ECCBench, measuring VLM memory across three axes: efficiency (FLOPs per answer), compression (accuracy gain on compressible inputs), and calibration (abstention under uncertainty).
