Papers

  • 🆕 VestigeKV: KV Cache Eviction via the Vestigial Branch in NoPE-MLA
    Long-lived KV caches must be compressed before future queries arrive, so standard attention-based eviction (H2O, SnapKV) collapses on NoPE-MLA models. VestigeKV exploits the 64-dimensional decoupled branch — a vestige of RoPE that NoPE training repurposes into a query-independent salience channel — to evict entries by reading only 11% of each KV row. Evaluated on Kimi Linear, it recovers near-full accuracy under aggressive cache budgets without retraining.
  • 🆕 Speculative Macro Commit for Faster Tool-Using Agents
    Tool-using LLM agents burn wall-clock time on serial action–observation turns. Speculative Macro Commit (SMC) introduces a two-tier runtime: a large authoritative actor produces the official trajectory while a fast speculative drafter continuously predicts and executes future tool chains on an isolated environment copy. When the actor validates the draft, committed results are absorbed in bulk — cutting latency without sacrificing correctness.
  • 🆕 Budget-Aware Compression Pipeline for Single-GPU 70B Inference
    Casts single-GPU deployment of 70B-parameter LLMs as a budget-aware design problem over memory, throughput, and integration cost. Controlled ablations reveal that layer-wise pruning makes weight quantization more robust, and KV-cache sparsification complements INT8 KV quantization — compression techniques interact non-trivially, so order and coupling matter.
  • 🆕 SafeRI: On-Demand Token-Level Safety Intervention for Vision-Language Models
    Existing VLM safety alignment modifies model behavior globally, perturbing the reasoning path even on safe generations. SafeRI reframes alignment as on-demand intervention: it recognizes unsafe tokens at decode time and activates safety parameters only when needed, preserving general multimodal capability on benign inputs while catching harmful outputs.
  • 🆕 Value-Preserving Architectures for Agentic AI Systems
    Proposes an architectural framework for LLM-based multi-agent systems that embeds privacy, fairness, and safety constraints directly into the system design. Argues that as agents automate complex socio-technical workflows, responsible software engineering must treat human-centered values as first-class design requirements alongside functional correctness.
  • 🆕 Expert-Aware Causal Tracing of Factual Recall in Sparse MoE
    Standard activation patching identifies which MoE block restores a corrupted factual prediction, but cannot tell whether recovery depends on a single expert or the routed set. Introduces expert-level causal tracing: corrupting subject-token embeddings, restoring block outputs, then restoring individual expert contributions — revealing whether factual knowledge in sparse MoE models localizes to specific experts.
  • 🆕 Select, Compress, Reinvest: Visual-Token Allocation in Long-Video MLLMs
    Long-video language models can afford only a small slice of 3,600+ frames per hour. This controlled study isolates each design decision — frame selection, spatial compression, resolution policy — by varying one at a time. Finds that visual-token allocation is not merely a preprocessing detail: the choice of which frames survive and how tokens are compressed significantly impacts downstream QA performance.
