  • 🆕 Extremely Sparse Supervision Incentivizes Reasoning Ability
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-09-09 · fundamentalUsing the Qwen3 family, shows that reasoning can be effectively incentivized by as few as one or two tokens per reasoning trajectory — just 0.05% of all tokens. This sparse supervision matches or surpasses full-token training across nine teacher–student configurations, coding tasks, Llama models, and PPO-based RLVR. Challenges the assumption that effective post-training must be token-intensive.
  • 🆕 Towards Understanding Pause Token Fine-Tuning Dynamics: A Mode Retention Perspective
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-09-09 · test-timeRecasts pause tokens as a training-dynamics intervention on the retention–adaptation trade-off, not just an inference-time computation device. Masked Boundary Pause (MBP) — pauses at reasoning-step boundaries with loss masked — achieves up to +6 points on math and +2.5 on code across 1B–8B Qwen and Llama models while preserving general understanding. Gains extend to GRPO.
  • 🆕 Don't Drop Dropout: Optimizing Layer Sparsity for Efficient LLM Training and Inference
