  • 🆕 CONDUIT: A Unified Residual-Stream Restoration Framework for KV Cache Reuse in Vision-Language Models
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-09-10 · kv cacheA training-free refresh policy that unifies single- and multi-image KV cache reuse as residual-stream restoration. Ranks cached visual tokens via norm-weighted attention and a cached-value-norm proxy, then applies image-level relevance amplification before global selection. At a 10% refresh budget, achieves 97.0–99.5% of full-prefill quality across three VLM backbones and delivers a 2.99x time-to-first-token speedup on MMLongBench-Doc.
  • 🆕 ACE: Adapter Consolidation across Experts for Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning of MoE LLMs
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-09-10 · MoEShows that expert-wise LoRA adapters in MoE models become functionally similar during fine-tuning, fragmenting capacity across narrow updates with sparse, imbalanced gradients. Proposes consolidating adapters across subsets of experts — reducing the number of independent adapters while preserving or improving task performance, and trading many small GEMMs for fewer efficient ones.
  • 🆕 Beyond Retraining-Free MoE Compression: A Cost-Normalized Study of Post-Compression Adjustment
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-09-10 · MoEArgues that compressed MoE checkpoints are better understood as compressed initializations that benefit from a tiny post-compression adjustment stage. Across two MoE backbones, four pruning/merging methods, three expert-retention ratios, and 28 benchmarks, finds that even a small fine-tuning or KD stage after compression substantially recovers lost quality — a practical finding for MoE deployment.
  • 🆕 RAPTOR: Role-Aware Private Training for Mixture-of-Experts
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-09-10 · MoEIdentifies three failure modes when DP fine-tuning treats MoE as a single dense block: global clipping suppresses expert gradients, batch normalization dilutes sparse updates, and fixed noise degrades low-load experts. RAPTOR alternates shared/expert optimization with expert-specific clipping and noise, proves parallel privacy composition across experts. Tested on Switch Transformer, OLMoE, and DeepSeek-VL2-Tiny with gains at the tightest privacy budgets.
  • 🆕 Kalman Delta Networks: Uncertainty-aware Associative Memory
    HF papers · 2026-09-09 · architectureExtends delta-rule linear attention with per-slot Kalman-style uncertainty tracking: each write adapts its strength based on accumulated evidence for that memory slot, preventing catastrophic overwrite of well-established associations. Addresses a fundamental limitation of fixed-size recurrent memory in frontier language models — the inability to reason about confidence when deciding how strongly to update stored state.

  • 🆕 DFlow: Enabling Verifier Information Flow in Block Diffusion Speculative Decoding
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-09-10Existing block-diffusion speculative decoding discards rejected suffixes, wasting the computation spent on those positions. DFlow enables verifier information to flow from rejected positions into subsequent drafting rounds, so the drafter no longer reconstructs representations from scratch. Improves acceptance rates and effective throughput over discard-and-redraft baselines.
  • 🆕 Some Tokens Behave like Magnets: Revealing Linguistic Organization in the Layers of Language Models
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-09-10 · fundamentalIdentifies &quot;magnetic vectors&quot; inside LLMs — tokens that attract or repel their neighbors, organizing geometric structure layer by layer. Function words consistently act as repelling magnets in early layers. Removing them devastates syntactic tasks (POS accuracy 91% → &lt;10%) while sparing semantic ones, and vice versa for late-layer attracting magnets. First probe-free path to understanding how LLMs geometrically organize linguistic computation.
  • 🆕 Deep belief networks are exact
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-09-10 · fundamentalProves that every strictly positive probability distribution on {-1,1}^n is represented exactly by a sigmoid belief network with finite parameters — answering a question of Sutskever and Hinton. The proof upgrades their probability-sharing approximation to exact representation using Brouwer&#x27;s fixed-point theorem. A clean, fundamental result in deep generative model theory.
  • 🆕 CoVeR: Coverage-Based Token Pruning for Multi-View 3D Reasoning in VLMs
    HF papers · 2026-09-09 · VLMReframes visual token pruning for multi-view VLMs from importance-based selection to coverage optimization: every pruned token must have a close surviving representative. Addresses the spatial redundancy unique to multi-view 3D reasoning, where learned importance methods keep near-duplicate tokens across views. Training-free, works on frozen VLMs and reduces visual token cost as views scale.
