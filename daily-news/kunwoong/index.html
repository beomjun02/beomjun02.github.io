  • 🆕 Natively Unlearnable Large Language Models
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-16 · trustworthyIntroduces NULLs, an LLM architecture with shared backbone neurons alongside sparsely activated sinks that naturally isolate source-specific knowledge. Unlearning a source at deployment requires no gradient updates and no retained-data access — just disabling its sinks. Scales to Wikipedia’s ~6M articles and resists both adversarial extraction and relearning.

  • 🆕 Decompose Sparsely Where You Should, Absorb Densely Where You Should Not

A rank-24 bottleneck reduces dense latent count by 84% while improving sparse probing.
  • 🆕 Temporal Backtracking Search for Test-time Generative Video Reasoning
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-16 · test-time · videoShifts test-time search to the temporal axis for video generation via a generate-verify-restart loop.   • 🆕 Mirage Probes: How Vision Models Fake Visual Understanding
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-16 · VLMReveals two distinct VLM failure regimes via contrastive probes: textual biases (answering from language priors without engaging vision) and spurious images (constructing false visual content in latent space). Text-distribution cleaning addresses the first but cannot reach the second, which lives in visual representations and requires representational-level intervention.
