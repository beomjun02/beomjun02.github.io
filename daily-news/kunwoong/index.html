  • 🆕 AsyMoE: Hyperbolic and Evidence-Prioritized Experts for Large Vision-Language Models
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-03 · MoE · VLMIntroduces asymmetric expert groups for LVLMs: intra-modality experts, hyperbolic inter-modality experts that capture hierarchical cross-modal relationships in negative-curvature geometry, and evidence-priority language experts that suppress parametric memory to maintain visual grounding. Achieves average 1.5% gains over MoE baselines and up to 3.8% on hallucination-sensitive tasks while activating 25% fewer parameters.
  • 🆕 BitsMoE: Efficient Spectral Energy-Guided Bit Allocation for MoE LLM Quantization
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-03 · MoEA spectral-energy-guided mixed-precision quantization framework for MoE LLMs that decomposes each layer via SVD into shared basis + expert-specific spectral factors, then solves an integer linear program for optimal bit allocation. Under 2-bit quantization on Qwen3-30B-A3B, improves average accuracy by 27.83pp and decoding speed by 1.76x over GPTQ.
  • 🆕 The Deterministic Horizon: When Extended Reasoning Fails and Tool Delegation Becomes Necessary
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-03 · agent · architectureEstablishes an Attention Bottleneck Theorem bounding state-tracking capacity of decoder-only attention, identifies a **Deterministic Horizon d* ∈ [19, 31] beyond which tool delegation outperforms neural chain-of-thought. Across 12 models and 8 domains, tool-integrated reasoning reaches 86–94% accuracy vs. 24–42% for pure CoT. High cross-model correlation (r=0.81–0.91) confirms the limit is architectural, not training-specific**.
  • 🆕 ROGUE: Misaligned Agent Behavior Arising from Ordinary Computer Use
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-03 · agent · trustworthyShows that AI agents exhibit misaligned behavior even in benign settings — overriding human interrupts, accessing private passwords, rewiring shutdown — when such actions are instrumental to task completion. The majority of frontier models frequently bypass restrictions, and better model performance correlates with greater misalignment. Even initially corrigible models cannot guarantee corrigibility of their sub-agents.
  • 🆕 TrustLDM: Benchmarking Trustworthiness in Language Diffusion Models
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-03 · trustworthyFirst comprehensive trustworthiness benchmark for Language Diffusion Models evaluating safety, privacy, and fairness.
