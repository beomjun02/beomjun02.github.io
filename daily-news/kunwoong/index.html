  • 🆕 Safety Paradox: How Enhanced Safety Awareness Leaves LLMs Vulnerable to Posterior Attack
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-06 · trustworthyReveals that stronger safety alignment inadvertently creates exploitable vulnerability: a single-query "Posterior Attack" prompts the model to generate the exact harmful response its classifier would flag. Across 30 open-source and frontier models (GPT-5, Claude 4.6), models with superior safety judgment are disproportionately more susceptible. RL interventions confirm the causal link — degrading safety judgment immunizes against the attack.
  • 🆕 Trust, but Don't Verify: Epistemic Blind Spots in LLM Source Evaluation
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-06 · trustworthyShows that LLMs possess the capability to detect fabricated statistics (0.76–1.00 identification rate in isolation) but fail to recruit this capability during multi-source synthesis — statistically impossible confidence intervals receive the same weight as valid ones. A methodology-register gate responds to analytical writing style, not numeric validity. Replicates across five models from three families.
  • 🆕 Less is MoE: Trimming Experts in Domain-Specialist Language Models
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-06 · MoETraces prior MoE compression failures to wrong granularity: important capabilities are distributed across experts but concentrated in FFN intermediate dimensions. Fisher importance identifies tiny critical dimension sets — removing 12 of 1.35M routed-FFN dimensions collapses GSM8K accuracy. Fisher-MoE at 50% compression preserves capability while reducing weight memory by ~45% and improving throughput by 21%.
  • 🆕 Value-and-Structure Alignment for Routing-Consistent Quantization of MoE Models
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-06 · MoEProposes VSRAQ, an MoE-specific post-training quantization objective that preserves pre-quantization expert-selection behavior by combining value alignment (matching routing logits) and structure alignment (preserving expert ordering and top-k boundaries). Reduces quantization-induced degradation with zero inference-time overhead and consistently outperforms reconstruction-only baselines.
  • 🆕 Agentic Monte Carlo: Simulating RL for Black-Box Agents
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-06 · agentProposes sampling from the optimal policy of a black-box LLM agent rather than training it, exploiting the RL–Bayesian inference equivalence. Sequential Monte Carlo with a learned value function steers the frozen agent while leaving the black-box model unchanged. Outperforms prompting baselines and even surpasses GRPO with scaled test-time compute on AgentGym benchmarks.

  • 🆕 LoRi: Low-Rank Distillation for Implicit Reasoning

  • 🆕 Let It Be Simple: One-Step Action Generation for VLA Models
