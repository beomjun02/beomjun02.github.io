  • 🆕 DART-SD: Diamond-topology Aware Retrieval and Tuning for Self-Distillation of Multi-Turn Tool-Calling Agents
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-08-21 · tool use · function callingAddresses a key bottleneck in training multi-turn tool-calling agents: the reliance on full-length expert trajectories that don’t cover the diamond-shaped topology of real tool-call graphs (branching paths, merging outcomes). DART-SD retrieves and composes trajectory fragments matching diamond substructures, then self-distills the agent on these synthetic multi-turn episodes — improving multi-turn tool-calling without expensive expert data.

  • 🆕 Bounded Agents: Delegation Security for Multi-Agent AI Systems
