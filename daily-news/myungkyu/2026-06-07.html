  • PiL-World: A Chunk-Wise World Model for VLA Policy-in-the-Loop Evaluation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-07 · Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models · World Models for RoboticsA chunk-wise world model enabling closed-loop VLA evaluation without real robot execution by alternating between VLA inference and multi-view future observation generation.   • 🆕 Flash-WAM: Modality-Aware Distillation for World Action Models
