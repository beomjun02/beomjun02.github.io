  • 🆕 DREAM-Chunk: Reactive Action Chunking with Latent World Model
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-18 · World Models for Robotics · Robot Policy ArchitecturesA test-time scaling method that augments chunking-based VLA policies with a lightweight latent world model — no additional policy fine-tuning required.
