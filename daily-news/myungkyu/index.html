    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-20 · Vision-Language-Action (VLA) ModelsFirst general framework for end-to-end SO(2)-equivariant VLA models, applicable to any architecture coupling a frozen vision-language backbone with a flow-matching Diffusion Transformer.   • 🆕 Tri-Info: Generalizable, Interpretable Failure Prediction for VLA Models via Information Theory
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-20 · Vision-Language-Action (VLA) ModelsFormalizes VLA control as a closed-loop information pipeline and derives Triple Information-theoretic signals that distinguish successful and failed rollouts.   • 🆕 HumanScale: Egocentric Human Video Can Outperform Real-Robot Data for Embodied Pretraining
    HF papers · 2026-06-20 · Robot Foundation ModelsShows that egocentric human video can outperform teleoperated real-robot trajectories as a pretraining source for embodied foundation models, despite lacking precise action supervision and embodiment alignment.   • 🆕 ImageWAM: Do World Action Models Really Need Video Generation, or Just Image Editing?
    HF papers · 2026-06-20 · World Models for RoboticsChallenges the assumption that world action models need video generation, proposing image-editing as a lighter alternative. Video-based WAMs face three coupled limitations — costly dense multi-frame tokens, wasted capacity on action-irrelevant details, and error accumulation in long-horizon imagination — which image-level prediction avoids.
