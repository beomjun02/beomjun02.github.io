  • 🆕 Your Teacher Can't Help You Here: Combating Supervision Fidelity Decay in On-Policy Distillation
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-02 · on policy rlIdentifies a critical bottleneck in on-policy distillation: as student-generated prefixes lengthen, teacher next-token distributions lose confidence and discriminative power ("Supervision Fidelity Decay"). Introduces Lookahead Group Reward, which evaluates student candidates by the teacher confidence they induce at the next step, improving mean@8 by 2.57 points over standard OPD for a 7B student.
