  • 🆕 AsyncWebRL: Efficient Multi-Step RL for Visual Web Agents
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-06 · Gui agent · on policy rlAsynchronous RL training pipeline for vision-language web agents that achieves 2.9x throughput over synchronous baselines. Identifies a normalizer bias in multi-step GRPO that down-weights gradient on failed (longer) trajectories, causing verbose outputs; replacing the per-trajectory normalizer with a constant fixes this and sets new SOTA on WebGym OOD test split (+5.8% relative, +42% on Medium, +48% on Hard).
  • 🆕 OPRD: On-Policy Representation Distillation
    HF Daily · 2026-06-05 · on policy rl · LLMFirst method to lift on-policy distillation from output-space (log-probability matching) into hidden-state space. OPRD aligns student intermediate representations with the teacher's across selected layers on the same on-policy rollouts, providing dense, deterministic supervision that bypasses the LM head entirely. Closes the student-teacher gap on AIME 2024/2025 and AIMO where output-space baselines plateau, while training 1.44x faster and using up to 54% less memory.
  • 🆕 Trust-Region Behavior Blending for On-Policy Distillation
    HF Weekly · 2026-06-02 · on policy rlAddresses the cold-start problem in on-policy distillation where early student rollouts are poor, placing teacher supervision on weak prefixes. TRB replaces the early rollout policy with the closest-to-teacher behavior policy inside a student-centered KL trust region, annealing back to pure student rollouts after warmup. Achieves strongest average across compared methods on two math-reasoning distillation settings.
  • 🆕 Policy-Conditioned Counterfactual Credit for Verifiable RL of Long-Horizon Language Agents
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-06 · on policy rl · LLMConstrained policy-gradient algorithm (CVT-RL) with dense verifiable rewards and a counterfactual contribution estimator for long-horizon language agents. Uses deletion, semantic substitution, and tool-output perturbation as controlled interventions to estimate step-level causal credit. Improves task success from 75.4% to 78.9% and reduces measured reward hacking from 7.2% to 3.9% across QA, ALFWorld, ScienceWorld, and web tasks.
  • 🆕 Selective-Advantage Entropy-Adaptive Horizon GRPO
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-06 · on policy rl · LLMTwo extensions to GRPO: Adaptive-Horizon GRPO weights each token's policy gradient using cumulative entropy-based discount that reduces effective horizon under uncertainty, and Selective-Advantage AH-GRPO applies this discounting only to negative-advantage rollouts, leaving successful trajectories unattenuated. On Qwen 2.5-3B, achieves Pass@1=0.858 on GSM8K with 3.6x reduced training variance relative to standard GRPO.
