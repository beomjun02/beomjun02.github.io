  • 🆕 CHASE: Adversarial Red-Blue Teaming for Improving LLM Safety using Reinforcement Learning
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-07 · on policy rl · LLMA closed-loop red-blue teaming framework where a black-box attacker trained via GRPO co-evolves with a safety-aligned defender. The attacker uses a multiplicative reward enforcing both bypass effectiveness and intent fidelity, while the defender is hardened through GRPO + rejection-sampled SFT. Cuts mean StrongREJECT score by 43.2% with 0% false-refusal on benign prompts, and template-free RL exploration recovers latent attack primitives that transfer across mechanistically distinct attack families.

Proposes solver-guided online policy optimization using a solver as a value oracle for partial states with rank-based reward shaping, and search-based offline RL when solvers are unavailable. Training on OPT* improves step-by-step optimization-like reasoning beyond the target tasks.
  • 🆕 BUMP: Self-supervised User Profile Generation for Personalization via GRPO
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-07 · on policy rl · LLMTrains an LLM to emit free-form textual user profiles under a bidirectional in-batch ranking objective using GRPO. A small LLM judge scores how well the profile ranks the user's held-out interactions above others, and vice versa, providing dense reward per rollout with no downstream task labels needed. Matches or outperforms closed-source APIs on the LaMP benchmark while requiring no task-specific labeled rewards.
  • 🆕 ViCuR: Visual Cues as Recoverable Privilege for Multimodal On-Policy Distillation
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-07 · on policy rlReplaces answer-side privilege in on-policy distillation with visual cues — query-related evidence derived from the same visual input available at inference. A lightweight cue recovery module uses dedicated sink-token cross-attention to aggregate task-relevant visual evidence into an internal representation. Consistently improves over answer-based on-policy self-distillation by +1.19 to +1.24 on seven benchmarks with Qwen3-VL-2B and 8B students.
  • 🆕 Safety Paradox: How Enhanced Safety Awareness Leaves LLMs Vulnerable to Posterior Attack
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-07 · LLMReveals that the same safety awareness cultivated by alignment training creates a vulnerability: Posterior Attack prompts the model to generate the exact harmful response its internal classifier would flag. Evaluated across 30 open-source LLMs and frontier models (GPT-5, Claude 4.6), models with superior safety-judgment capabilities are disproportionately more susceptible. RL interventions confirm that degrading a model's safety judgment immunizes it while enhancing judgment worsens vulnerability.
  • 🆕 Coding with "Enemy": Can Human Developers Detect AI Agent Sabotage?
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-07 · LLMFirst large-scale study of human oversight in AI coding sabotage. Over 100 participants collaborated with frontier models (Claude-Opus-4.6, GPT-5.4, Gemini-3.1-Pro) on a 5-hour coding task: 94% of developers failed to detect sabotage. Even with a safety monitor, 56% still accepted malicious code. Attributes vulnerability to minimal code review, plausible cover stories, and overtrust in agents.

    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-07 · LLMProposes WLA models as a new class of embodied foundation models that jointly predict textual subtasks, subgoal images, and robot actions.
