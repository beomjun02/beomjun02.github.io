  • 🆕 DRPO: Rethinking the Divergence Regularization in LLM RL
    arXiv · 2026-06-14 · LLMIdentifies that importance-ratio clipping (PPO/GRPO) is a poor trust-region proxy in long-tailed LLM vocabularies, and that DPPO's hard divergence mask discards useful gradient signal. Proposes DRPO, which replaces the hard mask with a smooth advantage-weighted quadratic regularizer on policy shift, producing bounded continuous gradient weights. Improves both stability and efficiency across model scales and architectures.
  • 🆕 N-GRPO: Embedding-Level Neighbor Mixing for Enhanced Policy Optimization
    arXiv · 2026-06-14 · LLMAddresses the exploration vs. coherence tradeoff in GRPO rollouts: token-level sampling yields redundant rephrasings, while embedding noise breaks semantics. Proposes Semantic Neighbor Mixing that blends each token's embedding with its nearest semantic neighbors, staying on the local manifold. Consistent gains over baselines on math reasoning benchmarks with robust OOD generalization.
  • 🆕 SWITCH: Demystifying Hidden-State Recurrence with On-Policy Reinforcement Learning
    arXiv · 2026-06-14 · on policy rlIntroduces explicit boundary tokens to mark entry/exit of latent chain-of-thought blocks, making hidden-state recurrence compatible with standard on-policy RL (GRPO).   • 🆕 SG-OPD: Sign-Gated On-Policy Distillation
    arXiv · 2026-06-14 · on policy rlIdentifies two failure modes in on-policy distillation: trajectory misalignment between student and teacher, and unreliable per-token teacher preferences. Uses a binary verifier as a trust signal at two granularities — phased teacher sampling at cold-start and a sign-consistency gate that extrapolates where teacher and verifier agree and interpolates where they disagree. Achieves +7.50 per-question gains on competition-level math.
