  • SWITCH: Demystifying Hidden-State Recurrence with On-Policy Reinforcement Learning
    HF papers · 2026-06-14 · on policy rlIntroduces explicit boundary tokens to mark entry/exit of latent chain-of-thought blocks, making hidden-state recurrence compatible with standard on-policy RL (GRPO).
