  • 🆕 VISTA: View-Consistent Self-Verified Training for GUI Grounding
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-16 · Gui agent · on policy rlAddresses a key failure mode of GRPO for GUI grounding: rollout groups from a single screenshot often degenerate to all-success or all-failure, killing the relative advantage signal. VISTA constructs comparison groups from multiple target-preserving views of the same GUI, raising Qwen3-VL 4B/8B/30B on ScreenSpot-Pro by +7.9/+13.1/+13.3 points.
  • 🆕 GAGPO: Generalized Advantage Grouped Policy Optimization
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-16 · on policy rlCritic-free RL method for multi-turn LLM agents that constructs a non-parametric grouped value proxy from rollouts and computes TD/GAE-style temporal advantages, recursively propagating outcome supervision backward through each turn. Outperforms baselines on ALFWorld and WebShop with faster convergence and smoother optimization dynamics.
  • 🆕 CacheRL: Multi-Turn Tool-Calling Agents via Cached Rollouts and Hybrid Reward
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-16 · on policy rl · LLMTrains small agent models (Qwen3-4B) to 92% process accuracy on multi-step tool-calling tasks, approaching GPT-5's 94% at 100x less compute. Key innovation: a three-tier fuzzy cache eliminates live tool execution during GRPO training, with cache-tier-aware rewards that dynamically adjust for cache-induced limitations.
  • 🆕 Closing the Reflection Gap: A Free Calibration Bonus for Agentic RL
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-16 · on policy rlLLM agents mis-assess their own outputs even after observing concrete environment feedback. RefGRPO augments standard RL with a free calibration bonus computed by contrasting the agent's self-reflection with actual outcomes — no extra reward model needed. Reduces underconfidence from 44.4% to 7.7% while improving task accuracy on text-to-SQL.
  • 🆕 CORA: Consistency-Oriented Reasoning Alignment via GRPO
