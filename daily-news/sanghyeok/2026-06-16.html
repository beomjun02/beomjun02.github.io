  • 🆕 APPO: Agentic Procedural Policy Optimization
    HF papers · 2026-06-16 · on policy rlMoves branching and credit assignment in agentic RL to fine-grained decision points rather than coarse tool-call boundaries. A Branching Score combines token uncertainty with policy-induced likelihood gains, and procedure-level advantage scaling distributes credit across rollouts. Improves strong baselines by ~4 points across 13 benchmarks.
