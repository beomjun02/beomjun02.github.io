  • 🆕 Zone of Proximal Policy Optimization: Teacher in Prompts, Not Gradients
    arXiv · 2026-06-18 · on policy rlInspired by Vygotsky's zone of proximal development, ZPPO keeps the teacher inside the prompt rather than the policy gradient. For hard questions, it constructs binary and negative candidate prompts from teacher/student outputs, with a prompt replay buffer that recirculates until graduation. On Qwen3.5 (0.8B–9B students, 27B teacher), ZPPO outperforms off/on-policy distillation and GRPO across a 31-benchmark suite spanning VLM, LLM, and video tasks.
  • 🆕 LoopCoder-v2: Only Loop Once for Efficient Test-Time Computation Scaling
    arXiv · 2026-06-18 · LLMA family of 7B Parallel Loop Transformer coders trained on 18T tokens. The two-loop variant improves SWE-bench Verified from 43.0 to 64.4 and Multi-SWE from 14.0 to 31.0, but three or more loops regress — revealing a strongly non-monotonic loop-count effect driven by the tradeoff between refinement gains and positional mismatch costs.
  • 🆕 OPD-Evolver: Cultivating Holistic Agent Evolver via On-Policy Distillation
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-18 · on policy rlA slow-fast co-evolution framework for self-evolving agents via on-policy self-distillation. Unlike existing memory agents that store trajectories or accumulate skills in isolation, OPD-Evolver cultivates holistic competence — selecting useful experience, acting on it, writing reusable knowledge, and maintaining a growing repository — through an integrated distillation loop.
  • 🆕 Dynamic Rollout Editing for Reducing Overthinking in RL-Trained Reasoning Models
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-18 · on policy rlTackles overthinking in GRPO-trained reasoning LLMs as a training-time credit-assignment problem rather than a decoding-time stopping problem. Observes that correct answers often emerge early in rollouts but models continue generating unnecessary reasoning. Proposes dynamic rollout editing that truncates training trajectories at the point of correctness, improving both efficiency and final performance.
  • 🆕 Beyond Domains: Reusing Web Skills via Transferable Interaction Patterns
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-18 · Gui agentLLM web agents typically emit one low-level action per turn, causing horizons and costs to grow quickly. This work wraps repeated interaction fragments as transferable web skills — callable tools built from successful trajectories — so one high-level skill call replaces many primitive actions, and skills generalize across domains that share interaction patterns.
