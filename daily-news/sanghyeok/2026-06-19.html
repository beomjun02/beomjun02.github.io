  • 🆕 Skill-Guided Continuation Distillation for GUI Agents
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-19 · Gui agentIntroduces SGCD, an iterative self-improvement framework that lets a GUI agent run unguided to reach realistic off-trajectory states, then uses skill-guided policy (continuation plans, critical targets, failure traps) to complete tasks from those states. Improves OSWorld-Verified success rate from the low-30% range to over 50% across three base models.
  • 🆕 VISUALSKILL: Multimodal Skills for Computer-Use Agents
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-19 · Gui agentProposes a hierarchical multimodal skill library for computer-use agents, organized as a central index over per-topic files with both text and visual figures, delivered via an MCP tool. A Claude Opus 4.6 agent with VISUALSKILL achieves +15.3 point absolute lift over no-skill baseline and +8.3 over text-only skills on CUA-World and OSExpert-Eval.
  • 🆕 Trust the Right Teacher: Quality-Aware Self-Distillation for GUI Grounding
    arXiv · 2026-06-19 · Gui agentAddresses on-policy self-distillation for GUI grounding where VLMs must predict precise screen coordinates in high-res screenshots. Combines a soft correctness-aware gate (checking if the teacher's coordinate prediction can still reach the ground-truth box) with teacher-probability scaling. Neither component alone helps, but combining them consistently improves across six GUI grounding benchmarks.
  • 🆕 Beyond Reward Engineering: A Data Recipe for Long-Context Reinforcement Learning
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-19 · on policy rl · LLMTakes a data-centric approach to LLM RL, showing that a curated recipe of retrieval, multi-evidence synthesis, and reasoning tasks (~14K examples) paired with minimal GRPO setup is enough to substantially boost long-context reasoning. Reports +7.2/+3.2/+6.4 average gains on Qwen3-4B/8B/30B-A3B across seven benchmarks, with gains transferring to agentic tasks (+4.8 on agent-tuned model, +7.0 on BrowseComp).
  • 🆕 STARE: Surprisal-Guided Token-Level Advantage Reweighting for Policy Entropy Stability
    arXiv · 2026-06-19 · on policy rl · LLMIdentifies a token-level credit assignment mismatch causing entropy collapse in GRPO-based LLM RL. Proposes surprisal-quantile-based advantage reweighting with a target-entropy closed-loop gate. Outperforms DAPO and competitive baselines by 4–8% accuracy on AIME24/AIME25 across 1.5B–32B model scales, maintaining stable training over thousands of steps.
  • 🆕 RODS: Reward-Driven Online Data Synthesis for Multi-Turn Tool-Use Agents
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-19 · on policy rlAddresses sample depletion in multi-turn tool-use RL by using reward variance as a zero-cost boundary detector to find the policy's capability frontier. Synthesizes new multi-turn variants matching frontier structural complexity via skill-aligned resampling. Starting from 400 human seeds, matches a 17K-sample offline pipeline with ~20x fewer trajectories.
  • 🆕 EfficientRollout: System-Aware Self-Speculative Decoding for RL Rollouts
    arXiv · 2026-06-19 · on policy rl · LLMTackles the rollout latency bottleneck in LLM RL post-training with a quantized self-speculative drafter that stays coupled to the evolving policy without separate pretraining. Features a system-aware toggle policy and acceptance-aware draft-length adaptation. Reduces rollout latency by up to 19.6% and end-to-end latency by 12.7% while maintaining model quality.
  • 🆕 Object-Centric Residual RL for Zero-Shot Sim-to-Real VLA Enhancement
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-19 · on policy rlProposes an object-centric residual RL framework that corrects VLA actions using object poses, creating a compact observation space that transfers consistently between sim and real. Trained only in simulation with pose noise and dropout, the residual policy transfers zero-shot to a Franka Research 3, improving success rate from 42% to 76% across five manipulation tasks.
  • 🆕 Beyond the Current Observation: Evaluating MLLMs in Controllable Non-Markov Games
    arXiv · 2026-06-19 · LLMIntroduces RNG-Bench, a benchmark isolating multimodal LLMs' ability to recall past observations during multi-step interaction via Matching Pairs and 3D Maze games. Hardest settings require ~128K tokens and 350 images per episode. Analysis reveals most errors stem from forgetting earlier observations, not poor action selection. Fine-tuning Qwen3.5-9B on optimal-policy rollouts transfers to other benchmarks.

  • 🆕 From Bounding Boxes to Visual Reasoning: An On-Policy Data Annotation Tool for Vision-Language Models
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-19 · on policy rlIntroduces ScreenAnnotator, an open-source annotation tool with a unified annotation atom schema, an on-policy annotation loop featuring a Bayesian Annotation Verifier achieving ~100% accept rate on flowcharts and 77% on GUI screenshots, and template-driven multi-task data synthesis. Fine-tuning on the flowchart scenario yields 76.1% average accuracy (+35.1 point gain).
