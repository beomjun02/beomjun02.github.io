  • 🆕 SAGE-OPD: Selective Agent-Guided Intervention for Multi-Turn On-Policy Distillation
    arXiv · 2026-06-20 · on policy rl · LLMA verifier-free selective intervention framework for multi-turn on-policy distillation of LLM agents. Instead of applying teacher supervision uniformly, SAGE-OPD uses environment feedback to decide which student responses to intervene on, and weights token-level distillation by teacher confidence.   • 🆕 Beyond Entropy: Learning from Token-Level Distributional Deviations for LLM Reasoning
    arXiv · 2026-06-20 · on policy rl · LLMIntroduces the ICT framework that shifts RL optimization from scalar entropy to distributional properties of token logits. Uses Jensen-Shannon divergence to identify critical branching tokens, then selectively updates only the top 10% of unique tokens. On Qwen2.5 (0.5B–7B), yields an average pass@4 improvement of 4.58% over GRPO and DAPO baselines across seven benchmarks.

  • 🆕 Playful Agentic Robot Learning
    arXiv · 2026-06-20 · LLMIntroduces RATs (Robotics Agent Teams) that use self-directed play as a continual skill-learning stage before downstream tasks.
