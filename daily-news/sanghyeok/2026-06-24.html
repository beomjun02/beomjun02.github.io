  • 🆕 A-Evolve-Training: Autonomous Post-Training of a 30B Model
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-24NVIDIA reports an autonomous system that post-trains a 30B Nemotron through four RL rounds with no human intervention, scoring 0.86 vs. the top human submission's 0.87 on the Nemotron-Reasoning Challenge (8th of ~4,000).   • 🆕 SafeDojo: Safe Reinforcement Learning for VLA via Interactive World Model
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-24First model-based safe RL framework for VLA policies that learns safe actions through world-model-based imagination. A ResNet success classifier and a lightweight safety head estimate per-step task progress and safety costs from imagined frames, balanced through a Lagrangian-based constrained GRPO objective. Best safe-success rate on SafeLIBERO with +8.25 pp over the strongest baseline.
  • 🆕 When Do Intrinsic Rewards Work for Code Reasoning?
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-24Systematic study of certainty-based intrinsic reward methods (RLIF) for code generation without ground-truth verifiers. Reveals that intrinsic rewards yield early gains but inevitably collapse — models shorten outputs and lose reasoning capability — with collapse speed sensitive to sample size and temperature. RLIF pre-training offers no significant improvement when used to initialize RLVR training.
