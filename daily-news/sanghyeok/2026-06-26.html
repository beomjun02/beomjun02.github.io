  • 🆕 BiPACE: Bisimulation-Guided Policy Optimization with Action Counterfactual Estimation for LLM Agents
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-26 · LLMExposes a state-action credit mismatch in stepwise group-based RL for long-horizon LLM agents: observation-hash partitions are too fine on the state side, creating singleton groups with zero variance. BiPACE uses bisimulation to cluster behaviorally equivalent states and counterfactual estimation to isolate action-level contributions.
  • 🆕 OPERA: Aligning Open-Ended Reasoning via Objective Perplexity-based Reinforcement Learning
