  • 🆕 Qwen-Image-2.0-RL Technical Report
    arXiv cs.CV · 2026-06-30 · on policy rlAlibaba applies GRPO-based RL and on-policy distillation to improve the Qwen-Image-2.0 diffusion model's visual quality and instruction-following. Task-specific composite reward models are built by fine-tuning VLMs with pointwise scoring and chain-of-thought reasoning. A hybrid CFG strategy preserves pre-trained knowledge during RL. The pipeline merges T2I and editing specialists via on-policy distillation into a single model, gaining +2.61 overall score and +78/+93 Elo in text-to-image/editing arenas.
  • 🆕 ATOD: Annealed Turn-aware On-policy Distillation for Multi-turn Autonomous Agents
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-30 · on policy rlProposes a hybrid online distillation algorithm that combines on-policy distillation (OPD) and RL with an annealing schedule: OPD dominates early to reach teacher-level behavior, then RL gradually strengthens for reward-based exploration. Turn-level disagreement-uncertainty reweighting amplifies high-utility turns in long trajectories. Outperforms OPD by +3.03 and GRPO by +23.62 success rate points on ALFWorld, WebShop, and Search-QA.
  • 🆕 Tandem Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-30 · on policy rlIntroduces tandem training into RLVR: a trained senior model and a frozen weaker junior alternate stochastically to co-generate reasoning chains, then both are rewarded as a team. Training Qwen3-4B on competition math, TRL matches vanilla GRPO on solo capability while producing chains that are more robust, less distribution-drifted, and more legible to the junior — addressing the compatibility problem where RLVR drifts reasoning toward idiosyncratic patterns.
  • 🆕 Internalizing the Future: A Unified Agentic Training Paradigm for World Model Planning
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-06-30 · LLMTrains a single autoregressive LLM to verbalize both prospective state rollouts and plan-conditioned success estimates — a textual analogue of Q-values for look-ahead planning. Identifies a format-capability gap: simply fine-tuning on look-ahead traces leads to superficial mimicry. A three-stage pipeline (World Model Mid-Training → Format-Eliciting SFT → Foresight-Conditioned RL) grounds the internal world model and consistently outperforms baselines on search and math reasoning tasks.
  • 🆕 Supersede: Diagnosing and Training the Memory-Update Gap in LLM Agents
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-30 · LLMIsolates temporal fact-currency as a distinct, unsolved failure mode in LLM agents: replacing full context with self-maintained memory drops accuracy from 92% to 77% on a frontier model, and the gap persists across model scale. Releases an open RL environment where agents are rewarded for using current facts and penalized for stale ones. GRPO fine-tuning Qwen2.5-3B nearly doubles supersession accuracy (9.0% → 16.7%), the first evidence this gap is trainable.
  • 🆕 S²-VLA: State-Space Guided Vision-Language-Action Models for Long-Horizon Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-30 · LLMIntroduces a State-Space Guided Adaptive Attention mechanism for VLA models that maintains a belief state tracking task progression and generates dynamic gating weights to adaptively fuse visual, language, and temporal action features. Despite its compact 2B parameter size, S²-VLA outperforms larger 7B-scale models and achieves SOTA on LIBERO and SimplerEnv long-horizon manipulation benchmarks.
  • 🆕 Ko-WideSearch: A Korean Breadth-Search Benchmark for Exhaustive Set Enumeration by Web Agents
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-30 · Gui agentIntroduces the first benchmark targeting breadth-search capabilities of web agents — exhaustively enumerating a closed set and filling per-item attributes, rather than finding one answer behind a chain of constraints. Spans 228 tables over 190 entities across 16 categories with three difficulty tiers. Across 20 web agents, all recover the set but not the full rows (Item-F1 92.8 vs Row-F1 53.7), with accuracy falling steadily as difficulty increases.
  • 🆕 Drop-Then-Recovery: How Redundant Are Vision-Language-Action Models?
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-30 · LLMStudies architectural redundancy in VLA models by removing transformer blocks and fine-tuning. Discovers a strong asymmetry: language backbones are highly redundant for standard robotic manipulation, while vision and action pathways are far less tolerant to removal. On LIBERO, removing half of the LLM blocks improves OpenVLA-OFT from 95.0% to 98.3%, suggesting current VLA benchmarks exert limited pressure on deep language grounding.
  • 🆕 The Context-Ready Transformer
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-30 · LLMA new recurrent architecture built from a D-layer transformer block that pre-contextualizes each token via a correction network before it enters the block. A D=5 model beats a 12-layer transformer while generating 1.7x faster on an A100. With K=10 unrolling, a single-layer (D=1) model beats a 6-layer transformer at 2.6x speedup. Pretrained transformers can be converted by adding a zero-initialized correction FFN and fine-tuning.
  • 🆕 PhysisForcing: Physics Reinforced World Simulator for Robotic Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-06-30Targets physical implausibility in video-based world simulators for robotic manipulation — discontinuous motion trajectories and inconsistent robot-object interactions. Proposes pixel-level trajectory alignment and semantic-level relational alignment losses to strengthen physical consistency. Improves the Wan2.2 and Cosmos3-Nano base models by 22.3% and 9.2% on R-Bench, with the Cosmos3-Nano variant achieving best overall score. As a world model, raises closed-loop success rate from 16.0% to 24.0%.
