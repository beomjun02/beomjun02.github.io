  • 🆕 A Task-State Representation for Long-Horizon Mobile GUI Agents
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-03 · Gui agentIntroduces Task-State Representation (TSR), a training-free framework that decouples persistent task state from transient screen observations in mobile GUI agents.   • 🆕 SLIM-RL: Risk-Budgeted Random-Masking RL for Diffusion LLMs Without Trajectory Slicing
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-07-03 · on policy rl · LLMShows that trajectory-aware RL training for diffusion LLMs (like TraceRL) can be replaced by a simpler random-masking approach with a risk budget, eliminating the expensive trajectory-slicing step. SLIM-RL matches or beats TraceRL while being significantly cheaper, since it avoids slicing each rollout into K/s aligned samples.
