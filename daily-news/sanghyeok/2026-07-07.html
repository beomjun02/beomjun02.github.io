  • 🆕 ACID: Action Consistency via Inverse Dynamics for Planning with World Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-07 · worldmodelIntroduces cycle action consistency into decision-time planning: the action inferred backward from a predicted transition by an inverse dynamics model should recover the originally conditioned action. A scale-invariant adaptive weight folds this per-step residual into the planning cost. Consistently improves planning across four world models and six tasks spanning rigid/deformable manipulation, articulated control, and visual navigation.

  • 🆕 TAP: Task-Agnostic Pretraining for VLAs
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-07Argues that acquiring physical competence (how to move) and semantic alignment (what to do) should be decoupled — only the latter requires language supervision. Matches models trained on 1M+ expert trajectories using orders of magnitude less labeled data, with a 10% absolute gain over standard behavior cloning.
