  • 🆕 SiLR: Structure-Preserving Admission and Process Reward for LLM Tool Agents
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-09-09 · LLMAddresses structural limitations of scalar reward signals for LLM tool agents in ReAct loops. The full product-order predicate recovers 21/21 multi-action episodes (vs. 0/21 for terminal gates), and reused as a GRPO process reward, it is the only tested reward whose ungated policy exceeds the untrained baseline (0.844 vs. 0.778).
  • 🆕 ConsensusBench: Benchmark of Consensus Nodes for LLM Reasoning via Outcome Reward Densifying
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-09-09 · LLMProposes process-level reward signals for RL-based LLM reasoning by identifying "consensus nodes" — verifiable intermediate conclusions clustered from correct trajectories. Integrating these rule-based process rewards into GRPO consistently surpasses outcome-only approaches across AIME 2024/2025, GSM8K, and MATH-500.
  • DRACO: Fine-Grained Credit Assignment with Dynamic Rubrics for Long-Horizon Agent Training
    HF papers · 2026-09-07 · LLMRL from verifiable rewards works well with programmatic checkers, but most long-horizon agent domains lack them. DRACO operates in an outcome-blind setting, using dynamically generated rubrics to provide fine-grained credit assignment without ground-truth labels.
  • 🆕 Iris: Climbing to the Search Frontier
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-09-09 · LLMPresents Iris-mini (35B-A3B) and Iris-pro (397B-A17B), search agents trained via "SFT-RL climbing" that alternates supervised fine-tuning and RL against live search. With context management enabled, Iris-pro achieves 88.6/85.1/92.9/56.4 on BrowseComp, BrowseComp-ZH, DeepSearchQA, and HLE — strongest open-source search agent results in their parameter ranges.
  • 🆕 τ^τ-Bench: An Environment for End-To-End, Realistic Agent Construction

  • 🆕 Train What You Deploy: Token-Faithful Post-Training of a Production Coding Agent
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-09-09 · LLMIdentifies severe token and control fidelity errors in existing post-training pipelines for coding agents — training environments mismatch production, and offline token reconstruction distorts prompts. Proposes Certified Divergence PPO (C-DPPO) with tight TV bounds, yielding a consistent +3.0-point gain over standard DPPO across model scales.
  • 🆕 Extremely Sparse Supervision Incentivizes Reasoning Ability
    arXiv cs.AI · 2026-09-09 · LLMCounter-intuitive finding: in on-policy distillation, supervising as few as 1–2 tokens per reasoning trajectory (0.05% of all tokens) matches or surpasses full-token training for reasoning ability. Validated across nine teacher–student configurations on math and coding tasks, Llama models, and PPO-based RLVR.
  • 🆕 Evidence Integration in Large Language Models
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-09-09 · LLMPresents a distributional theory of how LLMs integrate external evidence: candidates more probable to the receiver are more persuasive, and LLMs integrate candidates even after internally verifying their invalidity (93–100%). Causal interventions show the integration is implemented late in the network as a structured sequence of admitting, promoting, and transporting candidate answers.
