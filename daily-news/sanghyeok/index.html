  • Demystifying Hidden-State Recurrence: Switchable Latent Reasoning with On-Policy Reinforcement Learning
    arXiv (HF daily) · 2026-06-14 · on policy rlIntroduces SWITCH, a framework that makes hidden-state latent chain-of-thought reasoning trainable with standard on-policy RL (GRPO). Explicit boundary tokens mark latent reasoning blocks, enabling both gradient propagation through recurrent computation and mechanistic analysis. Outperforms prior hidden-state-recurrence approaches while revealing that latent steps perform problem-specific, causally important computation concentrated at a single state transition.

Achieves state-of-the-art operation semantics prediction and consistent task success rate improvements on Android World.
  • SG-OPD: Sign-Gated On-Policy Distillation via Sign-Consistency Gating and Phased Teacher Sampling
    arXiv (HF daily) · 2026-06-14 · on policy rlAddresses failure modes in on-policy distillation where trajectory-level alignment and token-level teacher reliability assumptions break down. Uses a binary verifier as trust signal at two granularities — phased teacher rollout sampling at cold-start and a sign-consistency gate for update interpolation/extrapolation. Outperforms standard OPD on competition-level math reasoning with average gains of 1.98 (per-sample) and 7.50 (per-question).
  • SENTINEL: Failure-Driven Reinforcement Learning for Training Tool-Using Language Model Agents

Boosts Pass^1 from 66.4 to 74.9 on Tau2-Bench Retail with Qwen3-4B, outperforming RL on general synthetic tasks.
