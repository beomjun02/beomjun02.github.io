Papers

  • 🆕 N0-VTLA: Vision-Tactile-Language-Action Foundation Model
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-29 · VLAIntroduces a VTLA foundation model for fine-grained contact-rich manipulation through tactile perception and feedback control, plus offline policy improvement from stored deployment data. Proposes a staged training recipe — visuo-tactile pre-training followed by tactile-pathway integration — to graft touch onto existing vision-based VLA backbones.
  • 🆕 N0-TWAM: Tactile-Native World-Action Model at Scale
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-29 · WAMThe first tactile world-action model trained at large scale, predicting both future vision and future contact for contact-rich manipulation. Pre-trained over six embodiments and 450 tasks, it demonstrates that tactile-native world modeling substantially improves performance on tasks requiring force-aware control.
  • 🆕 VLA policy benchmarking: π0/0.5 leads, but no single best policy
    VilleKuosmanen · 2026-07-28 · VLAA large-scale benchmark ran thousands of evaluations across 12 manipulation tasks, finding Physical Intelligence’s π0/0.5 wins overall with NVIDIA GR00T close behind, while non-VLA methods (Diffusion, ACT) remain competitive with lighter VLAs. Chris Paxton noted that top-line VLA benchmark progress has been surprisingly stagnant over the past year.
  • 🆕 FeelWorld: Visuo-Tactile World Model for Contact Prediction and Planning
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-29A hierarchical visuo-tactile world model that jointly predicts visual appearance dynamics and tactile contact states, addressing the gap where purely visual world models produce futures that look plausible but violate physical contact dynamics. Enables planning that reasons about both what things look like and how they feel.
  • 🆕 WorldDiT: Unified Diffusion Architecture for World and Action Modeling
    HF papers · 2026-07-28A unified diffusion transformer that couples action generation with visual world modeling, achieving strong manipulation performance without requiring a large pretrained VLM as the action backbone. Demonstrates that jointly training world prediction and action generation in a single DiT can substitute for the heavy VLM encoders used by current VLA approaches.
  • 🆕 LeapBot-WA: Predictive-Latent World-Anchor Action Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-29 · WAMProposes a Predictive-Latent paradigm for WAMs that eliminates the pixel-level video generation bottleneck. Instead of reconstructing full visual frames, it anchors actions to latent world representations, freeing capacity from task-irrelevant visual details and making policies more robust to visual distractors.
  • 🆕 Data Pyramid for Embodied Manipulation
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-29Organizes the embodied data ecosystem as a pyramid spanning multiple sources — from abundant internet video at the base to scarce real-robot demonstrations at the top — and proposes principled strategies for combining data of different modalities and fidelities to train generalizable manipulation policies.
