  • 🆕 Knowledge Offloading: Decomposing LLMs into Sparse Backbones and Memory Modules
    arXiv · 2026-05-31 · Inference efficiencyProposes decomposing a pretrained LLM into a sparse shared backbone and domain-specific LoRA + learned KV-cache memory modules.
