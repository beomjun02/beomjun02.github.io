    arXiv · 2026-06-26 · speculative decodingIntegrates lookahead signals from the draft model with historical attention from the target to identify critical KV-cache tokens during verification.   • 🆕 Local Branch Routing: Efficient Trainable Test-Time Scaling

Enables end-to-end RL with verifiable rewards, improving Pass@1 and Pass@16 over standard chain-of-thought on math reasoning benchmarks.
  • 🆕 Quantization Inflates Reasoning: Token Inflation as a Hidden Cost

Introduces the CoT Token Inflation Ratio metric and finds quantization-aware training is the most promising mitigation strategy.
  • 🆕 WinDOM: Self-Family Distillation for Small-Model GUI Grounding

  • 🆕 Why Do Accumulated Transformations Extrapolate?
    arXiv · 2026-06-26 · long contextProves that replacing RoPE's position-indexed rotations with accumulated token-dependent orthogonal transformations creates a finite mixing window independent of context length, explaining strong length extrapolation. Also proves degradation at extreme lengths is inevitable without explicit far-mass control.
  • 🆕 Plans Don't Persist: Context Management Is Load Bearing for LLM Agents
