  • 🆕 EpiKV: Epiphany-Aware KV Cache Eviction Without the Attention Matrix
    arXiv · 2026-06-27 · Inference efficiencyScores tokens by "epiphany score" — the change in internal representation during the forward pass — instead of attention weights. Requires no training, no custom kernels, and works directly in FlashAttention stacks, scaling to 16x longer feasible context than attention-based scoring. Reaches 72% on MATH-500 at 4096-token cache and 37% on AIME-2024 at 8192 tokens, at up to 2.8x the speed.
  • 🆕 JetSpec: Breaking the Scaling Ceiling of Speculative Decoding
    HF papers · 2026-06-26 · speculative decodingIntroduces parallel tree drafting to overcome the fundamental scaling limitation of speculative decoding: increasing draft budgets typically hits diminishing returns when acceptance drops or drafting overhead grows. JetSpec resolves the causality-efficiency dilemma that prior head-based methods face, enabling speculative decoding to scale to larger draft budgets.
  • 🆕 InfoKV: Information-Aware KV Cache Compression for Long Reasoning
    arXiv · 2026-06-27 · long contextShows that tokens selected by attention mainly influence nearby contexts, while tokens with high predictive uncertainty have stronger influence on distant future contexts. Combines token-level predictive entropy with layer-wise representation evolution to guide KV cache compression. Consistently outperforms attention-based methods on Llama-3.1, Llama-3.2, and DeepSeek-R1 in both long prefilling and decoding scenarios.
  • 🆕 SharQ: Bridging Activation Sparsity and FP4 Quantization for LLM Inference
    arXiv · 2026-06-27 · Inference efficiencyTraining-free method that decomposes activations into a sparse FP4 backbone (outlier-dominated) and a dense FP4 residual. Recovers 43–63% of the NVFP4-to-FP16 accuracy gap with no calibration or retraining. Delivers 2.2–2.4x latency reduction over FP16 and 1.2–1.4x throughput over FP8 on RTX 5090.
  • 🆕 Erase-then-Delta Attention: Decoupled Memory Management for Linear Attention
    arXiv · 2026-06-27 · long contextDecouples where to erase stale memory from where to write new content in delta-rule linear attention. Adds an independent erase step before the corrective write, expanding memory-management capacity. Best performance in both dense 2.5B and MoE 25B-A2.8B families; gains persist through 80B-token long-context midtraining from 4K to 128K contexts.
