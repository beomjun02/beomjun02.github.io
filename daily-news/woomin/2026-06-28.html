    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-28 · Inference efficiencyTraining-free inference method that bridges activation sparsity and FP4 quantization via online sparse–dense decomposition.     arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-28 · long contextDecouples where to erase from where to write in recurrent memory updates.
