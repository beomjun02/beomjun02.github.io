    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-19 · speculative decodingProposes a head-based speculative decoding framework combining one-forward drafting efficiency with branch-wise causal conditioning.     arXiv cs.LG · 2026-06-19 · long contextReplaces pairwise query-key comparison with routing through K learned Gaussian mixture components, achieving O(NK) activation storage instead of O(N²). An interpretable, fixed-K linear-time alternative for scenarios where quadratic cost is prohibitive.
  • 🆕 Dual Dimensionality for Local and Global Attention
    arXiv cs.CL · 2026-06-19 · Inference efficiencyFormalizes the observation that local tokens need richer representations than distant ones.
