Papers

  • 🆕 CLAP: Direct VLM-to-VLA Adaptation via Language-Action Grounding
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-14 · VLAAddresses the output-distribution mismatch when converting VLMs to VLAs by prepending natural-language action descriptions before numeric action tokens, causally conditioning predictions on language-action plans. With single-epoch fine-tuning on a 2B model, achieves 90.8% on LIBERO (+14.9 pt over VLA-0) and improved robustness under language, object, and spatial perturbations.
  • 🆕 Learning from Hindsight: Reinforcement Learning for VLA Post-Training
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-14 · VLA · reinforcement learningBrings hindsight relabeling to RL post-training of VLAs — a VLM scores failed rollouts against the tasks they actually achieved, and the policy trains on relabeled plus original rollouts jointly. Achieves 5× improvement in sample efficiency on out-of-distribution LIBERO-PRO tasks and outperforms dense progress-reward baselines. Gains hold across VLA backbones and on a physical Franka robot.
  • 🆕 FlowDAgger: Human-in-the-Loop Adaptation of Generative Robot Policies in Latent Space
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-14 · VLA · robot learningSample-efficient method for adapting frozen generative robot policies (flow matching and diffusion) from human interventions via action inversion in latent space. Maps expert actions to the noise that would have produced them under the frozen base policy, enabling rapid skill acquisition while preserving behavioral priors. Works with both action-head VLAs and world-action models from a handful of interventions.
  • 🆕 Prompt-Driven Exploration for VLA Reinforcement Learning
    arXiv cs.LG · 2026-07-14 · VLA · reinforcement learningUses prompt perturbation as a global exploration mechanism for RL with VLAs. A VLM reasons over rollout video, diagnoses how the policy responded, and rewrites the prompt to elicit better behavior — realizing posterior sampling at the level of prompts. Enables RL to learn successful policies even from zero-reward starts across manipulation and reasoning tasks.
  • 🆕 CD-LAM: Causally Debiased Latent Action Model for Embodied World Models
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-14 · robot learningIdentifies action-irrelevant bias in latent action models (LAMs) as a key obstacle to controllable action-conditioned world models for robots. Introduces three efficient fine-tuning objectives — embodiment-centric reconstruction, action-centric contrastive learning, and latent space calibration — that together improve controllability. Requires only 6k fine-tuning steps and 12× fewer robot-action adaptation updates.
  • 🆕 Tactile Genesis: GPU-Parallel Tactile Sensor Simulation at Scale
    arXiv cs.RO · 2026-07-14 · robot learningGPU-parallel tactile sensor simulation platform supporting 7 modalities (including first-ever voxelized temperature field) with configurable placement, resolution, and realistic noise. Scales past 20,000 parallel environments on a single GPU, 3–20× faster than prior tactile simulators. Key finding: sensor placement dominates sensor type, and 200 whole-hand taxels suffice across dexterous tasks.

  • Harness VLA: Steering Frozen VLAs into Reliable Manipulation Primitives via Memory-Guided Agents
