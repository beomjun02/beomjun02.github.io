Appendix · notes on the record (2026-09-04 16:30)

  1. Cross-cluster offset — the same recipe trained on ICLR27 (ctl) vs rlwrld (i5 base, 1×A100) differs by paired Δcos −0.026 [−0.045, −0.006] on the 288 shared arctic / dexycb / grab chunks (taco and oakink2 index differently on the two corpora). i5's cos 0.301 / ADE 8.74 vs zero 8.86 are on its own 480-of-576 subset. Arms are compared within one cluster only: every ICLR27 arm pairs against ctl on all 480 chunks.
  2. λ 1.0 / λ 0.001 preempted twice — background-QoS jobs 5471 / 5648 were preempted at 03:54 and again after 11:34, when jit1m and mirror took the freed GPUs. They wait for the next idle slot; no ETA. The contact verdict rests on λ 0.1 alone.
  3. DexYCB has no 5-chunk episodes — the AR-rollout grids cover taco, arctic, oakink2 and grab (2 episodes each); DexYCB episodes are shorter than 5 chunks and cannot be rolled out autoregressively for 5 s. hot3d AR rollouts exist for ctl and w_ep only.
  4. Metric reliability order — paired Δ with CI > ADE vs the zero predictor > Acc5 / Acc10 > cosine. cos_moving = cosine over point-frames whose GT displacement exceeds 5 mm (66.6 % of point-frames). Field-standard names: EPE3D (= ADE per frame), Acc3D strict / relaxed (= Acc5 / Acc10). The x10_sharpa cos reads 0.267 point-weighted, 0.273 per-chunk.
