Appendix A0 · How the numbers were made (1/2)

Unit
one episode (human embodiment, train + val)
Sources
GRAB / OakInk2 / TACO / ARCTIC / DexYCB = the text field of our v2 corpus manifests (server-mirror/pull4/manifests/<ds>/manifest.json) · HOT3D = the all-object manifest; its text is auto-generated “interact with the X and the Y”, so tasks are UNLABELED and only object names are parsed · VITRA = the converted v2 manifest on the Mac (~/xfer_allobj/vitra_v2/vitra/manifest.json, 99,999 selected episodes; text = the per-hand VLM phrase) · HOI4D = NOT on disk — the official task table (16 categories × T1–T6, github leolyliu/HOI4D-Instructions definitions/task/task_definitions.csv), one unit per (category, task) pair, so its pies are weights, not sequence counts
Task pie
text-based regex matching on the lowercased text; one bucket per episode = the first match in the priority order pick & place → open / close → pour / scoop → grasp / lift → press / turn → tool use → other (regexes in A1). Multi-step texts (OakInk2) therefore count once, by their first-priority verb
Relevance
GR-1-relevant % = share of episodes whose bucket is in {pick & place, grasp / lift, open / close, press / turn}, taken from the robocasa GR-1 task families (PnP, open/close, press/turn) · DexJoCo-relevant % = share in {pick & place, grasp / lift, open / close, pour / scoop, press / turn}, taken from the 11 DexJoCo task prompts · tool use counts for neither · ARCTIC “use the <object>” is mapped to open / close (articulated actuation) by decision

Appendix A0 · How the numbers were made (2/2)

Object pie
nouns parsed from the text with dataset-specific patterns (DexYCB: the phrase after “grasp the”; TACO: the target after “the” and the tool after “with the”; GRAB / ARCTIC: the last “the <noun>”; OakInk2 / HOT3D: a fixed household-object keyword list) · VITRA = the object ENTITY names stored in the episodes (VLM-labelled) · HOI4D = its 16 categories weighted by task count · top 9 shown, rest = other
Size
Σ n_frames from the manifests at 10 Hz → hours; chunks = Σ max(0, n_frames − 9) (T = 10, stride 1, BEFORE validity filtering) · HOI4D size = 4,000 sequences × 20 s at 15 fps from the paper · VITRA chunks after validity are lower (359,666 in the training index)
Mixture
temperature sampling: sharei ∝ chunksi0.5 over the five mocap sets, renormalised into the pool left after the fixed caps (VITRA 15 %, HOT3D 10 %, HOI4D 20 % when it lands) · “current” = chunk-proportional over the five mocap sets as trained so far
Caveats
text matching misses tasks phrased without the verb · HOT3D and HOI4D task shares are not measured on our data · relevance is about the TASK TEXT, not about motion quality

open / close\b(open|close|shut|unscrew|twist|use|uncap|cap)\b✓✓
pour / scoop\b(pour|fill|empty|scoop|weigh)\b–✓
grasp / lift\b(grasp|grab|hold|grip)\b✓✓
press / turn\b(press|push|click|turn|switch|type|flip|toggle|slide|pull)\b✓✓
tool use\b(brush|dust|cut|slice|chop|scrape|skim|stir|smear|hit|hammer|wipe|clean|scrub|paint|measure|stir-fry|screw|staple|peel|sew|knead|dip|drink|eat|browse|call|play|cook|read|write|squeeze|shake|heat|stamp|sharpen|fold|bind|clamp)\b––
other / unlabeledno match (HOT3D: no task text at all)––
