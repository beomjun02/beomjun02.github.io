Method (2): one gravity frame, and who is actually working

Every dataset put z-up with its table at z = 0, from its own evidence

  • Ground truth where it exists
  • Fitted only where it does not
  • No plane drawn when unmeasured

Method: per episode an affine p₀ = R(p + origin) + T with z up and the table at z = 0, shipped as a sidecar the loader reads; an episode that fails its check keeps the old hand-centred frame rather than a guessed plane. DexYCB's rotation independently confirmed by within-episode coplanarity (tilt ≤ 1.8°). GRAB's table moves per sequence (0.73–1.18 m), which is why a per-subject constant failed. Activity = ≥ 5 frames of 1 cm point contact · full numbers in season2/handedness/

HOI4D: raw vs lightly smoothed

Same episode, same window, same camera — only the filter differs

Savitzky-Golay over 5 source frames before the stride-2 decimation. On the shipped grid it costs 2–5 % of median step and at most 3 % of peak speed, while the acceleration correlation goes −0.35 → −0.19 (hand) and −0.21 → −0.03 (object).

Appendix A0 · How the numbers were made (1/2)

Unit
one episode (human embodiment, train + val)
Sources
GRAB / OakInk2 / TACO / ARCTIC / DexYCB = the text field of our v2 corpus manifests (server-mirror/pull4/manifests/<ds>/manifest.json) · HOT3D = the all-object manifest; its text is auto-generated “interact with the X and the Y”, so tasks are UNLABELED and only object names are parsed · VITRA = the converted v2 manifest on the Mac (~/xfer_allobj/vitra_v2/vitra/manifest.json, 99,999 selected episodes; text = the per-hand VLM phrase) · HOI4D = its own converted corpus (1,683 episodes).csv),
Task pie
text-based regex matching on the lowercased text; one bucket per episode = the first match in the priority order pick & place → open / close → pour / scoop → grasp / lift → press / turn → tool use → other (regexes in A1). Multi-step texts (OakInk2) therefore count once, by their first-priority verb
Relevance
GR-1-relevant % = share of episodes whose bucket is in {pick & place, grasp / lift, open / close, press / turn}, taken from the robocasa GR-1 task families (PnP, open/close, press/turn) · DexJoCo-relevant % = share in {pick & place, grasp / lift, open / close, pour / scoop, press / turn}, taken from the 11 DexJoCo task prompts · tool use counts for neither · ARCTIC “use the <object>” is mapped to open / close (articulated actuation) by decision

Appendix A0 · How the numbers were made (2/2)

Object pie
nouns parsed from the text with dataset-specific patterns (DexYCB: the phrase after “grasp the”; TACO: the target after “the” and the tool after “with the”; GRAB / ARCTIC: the last “the <noun>”; OakInk2 / HOT3D: a fixed household-object keyword list) · VITRA = the object ENTITY names stored in the episodes (VLM-labelled) · HOI4D = its 16 categories weighted by task count · top 9 shown, rest = other
Size
Σ n_frames from the manifests at 10 Hz → hours; chunks = Σ max(0, n_frames − 9) (T = 10, stride 1, BEFORE validity filtering) · HOI4D from its own converted corpus (1,683 episodes) · VITRA chunks after validity are lower (359,666 in the training index)
Mixture
temperature sampling: sharei ∝ chunksi0.5 over the five mocap sets, renormalised into the pool left after the fixed caps (VITRA 15 %, HOT3D 10 %, HOI4D 20 % when it lands) · “current” = chunk-proportional over the five mocap sets as trained so far
Caveats
text matching misses tasks phrased without the verb · HOT3D and HOI4D task shares are not measured on our data · relevance is about the TASK TEXT, not about motion quality
