Appendix A0 · How the numbers were made

Logs
season2/logs/s2_base_120m_{10981,11153,11270}.log — relaunch chain of the 120M base; loss/fm and lr parsed from the step lines (log_every 20), duplicate ranges dropped at the resumes (9,213 · 11,814)
Reads
season2/reads/<arm>/<ep>/ (read_all.json + roll/ + gr1/ + dexjoco/), pulled from ~/3d-wam/review/s2/ on iclr2027-gpu (ep01 jobs 11154 / 11155 / 11156); every npz records its frame provenance (gravity z-up, table-anchored origin); schedule = reads/schedule.json (models → epochs → step, eff. epoch, land / read KST)
Two arms
one axis (owner 2026-09-09 15:00), superseding the one-model-per-deck rule for this deck; axis = effective epoch; 320M reads hop MLXP → Mac → /s3ckpt, then the same read harness
Metric
mEPE@H = mean |pred − gt| over points whose GT displacement over the horizon exceeds 2 cm; hand and object separately; zero predictor = the same points held still; mpepe self-test passes (identity → 0, zero → the zero column)
Effective epoch
steps × eff. batch / 1,029,165 · 120M: batch 128, one epoch = 8,040 steps, final 67,200 = 8.36 ep · 320M: batch 256, one epoch = 4,020 steps, 24 h cap ≈ 33,600 = 8.35 ep
Assets
assets/<arm>/<ep>/{roll, gr1, dexjoco}/ per checkpoint · curves assets/curve_<analysis>.png · arch_main.png from decks/2026-09-05-new-base/ · loss_curve.png + mixture.png built 09-09
Generator
season2/deck22_update.py --step 120m=N --step 320m=N --kst HH:MM [--publish] — rewrites only the GEN regions, copies assets, exits non-zero on a missing asset or key
