Experiment log · 3D-WAM · action item 1.2

Point-budget gallery: 10 GRAB demos × 16 budgets, sampler deterministic and tracked — awaiting the owner's pick

A deterministic uniform point sampler (area-weighted dense sample → farthest-point sampling, sampled once on the template mesh and tracked per frame) applied to the object mesh and the MANO right hand of 10 GRAB demonstrations, rendered at every Nobj × Nhand ∈ {128, 256, 512, 1024}². The same sampler is exercised on the Sharpa Wave right-hand URDF. No image was judged by the tool author — the budget is chosen by eye by the owner.

01How to read the galleries

Every 4×4 grid: rows = Nhand 128 → 1024 top to bottom, cols = Nobj 128 → 1024 left to right. One frame per demo — the frame with the most hand vertices within 1 cm of the object surface (the grasp moment). Same camera and bounding box in all 16 panels; box size in cm in the panel title. Object points blue; hand points coloured by MANO part (16 parts, tab20). Clips: Nobj = Nhand = 512, the whole demo subsampled to ≤100 frames at 10 fps, camera fixed to the sequence's union box, every 8th point drags a 4-frame grey trail — this is what "tracked" looks like.

06Data facts surfaced (not judgments of the pictures)

  • !MANO hand-mean convention.GRAB's stored fullpose is reproduced from its PCA-24 params only with flat_hand_mean=True; smplx's default (False) adds the hand mean twice and lands 71 mm off. ManipTrans' main/dataset/grab_full_dataset_dexhand.py:83 uses the default — its GRAB hand poses carry that offset. Not touched here; relevant to the retargeter campaign that ran on it.
  • ·Subject templates missing.tools/subject_meshes/*_rhand.ply are not on disk, so the neutral MANO shape is used (ManipTrans does the same). Fingers may sit slightly off the mocap subject's.
  • ·Left hand, body, table not sampled.Loaded but out of scope for this gallery (right hand only).
  • ·No Sharpa motion.No retargeted GRAB→Sharpa trajectories locally; points_from_mesh_sequence(V_seq, faces, spec) is the ready entry point once per-frame robot vertices exist (any FK).
