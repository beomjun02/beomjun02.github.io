Back to basics: the pre-campaign 120M model on today's harness

Eleven arms this week were trained on a fast rig at 39 M parameters and 1.6 M samples, and every one of them was read against a control built the same way. p7 is the model that came before all of it — 120 M parameters, 3.5× the samples, 10 effective epochs — and it has never been measured on the evaluation basis the campaign settled on. This puts it there.

What p7 actually is

C2's architecture, three deliberate changes, one of them easy to misread.

architectured768 · depth 12 · heads 12 · H = 2identical
datasets6, including hot3d5 — hot3d dropped (the i5 composition)

learning rate3e-4, cosine to 0.1×3e-5 flat (min_lr_ratio 1.0)
corpus2 objects/scene2 objects/scene
  • !The learning rate is the thing to check3e-5 flat is a tenth of C2's peak with no decay, which reads like a resumed fine-tune rather than a fresh run. If p7 continued from a C2 checkpoint, its 180,608 steps are not comparable to a from-scratch 110,000, and "10 effective epochs" understates what it saw.
  • ·Dropping hot3d was the i5 finding+0.019 paired Δcos at 25k on the old basis, a gain that grew with training length.
03

Why the comparison is not clean, and what it still answers

  • ✗Different scene difficultyp7 saw 2 objects; the arms saw 6. Clutter is a first-order axis here — OMWC measures 0.005 on 1–2 object datasets against 0.29 on OakInk2 at 7.6.
  • ✗Different compositionp7 has no hot3d; the arms do.
  • ✗Different augmentationp7 has no origin jitter; the arms jitter the scene origin by up to 1 m, which removes absolute position as a cue and makes the task harder.
  • ✓What it does answerwhether the campaign's fast rig — a third of the parameters and half the samples — was measuring anything the 120 M model would not have shown more clearly. If p7 beats the eleven on the shared benchmark, the auxiliaries were being read at a scale too small to see them.
  • ·The clean version is already runningc4 puts C2's architecture and sample count on the v2 corpus with the object cap as the ONLY variable. That isolates clutter from scale.

  • ✓Three times the objects for p7's exact costk=16 at full 512-point density is 0.280 s/step, the same as p7's two objects at k=8 — because attention pays for TOKENS, not points. No affordance is discarded: a handle is still sampled at ~1 cm.
  • !k=16 coarsens the hand too2 patches per MANO part instead of 4. Keeping the hand at k=8 while objects use 16 needs a mixed-k data contract (~150 lines: per-entity k, padded patch_idx, two head widths) and lands at ~320 tokens, ~17.5 h.
  • ·Attention is not the problemat 5,120 tokens we sit just below the n > 8d crossover where attention overtakes the MLP. Video DiTs run full 3-D attention at 30–80k tokens; entity-factorised attention would be cheaper but would sever object–object relations, which is where tool use lives.
