Verdict

The tactile model validated on BMW transfers to PnP (micro-F1 0.70 vs manual GT) and Tool — and on the spray power-grip it recovers the ring and pinky it under-fires on PnP pinches, so it isn't pinky-blind; the grasp just has to load the finger. Separately, both new high-DOF hands each complete a scripted pick-lift 3/3 in the OpenArm sim, alongside Inspire 3/3 — the hand-swap integration runs end to end. Two honest asterisks: PnP pinky recall is low (0.08), and the sim rollouts are scripted open-loop — an integration check, not a policy result.

01Result

Two things at once: the three hands running the same scripted pick-lift side by side, and the learned contact firing on the right fingers as a hand closes on the object.

05Reads & caveats

What the numbers do and don't say.

  • ✓Contact prediction is not BMW-specific. PnP micro-F1 0.70 vs manual GT; contact lands on the correct fingers at closure across five object types.
  • ✓The model isn't pinky-blind. Pinky F1 0.15 on PnP pinches but 0.51 on the spray power grip — it fires the pinky when the grasp actually loads it.
  • ✓All three hands integrate in sim. Inspire / Wuji / Sharpa each load, actuate every finger, and lift the bottle 3/3 in the scripted rollout.
  • →PnP pinky recall is low (0.08). Under-fires the pinky in pinch/power grasps; precision stays high (0.80), so it's a miss-rate, not a false-alarm, issue.
  • →Tool manual GT is sparse & thumb-free. Ego view occludes the thumb, so SJ-vs-manual understates agreement; the auto/proximity reference is fairer. Microwave also shows genuine over-firing during reach.
  • ·Sim success is a scripted carry, not a grasp policy. The 3/3 lifts are an in-hand carry (bottle pinned at a fixed offset), validating integration + actuation — not autonomous force-closure and not a learned policy. Only Inspire runs a real closed-loop DPP (bonus clip).
