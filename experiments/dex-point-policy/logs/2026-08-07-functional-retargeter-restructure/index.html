Experiment log · Dexterous Point Policy (ICLR 2027)

One frozen human policy, N functional retargeters — idea verified 4/5, design v1 on the table

Restructure of the ICLR-2027 workstream around a human-centric base policy (real human data only, embodiment-free observations) composed with a learned per-embodiment retargeter whose objective is functional: the object must follow the demo's 3D trajectory. Four assigned papers read in full + a 26-work novelty sweep, via 5 parallel reading agents; design v1 written and under discussion.

Verdict

4/5, and the design closed same-day at v1.0 (authoritative spec: DPP-ICLR2027/retargeter/DESIGN.md). The risk concentrates in exactly one place — the retargeter's training signal — because "the object moves the same" is a closed-loop property; the fix is pairing-by-outcome in sim, discharged at data-generation time. Final form after five discussion rounds: MANO-θ interfaces both ways (R: θ→q residual over a geometric prior, R⁻¹: q→θ), W=1 per-step at control rate, wrist 6D + arm IK outside R, and a hard principle — the retargeter is task-agnostic: no robot demos AND no task data, sim play pairs only (two-hand play: MANO hand leads, robot tracks the recorded object trajectory on the same clock). A 26-work sweep found no 5/5 collision; the paper hangs on one ablation — the same retargeter trained on kinematic pairs vs object-motion-matched pairs.

01The idea, verified

Proposal: robot policy = RE ∘ πH. πH trained only on human (MANO) data over embodiment-free observations; RE is a small per-embodiment decoder. One base policy, N hands, only the retargeter is embodiment-specific.

Component of the proposalScoreWhy
Motivation (human data as the prior)5/5Standard and in-house-evidenced: W29 (prior irreplaceable, compounds with scale), BMW PT-vs-scratch.
Vision-side embodiment gap4.5/5Already solved by DPP's own design — the policy never sees the embodiment. Enabler, not a claimable contribution (we published it).
Functional definition of retargeting3→4/5Right objective; as stated it is not per-step trainable (closed-loop property). Fixed by pairing-by-outcome + RL fine-tune. Geometric proxy error is measured: ~29 mm median fingertip-vector residual on the easiest hand (Inspire).
Data plan (sim pairs)4/5Sound once rephrased: track each demo's object trajectory per hand in sim; successes = time-aligned pairs. Human side is free (PnP 500 has object clouds + contact per frame).
Experiment plan (openarm-sim)4/5All three hands integrated and smoke-passed; jitter + observation-parity lessons apply to any success-rate claim.

The five sharpenings the idea needs

  • →Object-trajectory equivalence is closed-loop, not per-step.You cannot supervise a_R = R(a_H) with it without rolling out. Resolution: (i) pairing-by-outcome in sim, (ii) RL fine-tune with an object-tracking reward on a geometric prior. Differentiable sim through contact: skip.
  • →The per-step map is ill-posed without context.Same human pose needs different robot joints depending on object geometry and contact state. R_E must be conditioned on (object cloud, proprio, contact) — a small policy, not a converter.
  • →The "hand pose similar" auxiliary is load-bearing, not aesthetic.Pure functional matching admits degenerate grasps that move the object identically but push closed-loop object-in-hand states off the human-data manifold — breaking frozen π_H at the next chunk (the exact DexWAM wrong-attractor failure). Formalize as a small-weight deviation penalty from the geometric retarget. Human2Sim2Robot's ablation independently shows hand-tracking rewards hurt when strong: keep it weak.
  • →Contact is the missing observable.Same object path under different force closure ≠ same robustness. DPP has a contact head — add contact-event timing preservation as a third term.
  • →Deployment needs the inverse map too — now learned, bidirectionally.π_H consumes human-hand proprio; each embodiment needs R⁻¹ (robot state → human hand state). Post-clarification: R⁻¹ is trained on the same functional pairs reversed, outputs MANO θ decoded to 21 kp (on-manifold by construction), regularized toward the validated geometric FK+alignment map. EgoVLA's released equivalent was <0.45 correlation — validate per hand, don't assume.

03Novelty sweep — 26 adjacent works, nothing scored 5/5

Claim at risk: "one frozen human-data policy + N per-embodiment adapters trained without robot demonstrations, closed-loop on ≥3 hands." Four works scored 4/5; each fails at least two legs of the conjunction.

Near-missHasLacks
DexMachina 2505.24853"Functional retargeting" is literally their term: RL tracks demo object states; decaying virtual-object-controller curriculum; 6 hands incl. Inspire.One policy per demo per hand — no reusable module, no human-data base, state-based. → our data engine + strongest mandatory baseline. What makes it unambiguous

  • →Lead with the pairing mechanism.Formalize functional (object-motion-matched) vs kinematic pairing, and run THE ablation: identical retargeter architecture trained on each. If that gap isn't there, the paper isn't there.
  • ·Show the adapter is a policy, not a converter.Perturbation-recovery probe (mid-rollout force pushes) that a DexPilot/GeoRT-class converter structurally cannot pass.
  • ·Quantify "cheap".Per-new-hand cost (GPU-hrs, params, wall-clock) + hands-scaling curve — the figure EgoScale cannot produce without new robot data.
  • ·Baselines reviewers will demand.dex-retargeting converter (no learning) · learned-kinematic MLP (our stage-0 #28/#29) · DexMachina per-demo RL (oracle) · monolithic per-hand controller (task #30 repurposed — tests the factorization) · adapter WITH a small robot-demo set · ablations: ±hand regularizer, frozen vs finetuned base.

05Timeline

  • 2026-08-07 Restructure proposed; theory verdict delivered

    User reframed the workstream: human-centric base policy + embodiment-specific retargeter, functional ("object moves the same") retargeting definition. Verified 4/5 with five sharpenings; five reading agents dispatched (4 assigned papers in full + a novelty sweep).

  • 2026-08-07 All five readers reported; digests filed

    Play2Perfect (pair-minting machinery + ε knob), Do-As-I-Do (objective validated at 10–30:1 + warmup weld), DexGrasp Anything (grasp synthesis + physics gates), HRDexDB (semantic-pairing contrast + kinematic-retargeting-is-worst evidence), sweep (26 works, no 5/5; DexMachina/DexTrack/EgoScale/LAD are the 4/5 near-misses).

  • 2026-08-07 Design v1 written up for discussion

    Four-stage plan above; six decisions open; no GPU spent, nothing submitted. DESIGN.md deferred until discussion closes (user's call).

  • 2026-08-07 Two clarifications from the user, folded in

    (1) The 6-point abstraction is removed — full-fidelity hand interface (MANO θ / 21 kp) and a bidirectional retargeter: robot → R⁻¹ → human → π_H → human → R → robot, both directions trained on the same time-aligned pairs (cycle-consistency auxiliary; base-policy retrain required — point-mode registry currently only has 6points). (2) Per-chunk pairing + per-step training confirmed sound: the closed-loop property is discharged at data time by rollouts; residual edge cases = label multimodality (proprio conditioning + one-strategy-per-demo), covariate shift (DAgger + perturbed generation + RL), dense-not-endpoint enforcement, no time-warping, and unconstrained pre-contact phases (hand prior carries them). Reference works all use per-step object STATE error vs a time-indexed reference — none use velocity, none endpoint-only.

  • 2026-08-07 Rounds 3–4: representation, structure, and the task-agnostic principle

    Representation unified on MANO θ (reversing an interim 21-kp ruling — θ/β factorizes pose from hand size, matches the robot's joint space, and kp inherit the same fitter bias anyway); geometry moves into FK losses ("represent in parameters, supervise in points"). R structure settled at W=1 per-step + proprio — the tracker-class field standard (proprio, not lookahead, is the converter/tracker dividing line); W∈{1,4} kept as an Inspire-only ablation. Object conditioning: v0 without, v1 as a measured arm under a parity-by-construction protocol (the ±8–10pt observation-parity scar makes sim-perfect clouds inadmissible). Hard principle adopted: the retargeter is task-agnostic — no finetune/task data anywhere in R/R⁻¹/g_E; pairs come from two-hand sim play (MANO hand + robot hand), DGA-style diverse objects, DexMachina demoted to a parts bin. DAgger/RL-polish/contact demoted to contingencies; the pilot simplified to S1 base → S2 pairs+R → S3 deploy.

  • 2026-08-07 Round 5: flaw sweep, wrist ruling — design CLOSED at v1.0

    Final checks: play episodes start non-grasped (approach+grasp+manipulate all learned), and with a W=1 per-step R the human-side naturalness gap reduces to a pose-coverage requirement — auditable (play-θ vs PT-θ distribution overlap), insured by a PT pose prior on the MANO play policy. Flaw sweep found one real defect — independently tracking shared goals breaks per-step alignment — fixed by making the MANO hand's clock authoritative (robot tracks the recorded object trajectory). Task-agnostic leaks patched (g_E distillation rebased off PnP FT streams; benchmark objects excluded from play). Wrist/arm closed by convention: wrist 6D + arm IK outside R (EgoVLA/EgoScale/DAID all do this; the old 2.8–5.4 cm IK scar was fingertip-IK, not wrist-target IK); θ-unification dissolves the R⁻¹ manifold concern. Spec frozen as DPP-ICLR2027/retargeter/DESIGN.md v1.0.
