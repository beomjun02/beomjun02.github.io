Experiment log · Dexterous Point Policy (ICLR 2027)

Retargeter & teleop-mix debugging in openarm-sim — seven bugs fixed, still 0/24

A debugging session on the openarm-sim deployment of the 21-keypoint human base policy with a dexret Inspire retargeter. Working from the rollout clips, we found and fixed seven implementation bugs (obs contamination, finger-open compression, object supervision, wrist orientation, …), each verified by measurement. All conditions still land at SR 0/24: the remaining bottleneck is not the retargeter but the policy's alignment/approach and a teleop→sim domain gap.

Verdict

Seven implementation-layer bugs removed and verified by measurement — finger open/close, wrist orientation, object supervision, obs contamination. Yet every condition remains SR 0/24. The grasp-success bottleneck is not the retargeter but (1) policy alignment / approach — the scratch-150k policy has not learned the grasp phase, so a PT-initialized fine-tune is the lever; and (2) a teleop→sim domain gap — even teleop-only 100% lands at 0, so the real demo distribution does not match the sim eval distribution. Both are decisions outside this session's implementation-fix scope.
