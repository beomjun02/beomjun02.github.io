Experiment log · Dexterous Point Policy (ICLR 2027)

A human→Inspire retargeter trained on 4-view high-quality assembly data

We train a GeoRT retargeter R (human 21-keypoint → Inspire qpos) and its inverse R⁻¹ on bmw_0821: 60 runs of a BMW assembly task (ecu / bracket / jig / drill), captured with a 4-view hand-pose rig that yields clean, high-precision hand keypoints. From 33,191 valid right-hand frames the retargeter's grasp-phase aperture mapping (0–5 cm) tracks the human hand almost 1:1 — the region a contact-rich task hinges on — while R⁻¹ reconstructs the full hand from 5 robot fingertips at 7.7 mm held-out MAE. A different task from the openarm-sim work, targeting Inspire hardware deployment.

Verdict

R and R⁻¹ trained and offline-verified. The 4-view capture gives a wide, clean aperture range (0–36 cm) with heavy sampling at contact onset. The retargeter uses position retargeting, so the grasp-region aperture (0–5 cm) maps nearly 1:1 (human 0/2.7/5.0 cm → robot 2.1/2.6/4.9 cm) and only saturates at the physically-unreachable open extreme — the failure mode that broke the earlier vector-mode retargeter is absent here. R⁻¹ reconstructs the full 21-keypoint hand from 5 robot fingertips at 7.7 mm all-joint / 6.1 mm intermediate-joint held-out MAE. Closed-loop rollout is deferred until a sim environment is provided.

Aperture mapping (offline — the key check)

The prior openarm-sim retargeter used vector retargeting, which compressed the hand into a 7–9 cm band and never opened or closed. Here position retargeting makes the human→robot thumb–index aperture follow almost 1:1 in the grasp region, saturating only at the unreachable open extreme.

Human apertureRobot thumb–index
0.0 cm (closed)2.1 cm
2.7 cm2.6 cm
5.0 cm (mid)4.9 cm
36.0 cm (open, max)10.3 cm — robot physical limit

Aperture correlation over 500 random frames: 0.664; robot achieves 0.4–8.8 cm. The grasp-onset region (0–5 cm) — what a high-precision contact task hinges on — is reproduced accurately.

03R⁻¹ — inverse (5 robot fingertips → 21 human keypoints)

Free pairs: human 21kp → R → qpos → FK → 5 robot fingertips; pair = (robot tips 5×3, human 21kp wrist-local). A small MLP (15→512×3→63) is trained supervised; held-out MAE is split by joint type — fingertips (R saw them) vs intermediate joints (R never saw; measures how much hand interior the tips determine).

Held-out MAEValue
Fingertips12.2 mm
Intermediate joints6.1 mm

Pairs / val split33,191 / 3,319

Fingertip MAE (12.2 mm) is higher than the earlier run101/pnp R⁻¹ (3.6–5.2 mm) because bmw_0821 spans a much wider aperture range — the reconstruction target is genuinely harder, not lower quality. Intermediate joints reconstruct at 6.1 mm, confirming the tips still determine the hand interior.
