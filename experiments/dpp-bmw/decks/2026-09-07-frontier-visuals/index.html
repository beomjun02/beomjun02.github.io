Appendix (1) · the object cloud

Object
SAM3 keeps every instance of every prompt (server/run_episode.sbatch), then viz/object_select.py picks the one nearest the ego camera by VGGT metric depth — IoU-0.55 merge across prompts, 5 cm continuity credit. Coverage 81/217 → 217/217 on ep0, 254/255 on ep1.
Depth edges
NEW 09-07 — lift() now drops pixels whose depth gradient exceeds 2 cm/px and erodes the mask 1 px. At a silhouette the depth map ramps from object to background and every pixel on that ramp unprojects into empty space; farthest-point sampling preferentially keeps those, because being far from everything is what FPS selects for. Gradient at the object points runs p50 0.1, p99 2.9, p99.9 9.5 cm/px. Cloud depth span 21.3 → 18.7 cm on ep0, p90 35.4 → 29.4 cm on ep1, with no frame losing its cloud.

Every number on the slides is recomputed at render time from the caches in viz/cache/; nothing here is transcribed by hand.

Appendix (2) · the hands, and what produced each frame

Hands
seungjun's MANO-pipeline, the two eyes as a 2-view hoi session, left eye = world, baseline 66.4388 mm. Re-run without --bbox_track (job 160289, 12 min): kept hand-frames 38/28/30% → 77/79/79% at an unchanged 5.5 px triangulation error, per-side starvation gone, spans converge on 9.3/8.6 cm. Then gated on his own used/reproj_err and held 1 s across gaps.
Ego gate
NEW 09-07 — viz/ego_gate.py drops a hand more than 400 px from both projected stored wrists. The person opposite is 1.6–6.0% of stereo-matched detections; own hands sit 66–129 px from the nearest stored wrist, theirs 866–1080 px, so one threshold keeps 99–100% of own and cuts 100% of theirs. A poor ruler (~130 px off, being retargeting IK) and an excellent discriminator — it also catches the solver's own misplacements: on-hand 71→78, 72→79, 64→75%.
No human hand tracking
Checked 09-07: the Quest emits 26 OpenXR joints per hand (tracking.jsonl) but 0 of the 160 released human episodes carry it — both merge manifests name already-converted sources, so the raw stream was consumed by retargeting. It survives only for a 2-episode, 3.6 s pilot on 260812.
