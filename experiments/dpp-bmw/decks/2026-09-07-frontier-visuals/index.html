Appendix (1) · the object

Object
SAM3 keeps every instance of every prompt (server/run_episode.sbatch); viz/object_select.py picks the one nearest the ego camera by VGGT depth (IoU-0.55 merge, 5 cm continuity). Coverage 81/217 → 217/217 on ep0, 254/255 on ep1.
Object class
NEW — viz/obj_label.py reads sel_pmask, which SAM3 prompts matched the selected instance, grouped into classes; most specific wins. ep0: cardboard box 148 · wrapped parcel 60 · styrofoam 6 · conveyor belt 3. ep1: wrapped parcel 105 · cardboard box 70 · conveyor belt 39 · styrofoam 38. A conveyor label is a selection error the overlay now surfaces.
Depth edges
NEW — lift() drops pixels with a depth gradient over 2 cm/px and erodes the mask 1 px. Silhouette-ramp pixels unproject into empty space and FPS preferentially keeps them. Cloud depth span 21.3 → 18.7 cm (ep0).
Work surface
NEW — viz/tabletop.py stacks depth frames in base coordinates, ±2 cm band, anchored to the mode of the package's resting height over stationary frames: z=0.435 (ep0), 0.405 (ep1); extent 166 × 107 cm.
Completed object
NEW — viz/complete_object.py, built only on what is reliable: the dominant face fits a plane to 7–8 mm, giving two dimensions. The third needs a second face, accepted only if it is flat, holds ≥8% of the cloud, sits ≥55° away AND shares an edge with the first — the edge test is decisive, since a plane fitted to the silhouette ramp trims itself planar (a rejected face scattered 44 mm vs 8.3 mm). Real corners: 108/217 frames on ep0, meeting face 1 to 0.1 mm; their short span IS the depth. Size is held rigid per (episode, class) — per-frame fitting jittered 6.6–9.2 cm at p90 — and only pose is fitted per frame. ep0 box 28.0×26.0×13.3 cm measured; its flat bag keeps 3 cm ASSUMED. Observed points stick out 8.5 mm = the face's own planarity. ⚠️ 15–17% of completed points lie within 1 cm of an observation.

Appendix (2) · the hands, timing, and the code

Hands
seungjun's MANO-pipeline, two eyes as one hoi session, baseline 66.4388 mm. Re-run without --bbox_track (job 160289): kept hand-frames 38/28/30% → 77/79/79% at an unchanged 5.5 px triangulation error. Then gated on his used/reproj_err and held 1 s.
Ego gate
NEW — drops a hand over 400 px from both projected stored wrists. Own hands sit 66–129 px from the nearest, the person opposite 866–1080 px; one threshold keeps 99–100% of own and cuts 100% of theirs. Also catches the solver's own misplacements: on-hand 71→78, 72→79, 64→75%.
Render
viz/full_episode_viz.py · viz/hands3d_multi.py --preset right-front --tabletop · viz/overlay_sources.py · viz/hold_hands.py · viz/ego_gate.py
Timing
NEW — every clip plays at real time. Caches are strided (7.5 Hz on ep0/ep1, 15 Hz on ep2) but clips were written at 20 fps, so they ran 1.3–2.7× fast. Playback is driven by timestamp, which also fixed a panel desync. Verified: at 10 s and 20 s all three panels sit at exactly 10.00 s and 20.00 s.
No human hand tracking
The Quest emits 26 OpenXR joints per hand (tracking.jsonl) but 0 of the 160 released human episodes carry it — both merge manifests name already-converted sources. It survives only for a 2-episode, 3.6 s pilot on 260812.
Not shown
