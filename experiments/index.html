Turning on 1 cm jitter moves the three-object mean 23.2% to 22.9% and changes what every number means: cells had saturated at 0/128 and 128/128, and the tennis ball scored 1.4% at the centre pose against 6.4% under jitter, a 4.6x per-object error. Cross-hand re-run at 96 eps/hand: Inspire 25.0% [17.4-34.5] vs Wuji and Sharpa 0/96 [0-3.85], now non-overlapping, mechanism reproducing to within a millimetre of IK residual. running
  • 2026-07-30 Dexterous Point Policy Rebuilding the object points beats the shipped pipeline; the contact-label winner flips by object Rebuilt object points (2048 pts, depth-gradient, sensor-metric fusion) + two finetunes differing only in the contact label. Mean success over bottle/box/ball at 512 eps each: learned tactile 36.8%, human 31.7%, shipped baseline 23.2%. The labels trade places by object and both checkpoints were selected on bottle alone, so no label claim survives. The human annotation turns out to be one grasp bit copied across five fingers. Also: the benchmark has four initial conditions, not 512. done
  • 2026-07-29 Dexterous Point Policy Contact generalizes to PnP/Tool; the 6-point abstraction breaks on hand scale Learned tactile-contact transfers to PnP (F1 0.70) and Tool. DPP in the openarm-sim PnP benchmark: 29.2% with Inspire (reproduces the ~27% baseline bit-for-bit), 0/24 with Wuji (20-DOF) and Sharpa (22-DOF) — finger reach, not DOF count. Rescaling fingertip targets fixes the IK and still scores 0/24, because the policy input is six points too. Two earlier sim claims withdrawn; the bottle cloud smears past 30 cm on 14.2% of fine-tune frames. done
