A Human→Inspire Retargeter Trained on 4-View High-Quality Assembly Data

Working note · 2026-08-24 · R / R⁻¹ trained, sim rollout pending

ABSTRACT We train a GeoRT retargeter R (human 21-keypoint → Inspire qpos) and its inverse R⁻¹ on the bmw_0821 dataset: 60 runs of a BMW assembly task (ecu / bracket / jig / drill), captured with a 4-view hand-pose rig that yields clean, high-precision hand keypoints. From 33,191 valid right-hand frames we obtain a retargeter whose grasp-phase aperture mapping (0–5 cm) tracks the human hand almost 1:1 — the region that matters for this contact-rich task — while R⁻¹ reconstructs the full hand from 5 robot fingertips at 7.7 mm held-out MAE. A closed-loop sim rollout is deferred until an environment is available.

1  Data

The task is BMW sub-assembly (four objects: jig, ecu, bracket, drill) recorded across 4 camera views, giving substantially cleaner hand reconstruction than the single-view / in-the-wild sources used previously. Because the task requires high-precision contact, the hand opens and closes over a wide range and the contact-onset region is heavily sampled — both useful properties for retargeter training.

2.1  Aperture mapping (offline, the key check)

The prior openarm-sim retargeter used vector retargeting, which compressed the hand into a 7–9 cm band and never opened or closed. Here we use position retargeting; the human→robot thumb–index aperture follows almost 1:1 in the grasp region and only saturates at the physically-unreachable open extreme.

Human apertureRobot thumb–index
0.0 cm (closed)2.1 cm
2.7 cm2.6 cm
5.0 cm (mid)4.9 cm
36.0 cm (open, max)10.3 cm  ← robot physical limit

Aperture correlation over 500 random frames: 0.664; robot achieves 0.4–8.8 cm. The grasp-onset region (0–5 cm) — exactly what a high-precision contact task hinges on — is reproduced accurately.

3  R⁻¹ — inverse (5 robot fingertips → 21 human keypoints)

Free pairs: run the human 21kp through R → qpos → FK → 5 robot fingertips; the pair is (robot tips 5×3, human 21kp wrist-local). A small MLP (15→512×3→63) is trained supervised; held-out MAE is split by joint type — fingertips (which R saw) vs. intermediate joints (which R never saw; it measures how much hand-interior structure the tips determine).

Held-out MAEValue
Fingertips12.2 mm
Intermediate joints6.1 mm
All (non-wrist)7.7 mm
Pairs / val split33,191 / 3,319

Fingertip MAE (12.2 mm) is higher than the earlier run101/pnp R⁻¹ (3.6–5.2 mm) because bmw_0821 spans a much wider aperture range — the reconstruction target is genuinely harder, not lower quality. Intermediate joints reconstruct at 6.1 mm, confirming the tips still determine the hand interior.
