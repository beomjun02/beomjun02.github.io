3  Base-work choice: ManipTrans

Chosen over DexTrack (the other candidate) on result quality and assets: highest citations in the functional class, four hands including both Inspire and articulated MANO, released imitator checkpoints, and — decisively — DexManipNet, whose episodes preserve per-frame human↔robot correspondence and hand us functional pairs without paying their 61 per-task RL trainings. For a data engine, per-task residual training is acceptable; amortization is our contribution, not theirs.

Positioning sentence: we adopt ManipTrans' transfer as the data engine and distill its output into a bidirectional per-hand retargeter that composes with a frozen human policy; “functional retargeting” as a problem name follows DexMachina; the amortized-controller lineage is DexTrack's.

Verified compatibility (2026-08-08): ManipTrans' Inspire URDF matches our dex-urdf Inspire on all 12 joint origins to 0.00 mm (same RH56 mechanism; tip sites differ 2.0–4.6 mm; mimic ratios and limits differ across conventions — handled by labeling in the 6 independent joints). The released dataset uses the inspire hand class (Inspire FTP's URDF is not redistributable and ships no public checkpoints).
