Retargeter & teleop-mix Debugging in openarm-sim

Working note · 2026-08-19 session · organized by the rollout clips

SUMMARY A debugging session on the openarm-sim deployment of the 21-keypoint human base policy with a dexret Inspire retargeter. Working from the rollout clips, we found and fixed seven implementation bugs (obs contamination, finger-open compression, object supervision, wrist orientation, …), each verified by measurement. All conditions still land at SR 0/24: the remaining bottleneck is not the retargeter but the policy's alignment/approach and a teleop→sim domain gap. The official metric is success_at_end (bottle, 24 ep, train-cells); qualitative reading is left to the rollout video (not analyzed here).

4  Verdict

The seven implementation-layer bugs were all removed and verified by measurement (finger open/close, wrist orientation, object supervision, obs contamination). Yet every condition remains SR 0/24. The grasp-success bottleneck is not the retargeter but:

  1. Policy alignment / approach — the scratch-150k policy has not learned the grasp phase; a PT-initialized fine-tune is the lever.
  2. teleop→sim domain gap — even teleop-only 100% lands at 0, so the real demo distribution does not match the sim eval distribution.

Both are decisions outside this session's implementation-fix scope (PT-init fine-tune / domain-matched re-extraction).
