DPP-ICLR27 — One Frozen Human Hand Policy, Many Robot Hands

ICLR 2027 (in preparation)

A manipulation policy trained only on human hand data drives multiple dexterous robot hands through small, per-hand retargeters trained with no robot demonstrations and no task data. Pairs are distilled from functional transfer in simulation.
